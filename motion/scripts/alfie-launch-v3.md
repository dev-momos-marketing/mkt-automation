# Momos Alfie v3: "The night shift"

**Format:** ~55s, 16:9 and 9:16, 30fps. Works muted (every beat has on-screen text).
**Voice:** a friend who has worked a Friday rush, talking you through one night. Warm, quick, a little wry. Every line hands off to the next.
**Concept:** one continuous shot through one Friday night. It starts with one missed order at 9:47 PM, scales to every location by morning, and ends on a quiet inbox. **The kitchen is the drum kit:** ticket printers, order bells, knife chops and steam make the beat until the drop.
**Brand:** Momos tokens throughout (Poppins, beige base, ink, brand red for the one thing that matters per scene, olive for "resolved"). Alfie is the real mascot art.
**Sources:** Alfie's behaviour (pulls order history, applies the brand's recovery policy, issues points or a replacement, works across web chat, app, post-visit surveys and delivery, hands over to staff with full context and a suggested next step) is from momos.com/alfie. The guest, order, store, counters and times are illustrative.

## Music: the kitchen is the drum kit (original, composed in code, 120 bpm)

- **Act 1:** the beat is made from the restaurant itself. Ticket-printer chatter is the hi-hat, the order bell is the clap, pan sizzle is the shaker, and the knife on the board is the snare. A low bass pulse creeps in under "Now they're telling everyone".
- **The freeze:** everything cuts to silence on "Unless someone's already on it", with just a steam hiss.
- **Drop 1, "Meet Alfie":** the steamer lid pop is the impact. Stomp-stomp-clap, pumping supersaw chords, a big brass-like lead hook.
- **Act 3:** the groove drops to half-time under the agent trace. Each `✓` is a clean UI tick, the points counter gets a coin cascade, and the stamp lands on the downbeat.
- **Act 4:** double-time energy for the tile wave, then a riser through the time-lapse and a filter sweep into the morning.
- **Act 5:** near silence and a single bell, then **drop 2 a key higher** on "Resolve. Recover. Bring them back." with one bell per word, and a final steamy chord to close.
