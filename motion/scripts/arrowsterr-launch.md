# Arrowsterr v4: "You need a navigator"

**Format:** under 60s, 16:9 and 9:16, 30fps. Works muted.
**Voice:** one friend talking you through a story, in one breath. Every line hands off to the next. Brand rules hold: "you", digits, no em dashes, no emoji, no "not X, Y", no lists of three in prose. Wording: "competitors", "recommend".
**Niche:** a startup buyer asking AI for a CRM. You are **Acme (acme.com)**; competitors **Northwind, Globex, Initech** (the guideline's sample names).
**Concept:** Arrowsterr is the Navigator. One continuous camera over a living flight grid. Act 1 is the problem told fast, Act 2 is a hard stop, Act 3 is the navigator boot-up and the route home, ending on Acme recommended.
**Real AI logos:** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity and Google appear as the engines (LobeHub icon set, MIT), unmodified, to name the engines Arrowsterr tracks.
**Sources:** product claims, step names and keyword data come from the arrowsterr.com homepage. The visibility climb, shortlist, topic values and the final recommendation are illustrative.

## Music (original, composed in code, 120 bpm)

- **Act 1:** filtered pluck arpeggio and pad, keystroke clicks under the question, card snaps, a sonar ping for each engine, a kick creeping in.
- **Act 2:** impact on the tear, then a **tape stop**: the whole track winds down in pitch and speed to silence. Glass shatter, a low drone.
- **Act 3:** HUD blips, then a snare roll from quarters to 32nds, a riser, a reverse-cymbal swell and a stutter into **drop 1** on "Meet Arrowsterr" (pumping supersaws, 4-on-the-floor, sub bass, lead hook).
- **Stations:** the groove under the voice, with split-flap clacks, a vortex whoosh, a laser zap and block thuds.
- **Ship it:** riser into a sonic boom. **Drop 2** on "Recommended", **a semitone higher**, with a chime on the Spark. A final major chord and a crash.

## Brand notes

- Colour, type and copy rules hold everywhere: greys and the grid carry the frame, Signal Blue for the route and payoff words, gold only at the Spark, Night only at the close, every number in Geist Mono.
- The website's motion limits (no spins, parallax or confetti) are deliberately relaxed for this film. Everything still travels up or right.
