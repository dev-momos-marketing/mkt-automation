# momos brand for video

Source: the live momos.com demo form (`lead-routing/framer/LeadForm.tsx`). Replace or
extend with the official brand guide when it's available.

## Palette (mirrored in `src/brand/theme.ts`)

| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| ink | `#15181E` | primary text |
| inkSoft | `#4D4D4D` | secondary text |
| inkMuted | `#8A8784` | meta text, empty stars (derived; confirm) |
| red | `#D73E33` | the one accent |
| coral | `#FF787A` | CTA gradient end only |
| cream | `#FBF8F4` | stage background |
| blush | `#FFF0F0` | selected / success tint |
| white | `#FFFFFF` | cards |

CTA button: `linear-gradient(139deg, #D73E33, #FF787A)` with a coral glow shadow, as on
the website.

## Type

Poppins 400 / 500 / 600 / 700 (OFL, in `public/fonts/`). Headlines 600 with tight
tracking; body 400.

## Shape

White cards, 32px radius on video (20px on web), warm layered shadows
(`rgba(191,187,186,…)`), hairline borders `rgba(21,24,30,0.08)`.
