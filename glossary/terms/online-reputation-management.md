Online reputation management (ORM) covers everything a business does to shape how it appears online: tracking reviews and mentions, replying to guests, fixing what they complain about, and encouraging happy guests to share their experience.

## Why it matters for restaurants

Most guests check reviews and star ratings on Google, maps apps and delivery platforms before choosing where to eat. Your rating, how recent your reviews are, and how you reply all affect whether someone books, walks in or orders.

## The core of ORM

1. **Monitor:** collect reviews and mentions from every platform and location in one place.
2. **Respond:** reply quickly and personally, especially to negative reviews.
3. **Analyse:** use [sentiment analysis](/glossary/sentiment-analysis) to find recurring issues across locations.
4. **Improve:** pass those insights to operations so the same complaint doesn't come back.
5. **Grow:** ask satisfied guests to leave a review, following each platform's rules.

## Common mistakes

- Replying with the same template to every review.
- Only looking at the star average instead of what guests actually say.
- Arguing with reviewers in public.
- Buying or incentivising reviews against platform policies.
