Sentiment analysis is the use of natural language processing (NLP) to work out the opinion behind a piece of text. For restaurants, that text is usually online reviews, survey comments, social media posts and messages.

## How it works

A model reads each piece of feedback and assigns a label (positive, negative, neutral) or a score. More useful systems go further and tag **topics** such as food quality, service, cleanliness, price or wait time, with a sentiment for each.

## What it's used for

- **Spotting trends:** see if complaints about wait times are rising at one location.
- **Prioritising replies:** surface the most negative reviews first.
- **Comparing locations:** find which sites do well on service but poorly on food, or the reverse.
- **Measuring change:** check whether a new menu or staffing change moved guest sentiment.

## Limits to keep in mind

Sarcasm, slang, mixed reviews and other languages are harder to read correctly. Treat sentiment scores as a way to find patterns and read the underlying comments before making big decisions.
