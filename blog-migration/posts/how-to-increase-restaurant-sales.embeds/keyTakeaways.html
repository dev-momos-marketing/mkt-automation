Key takeaways
  • Sales come from three levers that multiply: how many guests you serve, how much each spends and how often they return, so small gains in each add up quickly.
  • Start this week by completing your Google Business Profile and replying to every review, since ratings and visibility now decide whether many guests consider you at all.
  • Raise the average check with menu engineering, specific staff upsells, bundles and limited-time items, and send repeat guests to your own ordering channel to keep the commission.
  • Bring guests back with a digital loyalty program, targeted email and SMS offers based on what they order, and fast recovery when a visit goes wrong.
  • Track guest count, average check and visit frequency separately each week, so you can see whether price, traffic or repeat visits are driving your growth.
