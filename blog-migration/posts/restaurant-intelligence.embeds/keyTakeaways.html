Key takeaways
  • Restaurant intelligence connects sales, labor, inventory, guest feedback and financial data into one view that explains why performance changed and what to fix next.
  • Start with the decisions your team makes every week, then map your data sources, connect them with one shared location ID, and agree on how each metric is calculated.
  • Include guest feedback tagged by location, daypart and theme, because sales data shows what happened while feedback explains how guests experienced it.
  • Give each role a dashboard with only the metrics they can act on, set alerts with a named owner for each metric, and review the numbers on a fixed rhythm.
