4. Restaurant365

Category: End-to-end restaurant management software

Restaurant365 runs the back office of a restaurant management operation on top of the P&L: the general ledger, stock counts, rotas and payroll all live in a single system. For an operator, that means invoices, counts and labor hours land in the books without rekeying, and month-end close moves faster. In 2026 the company added R365 AI, which reads that P&L data to flag problems.

Notable customers: Villa Restaurant Group, Wholly Stromboli and Johnson's Landing.

What's new: Operators who walked the NRA Show floor in May 2026 got a first look at R365 AI, which brings AI dashboards and an AI advisor, a new labor management suite and an early-access AI scheduler. In June 2026 the company named Kate Hastings its Chief Customer Officer as it put more money into customer success.

Key features:

  • Restaurant accounting. Invoice processing, bank reconciliation and P&Ls for each entity are built for how restaurant groups are structured.
  • Food cost variance. Recipe costing and inventory counts produce a theoretical versus actual report, which shows where product is going missing.
  • Sales-linked scheduling. Schedules are built against sales forecasts, and payroll handles tips for hourly staff.
  • Automatic sales import. POS integrations post each day's sales to the general ledger without manual entry.
  • Budgets and royalties. Multi-location reports cover budgets and can track franchise royalty payments.
  • R365 AI advisor. Dashboards and an AI advisor point out shifts in labor, costs and profit that need a manager's attention.

What makes them stand out: Accounting and operations come from the same vendor, so your inventory and labor numbers flow into the P&L without a separate integration layer to babysit.

Pricing: Pricing comes in per-location bundles, paid quarterly or yearly, and larger groups negotiate a custom quote. Dollar amounts differ across the vendor's own web pages, so get the current number from sales.

It's a great fit for: Restaurant groups and franchisees with several units whose bookkeepers and managers spend too long reconciling separate systems.
