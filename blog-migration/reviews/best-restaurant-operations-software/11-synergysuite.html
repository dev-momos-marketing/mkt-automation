11. SynergySuite

Category: Enterprise Back-of-House Management Suite

Still independently owned, SynergySuite sells a modular back-of-house platform to chains and franchise groups. You pick the modules you need, from inventory and purchasing to labor, food safety and cash controls, and run them the same way at every location. AI forecasting now drives its suggested orders and schedules.

Notable customers: Playa Bowls, Church's Chicken, Friendly's Restaurants and Shipley Do-Nuts.

What's new: SynergySuite added $12 million of funding in August 2025, earmarked for building out AI across the platform. A month later, Playa Bowls, with more than 200 locations, picked it to run food and labor management and automate inventory and purchasing.

Key features:

  • Stock across stores. Counts and live stock levels are tracked for every location from one account.
  • Suggested orders. AI demand forecasts generate order suggestions, so managers adjust a draft instead of building one from scratch.
  • Food cost variance. Recipe costing compares theoretical and actual food cost to expose waste or theft.
  • Labor and attendance. AI-assisted scheduling works with time and attendance tracking.
  • Food safety and cash. Compliance checklists sit next to cash management with controls on voids and refunds.
  • Chain reporting. Multi-location reports draw on POS integrations, including Toast and PAR Brink.

Pricing: Custom; SynergySuite quotes each account through sales.
