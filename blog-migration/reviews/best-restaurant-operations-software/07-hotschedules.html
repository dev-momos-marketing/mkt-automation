7. HotSchedules

Category: Enterprise Labor Forecasting & Workforce Analytics

HotSchedules is the scheduling product inside Fourth, the company it merged with in 2019, and it now sits alongside Fourth's inventory, HR and payroll modules. It is aimed at larger groups where getting labor right at hundreds of stores matters more than a simple shift calendar. Managers get forecasts, schedules and alerts through the Fourth iQ app.

Notable customers: Noodles & Company, Texas Corral, FreeRange Concepts and Snooze, an A.M. Eatery.

What's new: Since July 2025, a concert or play with tickets on sale near a store feeds into that store's AI forecast by default. Fourth iQ 2.0 arrived that September with a manager app, and the November 2025 update (2.2) warns managers before they run short of inventory.

Key features:

  • AI labor forecasts. Demand is projected from sales history, weather and local events, so schedules start from expected traffic.
  • Scored schedules. Each draft schedule gets a score for coverage and cost before a manager publishes it.
  • Shift marketplace. Hourly staff post and pick up shifts, flag the days they're free, and keep shift chatter in one app.
  • Compliance controls. Time and attendance includes rules that help you follow local labor laws.
  • Scheduled versus actual. Labor budget reports show where actual hours ran over plan.
  • Manager alerts. The Fourth iQ App suggests actions across staffing, HR and inventory on phone, tablet or web.

Pricing: Fourth doesn't publish prices; quotes come from its sales team.
