13. Mitti

Restaurant teams who used SafetyCulture will find the same app under a new brand: in August 2026 the company renamed itself Mitti, and it now describes itself as one operating system for frontline work. For restaurants, that means mobile inspections and checklists for food safety, hygiene and brand standards, with the issues they uncover assigned and followed up in the same app. It also offers training, sensors and asset tracking, plus a free tier for small teams.

Notable customers: Fishbowl, Trippas White Group, Sodexo and Snooze, an A.M. Eatery.

What's new: Alongside the August 2026 rename came new AI tools (an AI Assistant, issues drafted by AI and monitoring agents) and 5 Star Benchmarking, a one-number rating for every site in a group. In April 2026 it brought on the team behind Twine, a Sydney AI startup, and a pricing tracker noted in July 2026 that all plans, free included, now come with a monthly allowance of AI credits.

Key features:

  • Mobile inspections. Staff fill in checklists on a phone, adding photos, comments and scores; public templates cover common food safety audits.
  • Corrective actions. Problems found during an inspection become tasks with an owner and a due date.
  • Site benchmarking. Analytics compare locations, and 5 Star Benchmarking rolls each site into a single score.
  • Bite-size training. Courses are broken into lessons that new hires can finish during a quiet half hour.
  • Sensors and assets. Temperature sensors send alerts, and equipment maintenance records are kept per asset.
  • AI help. AI can log issues on its own, answer questions and watch for routine problems through monitoring agents.

Pricing: Teams of 10 or fewer can use it free. Premium is listed by outside directories at roughly $24 per seat monthly with annual billing, or $29 without a contract. Cheaper Lite seats suit employees whose only job in the app is inspections and lessons. Enterprise is quoted site by site. Check these with Mitti, since its own pricing page hasn't been confirmed since the rename.
