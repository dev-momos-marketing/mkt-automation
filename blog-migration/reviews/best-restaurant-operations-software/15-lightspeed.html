15. Lightspeed

Category: Restaurant cloud POS & multi-location management software

Lightspeed Commerce (NYSE/TSX: LSPD) makes Lightspeed Restaurant, known in Europe as the K-Series. It handles the dining room in detail, with floor plans, coursing, pay-at-table and kitchen routing, and adds inventory and group reporting behind it. Since 2026, Lightspeed AI lets operators ask questions about their sales in plain language.

Here's how the Lightspeed product suite fits together:

Notable customers: Five Guys (Koeppel Companies franchise), Dean & David and Cafe Lola (Johnson City, Tenn.).

What's new: The March 2026 update gave managers Lightspeed Tasks for digital checklists and improved table pacing and bookings. In January 2026 it launched Lightspeed AI with conversational assistants, and in November 2025 it introduced Tempo, a pacing tool that reads the room in real time and shows how full it is, how orders are moving and which tables are slow.

Key features:

  • Floor and coursing control. The iPad POS manages table layouts and fires courses in order.
  • Pay where you sit. Guests settle the bill at their table, and servers can accept taps on a phone through Lightspeed Payments.
  • Station-by-station KDS. Grill items go to the grill screen and cold items to the garde manger, so tickets don't need sorting.
  • Inventory and recipes. Stock and recipe tracking feed menu performance reports.
  • Group menus and reports. Multi-location operators manage menus centrally and see consolidated results.
  • Service pacing. Tempo shows full-service teams where tables are moving slowly, though availability varies by region and plan.

What makes them stand out: Lightspeed puts a lot of detail into running a full-service dining room, and the newer pacing and checklist tools reach beyond the register into how a shift is run.

Pricing: In the US, Essential starts at $189 per month and Premium at $399 per month, plus card fees and any extras you add, like reservations or more KDS screens. Larger groups get custom pricing, and prices vary by region.

It's a great fit for: Table-service restaurants and bars, from one room to a growing group, that care about pacing the dining room and want payments built into the POS.
