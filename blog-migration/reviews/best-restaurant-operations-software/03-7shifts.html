3. 7Shifts

Category: Restaurant workforce & employee management software

Built solely for restaurant teams, 7shifts covers the work a manager does around every shift: building the schedule, clocking people in, splitting tips and sending hours to payroll. In 2026 it added training courses, so onboarding a new server can happen in the same app that holds their shifts.

Notable customers: HCI Hospitality (Freddy's Frozen Custard & Steakburgers franchisee), Boston Pizza (Regina), Conshy Girls Restaurant Group and Pitango Gelato.

What's new: September 2026 brought Employee Training to the Premium tier: a manager builds a lesson with a short quiz, sends it to every line cook (or every new hire at one store) and checks who has passed. Its current plan lineup of Comp, Essentials, Pro and Premium took effect in July 2025.

Key features:

  • Forecast-based scheduling. The drag-and-drop builder shows projected sales and labor, so you can staff a slow Tuesday differently from a busy Friday.
  • Clock-in rules. The time clock can block early punches through POS and payroll integrations, which keeps unplanned overtime down.
  • Labor against sales. You can watch labor percentage climb or fall during the week, well before payroll closes.
  • Tip handling. Pooling rules, tip calculations and payouts are managed in the app, with payroll offered as an add-on.
  • Staff app. Team members swap shifts, update availability and message each other from their phones.
  • Shift checklists. Managers can assign opening and closing tasks to each shift and see what got done.

What makes them stand out: Every screen is designed around hourly restaurant teams, from tip pooling to role-based training, so you don't have to bend a general HR tool to fit a kitchen.

Pricing: Comp is the free tier, limited to a single site with no more than 15 staff. On a yearly contract, Essentials is $39.99, Pro $79.99 and Premium $134.99 per location per month (month-to-month: $44.99, $89.99, $149.99). Enterprise needs a quote. Payroll costs extra, and you can test Pro free for 14 days.

It's a great fit for: Independents and multi-unit groups that want to keep labor costs in check and get scheduling, tips and payroll out of spreadsheets.
