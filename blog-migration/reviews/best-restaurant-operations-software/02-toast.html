2. Toast

Category: Restaurant point of sale (POS) & operations software

Toast is a publicly traded company (NYSE: TOST) whose system starts at the register and reaches into the kitchen, the payroll run and the marketing calendar. For an operations team, the appeal is that orders, menus, staff hours and guest data sit with one vendor, so a menu change or a new hire only has to be entered once. By its Q2 2026 results, about 180,000 locations were live on the system.

Notable customers: Captain Parker's Pub, Kaldi's Coffee and Craft Lounge.

What's new: Toast's May 2026 product drop added Toast IQ Grow, a paid upgrade in which campaigns are handled by an AI Marketing Agent with backup from a human marketing success manager; the same release rebuilt the Toast Local app for guests around loyalty. In July 2026, Toast named a new advisory board of independent restaurant customers for 2026-2027.

Key features:

  • Hardware for every station. Orders can be taken on counter terminals, self-service kiosks or Toast Go handhelds that servers carry to the table.
  • KDS on the line. Cooks read orders off screens at each station, so paper chits stop getting lost under the pass.
  • Central menu control. Multi-unit groups can push menu items and price changes to every location from one place.
  • Staff and payroll. Scheduling runs on Sling by Toast, and payroll draws on the same employee records the POS uses.
  • Off-premise ordering. Web orders, third-party delivery and guest-app orders all land in one kitchen queue.
  • Group reporting. Owners can compare sales and labor across sites without exporting data from separate systems.

What makes them stand out: Because Toast handles the front of house, the kitchen and the team in one product, you have fewer integrations to maintain and fewer places where data can fall out of sync.

Pricing: Expect a quote from Toast's sales reps. A free starter kit and paid tiers have both appeared on toasttab.com, so look at the live pricing page and make sure hardware is in the number you're given.

It's a great fit for: Single restaurants through franchise groups that would rather run ordering, payroll and guest marketing on one vendor's system.
