8. Sling

Category: Free-First Employee Scheduling & Team Communication

Toast bought Sling, a low-cost app for staff schedules and team messages, in 2021. You can buy it on its own for any shift-based business, and Toast POS customers find a version of it inside Toast as Sling by Toast. For a small restaurant, it covers the weekly rota, shift trades and staff announcements without a large monthly bill.

Notable customers: Sling doesn't publish a list of restaurant customers.

What's new: Sling hasn't announced any dated product releases in 2025 or 2026; its recent development shows up mostly in the Toast integration.

Key features:

  • Template scheduling. Drag shifts onto a calendar and save weeks as templates you can reuse when the pattern repeats.
  • Swaps and time off. Staff pick up open shifts, trade with coworkers and request time off from their phones.
  • Time clock. Paid plans add clock-in and clock-out, so hours worked can be checked against the schedule.
  • Overtime watch. Paid plans also track labor cost and flag shifts that would push someone into overtime.
  • Team feed. Messaging, a newsfeed and task lists keep shift notes in one place.
  • Toast sync. Employees, wages, positions and POS sales flow from Toast into schedules.

Pricing: The free plan covers scheduling, capped at 30 users for accounts not opened through Toast. Premium costs $2.00 and Business $4.00 per user per month, or $1.70 and $3.40 on annual billing, with a 15-day trial. Toast POS packages include Sling Lite, and Sling Pro is sold through Toast Shop.
