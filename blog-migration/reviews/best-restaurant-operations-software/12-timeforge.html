12. TimeForge

Category: AI Labor Forecasting & Scheduling

TimeForge is a labor management system for restaurants and retailers, founded in 1998 and based in Lubbock, Texas. Its job is the hourly workforce: building schedules, recording hours and keeping you on the right side of labor law. Since August 2024 it has included Lineup.ai's AI forecasting, which predicts sales and staffing needs from your POS history. That gives quick-service and casual dining groups a schedule built from expected demand, with alerts when labor runs over.

Notable customers: Hard Eight BBQ and Great Harvest Bread Co. (Great Harvest Franchising, preferred vendor).

What's new: Square sellers gained a TimeForge app in July 2025, which reads Square sales and uses them to forecast labor and set how many people each shift needs.

Key features:

  • 15-minute forecasts. Lineup.ai's models project sales and labor needs in 15-minute blocks from your POS data.
  • Auto-built schedules. Schedules are generated to match forecast demand, which managers then review and adjust.
  • Over-target alerts. Punches are recorded as staff arrive, and a manager gets a live warning once hours pass the target for the day.
  • Labor law support. The system applies overtime rules and fair workweek (predictive scheduling) laws while you build the week.
  • Self-service for staff. Staff handle their own availability, trades and leave requests, which saves the manager a stream of texts.
  • Labor versus sales. Area managers see each store's labor spend next to its revenue, with sales pulled from Toast, Square and other POS systems.

Pricing: Custom; contact TimeForge for a quote.
