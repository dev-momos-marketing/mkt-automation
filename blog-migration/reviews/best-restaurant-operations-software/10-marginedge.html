10. MarginEdge

Category: Real-Time Food Cost and Back-Office Automation

Every morning, MarginEdge takes the invoices piling up in the office and the sales in your POS and turns them into a daily food cost number. A single subscription bundles paying bills, placing orders, costing recipes and handling invoices, so a manager can see cost creep this week instead of after the books close. The company reached 10,000 restaurant customers in 2025.

Notable customers: Skopos Hospitality Group, Crooked Hammock Brewery, South Block and The Tavern at Ivy City Smokehouse.

What's new: MarginEdge closed $80 million in August 2026, a Series D mixing equity with debt, with Schooner Capital and Ten Coves Capital leading; the money is earmarked for AI tools built from the purchasing data passing through it. The company turned 10 in April 2025.

Key features:

  • Hands-off invoice entry. Invoices arrive as phone photos, emails or EDI files, and MarginEdge's team does the data entry.
  • Daily food cost. POS sales and invoice data combine into food and beverage cost you can check each morning.
  • Supplier payments. Paying vendors happens inside MarginEdge, and the subscription already covers it.
  • Order guides. Ordering from vendors runs off saved order guides for each location.
  • Price alerts. You choose the items to watch and get notified when their price changes.
  • Budgets and accounting sync. Budget and P&L screens push to QuickBooks and similar accounting software, with integrations for dozens of POS brands.

Pricing: Each location pays the same monthly fee no matter how many invoices you send or bills you pay. MarginEdge's own page has displayed $330, while third-party trackers in 2026 list $350, with Freepour (bar inventory) at $150 more. Check the live page before budgeting.
