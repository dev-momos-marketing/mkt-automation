5. MarketMan

Category: Restaurant inventory & food cost management software

MarketMan handles the purchasing side of restaurant operations: stock counts, supplier orders, invoices and recipe costs. When an invoice comes in at a higher price, the cost of every dish that uses that ingredient updates, which helps you catch margin drift before the monthly P&L does. Since 2026 it is also sold inside Square's platform.

Notable customers: Westville Restaurant Group, ATFC, Ltd. and Taco del Mar (multi-unit franchisee).

What's new: Since April 2026, restaurants on Square in every market can switch on Square Restaurant Inventory by MarketMan, which gives them ingredient and recipe tools, purchase orders, vendor records, invoice scanning and waste logging to Square accounts.

Key features:

  • Location-level counts. Staff count stock by location on a phone or tablet, and on-hand levels update as orders and sales come through.
  • Live recipe costs. Recipes and menu items are costed from the latest ingredient prices, so plate margins stay current.
  • Supplier ordering. Purchase orders can be generated automatically and sent to vendors from the app.
  • Invoice scanning. Scanned invoices update item prices and trigger an alert when a supplier raises a price.
  • Waste logs. Recording spoilage and comps shows which items you lose most often.
  • COGS reporting. POS and accounting integrations feed food cost reports across all your locations.

What makes them stand out: MarketMan concentrates on purchasing and food cost, so the inventory workflow goes deeper than the inventory module in most all-in-one suites.

Pricing: MarketMan doesn't publish current prices; plans are quoted per location, so ask its sales team for a figure.

It's a great fit for: Independents and restaurant groups that want tighter control of ordering, waste and food cost, including operators already on Square.
