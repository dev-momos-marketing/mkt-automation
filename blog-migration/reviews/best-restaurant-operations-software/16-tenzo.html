16. Tenzo

Category: POS sales, labor, inventory, reservation analytics

From its base in London, Tenzo connects to the tools a restaurant group already owns and lays their data side by side: POS sales, labor, inventory, reservations and reviews. It doesn't count stock or cost recipes itself; its operational value is a sales forecast that can feed your scheduling tool, plus daily reports that tell each manager how yesterday went.

Notable customers: Lettuce Entertain You, PrimoHoagies, Parker Restaurant Group and Pizza Pilgrims.

What's new: Edge VC led the first close, $5M, of an extension to Tenzo's Series A in April 2026, aimed at doubling its integrations within 18 months and expanding in the US. Jane O'Riordan became chair of its board.

Key features:

  • Single view of the business. Sales, labor, stock, bookings and reviews appear together for each site.
  • Demand forecasts. Each site gets a sales prediction that adjusts for the weather, public holidays and nearby events.
  • Forecasts into rotas. Those numbers flow into Planday, Deputy and other rota apps, so shifts are planned against the forecast.
  • Site comparisons. Benchmarks rank locations to show which ones lag and which ones lead.
  • Scheduled reports. Daily and weekly summaries go out automatically to managers.
  • AI assistant access. Through an MCP connector, you can ask an AI assistant about Tenzo figures in plain language.

Pricing: Custom; Tenzo doesn't publish prices.
