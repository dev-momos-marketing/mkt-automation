14. TouchBistro

Category: POS-Based Sales & Performance Reporting

Running on iPads, TouchBistro gives full-service restaurants a POS with sales and staff reports included, and you can bolt on payments, reservations, online ordering, inventory or labor tools. In July 2026 it was acquired by Harris, part of Constellation Software, and it continues to sell under the TouchBistro name. For an independent operator, it handles table service and daily reporting without a separate analytics tool.

Notable customers: Café Crêpe (multi-location group with central commissary), Uncle Tetsu (multi-unit QSR, North America), Koyo (sushi, Queens NY) and Masseria.

What's new: Harris completed its purchase of TouchBistro in July 2026 and moved the whole team in, promising continued product investment; the price was not made public.

Key features:

  • Tableside ordering. Servers take orders on iPads and manage the floor plan from the same screen.
  • Sales from anywhere. Cloud reports break down sales by hour, item and staff member while you're away from the restaurant.
  • Menu mix. You'll see which plates carry the menu and which might come off at the next reprint.
  • Shift and server results. Reports compare servers and shifts, which helps with coaching and section planning.
  • Exception tracking. Discounts, voids and refunds are logged, so unusual patterns are easy to spot.
  • Optional modules. Integrated payments, kitchen display, reservations, loyalty, inventory and labor tools can be added as you need them.

Pricing: The POS software alone is $69 a month for each location, before hardware. A bundle called Essentials, which adds payments and hardware, shows $119 a month on the pricing page and $99 on the homepage, so ask which applies. Extras are charged separately; enterprise deals are quoted.
