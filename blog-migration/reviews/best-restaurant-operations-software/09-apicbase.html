9. ApicBase

Category: Inventory and Food Cost analytics

At the heart of Apicbase sits a shared recipe library, used by multi-site food businesses to run the back of house. Once a recipe is entered, its cost, allergens, stock usage and supplier orders all follow from it, which suits groups with central kitchens or hotel F&B outlets. Apicbase now markets the platform as AI-powered.

Notable customers: Poule & Poulette, Pentahotels and The Avocado Show.

What's new: Apicbase hasn't published dated product releases recently. Its most recent dated milestone is an event: APX·25, first held in November 2025, which brought together executives from hotels, foodservice and tech partners.

Key features:

  • One recipe library. Recipes and ingredients are stored centrally, and costs recalculate for each recipe and menu as prices move.
  • Menu engineering and allergens. Profitability and allergen details come from the recipe data you already keep.
  • Multi-site stock. Counts and stock levels are tracked for every location in the group.
  • Purchasing. Supplier records and purchase orders are handled in the same system as recipes.
  • Production planning. Central kitchens can plan batches for the outlets they supply.
  • Connected sales. Sales arrive from Square, Deliverect and other ordering tools to drive forecasts and food cost reports.

Pricing: Not published. Apicbase prices each deal by site count and the modules chosen.
