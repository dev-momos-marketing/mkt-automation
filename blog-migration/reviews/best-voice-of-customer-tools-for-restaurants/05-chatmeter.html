5. Chatmeter

For a voice of customer program, Chatmeter covers the public side: online reviews, business listings and local visibility for chains with many locations. In September 2025 it was acquired from PSG by Alchemer, which makes survey and feedback software, and the product carries the name Alchemer Reputation Management today. That pairing lets restaurant chains read reviews next to the survey feedback Alchemer collects.

Notable customers: A&W, MR MIKES Steakhouse Casual and Driven Brands.

What's new: March 2026 brought Alchemer's an AI Auto-Responder that drafts and posts on-brand replies according to rules for rating, platform and wording, while routing touchy reviews to a human before anything is posted. In July 2025, before the sale, Chatmeter shipped Pulse Ai: Competitive Intelligence, whose regional insights map zooms into individual stores.

What you can do with Chatmeter:

  • Watch reviews from more than a hundred sites. One dashboard covers 100+ review sites, so you don't log into each platform separately to see what guests wrote.
  • Count off-premise guests. Delivery-app ratings from DoorDash, Grubhub and Uber Eats flow in with your dine-in reviews, which matters if off-premise is a big share of sales.
  • Query feedback by typing a question. With Pulse Ai: Signals you can ask what guests are saying across reviews, social and surveys, and it even looks at review photos.
  • Benchmark against nearby rivals. Competitive Intelligence compares each location with competitors in its area, so a dip can be read in local context.
  • Fix the basics per store. Each location gets its own listings upkeep and local SEO tracking, and review generation tools help you build up recent reviews.

Why Chatmeter stands out: Public reviews, listings, competitor data and, through Alchemer, direct surveys all sit in one enterprise platform, which gives you both what guests post and what they tell you privately.

It's best for: Franchisors and chains with many stores that would like one vendor for reviews, listings, AI analysis of guest comments and surveys.

Pricing: Quoted per account; Alchemer doesn't post a price list.
