2. Medallia

Built for big brands, Medallia is an experience management system that pulls guest and employee feedback from surveys, public reviews, contact-center calls and digital channels into one program. Its AI groups what guests say into themes, then hands follow-up tasks to the managers who can fix things at each restaurant. In June 2026 a lender group led by Blackstone, Apollo and FS KKR took over ownership from Thoma Bravo, and the product continues to be sold and developed.

Notable customers: Noodles & Company and Best Western.

What's new: At Experience '26 in February 2026, Medallia showed AI topic modeling and action planning built into the screens front-line teams use. Its September 2026 release widened multilingual analysis, let teams distribute surveys themselves and previewed agentic automation, while the June ownership deal added $150 million in fresh capital.

What you can do with Medallia:

  • Hear guests on every channel. Surveys can go out on the web, by email or SMS, through a QR code, in your app or printed on the receipt.
  • Combine solicited and public feedback. Review and social listening runs next to your own surveys, so the voice of the customer includes people who never answer a survey.
  • Spot urgent issues early. Text analytics read open comments for themes and sentiment and flag anything that needs a same-day response from the store.
  • Listen to the call center too. Speech and conversation analytics turn phone and chat interactions into the same kind of themed insight as survey answers.
  • Give managers a plan. Frontline-Ready AI summarizes each location's feedback and suggests actions, and closed-loop cases track whether someone actually followed up with the guest.

Why Medallia stands out: Its role-based dashboards compare restaurants, regions and brands side by side, and every alert feeds a case that a named person owns, which suits chains that need proof each complaint was handled.

It's best for: Large multi-location restaurant and hospitality brands with a CX team running a formal feedback program; independents will find it more than they need.

Pricing: Medallia doesn't publish prices; you get a custom enterprise quote from its sales team.
