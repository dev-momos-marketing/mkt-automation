11. Thematic

Thematic is an AI analytics layer that sits on top of the surveys, reviews and support data you already collect. It sorts open-ended comments into themes with sentiment attached, and its generative tool, Answers, lets you question that feedback in plain language. It doesn't collect feedback itself, so you pair it with your existing survey or review tools.

Notable customers: DoorDash, Greyhound, Sky TV and Melodics.

What's new: In January 2025, Thematic released tools built on the Model Context Protocol (MCP) that bring its insights into Claude, ChatGPT and Microsoft Copilot. It hasn't shared dated product news from 2026.

What you can do with Thematic:

  • Analyze every source together. Surveys, reviews, support tickets, chat logs and social comments are pooled, so a theme like slow pickup shows up wherever guests mention it.
  • Skip manual tagging. Themes and sub-themes are found automatically, and your team can rename or merge them to match how you talk about the business.
  • Catch rising issues. Emerging-theme detection flags topics that are growing, such as a new complaint after a recipe change.
  • Ask Answers a question. Type something like what guests say about portion sizes and get a summary with charts and verbatim quotes.
  • Link themes to scores. Filter by location, segment, period or channel and see which themes pull NPS or CSAT up or down.

Why Thematic stands out: It focuses on analysis alone, so it can plug into platforms you already pay for, including Qualtrics and QuestionPro, and it carries SOC 2 security for teams with strict IT reviews.

It's best for: Larger restaurant brands with an insights or CX team that already gather thousands of written comments and need them read at scale; single locations will find the pricing steep.

Pricing: Thematic doesn't publish prices; pricing depends on feedback volume and is quoted by sales.
