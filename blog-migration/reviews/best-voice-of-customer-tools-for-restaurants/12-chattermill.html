12. Chattermill

Chattermill is a customer experience intelligence platform that gathers feedback from surveys, reviews, support conversations and social media into one dataset. Its own AI, Lyra, tags each comment by topic and sentiment so you can see which parts of the experience guests praise or criticize. It's a general CX analytics tool used by large consumer brands, with no restaurant-specific product.

Notable customers: HelloFresh, Uber, Wise and Limehome.

What's new: In September 2025, Chattermill added Observations and Highlights, which surface shifts in feedback without anyone running a report, and Ask Lyra for questioning the data in everyday language. It hasn't published dated product news since then.

What you can do with Chattermill:

  • Merge every feedback stream. NPS and other surveys, app and public reviews, support tickets and social comments feed a single dataset.
  • Score topics separately. Lyra tags themes and the sentiment on each, so praise for the food and complaints about wait times don't cancel out in one average.
  • Get told when things change. Observations and Highlights flag emerging issues automatically, which helps you spot a problem at one market before it spreads.
  • Slice by market or location. Segment results by country, site, customer type or period, and give each team a dashboard for its role.
  • Connect AI agents. APIs and MCP support let other tools use the analyzed data, and Lyra Agent, in early preview, runs automated analysis across the platform.

Why Chattermill stands out: Topic-level sentiment across many channels shows which specific parts of the visit move satisfaction, which suits groups operating in several countries with a lot of feedback.

It's best for: Large restaurant and hospitality groups with high feedback volume across channels and an insights or CX team ready to act on it.

Pricing: Pricing is custom and isn't listed on Chattermill's site.
