3. Qualtrics XM

Qualtrics XM is a research-heavy platform where restaurant chains can run guest surveys, review monitoring, brand studies and employee programs side by side. For a voice of customer program, the useful pieces are location dashboards, an online reputation module and AI Experience Agents that act on what guests report. Silver Lake's 2023 buyout made the company private, and it remains so.

Notable customers: KFC and Shake Shack.

What's new: Jason Maynard's first X4 as CEO, held in Seattle in March 2026, brought word that Experience Agents now work for paying customers, plus new agents that step in on service recovery as complaints arrive. Qualtrics then wrapped up its Press Ganey Forsta deal, worth $6.75 billion, in May 2026. Because Press Ganey mostly serves healthcare providers, restaurant buyers see no change.

What you can do with Qualtrics XM:

  • Ask guests right after the visit. Surveys sent after a transaction can reach people by text, email, a web link, your app, chat or a QR code, so you catch the experience while it's fresh.
  • Read reviews and surveys together. The reputation module brings Google and other public reviews into the same data your surveys feed.
  • Find what drives loyalty. Predictive analytics estimate which parts of the visit move satisfaction most and which guests look likely to stop coming back.
  • Recover guests automatically. An Experience Agent can pick up a poor score as it arrives and start a service recovery step without waiting for a manager.
  • Serve each level of the business. A GM sees one store, a market leader sees a cluster, and executives see the whole system, each on a dashboard built for that role.

Why Qualtrics XM stands out: Brand studies and staff engagement programs can live on the platform that holds your guest data, and connectors for Genesys, NICE and Salesforce carry insights into the contact-center and CRM tools your teams already work in.

It's best for: Sizable chains and hospitality companies staffed with dedicated insights or CX staff who want one enterprise program for surveys, reviews and operational feedback.

Pricing: No public price list exists for the CX products, so expect a custom quote after talking to Qualtrics.
