6. Birdeye

Birdeye pitches itself today as agentic software for brands with many locations, handling reviews, business listings, guest messaging, social posts, surveys and how often you show up in AI answer engines. AI agents, and newer "AI Coworkers" built from them, carry out much of the daily work. For voice of customer, its Surveys and Insights AI modules turn reviews and survey answers into sentiment trends by location.

Notable customers: The Saxton Group (a McAlister's Deli franchisee) and Black Bear Diner.

What's new: View 2026 in September 2026 was where Birdeye debuted AI Coworkers, with Jay covering marketing, Myna operations and Robin customer experience; the same month it added a SkyTab POS integration so restaurant menus stay in sync with listings. A year earlier it had released its agentic platform, and in January 2025 a restaurant edition trained on industry data with Yelp review monitoring.

What you can do with Birdeye:

  • Send each complaint to its GM. Google, Yelp, Facebook and industry-site reviews are monitored, and a ticket sends each negative one to the manager of that location.
  • Ask for more reviews. Requests go out by text and email after a visit, which grows the volume of guest opinions you have to analyze.
  • Measure sentiment by district. Surveys and Insights AI score what guests say and roll results up from single stores to districts and regions.
  • Answer guests in one inbox. SMS, webchat, social messages and email land together, so a question about a missing item isn't lost in a separate app.
  • Let agents draft replies. Review responses can be written by AI or handled end to end by an agent, in a voice that matches your brand.

Why Birdeye stands out: It folds review handling, surveys, messaging and listings into one product and adds competitor benchmarking, so a regional manager can see how a location compares with the restaurants around it.

It's best for: Franchise groups and growing chains that would rather have AI agents do the routine reputation and messaging work across all their stores.

Pricing: Birdeye shares pricing only after a demo; nothing is listed on its site.
