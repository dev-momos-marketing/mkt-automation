7. Reputation.com

Reputation.com has rebranded as Reputation, and it still runs from the reputation.com domain. On one platform it handles public reviews, guest surveys, listings, social media and AI search presence for brands with many locations, including restaurant and hospitality groups. Founded two decades ago, it celebrated that 20th anniversary in March 2026, and its home base is San Ramon, California.

Notable customers: Tortilla and Mitchells & Butlers.

What's new: In May 2026, Reputation released Reputation IQ for asking plain-English questions of your data, and gave its AI Reputation Manager wider coverage of what Perplexity, Gemini and ChatGPT say when guests ask about your restaurants. September 2025 saw new AI products for brand intelligence, after a January 2025 update aimed at deeper customer insights.

What you can do with Reputation.com:

  • Trigger review asks from real visits. Requests go out by SMS, email or QR code after dine-in, delivery or POS events, so reviews reflect recent guests.
  • Pair surveys with public reviews. Customer feedback from your own surveys sits next to Google, Yelp, Facebook and Tripadvisor reviews in one inbox.
  • Group comments into themes. Text analytics sort what guests write into topics such as service or cleanliness and track the sentiment on each.
  • Interrogate the data. Reputation IQ lets an operator type a question like which stores slipped on cleanliness and get an answer from the combined feedback.
  • Check how AI search sees you. Location Presence Insights, an AI tool added in 2026, reports how each restaurant appears when guests ask an assistant for somewhere to eat.

Why Reputation.com stands out: Reviews, surveys, listings and social live under one enterprise login, which helps a central team report consistently across a large estate of restaurants.

It's best for: Restaurant and hospitality brands with many sites that want reviews, surveys and listings run from headquarters with enterprise-level reporting.

Pricing: Reputation sells on custom contracts and doesn't publish prices.
