4. InMoment

InMoment combines guest surveys, online review management, social listening and AI analysis of text and conversations, so a chain can study every kind of guest comment in one place. Press Ganey Forsta acquired it in May 2025, and it now operates as "InMoment, a PG Forsta company" under its own name. Restaurant brands use it mainly for location-level reporting across large franchise systems.

Notable customers: Jack in the Box, The Melting Pot and The Coffee Club.

What's new: In July 2025, InMoment released AI Auto Responding, which writes review replies based on each review's sentiment and your brand guidelines, then posts them or holds them for approval. The company hasn't announced dated product news in 2026 so far.

What you can do with InMoment:

  • Survey guests after they leave. Email, SMS, web and app surveys fire automatically once an order or visit is complete.
  • Handle reviews from one screen. Google and other review sites are monitored in a single dashboard, with AI drafting replies you can publish directly or approve first.
  • See themes across all feedback. Text analytics surface topics, sentiment and intent whether the comment came from a survey, a review or a social post.
  • Include the contact center. Conversation analytics bring calls and chats with guests into the same analysis as written feedback.
  • Coach each restaurant. Frontline reports show a location's top positive and negative drivers, while brand leaders compare scores by region.

Why InMoment stands out: Structured survey scores and unstructured review text land in one view, which helps you check whether what guests rate privately matches what they post in public.

It's best for: Big multi-location restaurant brands and franchise systems that want surveys, reviews and call-center feedback analyzed together with reporting down to each store.

Pricing: Not published; InMoment prices each contract individually.
