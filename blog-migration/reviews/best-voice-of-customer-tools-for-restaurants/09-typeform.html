9. Typeform

Typeform is a general form and survey builder whose signature is showing guests one question at a time, which keeps a feedback survey feeling short on a phone. Recent releases added AI that drafts forms from a prompt and summarizes open-text answers. It has no restaurant-specific features, so it works best as a lightweight way to hear from guests.

Notable customers: Barry's, Calm, Designity and Double Denim Marketing, all from Typeform's own case studies outside hospitality.

What's new: At Typeforum in October 2025, Typeform launched an AI engagement platform with Typeform AI in beta, which builds a form and its follow-up workflow from a chat prompt and can ask respondents clarifying questions mid-form. Its Winter 2025 release in February 2025 brought AI sentiment analysis and insight summaries.

What you can do with Typeform:

  • Keep surveys light for guests. The one-at-a-time layout suits a quick post-meal check-in that someone fills out before leaving the parking lot.
  • Branch on the answer. Conditional logic shows a different follow-up to a guest who rated the food poorly than to one who loved it.
  • Start from a template. Restaurant and hotel feedback templates, or the AI form builder, give you a working draft in minutes.
  • Meet guests where they are. Share the survey by link or email, embed it on your site, or print a QR code for the table or receipt.
  • Read comments faster. AI sentiment analysis and summaries condense open-text answers, and integrations push responses to Google Sheets, HubSpot, Salesforce, Slack, Mailchimp or Zapier.

Why Typeform stands out: Few survey tools look this polished out of the box, and from the Plus plan up you can remove Typeform's logo so the form carries only your restaurant's branding.

It's best for: Smaller restaurant groups and marketing teams that want good-looking feedback, catering or event forms quickly and can live without POS links, location benchmarking or closed-loop alerts.

Pricing: According to Typeform's pricing page, a free plan covers 10 responses a month. Billed yearly, Basic is $28/month for 100 responses, Plus $56 for 1,000 and Business $91 for 10,000; monthly billing costs $39, $79 or $129, and Enterprise is quoted.
