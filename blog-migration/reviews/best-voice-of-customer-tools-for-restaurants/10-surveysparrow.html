10. SurveySparrow

Chat-style surveys that feel like a text conversation are what SurveySparrow is known for, and the company wraps a broader feedback platform around them. Alongside those surveys you get NPS and CSAT tracking, AI analysis of written comments, reputation management and tickets for following up with unhappy guests. It sits between basic form builders and enterprise CX suites in both depth and cost.

Notable customers: Pechanga Resort Casino, Paragon Casino Resort and OneTable.

What's new: October 2025 brought SparrowDesk, a standalone AI-first helpdesk for support teams that's priced apart from the survey tools. It hasn't announced dated updates to the survey product itself in 2026.

What you can do with SurveySparrow:

  • Reach guests on WhatsApp. Besides email, SMS, links and QR codes, surveys can go out over WhatsApp or run on an offline kiosk app at the host stand.
  • Track loyalty on a schedule. Recurring sends of CES, CSAT and NPS questions run on their own, so you see whether scores move after a menu or staffing change.
  • Theme the comments. CogniVue groups open-ended answers by topic and sentiment, which saves a manager from reading every response.
  • Close the loop. A low score can open a ticket assigned to a team member, so somebody contacts the guest and the follow-up is recorded.
  • Watch public reviews too. Reputation management puts online reviews beside survey results, and integrations connect to Salesforce, HubSpot, Zendesk, Slack, Intercom and Zapier.

Why SurveySparrow stands out: The conversational format is friendly on mobile, and you get closed-loop tickets and AI text analysis without signing an enterprise contract.

It's best for: Restaurants and hospitality groups that want mobile-first guest surveys, recurring NPS or CSAT tracking and AI comment analysis on a mid-market budget.

Pricing: You can start free or take a 14-day trial of paid features. Third-party trackers commonly list annual plans of Basic $19/month, Starter $39, Business $79 and Professional $249, though some 2026 sources show different tiers, so check the live price list before budgeting; Enterprise and the CX suite are quoted.
