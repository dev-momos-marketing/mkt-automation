8. Tattle

Tattle is a guest feedback platform made for restaurants, which it calls customer experience intelligence (CXI). Brief surveys are sent automatically once someone places an order, using hooks into your existing POS, loyalty program or online ordering, and AI then points each location at the fix likely to matter most. It also tracks public reviews and remains an independent company.

Notable customers: Hooters, Red Robin, Brinker International (Chili's) and Kung Fu Tea.

What's new: Kung Fu Tea signed on in January 2026 to follow guest sentiment across its channels, five months after Tattle raised $5 million in August 2025 for its AI tools. Managers and franchisees can now follow feedback in a dedicated iOS and Android app; its release month is unconfirmed.

What you can do with Tattle:

  • Collect feedback from actual orders. Integrations with Square, Thanx, Paytronix, Punchh, Olo and Toast trigger each survey, so the people answering are verified guests.
  • Sort answers by station of service. Each response is scored on hospitality, food quality, order accuracy and speed, so you learn whether to coach the kitchen or the front.
  • Get one priority per store. AI recommendations rank the change that should move each location's scores the most.
  • Watch Google and Yelp alongside surveys. Public reviews sit next to private answers, so you can compare what guests say to you with what they post.
  • Win back unhappy guests. Service recovery workflows help a manager reach out after a poor visit, and the app pushes alerts for critical incidents.

Why Tattle stands out: Every survey is tied to a real transaction and scored against the operational areas a GM controls, with dashboards for top box score and recovery rate by location.

It's best for: Multi-unit operators and franchisees running QSR, fast casual or casual dining concepts that want structured guest feedback linked to what happens in each restaurant.

Pricing: Tattle doesn't publish prices; ask the company for a quote.
