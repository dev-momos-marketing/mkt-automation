15. 7Shifts

Category: Labor & Workforce Performance Analytics

7shifts is workforce software made only for restaurants, with tools for shift scheduling, clocking in, tip payouts and payroll, plus a training module added in 2026. In a QSR management setup, it's the place where managers balance labor hours against sales and crew members check their shifts from their phones.

Notable customers: HCI Hospitality (Freddy's Frozen Custard & Steakburgers franchisee), Boston Pizza (Regina), Conshy Girls Restaurant Group and Pitango Gelato.

What's new: A revised plan structure took effect on July 2, 2025, with four tiers: Comp (free), Essentials, Pro and Premium. Employee Training followed in September 2026 as part of Premium: managers create lessons and quizzes, hand them out by job, department or store, and see who has finished.

Key features:

  • Forecast-based scheduling. A drag-and-drop editor puts forecast sales and recommended hours next to each week you build.
  • Crew mobile app. Staff trade shifts, update availability and message teammates from their phones.
  • Time clock enforcement. POS and payroll integrations keep clock-ins in line with the published schedule.
  • Labor budgets. Reports track labor cost against sales so you can adjust before a week overruns.
  • Tip pools and payroll. Shift tips are divided by your pooling rules and distributed to staff, while a payroll add-on handles wages for a separate fee.
  • Tasks and training. Shift checklists, onboarding packages and in-app courses keep standards consistent.

Pricing: Comp costs nothing for a single store with a team of 15 or fewer. Every paid tier is charged per store each month. On a yearly commitment you'd pay $39.99 for Essentials (teams up to 30), $79.99 for Pro and $134.99 for Premium; without one, expect $44.99, $89.99 or $149.99. Larger chains negotiate Enterprise terms, payroll sits outside these prices, and new accounts can run Pro for 14 days at no charge.
