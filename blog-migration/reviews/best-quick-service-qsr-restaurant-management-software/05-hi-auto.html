5. Hi Auto

Category: Drive-thru voice AI

Founded in Israel, Hi Auto puts all its effort into a single drive-thru tool: an AI that greets cars, keys each order into your POS and recommends extras. The models are trained for the engine rumble, wind and back-seat chatter that make lane audio messy. By 2025, the company reported more than 100 million orders a year flowing through its system.

Notable customers: Bojangles, Checkers & Rally's and Lee's Famous Recipe Chicken.

What's new: Bojangles renewed for multiple years in 2026, with about 500 lanes already running Hi Auto and 100 additional stores planned before mid-2027. The renewal also brings upsells that respond to how many cars are waiting.

Key features:

  • Menu-board conversations. The AI runs the entire ordering exchange, leaving crew members on food assembly and payment.
  • Built for lane noise. Speech recognition is tuned specifically for loud outdoor ordering conditions.
  • Kitchen display sync. Confirmed orders appear on the POS and KDS immediately.
  • Upsells tied to line length. Recommendations scale back when the queue grows, protecting speed of service.
  • Spanish and named voices. Spanish ordering is supported, and chains can brand the persona, as Bojangles did with Bo-Linda.
  • Window Intelligence. A beta tool monitors the pickup window, trialed with brands such as Lee's.

What makes them stand out: Single-minded attention to lane conditions, backed by a flagship customer that keeps adding stores.

Pricing: Custom software subscription; rates aren't posted.

It's a great fit for: Busy drive-thru brands wanting a branded AI voice at the board across a large store count.
