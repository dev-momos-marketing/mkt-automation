12. xtraCHEF

Category: AI invoice processing & food cost (Inventory)

xtraCHEF is the back-office module Toast bought in 2021 and still sells as xtraCHEF by Toast. It reads supplier invoices, pulls out line-item costs and lines them up against what your Toast POS sold. That makes it a cost-control layer for stores already running their front of house on Toast.

Notable customers: Toast doesn't publicly name xtraCHEF customers.

What's new: Toast hasn't announced any dated xtraCHEF updates in the past two years.

Key features:

  • Invoice capture. Supplier invoices are digitized and broken into line-item costs, ending manual entry.
  • Recipe costing (Pro). Recipes and sub-recipes account for yields and unit conversions to show true plate cost.
  • Inventory counts (Pro). Stores record counts and track stock in the same tool.
  • Actual vs. theoretical. Variance reports compare what food should have cost with what it did.
  • Vendors and accounting. Product catalogs, ordering and accounting integrations keep cost data moving to the books.

What makes them stand out: Because it uses Toast sales data directly, costs and menu mix sit side by side with no extra integration work.

Pricing: Sold through Toast in Essentials and Pro tiers without public prices; invoice automation comes in both, while recipe costing and inventory need Pro.

It's a great fit for: Toast restaurants that want to stop typing invoices and keep a closer eye on food cost and recipe margins.
