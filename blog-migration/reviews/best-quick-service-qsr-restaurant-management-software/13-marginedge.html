13. MarginEdge

Category: Invoice automation & real-time food costing (Inventory)

Each day, this platform matches POS sales with supplier invoices to produce daily food cost, inventory value and P&L figures, so you aren't waiting for month-end close. Invoice processing, bill pay, ordering and recipe costing come in one subscription. For a QSR operator, that means spotting a cost problem in days instead of weeks.

Notable customers: Skopos Hospitality Group, Crooked Hammock Brewery, South Block and The Tavern at Ivy City Smokehouse.

What's new: In April 2025, MarginEdge celebrated 10 years and 10,000 restaurant customers. In August 2026, it raised $80 million in Series D equity and debt, co-led by Schooner Capital and Ten Coves Capital, to develop AI products from the purchasing data it handles.

Key features:

  • Invoices from anywhere. Snap a photo, forward an email or connect EDI, and invoices get processed for you.
  • Daily food and beverage cost. Sales and invoice data combine into a cost figure you can check every morning.
  • Bill pay included. Vendor payments run inside the same subscription.
  • Ordering, counts and recipes. Order guides, inventory counts and menu-item costing share one dataset.
  • Price alerts and P&L. You're warned when item prices change, and budgets and P&L views sync with accounting tools such as QuickBooks.

What makes them stand out: It sits between dozens of POS systems and your accounting software, cutting daily paperwork down to a short routine.

Pricing: One flat monthly fee per store includes unlimited invoices and bill pay. The company's pricing page has shown $330, while 2026 third-party trackers report $350, plus $150 for the Freepour bar inventory add-on; check marginedge.com for the current rate.

It's a great fit for: Independents and small-to-midsize groups that want food cost visible close to real time with less manual invoice entry.
