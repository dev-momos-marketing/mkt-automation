9. Winnow

Category: Restaurant AI-powered food waste management software

Food waste is a line on the P&L that's easy to ignore because nobody sees it pile up. Winnow makes it measurable with Winnow Vision, a bin fitted with a camera and scale that recognizes and weighs whatever is thrown away.

A second product, Winnow Foresight, forecasts demand so kitchens produce less in the first place. Its customer list leans toward hotels and contract catering, so in a quick-service setting it suits large groups with real prep volume more than small units.

Notable customers: Hilton, IHG Hotels & Resorts, and Accor, Marriott and Mandarin Oriental.

What's new: Winnow bought Singapore's Lumitics in July 2026 to grow across Asia-Pacific and the Middle East. In September 2026, it released Foresight, a phone-friendly forecasting web app built alongside Hilton chefs that needs no extra hardware.

Key features:

  • Camera-and-scale bin. Winnow Vision identifies discarded items and records their weight automatically.
  • No tagging needed. With its throw-and-go design, cooks scrape plates and pans as usual and the bin does the recording.
  • Waste broken down by cause. Data splits by item, shift and stage, covering prep, spoilage and plate waste, each priced in dollars.
  • Screens in the kitchen. Cooks see what's being binned and its value, which nudges habits on the line.
  • Forecasting and multi-site reports. Foresight plans production by item, while dashboards compare sites for chefs and managers.
  • Lower-cost manual mode. Kitchens without a need for cameras can log waste manually at a reduced price.

What makes them stand out: Cooks see the dollar value of each discard as it happens, which shifts behavior without constant policing from managers.

Pricing: Custom quotes; Winnow publishes no price list.

It's a great fit for: High-volume kitchens such as hotel restaurants, buffets, caterers and big multi-site groups with meaningful production waste.
