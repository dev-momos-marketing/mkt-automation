6. Tattle

Category: Guest feedback & CX improvement

From an operations point of view, Tattle answers one question per store: what should this team fix first? The independent platform triggers brief surveys after each transaction via your ordering, loyalty and POS tools, keeps an eye on public reviews, and has AI rank the biggest opportunity at each unit. Area coaches get a practical priority list instead of a pile of comments.

Notable customers: Hooters, Red Robin, Brinker International (Chili's) and Kung Fu Tea.

What's new: A $5 million raise in August 2025 funds more AI development. Kung Fu Tea came aboard in January 2026 to measure guest sentiment across channels. A GM and franchisee app for iOS and Android, with incident alerts and AI Coach tasks, has also launched.

Key features:

  • Automatic surveys. They go out after each order through connections to Olo, Toast, Square, Punchh, Thanx, Paytronix and similar tools.
  • Answers sorted by station. Feedback lands under speed, accuracy, food quality or hospitality, giving managers something concrete to coach.
  • Top fix per unit. AI identifies the improvement likely to matter most at every location.
  • Reviews in context. Google and Yelp ratings appear beside survey data for a fuller picture of each store.
  • Recovery and benchmarks. Recovery workflows reach dissatisfied guests, and dashboards rank stores by top box score and recovery rate.

What makes them stand out: Each comment is connected to an operational area, so franchisees know exactly which part of service to work on next.

Pricing: Quote-based; Tattle shares rates on request.

It's a great fit for: Multi-location quick-service and fast-casual groups that want guest input tied to store operations.
