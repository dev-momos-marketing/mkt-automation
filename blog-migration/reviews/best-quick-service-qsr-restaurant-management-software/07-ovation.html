7. Ovation

Category: Guest feedback & reputation management

Ovation, marketed at ovationup.com under founder and CEO Zack Oates, helps multi-unit restaurant operators hear about a bad visit within minutes. A two-question text goes to guests after they order, alerts route to the manager on duty, and reviews plus directory listings live in one dashboard. Company updates appear on Ovation's LinkedIn page, and QSR Web's guide to guest feedback covers why quick follow-up pays off.

Notable customers: Dave's Hot Chicken, Curry Up Now, Bareburger and Taziki's Mediterranean Cafe.

What's new: Its Fall 2025 release (October) brought review management and listings on 50+ directories. A February 2026 round of $3 million, funded by restaurant clients, lifted total capital raised to $12 million. The Spring 2026 update in May introduced Brand Voice and menu-item sentiment reporting.

Key features:

  • Short text surveys. Guests receive a two-question SMS once they order, sent through your POS or ordering integration, or opened from a QR code on the table.
  • Real-time recovery alerts. Managers see unhappy responses instantly and can reach out before the guest gives up on you.
  • Drafted responses. AI proposes replies to feedback and public reviews in your configured Brand Voice, and staff edit before sending.
  • Comment themes. Open-text answers get grouped into categories like cleanliness, speed or food quality.
  • Competitive Pulse. Operators can compare each store with as many as five local rivals.
  • Dish-level insight. Reports tie guest sentiment back to particular menu items and orders.

What makes them stand out: Because the survey takes seconds, more guests respond, and recovery begins privately before a complaint turns into a public review. Our guest experience software roundup compares more options.

Pricing: Ovation quotes per account, counting physical stores and ghost kitchens, with discounts at volume; prices aren't published.

It's a great fit for: Growing quick-service and fast-casual groups wanting fast service recovery and review handling in a single tool.
