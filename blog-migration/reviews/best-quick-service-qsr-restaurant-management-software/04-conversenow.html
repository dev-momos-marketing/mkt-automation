4. ConverseNow

Category: Conversational voice AI for phone & drive-thru

For stores where the phone keeps ringing during the dinner rush, ConverseNow supplies AI assistants that chat with callers or lane guests in plain language and push completed tickets into your POS. Its drive-thru capabilities expanded when it acquired Valyant AI in 2024. Operationally, it shifts call handling off the make line.

Notable customers: Anthony's Coal Fired Pizza & Wings and Wingstop (pilot at select locations).

What's new: An April 2026 tie-up with Deliverect means operators who manage menus and orders in Deliverect can now add ConverseNow's voice ordering for calls and lanes.

Key features:

  • End-to-end call handling. Each incoming call gets answered and the whole order captured, a big help for delivery-heavy pizza and wing concepts.
  • Lane assistant. The same conversational technology is available at drive-thru speaker posts.
  • Direct POS entry. Tickets arrive in the register automatically, removing the step of copying phone orders.
  • Consistent add-on offers. Suggestions appear on every order regardless of who's working that night.
  • Escalation to people. When a caller wants a human, partner OrderSolutions can take over from its call center.

What makes them stand out: Generative AI lets callers speak the way they normally would, and Deliverect support puts voice orders under the same menu management as third-party delivery.

Pricing: Brand-specific quotes; ConverseNow publishes no rate card.

It's a great fit for: Quick-service chains, especially pizza and wings, that still take a heavy volume of orders by phone or at the lane.
