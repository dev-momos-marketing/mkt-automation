10. Kitro

Category: AI food waste analytics (Kitchen & BOH)

KITRO, a Swiss company, mounts a camera-and-scale unit above the kitchen bin that captures and sorts discarded food on its own. Chefs then use its dashboards to tighten purchasing and production. Hotel and resort brands make up most of its client list, so quick-service operators should treat it as a specialist tool for larger kitchens.

Notable customers: Marriott International, Hyatt, Pestana (Algarve hotel) and Hyatt Inclusive Collection.

What's new: KITRO became a preferred Marriott partner in 2025. A February 2026 Hyatt EAME case study claims over EUR 1.7M saved and 31% less food waste (vendor figures), and in May 2026 the company launched in Portugal with a Pestana hotel.

Key features:

  • Hands-free capture. Every disposal is photographed and weighed with no manual entry from staff.
  • AI sorting. Waste gets classified by food type and by edible versus inedible.
  • Money as well as weight. Cost values per food category show the financial hit of each bin.
  • Chef dashboards. Reports guide changes to ordering, production quantities and buffet sizing.
  • ESG reporting. Built-in sustainability exports support compliance with European food waste regulation.

What makes them stand out: The device runs itself, so kitchens build an accurate waste record without adding a task to anyone's shift.

Pricing: A single kitchen on the Business plan pays from CHF 349 monthly, with the device rented as part of the subscription; Enterprise is priced to fit, and a free assessment is available.

It's a great fit for: Hotels, resorts and big catering operations tracking waste for cost and ESG goals. Like Winnow, it suits hospitality kitchens more than QSR counters.
