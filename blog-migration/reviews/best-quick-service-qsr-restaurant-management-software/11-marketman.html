11. MarketMan

Category: AI inventory & food cost (Inventory)

MarketMan is cloud software for the purchasing and inventory part of running a restaurant: stock counts, recipe costs, supplier orders and invoice capture. For a QSR group, it gives each store a clear view of what's on the shelf and what every menu item costs to make this week.

Notable customers: Westville Restaurant Group, ATFC, Ltd. and Taco del Mar (multi-unit franchisee).

What's new: Square Restaurant Inventory by MarketMan went global in April 2026, so Square sellers now get ingredient and recipe management, purchase orders, invoice scanning and waste tracking inside Square.

Key features:

  • Live stock levels. Inventory and counts are tracked per location, so managers order from real numbers.
  • Current recipe costs. Menu item costs update as ingredient prices move.
  • Supplier ordering. Purchase orders go to vendors automatically from par levels and usage.
  • Invoice scanning. Scanned invoices refresh prices and flag any supplier increases.
  • Waste and COGS reporting. Food cost and loss trends are reported for every store, with POS and accounting integrations.

What makes them stand out: Purchasing, recipe costing and vendor price tracking share one system, so a price jump shows up in your menu margins right away.

Pricing: MarketMan's current prices couldn't be confirmed; plans are sold per location, so ask MarketMan for a quote.

It's a great fit for: Independents and multi-unit franchise groups wanting firmer control over inventory, ordering and food cost, including Square users.
