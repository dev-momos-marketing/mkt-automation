14. TimeForge

Category: Labor management & AI forecasting (Labor & Scheduling)

Headquartered in Lubbock and operating since 1998, TimeForge sells workforce tools to restaurant and retail operators, so managers can build schedules, track hours worked and stay within labor laws. Lineup.ai's forecasting engine became part of TimeForge in August 2024. For QSR operators, that puts the forecast and the schedule built from it in the same system.

Notable customers: Hard Eight BBQ and Great Harvest Bread Co. (Great Harvest Franchising, preferred vendor).

What's new: Since July 2025, TimeForge has been listed in Square's app marketplace, with an integration that feeds Square POS sales into its labor forecasts and staffing levels.

Key features:

  • Forecasts in 15-minute blocks. The Lineup.ai engine reads your POS history to project sales and the hours needed to cover them.
  • Schedules that follow demand. Each daypart gets staffed from the forecast, with shifts drafted by the software.
  • Time clock with overage alerts. Clock-ins are tracked, and an alert fires the moment labor creeps above its goal.
  • Labor law guardrails. Overtime rules and predictive, or fair workweek, scheduling requirements are built into scheduling.
  • Self-service for staff. Your team updates when they can work, picks up swaps and asks for days off without calling the manager.
  • Labor vs. sales reports. Multi-unit reporting compares labor cost with sales, pulling sales from Square, Toast and other POS systems.

What makes them stand out: AI forecasting and day-to-day scheduling, time tracking and compliance live together, so a forecast turns straight into a staffed schedule.

Pricing: Custom; contact TimeForge for a quote.

It's a great fit for: Franchisees and restaurant groups in quick service or casual dining looking for one tool that plans labor from sales, records hours and watches compliance.
