8. PreciTaste

Category: AI kitchen management (Kitchen & BOH)

PreciTaste handles the back-of-house side of QSR management: it predicts demand for each location from POS history and camera feeds, then tells crews how much to prep, when to drop product and what to reorder. Chipotle piloted its kitchen system in 2022, though a current deployment there hasn't been confirmed. Most of today's named users are franchise groups.

Notable customers: Bluemont Group (Dunkin' franchisee, 90+ locations) and Mac Haik Restaurant Group (First Watch franchisee).

What's new: A December 2025 partnership with PAR Technology imports past sales from PAR POS automatically, which shortens setup. In February 2026, Bluemont Group switched on Do'Cast donut and Munchkin ordering in its Dunkin' shops, and in July 2026 PreciTaste secured seven-figure financing from Round2 Capital for growth in Europe.

Key features:

  • Prep Assistant. Each morning, managers get prep lists built from that store's own demand forecast.
  • Planner and Station Assistants. Production plans span the full day, and line cooks get live guidance on what to cook next.
  • Camera monitoring. Computer vision tracks ingredient pans and display cases so refills happen before a stockout.
  • Do'Cast bakery ordering. Donut and pastry orders are generated from forecasts combined with shelf photos.
  • Forecast inputs. Predictions factor in sales trends, weather, seasonality and local events, using data from PAR, Toast and other POS systems.

What makes them stand out: Guidance reaches the person at the station in real time, which helps weaker locations prep like your strongest ones.

Pricing: Custom; PreciTaste doesn't list prices.

It's a great fit for: QSR, fast-casual and bakery-case franchisees aiming to reduce prep waste and running out of product.
