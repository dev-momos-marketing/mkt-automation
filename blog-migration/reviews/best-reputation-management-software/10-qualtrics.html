10. Qualtrics

Category: Enterprise survey, CX analytics and reputation suite

Large companies use Qualtrics, an experience management (XM) suite, to research how customers, staff and the wider market see them. Its reputation features sit in an online reputation management module that brings public reviews into the same place as survey results, plus location dashboards and AI Experience Agents that act on feedback.

The private equity firm Silver Lake has owned Qualtrics since 2023. It fits large multi-unit restaurant brands with their own CX or insights team.

Notable customers: KFC and Shake Shack.

What's new: May 2026 saw Qualtrics close a $6.75 billion deal for Press Ganey Forsta, bringing healthcare patient-experience data and the InMoment business. At its X4 event in March 2026, the company reported that paying clients were already running its Experience Agents, and it unveiled agents for recovering service failures as they happen.

Key features:

  • Post-visit surveys everywhere. Guests can answer after an order through a QR code, text message, email, chat window, website or app.
  • Reviews next to surveys. Your survey answers share a dataset with public ratings pulled from Google and comparable review sites.
  • Dashboards by role. Location CX dashboards adapt to the viewer, whether a store manager, a market lead or an executive.
  • Text analytics. AI groups open-ended comments and reviews into themes with sentiment attached.
  • Experience Agents. AI agents act on feedback, for example by starting service recovery in real time.
  • Driver and churn analysis. Predictive analytics point to what moves satisfaction and which guests are at risk of leaving.

Pricing: Qualtrics doesn't publish CX suite prices. Contact sales for a custom quote.
