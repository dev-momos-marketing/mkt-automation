10. Qualtrics

Qualtrics makes experience management (XM) software that big organizations use to measure how customers, employees and the wider market see them. Reputation spans several of its products: online reputation management for public reviews, BrandXM for brand research, and surveys that capture private feedback.

Silver Lake has owned the company since taking it private in 2023. In May 2026, Qualtrics closed its $6.75 billion purchase of Press Ganey Forsta, which mostly adds healthcare patient-experience data and brings InMoment along with it.

Key features

  • Review monitoring: Public reviews from Google, Yelp, Facebook and TripAdvisor flow into one feed with sentiment scored as they arrive.
  • Brand research: BrandXM studies run on the same platform as customer feedback, so perception data and review data can be compared.
  • Surveys on six channels: Customers can respond by email, SMS, web, app, QR code or chat after a transaction.
  • Suggested responses: Reply drafts follow your brand voice and go out from Qualtrics dashboards.
  • Contact-center connectors: Feedback moves to and from Genesys, NICE and Salesforce.

Pros

  • Three audiences in one system: Customer, employee and brand programs share data, which helps when several departments care about reputation.
  • Views for each level: Location dashboards give a store manager, a regional lead and an executive the slice of data they act on.
  • Forward-looking analytics: Predictive models flag the drivers of satisfaction and the customers at risk of leaving.
  • User rating: 4.3/5 on G2 from 751 reviews of Qualtrics Customer Experience.

Cons

  • No list prices: Plans on the pricing page carry a "Request Pricing" button, and CX costs depend on interaction volume.
  • Enterprise scope: Smaller businesses that only track reviews would use a small part of a platform built for research teams.
  • Reply coverage: Responding from inside Qualtrics is supported for reviews connected through Google Places or Facebook.

Price and plans

Qualtrics doesn't publish prices. Its CX plans, including one for location-based industries such as restaurants, are priced by interactions, and you get a quote after a demo.
