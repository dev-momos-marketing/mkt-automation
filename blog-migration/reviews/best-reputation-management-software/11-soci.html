11. SOCi

Category: Local marketing suite run by AI agents

SOCi builds marketing software for franchises and chains, and it recently rebranded and moved its site to soci.ai. Reviews, listings, local search and social all run through Genius Agents, AI agents trained on each brand's guidelines, while corporate sets the rules.

It's aimed at groups with hundreds or thousands of locations that want central control and want AI to carry out most of the local work.

Notable customers: Lee's Famous Recipe Chicken, Sport Clips Haircuts, WOWorks with its six restaurant brands, and On The Border Mexican Grill & Cantina.

What's new: By the company's own count, SOCi had more than 412,000 agents at work by September 2026, together completing upward of 27 million local marketing jobs per year. That compares with the 300,000 it announced in May 2026.

Key features:

  • Genius Reviews. AI replies follow your brand guidelines and pass sensitive reviews to staff.
  • Review monitoring. Track reviews and sentiment for every location in one report.
  • Listings and local search. Hours and addresses stay correct across directories such as Bing, Yelp and Google.
  • Central social publishing. Corporate posts can be tailored per location, with an AI social agent helping.
  • Guest surveys. Gather first-party feedback directly from your customers.
  • Role-based controls. Head office sets the rules, and local managers act inside them.

Pricing: SOCi quotes each deal based on location count and products and doesn't publish prices.
