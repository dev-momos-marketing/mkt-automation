11. SOCi

SOCi makes reputation and local marketing software for restaurant chains, franchises and other multi-location brands. Reviews, listings, local pages and social media for every store live in one platform, and AI agents trained on your brand rules do much of the routine work.

Restaurant customers include Jersey Mike's Subs, Scooter's Coffee, Lee's Famous Recipe Chicken and On The Border Mexican Grill & Cantina. If you know the company from meetsoci.com, it has rebranded and now runs at soci.ai.

Key features

  • Review management: Track ratings and sentiment for each store, and reply yourself or let the reputation agent draft responses that follow your guidelines.
  • Listings and local SEO: Store details stay consistent across Google, Bing, Yelp and other directories, so nearby diners see the right hours.
  • Local pages: Every location gets its own search-optimized page, plus localized paid social campaigns if you want them.
  • Social publishing: Head office schedules posts that franchisees can adapt for their own neighborhood.
  • Delivery and ordering integrations: Connections to Olo, Uber Eats and Grubhub keep location details in sync and bring delivery ratings into SOCi.

Pros

  • One home for local reputation: Reviews, listings, pages, social and surveys share a single platform and one set of brand rules.
  • Unlimited users: SOCi charges nothing per user or per listing, so every manager and agency partner can log in.
  • Escalation built in: Automated replies cover routine reviews, while anything sensitive is flagged for your team.
  • User reviews: G2 users give SOCi 4.5 out of 5.

Cons

  • Quote-only pricing: Costs aren't listed anywhere; they depend on how many locations you run and which agents you add.
  • Usage limits: Some skills come with limits tied to your package, so check them against your review and post volume.
  • Enterprise scale: The platform is sized for networks of hundreds of locations, and a small group may find a simpler review tool covers its needs.

Price and plans

SOCi doesn't publish prices and has no free trial. Each quote is based on your location count and the Genius Agents and skills you deploy, with no per-user or per-listing fees.
