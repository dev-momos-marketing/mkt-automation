3. Tattle

Tattle is a guest feedback and customer experience platform built only for restaurants. It sends a short survey after each order through your ordering, loyalty and POS systems, watches public reviews, and uses AI to tell each location which fix will lift its scores most.

For reputation work, that lets you see whether a slide in star ratings matches what guests report privately, and deal with the cause at store level. Hooters, Red Robin, Brinker International (Chili's) and Kung Fu Tea use it, and Tattle says more than 250 brands do.

Key features

  • Order-triggered surveys: Connections to Toast, Olo, Punchh, Paytronix, Thanx and Square send a survey once a transaction closes.
  • Operational scoring: Each response is filed under speed, accuracy, food quality or hospitality, and item-level feedback shows which dishes draw complaints.
  • AI Coach: For every store, AI picks the change likely to raise guest scores most and sets a monthly goal.
  • Review replies on the go: The manager app follows Google, Yelp, Facebook and TripAdvisor and lets GMs respond to reviewers directly.
  • Recovery and trends: Service recovery workflows reach unhappy guests, and dashboards compare top box score and recovery rate by location.

Pros

  • Private and public feedback together: Survey answers sit beside public reviews, so you can tell a one-off complaint from a pattern.
  • No extra step for staff: Surveys ride on the POS, ordering and loyalty tools restaurants already use, so nobody has to hand out a link.
  • Clear priorities: AI Coach gives each general manager one ranked fix to work on instead of a pile of comments.
  • Transparent pricing: Tattle publishes its per-location rates, and every plan includes the same features.

Cons

  • Reputation tools cost extra: Full Reputation Management, for monitoring and replying across review platforms, is a separate add-on without a listed price.
  • Five-location minimum: Published tiers start at 5 units, so a single restaurant falls below the lowest plan.
  • No trial listed: Each plan's Get Started button leads to the sales team.

Price and plans

Tattle charges per location, per month, with lower rates as you add locations. Every plan includes the same features and onboarding; Reputation Management is an add-on.

  • 5 to 10 units: $125 per location, per month.
  • 11 to 20 units: $100 per location, per month.
  • 21 to 50 units: $85 per location, per month.
  • 51 to 200 units: $65 per location, per month.
  • 201+ units: Custom pricing.
