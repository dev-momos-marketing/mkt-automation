3. Tattle

Website: Tattle

Category: Restaurant guest feedback and customer experience platform

Tattle approaches reputation from the inside of the restaurant. Short surveys go out after each order through your ordering, loyalty and POS systems, and the answers are filed under the parts of service guests actually judge, like speed, accuracy, food quality and hospitality. Public reviews from Google and Yelp are tracked alongside, so you can see whether a dip in star ratings lines up with what guests report privately.

It suits QSR, fast casual and casual dining groups with many units, where head office wants each location to know which single fix will move its scores most.

Notable customers: Hooters, Red Robin, Brinker International (Chili's) and Kung Fu Tea.

What's new: Tattle raised $5 million in August 2025 to build out its AI tools, and from January 2026 Kung Fu Tea has relied on Tattle as a partner for reading sentiment on every channel. The company has also released an iOS and Android app for GMs and franchisees, at no extra cost to subscribers, though it hasn't confirmed the launch month.

Key features:

  • Surveys triggered by orders. Connections to Toast, Olo, Punchh, Paytronix, Thanx and Square send a survey once a transaction closes.
  • Operational scoring. Every response maps to speed, accuracy, food quality or hospitality, which points managers at the shift problem behind a complaint.
  • Ranked AI recommendations. For each store, the AI picks out the change likely to have the largest effect on guest scores.
  • Reviews beside surveys. Google and Yelp reviews are monitored in the same view as private feedback.
  • Recovery workflows. Unhappy guests can be contacted and won back, and dashboards report recovery rate and top box score by location.
  • Manager app. Push alerts for critical incidents and AI Coach action items reach GMs on their phones.

Pricing: Tattle quotes each brand individually and doesn't list prices on its site.
