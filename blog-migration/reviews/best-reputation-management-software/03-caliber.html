3. Caliber

Website: Group Caliber

Category: Stakeholder intelligence and corporate reputation platform

Caliber is a Copenhagen company, founded in 2016, that measures how customers, employees, investors and other audiences see a business through continuous surveys. Its headline metric is the Trust & Like Score, an average of how much respondents trust and like a company.

It serves corporate communications and brand teams at large companies. Caliber has no tools for answering online reviews or handling guest feedback, so most restaurant operators will find it a poor match unless they need boardroom-level perception research.

Notable customers: Caliber says it advises Airbus, BASF and Novo Nordisk.

What's new: In March 2026, Caliber set up a U.S. Expert Advisory Council to grow its American business and released its yearly U.S. Trust & Like ranking, with Costco in first place.

Key features:

  • Always-on surveys. Perception is tracked continuously in real time, which replaces the occasional poll most companies run.
  • Attribute-level scores. Brand and reputation score breakdowns show which qualities lift or drag how people see you.
  • Stakeholder segments. Results split by customers, staff, investors, talent, policymakers and opinion leaders.
  • Peer benchmarks. You can compare your scores with sector peers, other industries and different markets.
  • AI Assistant. Also called Ask Sherlock, it lets you query scores for board prep or campaign planning.
  • AI search and crisis tracking. AI Search Overview checks how generative AI describes your brand, and event impact measurement follows a crisis.

Pricing: Caliber doesn't list prices. Contact the company for a custom quote.
