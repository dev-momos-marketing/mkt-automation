4. Reputation.com

Reputation sells reputation and guest experience software to brands with many locations. The company dropped the ".com" from its name but kept the domain, is based in San Ramon, California, and marked 20 years in March 2026. Reviews, surveys, listings, social and AI search visibility all run from one platform.

Restaurant groups are a core market. Mitchells & Butlers, with 15+ brands, gathers guest feedback from 1,600+ locations in Reputation, and the vendor reports the group's average star rating rose from 3.7 to 4.2. Tortilla, the UK burrito chain, and Stonegate Group are also customers.

Key features

  • Unified review inbox: Read and answer Google, Facebook and other reviews in one queue, with AI drafts ready to edit.
  • Guest surveys: Collect item-level and service feedback by SMS, QR code or email and set it beside public ratings.
  • Listings and local SEO: Keep menus, hours and specials current on Google, Yelp and AI overviews for every location.
  • Competitive benchmarks: Compare your ratings, themes and response times with nearby restaurants through the Competitive Insights add-on.
  • Social publishing: With the Social Suite add-on, locations post and reply on social while corporate keeps an eye on tone.

Pros

  • Made for restaurant groups: Its food and beverage offering targets multi-location restaurants, whether corporate-owned or franchised.
  • Feedback and reviews together: Survey answers and public ratings feed the same dashboards, so you see both sides of each location.
  • Transparent entry pricing: The core plans carry a list price per location.
  • User rating: On G2, Reputation scores 4.5 out of 5.

Cons

  • Many paid add-ons: Social Suite, Actions & Ticketing and Competitive Insights are each sold on top of a plan.
  • Limited surveys below the top plan: Rep Core + Pulse includes 1 basic survey template, and custom surveys need Rep Core + Surveys.
  • Premium review sites: Feedback from Yelp and Tripadvisor comes through Premium Sources, which costs extra.

Price and plans

Reputation publishes per-location monthly prices for its core plans and sells through its sales team; add-ons are quoted separately.

  • Rep Core: $80 per location, per month.
  • Rep Core + Pulse: $115 per location, per month.
  • Rep Core + Surveys: $150 per location, per month.
  • Enterprise: Custom pricing for 125+ locations.
