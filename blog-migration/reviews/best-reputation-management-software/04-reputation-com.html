4. Reputation.com

Website: Reputation

Category: Enterprise reputation intelligence and benchmarking

Reputation, which rebranded from Reputation.com and still runs on that domain, sells enterprise reputation and guest experience software to brands with many locations. Guest surveys, review replies, listings, social and visibility in AI answers all live in one system, which is aimed at restaurant, hospitality, automotive and healthcare groups.

The company is based in San Ramon, California, and marked its 20th year in March 2026.

Notable customers: Tortilla and Mitchells & Butlers.

What's new: A May 2026 release brought Reputation IQ, which lets you type everyday questions and get answers drawn from your feedback, along with an expanded AI Reputation Manager that reports what ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity say about your brand and a new AI Location Presence Insights product.

Key features:

  • One review inbox. Google, Yelp, Facebook and Tripadvisor reviews, plus those from other sites, can be read and answered without switching tabs.
  • Requests tied to visits. A dine-in check, a delivery order or another POS event can trigger an ask for a review by text, email or QR code.
  • Private feedback too. Survey answers from guests sit next to public ratings, so you see both sides of a location's performance.
  • Themes from text. Sentiment analytics group guest comments by subject, for example cleanliness or service.
  • Listings and social. Manage location listings plus social listening and publishing in the same platform.
  • AI search visibility. Two AI tools follow how assistants like ChatGPT portray your brand and each of your restaurants.

Pricing: Reputation sells on custom quotes and doesn't publish prices.
