2. Birdeye

Website: Birdeye

Category: Reputation, listings and guest messaging suite

Birdeye is a long-standing name in AI for reputation management that now pitches itself to chains and franchise groups as an agentic marketing platform. One login covers review management, directory listings, guest texting, social posts, surveys and tracking of how AI answer engines mention you, with AI agents doing much of the day-to-day work.

For a restaurant group, the draw is that one vendor can watch reviews, keep menus and hours current on directories, and route a bad review to the manager of the store it concerns.

Notable customers: The Saxton Group (which franchises McAlister's Deli) and Black Bear Diner.

What's new: In September 2026, Birdeye used its View 2026 conference to debut AI Coworkers, three named assistants that cover marketing (Jay), operations (Myna) and customer experience (Robin). Later that month it announced a SkyTab POS integration that pushes restaurant menu details to listings.

Key features:

  • Review alerts with ticketing. New ratings on Yelp, Facebook, Google and niche industry sites raise an alert, and a ticket carries each negative one to the responsible location manager.
  • More reviews by text and email. Automated SMS and email requests ask recent guests to rate their visit, which helps grow your review count.
  • Replies drafted by AI. AI agents can draft or handle review responses in your brand's voice, so locations keep up with volume.
  • Accurate listings and menus. Listings management keeps details consistent across directories, and SkyTab users can sync menus straight from the POS.
  • One messaging inbox. Webchat, SMS, email and social messages arrive in a single feed for your team.
  • Sentiment by region. Surveys and Insights AI turn feedback into sentiment analysis you can view by location, district or region, next to competitor benchmarks.

Pricing: Custom. Birdeye keeps its prices private and sends a quote once you've had a demo.
