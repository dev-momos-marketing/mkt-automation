2. Birdeye

Birdeye is a long-standing reputation platform, now sold to chains and franchise groups as an agentic marketing platform. One account covers review management, directory listings, guest texting, social posts, surveys and tracking of how AI answer engines mention you.

For a restaurant group, the appeal is one vendor that watches reviews, keeps menus and hours right on directories and sends a bad review to the store it concerns. If you're deciding how much of that to hand to automation, our guide to AI for reputation management compares the options.

Key features

  • Monitoring and alerts: New ratings on Google, Yelp, Facebook and niche industry sites trigger alerts, and tickets assign each negative one to the responsible location.
  • Review generation: Automated SMS and email requests go to recent guests and build up your review count over time.
  • AI responses: Replies are drafted by AI or handled by agents, written in your brand's voice.
  • Listings management: Business details stay consistent across directories, with menu sync for restaurants using SkyTab or Uber Eats.
  • Sentiment reporting: Surveys and Insights AI turn feedback into sentiment analysis by location, district or region, next to competitor benchmarks.
  • AI search visibility: Search AI shows how your brand appears in AI answer engines.

Pros

  • Broad coverage: Reviews, listings, messaging, social and surveys sit in a single platform, so you manage fewer vendors.
  • Agents included: The AI Coworkers launched at View 2026 direct 27 agents that take on routine reputation tasks.
  • Restaurant integrations: SkyTab (announced September 2026) and Uber Eats (June 2025) connections push menu details to listings.
  • Strong G2 score: Birdeye is rated 4.7 out of 5 on G2.

Cons

  • Private pricing: There's no public price list, and the cost depends on the locations and modules you deploy.
  • Many industries: The platform serves many sectors, and parts of the AI Coworkers' remit, like insurance verification, won't apply to a restaurant.
  • Big for one location: A single restaurant may use only a fraction of what the platform offers.

Price and plans

Birdeye doesn't list prices. It quotes each brand based on the number of locations and the products you deploy, under one contract.
