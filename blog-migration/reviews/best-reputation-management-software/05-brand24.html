5. Brand24

Website: Brand24

Category: Mention tracking and social listening

Brand24 scans the open web for anyone talking about your restaurant, from TikTok and news coverage to forum threads and podcast episodes. AI then rates how positive each mention is, and a newer module checks what AI chatbots say about your brand.

It watches conversations but has no review-response workflow, so restaurant teams usually pair it with a review tool and use it to catch a viral complaint or PR spike early.

Notable customers: Uber, the Satoria Group hotel company and Pasibus, a fast-casual chain in Poland.

What's new: December 2025 brought an LLM Monitoring tab to the Brand24 panel, reporting where your brand ranks and how it's described when people ask ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. In 2026 it also released a ChatGPT app and an MCP server for querying your mention data from AI assistants.

Key features:

  • Wide mention coverage. Your name gets caught wherever it appears, including newsletters, video, podcasts, review sites and social platforms.
  • Sentiment and emotion. Every mention is tagged as good, bad or neutral, and emotion analysis adds the mood behind the words.
  • Spike alerts. Volume and reach charts with alerts help you notice a sudden wave of posts about a location.
  • Competitor share of voice. Compare how often people talk about you versus nearby rivals.
  • Brand Assistant. Type a question about your mentions into a chat window and get a written answer.
  • AI visibility tracking. LLM monitoring reports where AI assistants bring you up; on most plans it costs extra.

Pricing: Five published plans, billed annually: Individual $199/month, Team $299, Pro $399, Business $599 and Enterprise from $1,499/month (monthly billing costs more, e.g. Individual $249). You can test any plan for 14 days, though no plan is free forever. Data on Individual updates roughly twice a day, while Pro and higher plans update in real time.
