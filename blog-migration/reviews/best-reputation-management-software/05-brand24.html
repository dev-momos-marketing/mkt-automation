5. Brand24

Brand24 is a media monitoring tool that finds conversations about your brand across the open web, from social media posts and news coverage to forum threads, podcast episodes and review sites. It shows how fast talk about you is growing and whether the tone is turning.

Reputation teams use it as an early warning system: a viral complaint or a local news story shows up as a spike before it reaches your inbox. It has no review-response workflow, so restaurant brands usually pair it with a review platform. Pasibus, a fast-casual chain in Poland, is one of its published case studies.

Key features

  • Wide source coverage: Your name is picked up on social platforms, news sites, blogs, forums, podcasts, newsletters, video and review sites.
  • Spike alerts: Volume and reach charts with alerts reveal a sudden wave of posts about a location or campaign.
  • Sentiment and emotion: Each mention is scored as positive, negative or neutral, so a shift in mood stands out.
  • Share of voice: Compare how often people talk about you and about competitors.
  • Influencer discovery: Find the accounts whose posts about you reach the most people.
  • AI visibility: LLM monitoring tracks how AI assistants mention and rank your brand.

Pros

  • Early warning: Real-time updates on Pro and higher plans help you respond to a story while it's still small.
  • Beyond review sites: It catches complaints posted on social media, forums and podcasts that review tools can miss.
  • Published prices: Every plan has a price on the website, with Enterprise shown as a starting price.
  • User rating: On G2, Brand24 averages 4.6 out of 5 from 332 reviews.

Cons

  • Listening only: You can't reply to reviews from Brand24, so it adds a tool to your stack.
  • Keyword limits: The Individual plan tracks three keywords, and mentions above a plan's limit incur overage fees.
  • Paid extras: API access costs $149 more, and AI Visibility is listed as an add-on below Enterprise.

Price and plans

Brand24 has no free plan, but every plan can be tested free for 14 days without a credit card. Annual billing costs less per month.

  • Individual: $249 per month, or $199 per month billed annually.
  • Team: $349 per month, or $299 per month billed annually.
  • Pro: $499 per month, or $399 per month billed annually.
  • Business: $699 per month, or $599 per month billed annually.
  • Enterprise: From $1,499 per month, billed annually.
