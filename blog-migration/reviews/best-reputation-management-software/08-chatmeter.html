8. Chatmeter

Chatmeter helps multi-location brands manage reviews, business listings and local search from one place. Survey vendor Alchemer has owned it since September 2025 and sells it as Alchemer Reputation Management, alongside its own feedback tools.

For a restaurant chain, the appeal is coverage. Reviews from more than 100 sites, listings across dozens of directories and a page for every location all report to the same dashboards, so a regional manager can see which stores need attention this week.

Key features

  • Review inbox for 100+ sites: Read and reply to reviews for every store in one place, using templates, bulk responses or suggested replies.
  • Rules-based auto-replies: AI Auto-Responder, added in March 2026, posts replies in your brand voice for the reviews you choose and flags delicate ones for a manager.
  • Listings management: Bulk-edit hours and details across Google, Apple Maps and 70+ directories, with alerts when something changes.
  • Local Pages: Each location gets its own search-optimized landing page, with support for several languages.
  • Analytics Studio: Dashboards and scheduled reports compare locations, and the data can flow on to Tableau or Domo.
  • Competitor benchmarks: Competitive Intelligence sets each store against the businesses around it.

Pros

  • Reviews, listings and local SEO together: One vendor covers three jobs that many chains split across separate tools.
  • Built for scale: Filters by location, region and review site make it practical for brands with dozens or hundreds of sites.
  • Private and public feedback: Alchemer's survey tools sit beside public reviews, so you can compare what guests tell you directly with what they post.
  • Well rated: G2 reviewers give it 4.5 out of 5 under its new name, Alchemer Reputation Management.

Cons

  • No public pricing: Reputation management isn't on Alchemer's price list, which only shows survey licenses.
  • More than a single store needs: The platform is designed for multi-location brands, so an independent restaurant may pay for features it won't use.
  • New owner, new name: Chatmeter's own site now points customers to Alchemer, and the product is sold under Alchemer's brand.

Price and plans

Chatmeter's pricing isn't published. Alchemer quotes it individually after a 30-minute demo.
