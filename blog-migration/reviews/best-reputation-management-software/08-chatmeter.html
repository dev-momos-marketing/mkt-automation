8. Chatmeter

Category: Reviews, listings and local search for chains

Chatmeter handles reviews, listings and local search for brands with many locations. Since September 2025 it has belonged to the survey vendor Alchemer, which markets it under the names Alchemer Reputation Management and Chatmeter by Alchemer.

Restaurant chains get review management, listings and AI review analysis next to Alchemer's survey tools, including review data from delivery apps.

Notable customers: Driven Brands, A&W and MR MIKES Steakhouse Casual.

What's new: Since March 2026, an AI Auto-Responder can write and publish replies in your brand voice, following rules you set by star rating, review site and wording. Reviews it judges sensitive go to a staff member before anything is posted.

Key features:

  • 100+ review sites. Track and answer reviews from one dashboard, whatever site the guest used.
  • Rules-based auto-replies. You decide which reviews get an automatic answer, and risky ones are flagged for a manager.
  • Pulse Ai: Signals. Ask plain-language questions about reviews, social posts and surveys, including photos guests attach.
  • Competitor comparisons. Pulse Ai: Competitive Intelligence, released in July 2025, sets each location against the restaurants around it.
  • Delivery app reviews. Restaurant brands can pull in ratings left on DoorDash, Grubhub and Uber Eats alongside Google and other sites.
  • Listings and local SEO. Keep listings accurate and follow local search performance store by store.

Pricing: Custom. Contact Alchemer sales for a quote.
