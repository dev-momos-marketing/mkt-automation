12. NiceJob

Category: Hands-off review collection for local service companies

Owned by Paystone since October 2021, NiceJob automates review requests, website review widgets, referrals and repeat-customer campaigns. It connects to the job and invoicing tools small service businesses use.

The software is built around home-service companies like movers, trades and cleaning crews, and restaurants aren't a target market, so it suits a small operator who mainly wants more reviews with little effort.

Notable customers: Evergreen Cleaning and You Move Me.

What's new: A partnership with SendJim, announced in October 2025, puts verified five-star reviews collected by NiceJob onto printed postcards for direct mail.

Key features:

  • Automatic review requests. Customers get email and SMS invitations with reminders if they don't respond.
  • Tool integrations. Connects with Jobber, Housecall Pro, QuickBooks and Zapier to trigger requests.
  • AI review replies. Adjust tone, length and rules, with optional auto-replies on the Pro plan.
  • Website widgets. Show recent reviews on your site as social proof.
  • Referrals and repeat visits. Pro adds referral campaigns, rebooking campaigns and marketing broadcasts.

Pricing: Grow costs from $75/month if you serve up to 2,500 customers annually, rising to $95/month between 2,501 and 5,000. Pro, from $125/month, includes AI review replies, broadcasts, referral and repeat campaigns. You can try it free for 14 days, and plans carry no contract.
