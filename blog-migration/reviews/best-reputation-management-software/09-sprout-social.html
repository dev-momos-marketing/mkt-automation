9. Sprout Social

Sprout Social, listed on Nasdaq as SPT, is built for running social media, and it earns a place on this list through its review management. The Reviews tab and Smart Inbox let one team watch ratings and social conversations side by side.

Restaurant brands Papa Johns and Lettuce Entertain You Enterprises are Sprout customers. It makes the most sense for groups with a dedicated social or marketing team that can justify paying per seat.

Key features

  • Reviews tab: Pulls ratings from Yelp, Tripadvisor, Trustpilot, Facebook and Google Business Profile into one view, and Google and Facebook reviews can be answered without leaving Sprout.
  • Review reporting: Compare review scores across locations and networks, and sort or filter the stream with custom views.
  • Smart Inbox: Comments, DMs, mentions and reviews from your connected accounts land in a single stream.
  • Listening and news monitoring: Sentiment and trend analysis covers social channels, and NewsWhip, acquired in July 2025, adds news and earned media.
  • Publishing for each location: Schedule posts across networks, including TikTok, Threads and Bluesky.
  • Team controls: Approvals, routing, task assignment and role permissions keep a multi-person team organized.

Pros

  • Reputation and social in one tool: A guest who complains on Facebook and in a Google review shows up in the same inbox.
  • Used by restaurant brands: Papa Johns and Lettuce Entertain You Enterprises have published case studies with Sprout.
  • Published prices: Every plan except Enterprise has a list price, and a 30-day trial lets you test it first.
  • Solid rating: Sprout Social averages 4.4 out of 5 on G2.

Cons

  • Seat costs add up: At $199 per seat per month on Standard, giving every general manager access gets expensive.
  • No reviews on the entry plan: Review management is listed from Standard upward, so Essentials at $79 won't cover it.
  • Social-first scope: Listings management and review requests aren't part of Sprout, so you may still need a local SEO or review generation tool.
  • Listening is extra: The listening add-on is priced separately after a demo.

Price and plans

Sprout Social offers a 30-day free trial with no credit card. Prices are per seat, per month, billed annually.

  • Essentials: $79 per seat, per month ($99 billed monthly).
  • Standard: $199 per seat, per month.
  • Professional: $299 per seat, per month.
  • Advanced: $399 per seat, per month.
  • Enterprise: Custom pricing.
