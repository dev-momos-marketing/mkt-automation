9. Sprout Social

Category: Social media suite that also monitors reviews

Sprout Social, traded on Nasdaq as SPT, is first and foremost a tool for running social media. It earns a place here because its Smart Inbox, review tab and listening tools let one team handle social engagement and reviews together. Its Trellis AI agent now runs across the product.

It makes the most sense for restaurant groups with a dedicated social or marketing team that can justify paying per seat.

Notable customers: Papa Johns and Lettuce Entertain You Enterprises.

What's new: In May 2026, Sprout Social presented its AI-powered social intelligence platform, turning Trellis into a conversational layer across the whole product and launching Trellis Studio, where teams can assemble their own AI workflows. Before that, in July 2025, it bought the news monitoring company NewsWhip.

Key features:

  • Smart Inbox. Comments, DMs, mentions and reviews from your connected accounts land in a single stream.
  • Reviews tab. Pulls in ratings from Yelp, Tripadvisor, Trustpilot, Facebook, Google Business Profile and other sites, with replies posted directly on Google and Facebook (Tripadvisor answers stay on Tripadvisor).
  • Publishing across networks. Schedule posts for every location's channels, including TikTok, Threads and Bluesky.
  • Listening with news coverage. Sentiment and trend analysis now reaches news and earned media through NewsWhip.
  • Trellis and AI Assist. The AI tools write first drafts of posts and replies, condense reports and propose tags for incoming messages.
  • Team controls. Approvals, routing, task assignment and role permissions keep a multi-person team organized.

Pricing: On annual billing, each seat costs $199 a month on Standard, $299 on Professional or $399 on Advanced, and Enterprise is quoted. Month-to-month billing costs more, and a 30-day free trial is available. Seat costs add up as you give more managers access.
