12. Marqii

Marqii is a reputation and listings platform made only for restaurants. It treats a good reputation as the result of correct information: menus and listings stay current on 70+ sites, and reviews from search engines and delivery apps are tracked in the same dashboard.

Most platforms in this list serve many industries. Marqii is the narrower pick, worth a look if you run a restaurant group and want one vendor whose features, from POS menu sync to delivery review tracking, follow how restaurants work.

Key features

  • Listings management: Hours, holiday hours, photos, dining attributes and preferred ordering links update across every publisher Marqii supports, with no limit on edits.
  • Search-ready menus: Menu changes flow from your POS to the big listing sites, and Marqii marks menus up in code that search engines and AI bots can read.
  • Review tracking: Ratings from Uber Eats, ezCater, DoorDash and Grubhub appear next to Google, Yelp and Facebook reviews, with custom notifications for the ones you care about.
  • Reply options: The Scale plan adds AI-suggested replies, or Marqii's managed team can write the responses for you.
  • Location pages: Host builds a search-optimized page for each location, with menus and hours embedded, on the Marqii Package.

Pros

  • Restaurant focus: POS menu sync and delivery-app review tracking are built around the way restaurants operate.
  • One source of truth: Listings, menus, FAQ widgets and location pages draw on the same data, which keeps your details consistent wherever guests search.
  • Clear costs: Per-location prices for all four plans are listed on Marqii's website.

Cons

  • Restaurants only: If you also manage brands outside hospitality, you'd need a second reputation platform for them.
  • Reviews start on Growth: The $90 Base plan covers listings and menus only; review monitoring and replies begin at $145.
  • Annual contract: Each location signs up for twelve months, and a free demo takes the place of a trial period.

Price and plans

Marqii doesn't offer a free trial or free plan; you can book a free demo. Plans are priced per location, per month, on an annual contract, and managed replies cost extra, from $100 a month for 50 reviews.

  • Base: $90 per location, per month (listings and menus).
  • Growth: $145 per location, per month (adds review monitoring and replies).
  • Scale: $180 per location, per month (adds AI-suggested replies, review generation and social).
  • Marqii Package: $250 per location, per month (adds Host location pages).
