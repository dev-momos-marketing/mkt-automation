12. Marqii

Category: Restaurant menus, listings and review response

Marqii treats reputation as part of keeping a restaurant's online details correct. Menu changes in your POS flow out to Google, Yelp and other sites, listings carry the right hours and photos, and reviews from delivery and booking platforms land in the same dashboard as Google and Facebook ratings. For a multi-location brand, that means fewer guests complaining about a dish that's no longer served or a closing time that changed months ago.

Notable customers: Romulus Restaurant Group (IHOP franchisee), Uccello's Hospitality Group and California Tortilla.

What's new: Marqii added social scheduling for Facebook and Google Business Profile in May 2025, then branded first-party review forms in November 2025. In July 2026 it began texting guests after a purchase to ask for a Google, Facebook or TripAdvisor review, through a partnership with Thanx.

Key features:

  • POS menu sync. Update a dish or price once and it's pushed to Google, Yelp and other listing sites.
  • Wide review coverage. The inbox pulls in Google, Yelp, Facebook, OpenTable, Uber Eats, Grubhub and ezCater reviews, among others.
  • Reply help. AI suggests responses, and high-volume brands can hand replies to a managed response service.
  • Owned feedback. First-party review forms can be shared by link, QR code or website embed, and SMS requests follow purchases.
  • Location insights. A multi-location dashboard compares review trends across your restaurants.

Marqii publishes per-location monthly prices on an annual contract: the $74.99 Base tier handles menus and location details, the $119.99 Pro tier brings in reviews, and Kitchen Sync at $149.99 adds advanced review tools. AI-suggested responses cost $15 more per location each month and social management $20, with custom quotes for large multi-unit groups.
