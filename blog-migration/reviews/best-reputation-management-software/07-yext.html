7. Yext

Website: Yext

Category: Digital presence and listings-first reputation management

Listed on the New York Stock Exchange as YEXT, Yext feeds your directory listings, store pages, review replies and AI search presence from a single central database. Its strength for restaurant chains is keeping hours, menus and location details accurate everywhere guests search, with review management alongside.

A proposal to take the company private was withdrawn in February 2026, and Yext remains listed.

Notable customers: Taco Bell, Arby's, Bojangles and Marston's.

What's new: In April 2026, Popmenu widened its Yext partnership letting restaurants push menu and listing changes and answer reviews across 70+ platforms. In March 2025 Yext launched Scout, an agent that tracks visibility and sentiment in Google, Bing and AI assistants.

Key features:

  • Knowledge Graph. A central record stores each location's menus, hours and other details, and feeds every listing from there.
  • Listings sync. One edit reaches Apple Maps, Google, Facebook, Bing and a long list of other apps and directories.
  • Review response with AI drafts. Watch review sites and answer guests with replies that AI writes first.
  • Review generation. AI campaigns ask guests for reviews, including on third-party sites since 2024.
  • Scout visibility tracking. See how you appear in traditional and AI search, benchmarked against competitors.
  • Local pages and permissions. Yext Pages builds a landing page per location, and approval workflows keep franchise networks in line.

Pricing: Multi-location and enterprise brands get custom quotes. Third-party sites list self-serve tiers with conflicting amounts, so confirm current prices with Yext.
