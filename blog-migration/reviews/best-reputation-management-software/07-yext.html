7. Yext

Yext manages the online presence of multi-location brands from a central knowledge base that feeds directory listings, store pages and review management. Restaurant chains mostly use it to keep hours, menus and addresses correct on every site guests check before a visit, and handle reviews in the same place.

Yext is still listed on the New York Stock Exchange after its CEO's proposal to take the company private was withdrawn in February 2026. Its restaurant customers include Taco Bell, Arby's, Bojangles and Marston's.

Key features

  • Listings sync: Edit a location once and the change reaches Google, Apple Maps, Bing, Facebook and a long list of other apps and directories.
  • Review dashboard: One view collects reviews across Google, Facebook, Yelp and 80+ industry sites, with smart filters and alerts for every location.
  • Approved responses: Your team edits and signs off on AI-written replies before they're posted under the brand's name.
  • Review generation: Email, text and QR code requests ask guests for a rating at the moment they're most likely to leave one.
  • Local pages: Yext Pages builds a search-friendly landing page for every restaurant.
  • Scout benchmarks: Compare your visibility and sentiment with competitors in both traditional and AI search.

Pros

  • Accurate details everywhere: One record per location lowers the chance that a guest finds old hours or a closed address on some directory.
  • Reputation and listings together: Review work and listings share a platform, so one team can own both.
  • Built for franchise networks: Role-based permissions and audit trails show who changed or answered what at each location.
  • Strong user score: On G2, the average score from users is 4.4 out of 5.

Cons

  • Quote-only pricing: Cost depends on how many locations you have and which products you buy, and Yext lists no figures.
  • Shorter terms cost more: A one-year contract carries a higher rate than a multi-year commitment.
  • Small operators go through resellers: Yext points smaller businesses to reseller partners instead of selling to them directly.

Price and plans

Yext doesn't publish prices or offer a standard free trial; you get a quote after a demo. Pricing is custom by location count and solution, and billing is usually annual.
