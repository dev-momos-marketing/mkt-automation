6. ReviewTrackers

Website: ReviewTrackers

Category: Multi-location review monitoring and reporting

ReviewTrackers is a review and feedback monitoring platform for multi-location businesses. InMoment has owned it since 2022, and in May 2025 InMoment was itself bought by Press Ganey Forsta. The product now sits inside that larger customer experience group while keeping its own brand and site.

It suits franchise and multi-unit teams that want every location's reviews, approved reply templates and reporting in one dashboard, especially if they already use InMoment.

Notable customers: Ted's Montana Grill, PACK & SEND and Chem-Dry.

What's new: The latest dated news came from the parent company: in July 2025, InMoment's reputation software gained AI Auto Responding, with generative replies built on prompts your brand stores. It shipped on two plans, Locations Starter and Locations Essential, the same names ReviewTrackers uses for its tiers, although the release never names ReviewTrackers.

Key features:

  • Reviews in a single dashboard. New ratings from Google, Facebook, TripAdvisor and other sites trigger alerts, so nothing sits unread.
  • Approved reply templates. With Smart Response, a store manager picks from replies your brand has signed off and posts one in a few clicks.
  • AI-written replies. AI Auto Responding drafts personal responses from the prompts your brand team saves.
  • Review requests. Guests receive automated invitations by email or text after their visit.
  • Local competitor tracking. Compare your ratings and review data with nearby restaurants.
  • Franchise-shaped reporting. Reports by location and permissions by role mirror how your franchise is organized, while topic and sentiment analytics flag recurring problems.

Pricing: Quoted per location. ReviewTrackers lists its plans without prices and invites you to schedule a call.
