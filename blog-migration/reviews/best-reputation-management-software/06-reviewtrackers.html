6. ReviewTrackers

ReviewTrackers is a reputation management platform for chains and franchise systems. It pulls review data from more than 100 sources into one account, helps each location respond in an approved voice, and reports on ratings and themes across your portfolio.

It has belonged to InMoment since 2022, which in turn joined Press Ganey Forsta in May 2025, yet it still sells under its own name. Its review data can also live inside InMoment's customer experience platform, alongside the rest of your guest feedback.

Key features

  • Portfolio dashboard: Ratings and reviews for every location sit in a single view, with an alert whenever a new review posts.
  • Approved responses: Smart Response templates keep local replies on brand, and AI Auto Responding can draft answers from prompts head office saves.
  • Review generation: Customers receive automated invitations by email or text after a purchase or visit.
  • Competitor insights: On the Essential plan, your ratings and review data appear next to those of nearby rivals.
  • Franchise permissions: Location reports and role-based access let a franchisee see their own stores while the brand team sees all of them.

Pros

  • Multi-location focus: Reporting, permissions and per-location pricing are all designed around brands with many sites.
  • Wide coverage: Smaller review platforms are tracked together with the big ones, so fewer comments slip past.
  • Room for the whole team: Starter comes with unlimited users.
  • Well reviewed: ReviewTrackers holds a 4.6 rating on G2 from 161 reviews.

Cons

  • Listings sold separately: Local listings management, local search rank and managed services are add-ons you pay for on top of a plan.
  • Annual billing: Every plan is paid once a year, which means a larger upfront payment than monthly billing.
  • Quote only: With no prices on the site, comparing costs takes a sales call.

Price and plans

ReviewTrackers doesn't publish prices; you book time with its team for a custom quote. Plans (Data Only, Starter and Essential) are priced per location, per month and paid annually.
