1. Momos

Momos gives multi-location restaurant brands an AI-powered reputation management platform built around their locations. It gathers reviews, guest messages and survey feedback from every location, answers guests through an AI agent, and turns what guests say into fixes for your operations teams.

Notable customers: restaurant chains including Potbelly, Caribou Coffee, Papa Murphy's and Condado Tacos.

What's new: Through 2025, Momos shipped AI copilots, AI summaries of each ticket, AI brand guidelines and reports designed for phones, along with integrations for Paytronix, Square and Otter. Potbelly also joined in 2025 and now tracks guest messages, cases and reviews for its 472 locations in a single view.

What you can do with Momos:

  • Work from a single queue. Your Google reviews sit next to social DMs, POS data and App Store feedback in the Unified Inbox, where AI triage sends each item to the right store.
  • Answer reviews automatically. Alfie, the Experience Agent, writes replies trained on your brand voice and remembers each guest's past visits and preferences.
  • Win back unhappy guests. Alfie can hand out points, coupons and offers from your loyalty platform, so a bad review can end with an invitation to return.
  • Match the reviewer's language. Built-in real-time translation means a location can respond in whatever language the reviewer used.
  • Spot operational patterns. Store-level analytics reveal the causes behind your best and worst ratings, which feeds your operational excellence work.

Here, survey responses, Yelp and Google reviews and Instagram comments sit in the same queue for your team.

Alfie drafts each reply in your tone, and you can give it a brand guideline or a response library to follow. It also powers the AI agent for customer support side of Momos.

The analytics view splits a demo brand's overall satisfaction into categories next to each location's major incidents, so you can fix a problem before it spreads and improve the guest experience across the chain.

Papa Murphy's, a chain of 1,000+ locations, now has 97.7% of its review replies handled automatically, and its recovery offers have brought back $2M+ in revenue.
