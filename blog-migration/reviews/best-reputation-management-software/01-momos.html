1. Momos

Momos is a reputation and guest experience platform made specifically for restaurant and hospitality brands with many locations. It collects reviews, guest messages and survey responses from every store, replies through an AI agent, and shows operators what the feedback says about each location.

If you run restaurants, it's one of the AI-powered reputation management platforms designed around your industry. FoodVest Group, which runs Caribou Coffee in Bahrain and Saudi Arabia, cut its reply time from 19 days to 1 hour and gained 61% more Google reviews across 57 locations.

Key features

  • Single review queue: The Unified Inbox lines up Google reviews with App Store ratings, social DMs and POS data, and AI triage sends each one to the store that owns it.
  • AI replies: Alfie writes on-brand answers to reviews around the clock and can include a recovery offer for an unhappy guest.
  • Tagging and alerts: AI sorts feedback by sentiment and topic, then alerts your team in real time when something needs attention.
  • Listings management: Each location's business listings are kept up to date from one place.
  • Operational analytics: Store-level reports reveal what drives your best and worst ratings, which supports operational excellence work.

Pros

  • Restaurant data in context: Loyalty, POS and delivery-app data sit beside the reviews for each store.
  • Replies and support in one: Alfie also answers guest messages, and it's covered in our guide to AI tools for responding to reviews.
  • Every store at a glance: Category scores and incidents by location help you fix problems and improve the guest experience across the chain.
  • Used by chains: Potbelly, Papa Murphy's and Condado Tacos are among the brands on Momos.

Cons

  • Restaurants only: It's designed for restaurant and hospitality brands, so other industries are better served elsewhere on this list.
  • Custom quotes: There's no public pricing, and a demo comes first.
  • Managed onboarding: Plan for roughly seven days of guided onboarding before launch.

Price and plans

Momos doesn't publish prices; you get a custom quote after a demo, which you can book on the Momos website.
