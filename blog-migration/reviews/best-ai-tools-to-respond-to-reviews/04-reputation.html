4. Reputation

Reputation, formerly Reputation.com, is an enterprise customer experience platform that puts reviews, surveys, listings and social in one place for multi-location brands. Restaurants and hospitality groups are among its core markets, together with automotive and healthcare.

Notable customers: Tortilla and Mitchells & Butlers.

What's new: In May 2026, Reputation launched Reputation IQ, which answers plain-English questions about your feedback data, and expanded its AI Reputation Manager, which tracks the way answer engines such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity present you.

What you can do with Reputation:

  • Reply from a single inbox. Every store's feedback from Google, Yelp, Facebook, Tripadvisor and more lands in a shared queue your managers can clear.
  • Trigger review requests from orders. Requests by text, email or QR code go out when the POS records a sale, whether the guest ate in or ordered delivery.
  • Read feedback by theme. Analytics cluster what guests write into themes like cleanliness and service, which shows you what's pulling ratings up or down.
  • Run surveys next to reviews. You can compare what guests tell you privately in a survey with what they post publicly.
  • Query your data in plain words. Reputation IQ lets you type a question about your feedback and get an answer without building a report.

Why Reputation stands out: It pairs review replies with surveys, listings, social and AI search tracking, which gives head office at a large group the reporting depth it needs across hundreds of stores.

Who it's best for: Big restaurant and hotel groups whose head office wants one team managing reviews, surveys and listings for every site.

Pricing: Reputation doesn't publish prices. Plans are custom-quoted by its sales team.
