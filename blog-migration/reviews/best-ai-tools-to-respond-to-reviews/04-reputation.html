4. Reputation

Reputation, formerly Reputation.com, is an enterprise reputation and customer experience platform for multi-location brands. Every plan includes review management with AI responding, and higher tiers add surveys, sentiment analytics and tools that track how AI search engines describe you.

For a restaurant group, the draw is answering reviews for every location from one queue while new review requests go out automatically after orders. Tortilla and Mitchells & Butlers are among its hospitality customers.

Key features

  • AI responding: Rep Core, the entry plan, covers review collection, review requests and AI-drafted responses.
  • Shared review queue: Feedback from Google, Yelp, Facebook, Tripadvisor and more reaches one inbox your managers work through together.
  • Order-triggered requests: Invitations by text, email or QR code go out after a dine-in check, delivery order or POS event.
  • Theme analytics: Sentiment and text analysis group comments into topics like cleanliness and service, which shows what moves your ratings.
  • Reputation IQ: Launched in May 2026, it answers questions you type about your feedback without a custom report.

Pros

  • Published prices: Plans start at $80 per location per month, so you can estimate costs before a sales call.
  • Requests and replies in one place: The same system that asks guests for reviews also drafts the answers.
  • AI search tracking: AI Reputation Manager shows how ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity present your brand to people asking where to eat.
  • Strong user rating: Reputation holds a 4.5 out of 5 on G2 across 2,470 reviews.

Cons

  • Cost per location: At $80 to $150 per location per month, the bill grows quickly for a large group.
  • Extras cost more: Social tools, actions and ticketing, competitive insights and premium integrations are add-ons without a published price.
  • No free trial: The pricing page has no trial; you request a demo instead.

Price and plans

Reputation publishes its prices per location, per month, and quotes Enterprise plans separately.

  • Rep Core: $80 per location, per month.
  • Rep Core + Pulse: $115 per location, per month.
  • Rep Core + Surveys: $150 per location, per month.
  • Enterprise: Custom pricing for 125+ locations.
