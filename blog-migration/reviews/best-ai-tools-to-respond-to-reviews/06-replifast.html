6. RepliFast

RepliFast is a budget AI tool that writes and posts replies to reviews on your Google Business Profile. It handles Google only, which keeps it narrow and easy to set up for a small restaurant team.

Notable customers: RepliFast hasn't shared the names of any businesses that use it.

What's new: The company hasn't put out any dated product announcements over the past two years.

What you can do with RepliFast:

  • Reply in your own tone. The AI drafts each response to sound like your restaurant, whether that's a casual diner or a white-tablecloth room.
  • Clear an old backlog. Batch generation writes replies to a stack of unanswered reviews in one pass.
  • Turn on autopilot. On the Pro plan, replies go live within minutes of a new Google review landing.
  • Set rules by star rating. You might auto-post thank-yous for 4- and 5-star reviews and hold anything from 1 to 3 stars for a manager to check.
  • See recurring themes. Sentiment analytics point out what guests mention again and again across your reviews.

Why RepliFast stands out: It sticks to Google review replies and charges little for them, so an owner who only needs that one job doesn't pay for a full reputation suite.

Who it's best for: Independent restaurants and small teams whose main need is quick, consistent answers to Google reviews.

Pricing: A free plan includes 5 AI replies a month. Starter costs $15 a month, or $12 billed annually, and Pro with autopilot is $29 a month, or $19 billed annually. Third-party sites quote different figures, so confirm on RepliFast's pricing page.
