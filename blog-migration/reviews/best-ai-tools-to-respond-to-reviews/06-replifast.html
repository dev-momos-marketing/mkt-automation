6. RepliFast

RepliFast is an inexpensive AI reply tool that helps small businesses keep up with their Google Business Profile reviews. It connects through Google's official API, writes each reply in your tone and in the reviewer's language, and on its Pro plan can post replies by itself within minutes of a new review.

It handles Google reviews only, which keeps it simple for an independent restaurant whose main job is answering every Google review. Kevin Hofmann founded it in 2025, and updates arrive often: a backlog handoff to its review agent shipped in July 2026, followed by per-location pricing in August.

Key features

  • Replies in your voice: You set the tone, signature and house rules, and the AI drafts a response to each review in any of 50+ languages.
  • Star-rating rules: Choose which ratings post on their own, for example 4- and 5-star thank-yous, while 1- to 3-star reviews wait in a queue for you.
  • Context check: When a critical review names a staff member or describes an incident, RepliFast pauses and asks you for one line of context.
  • Backlog catch-up: Batch generation works through older unanswered reviews in a single pass.
  • Review insights: Pro adds weekly summaries of recurring complaints and praise.
  • Visibility Pack: Pro also drafts Google Posts and service updates from your reviews and website.

Pros

  • Low cost: Paid plans start at $12 a month billed annually, and the free plan covers 5 replies a month.
  • Careful with bad reviews: Replies to 1- and 2-star reviews never post without your approval, whatever rules you set.
  • Easy to try: The Pro trial needs no credit card, and nothing is charged automatically when it ends.

Cons

  • Google only: Reviews on Yelp, Tripadvisor, delivery apps and other sites aren't covered.
  • No team seats: Team seats aren't included, so it suits an owner or a single manager.
  • Autopilot on Pro only: On Starter you approve and post every reply yourself, up to 200 a month per location.
  • Early-stage company: RepliFast started in 2025, and the testimonials on its site come from shops and a tradesman.

Price and plans

RepliFast has a free plan, and you can try Pro free for 14 days or 15 posted replies, whichever comes first, with no credit card. Paid plans are priced per location, billed monthly or annually.

  • Free: $0, with 5 AI replies a month for one location.
  • Starter: $15 per month, or $12 per month billed annually.
  • Pro: $29 per month, or $19 per month billed annually.
