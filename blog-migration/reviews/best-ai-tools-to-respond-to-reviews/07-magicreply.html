7. MagicReply

MagicReply writes replies to your Google reviews with AI, matching the way your business already talks to guests, and can post them to your Business Profile for you. It's a small, low-cost product for local businesses, and its restaurant page says replies can mention the dishes, service speed and ambiance a guest wrote about.

Most of its recent updates landed in March 2026: voice profiles set per location, branded review request emails with a direct Google review link for each storefront, and Chrome Extension 2.0, which fills AI replies straight into Google's review box.

Key features

  • Editable drafts: Each Google review gets an AI reply you can change or regenerate before it goes live.
  • Voice profile: MagicReply learns your greeting, sign-off and vocabulary from 10+ past replies, and each location can keep its own.
  • Auto-reply rules: Replies can post automatically based on star rating, keywords or sentiment, with delays and approvals.
  • Chrome extension: Insert a reply inside Google's own interface and adjust its tone and length there.
  • Team approvals and analytics: Managers can approve replies before they publish, and a dashboard tracks response rates and sentiment trends.

Pros

  • Low monthly cost: Pro is $19.99 a month, and Business covers unlimited locations for $49.99.
  • Free to start: A free plan lets one location try AI replies before paying.
  • Restaurant templates: Its free template library includes replies for praise of food and service, food quality complaints and wait times.
  • Hands-off option: Rules can thank five-star reviewers automatically during a busy service.

Cons

  • Google only: Yelp, TripAdvisor, Facebook and delivery app reviews aren't supported.
  • Small free allowance: The free plan's AI replies and Google posts are a one-time total that doesn't renew each month.
  • One location on Pro: A second storefront means moving up to Business.
  • Card for the trial: Paid-plan trials need a card and bill automatically unless you cancel.

Price and plans

MagicReply has a free plan for one Google location, and both paid plans start with a 7-day free trial. Businesses with 10+ locations can ask for a custom enterprise plan.

  • Free: $0, with a limited total of AI replies and Google posts.
  • Pro: $19.99 per month for one location, with unlimited AI replies.
  • Business: $49.99 per month for unlimited locations and team collaboration.
