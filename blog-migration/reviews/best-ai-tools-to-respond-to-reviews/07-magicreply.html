7. MagicReply

MagicReply is a small AI tool from Somnus Labs that drafts and posts replies to Google Business Profile reviews in your own voice. It pitches itself to restaurants and cafes, with templates for the topics diners raise most.

Notable customers: MagicReply doesn't publish a customer list.

What's new: In March 2026, MagicReply made voice profiles location-specific, so each storefront can sound like itself, and added review request emails carrying each location's Google link. Chrome Extension 2.0, also from 2026, drops replies straight into Google's review box.

What you can do with MagicReply:

  • Edit before posting. AI drafts a response to each Google review, and you can tweak it or ask for a fresh version.
  • Sound like yourself. A voice profile picks up your usual greeting, sign-off and wording from past replies.
  • Automate by rule. Auto-reply rules can key off star rating, keywords or sentiment, so routine praise gets answered while you're on the line.
  • Start from restaurant templates. Ready-made replies cover food quality, service, wait times and value.
  • Share the work. Team approval flows and review analytics help when several managers handle replies.

Why MagicReply stands out: Its templates and per-location voices are written around restaurant feedback, so replies can speak to the meal and the service a guest describes.

Who it's best for: Independent restaurants, cafes and small groups that want fast replies to Google reviews in their own voice. It doesn't cover Yelp, Tripadvisor or delivery apps.

Pricing: There's a limited free plan for one location. Pro costs $19.99 a month and Business, with unlimited locations, $49.99 a month, and paid plans come with a 7-day trial.
