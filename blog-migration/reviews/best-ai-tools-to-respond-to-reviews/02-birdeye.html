2. Birdeye

Birdeye is a marketing and reputation suite for chains and franchise groups, where review management shares one login with listings, messaging, social and surveys. When a review comes in, its AI can write a reply in your brand voice for a manager to check, or an agent can answer and post it without waiting.

Restaurants get their own version. Birdeye released it in January 2025 with models trained on restaurant data and Yelp monitoring through a partnership with Yelp, so feedback from diners on Yelp is watched alongside Google.

Key features

  • Two reply modes: Choose AI drafts that a person approves, or let Birdeye's agents handle responses end to end once your voice is set.
  • Negative review routing: Alerts and tickets send a poor rating straight to the manager of the store it mentions, so the reply comes from someone who knows what happened.
  • Requests after a visit: Text and email invitations ask guests to leave a rating, giving you more recent reviews to answer.
  • Listings with menus: Directory details stay current, and restaurants on SkyTab or Uber Eats can sync menu details to their listings.
  • Insights AI: Review sentiment is reported by location, district and region, so you can tell which stores draw the most complaints.

Pros

  • Replies at volume: Agents keep pace when dozens of locations collect reviews over a busy weekend.
  • Beyond reply drafting: Since View 2025, more than 10 agents have worked across reviews, listings, social and surveys.
  • Reviews and messages together: Texts, webchat, social messages and email land in the same platform as reviews, so one team can answer both.
  • User rating: Reviewers on G2 give Birdeye 4.7 out of 5.

Cons

  • Quote only: No price list is public; Birdeye prices by what you deploy across your locations.
  • Broad purchase: If you only want AI review responses, you're buying into a wide platform built for multi-location brands.

Price and plans

Birdeye keeps its prices private. You share your number of locations on its pricing page and get a custom quote.
