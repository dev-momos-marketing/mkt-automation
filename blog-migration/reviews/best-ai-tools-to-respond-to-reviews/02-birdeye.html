2. Birdeye

Birdeye describes itself as agentic marketing software for chains and franchise groups, and review management sits alongside listings, messaging, social and surveys. AI agents handle much of the routine work, including drafting and posting replies to guest reviews in your brand voice.

Notable customers: Black Bear Diner and The Saxton Group, a McAlister's Deli franchisee.

What's new: In January 2025, Birdeye released a restaurant version of its AI marketing platform, trained on restaurant data and able to watch Yelp reviews through a partnership with Yelp. In September 2026 it introduced AI Coworkers, named Jay, Myna and Robin, and added a SkyTab POS link that pushes menu details to your listings.

What you can do with Birdeye:

  • Answer reviews with agents. AI drafts or fully handles replies in your voice, so a weekend of Google and Yelp feedback doesn't pile up until Monday.
  • Route bad reviews to the right store. Alerts and tickets send a negative review straight to the manager of the location it mentions.
  • Ask more guests for reviews. Text and email requests go out after a visit to build up fresh ratings on the sites diners check.
  • Keep menus and hours current. Listings management updates directories for every location, and SkyTab users can sync menu items automatically.
  • Spot patterns by region. Insights AI groups sentiment by location, district and region and benchmarks you against nearby competitors.

Why Birdeye stands out: It goes further than reply drafting, with more than 10 agents working across reviews, listings, social and surveys, which suits chains that want software to carry out tasks on its own instead of waiting for a manager to click approve.

Pricing: Quotes are custom and come after a demo, since Birdeye keeps its price list private.
