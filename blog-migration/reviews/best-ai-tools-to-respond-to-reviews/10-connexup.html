10. Connexup

Connexup is a restaurant growth platform that combines commission-free online ordering, kiosk and QR ordering, loyalty and delivery app connections with AI review replies. Its review tool is trained on your restaurant's profile, menu and policies, and you can buy it on its own for each Google Business Profile.

It suits independent and small restaurants, especially on Square POS, that want quick, multilingual replies to Google reviews without signing a contract.

Key features

  • Trained replies: AI drafts each response from your restaurant's profile, menu and policies, in a tone you set anywhere from playful to formal.
  • Manual or automatic: Staff can review and publish each draft themselves, or a fully automated mode posts replies with no manual step.
  • 72-hour backstop: If a review sits unanswered for 72 hours, the AI writes a reply and, with Auto-Publish on, posts it right away.
  • Multilingual responses: Replies can go out in English, Spanish, Chinese and other languages without hiring multilingual staff.
  • Receipt review prompts: Online and in-store receipts link guests to Google and Yelp, and reviews can be traced to specific orders.
  • Review analytics: Sentiment summaries and rating trends show which dishes or staff members guests mention most.

Pros

  • Low entry cost: Review replies are sold separately at $39 per Google Business Profile each month, billed month to month.
  • Free trial: You get 30 days free with no credit card or contract.
  • Control over posting: You choose between approving every draft and letting the AI publish on its own.
  • Bundle option: Ordering, kiosk, loyalty and review replies can all come from one vendor.

Cons

  • Google only for now: Connexup lists Yelp and TripAdvisor replies as coming soon.
  • Per-order fee on the suite: The Starter Suite charges 2.5% per order on top of $149 a month.
  • Loyalty is quoted: The loyalty program sits in the Advanced & Custom Suite, which has custom pricing.
  • Square only: Square is the only POS with a native integration.

Price and plans

The Starter Suite and each standalone product come with a 30-day free trial and no credit card. Billing is monthly and prorated, with no setup fees, and you can cancel anytime.

  • Reviews Management Only: $39 per Google Business Profile, per month.
  • Starter Suite: $149 per month plus 2.5% per order (web, kiosk and QR ordering, Square POS integration).
  • Advanced & Custom Suite: Custom pricing (mobile app, brand website, loyalty and promotions).
