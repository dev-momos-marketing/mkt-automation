10. Connexup

Connexup is a restaurant growth platform that combines commission-free online ordering, kiosk and QR ordering, loyalty and delivery app connections with AI review replies. It integrates natively with Square, and other POS systems are available on request.

Notable customers: Connexup hasn't named customers or published case studies.

What's new: In October 2025, Connexup released online ordering, then added a self-ordering kiosk app in November and QR code table ordering in December.

What you can do with Connexup:

  • Reply to Google and Yelp. AI writes responses to reviews on both sites, and you can have it post one automatically once a review has waited 72 hours.
  • Ask guests after a visit. Post-visit requests nudge diners to leave a review and build up your volume.
  • Take orders directly. A branded ordering site, a kiosk and QR table ordering let you skip third-party commissions.
  • Bring guests back. A loyalty program and personalized promotions give regulars a reason to return.
  • Connect delivery apps. DoorDash and Uber Eats orders flow in, and menu suggestions point out upsell and cross-sell chances.

Why Connexup stands out: Review replies come bundled with direct ordering and loyalty, so a small restaurant can cover several jobs with one vendor on a month-to-month plan.

Who it's best for: Independent and small restaurants, especially on Square, that want ordering, loyalty and AI review replies from a single provider.

Pricing: Starter Suite is published at $149 a month, billed month to month without setup costs, and you can try it free for 30 days first. Advanced and Custom plans are priced by features.
