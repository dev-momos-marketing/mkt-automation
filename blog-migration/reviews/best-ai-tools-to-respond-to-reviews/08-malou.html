8. Malou

Malou is a restaurant marketing platform from Paris that puts AI review replies, local SEO, listings, social media and AI search visibility under one login. Its reply tools start from your brand's own voice guidelines and score each answer before it goes out.

Groups can use the self-serve MalouApp or pair it with Copilot, which assigns a dedicated marketing expert. Riviera Dining Group and MELT Oberkampf are among its named customers.

Key features

  • Tone matching: Drafts follow your written brand guidelines and adjust to each review's sentiment, warmer for praise and more careful for complaints.
  • Reply quality score: Each answer is rated against Google's best practices, with tips that help managers fix a weak reply before posting.
  • Sensitive review alerts: Mentions of hygiene or discrimination raise an alert, so a person can answer those reviews.
  • Theme detection: Semantic analysis shows which topics keep coming up, location by location.
  • Local search tie-in: Listings and local SEO sit beside the reply queue, so each restaurant's search presence is managed in the same place.

Pros

  • Coaching built in: The quality score shows managers what a good reply looks like as they write.
  • Escalation for risky topics: Alerts separate hygiene and discrimination complaints from everyday reviews.
  • Hands-on help: Copilot adds a marketing expert when your team can't keep up with replies and posts.

Cons

  • No public prices: Each plan is quoted by location count.
  • Add-ons: Store Locator and Boosters are sold separately from the plans.
  • Restaurant-only: A brand that also runs other kinds of locations would need a second tool for those.

Price and plans

Malou doesn't publish prices. Its Essential, Growth and Full plans are quoted by the number of locations, and locations added mid-contract are prorated.
