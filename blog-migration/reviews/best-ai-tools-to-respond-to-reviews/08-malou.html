8. Malou

Malou is a Paris-based AI marketing platform for restaurant groups that puts review replies, local SEO, listings, social media and AI search visibility under one login. You can run it yourself through MalouApp or add Copilot, which pairs you with a dedicated marketing expert.

Notable customers: Krispy Kreme, Tudor Steak House NYC, Riviera Dining Group and MELT Oberkampf.

What's new: Malou hasn't announced any dated product releases recently.

What you can do with Malou:

  • Reply in your house voice. AI responses follow your brand guidelines and shift tone to match how happy or upset each guest sounds.
  • Score each reply. A quality rating with tips drawn from Google's best practices helps managers tighten their answers before posting.
  • Catch sensitive reviews. Alerts fire when a guest mentions topics like hygiene or discrimination, so those don't sit in the queue.
  • Find recurring themes. Semantic analysis pulls out what keeps coming up across your reviews, location by location.
  • Grow local search presence. Listings, a store locator and local pages are tuned for Google and AI search, alongside social posting.

Why Malou stands out: Review replies sit next to local SEO and social, so the work you put into answering guests feeds into how each restaurant shows up when diners search nearby.

Who it's best for: Restaurant groups with three or more locations that want reviews, local search and social in one restaurant-specific tool, with optional hands-on help.

Pricing: Malou has no public price list. Its sales team quotes each plan.
