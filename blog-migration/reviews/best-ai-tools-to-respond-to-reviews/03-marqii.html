3. Marqii

Marqii is a digital operations platform for restaurants that keeps menus, listings, reviews and social posts up to date from one dashboard. For review replies it offers two routes: AI drafts that your managers approve, and a done-for-you service in which Marqii's own team writes the responses.

Check where your guests leave reviews first: Marqii also monitors delivery and catering apps, and it posts replies on Google, Yelp and Facebook.

Key features

  • Suggested Review Response: One click creates a personalized draft, shaped by the prompts and tone settings you define for positive, neutral and negative reviews.
  • Managed Review Response: Brands that get more than 50 reviews a month can hand replies to Marqii's team, which also escalates critical reviews straight to you.
  • AI review summaries: Sentiment analysis and short summaries show what guests repeat across your locations.
  • Review Generation: Guests who order through a Marqii partner receive a text with a direct review link, which brings in fresh reviews to answer.
  • POS menu sync: Menu edits made in your POS reach Google, Yelp, Facebook and Apple Maps, which heads off complaints about dishes you no longer serve.

Pros

  • A person signs off: Marqii describes its drafts as "suggested by AI, approved by you," so a manager reviews each AI reply before it posts.
  • Room to scale: You can start with AI drafts and move your busiest locations to the managed service later, which is priced per review.
  • Restaurant focus: The reply tool is designed for restaurants, and DoorDash, Grubhub and ezCater ratings show up beside your Google reviews.
  • Published prices: Every plan and add-on has a price on Marqii's website.

Cons

  • Replies on three sites: Responses go out on Google, Yelp and Facebook only, while reviews on delivery and catering apps stay read-only.
  • AI drafts start on Scale: Suggested replies come with the Scale plan at $180 per location, per month.
  • No free trial: You can book a free demo instead, and plans run on a yearly contract.

Price and plans

Marqii doesn't offer a free trial or free plan; you can book a free demo. Plans are priced per location, per month, on an annual contract, and managed replies cost extra, from $100 a month for 50 reviews.

  • Base: $90 per location, per month (listings and menus).
  • Growth: $145 per location, per month (adds review monitoring and replies).
  • Scale: $180 per location, per month (adds AI-suggested replies, review generation and social).
  • Marqii Package: $250 per location, per month (adds Host location pages).
