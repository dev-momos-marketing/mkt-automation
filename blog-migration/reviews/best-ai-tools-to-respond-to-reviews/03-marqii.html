3. Marqii

Marqii is a restaurant operations platform that manages menus, listings, social posts and reviews from one dashboard. Its review side gives you AI-suggested replies for feedback coming from booking, delivery and catering sites as well as Google and Yelp, and brands with heavy review volume can pay Marqii's team to write responses for them.

Notable customers: Romulus Restaurant Group (IHOP franchisee), Uccello's Hospitality Group and California Tortilla.

What's new: In July 2026 Marqii launched Review Generation with Thanx, which texts guests after they buy and invites them to review you on Google, Facebook or TripAdvisor. November 2025 brought branded first-party review forms, and social scheduling in May 2025.

What you can do with Marqii:

  • Answer reviews from many platforms. Uber Eats, Grubhub, ezCater and OpenTable feedback sits next to Google, Yelp and Facebook reviews in a single inbox.
  • Start from an AI draft. Suggested replies give your managers a first version to adjust and post.
  • Outsource the replies. A managed response service takes the writing off your team when volume gets too high.
  • Collect reviews you own. First-party forms shared by link, QR code or embed feed into Marqii's review insights.
  • Fix the cause of bad reviews. POS menu sync keeps dishes and prices on Google and Yelp current, which cuts complaints about outdated menus.

Why Marqii stands out: It's built only for restaurants and covers delivery and catering review sites that general tools often skip, and it lists its prices publicly.

Who it's best for: Multi-location restaurant brands and franchisees that want review replies handled in the same tool that keeps their menus and listings accurate.

Pricing: Per location per month on an annual contract, Pro costs $119.99 and includes reviews, Kitchen Sync costs $149.99 with advanced review features, and the menus-only Base plan is $74.99. AI-suggested responses are a $15 add-on and social management $20, and large groups get custom pricing.
