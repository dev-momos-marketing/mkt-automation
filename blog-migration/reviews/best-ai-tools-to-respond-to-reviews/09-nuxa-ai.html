9. Nuxa.ai

Nuxa.ai now sends visitors to Semperi, the AI service its company, Nuxa, Inc., sells to independent restaurants and other local businesses. Semperi drafts a reply in your voice to every new Google review, and it also builds your website, writes social posts, takes orders and bookings, and sends a weekly report.

Semperi is made by the team behind Fleksa, and the company says 4,200+ restaurants run on the platform behind it. Its approach to review replies is cautious: nothing goes live until you approve it, and a low rating reaches you first with a calm draft.

Key features

  • Drafts for every Google review: Semperi checks your Google Business Profile daily and writes a reply in your voice to each new review.
  • Low ratings first: A two-star review comes to you with a reply that offers to make things right, and it waits for your yes.
  • Tone you can adjust: Ask for "shorter" or "warmer" in chat, and Semperi keeps that preference for later replies.
  • Weekly report: Orders, sales, reviews, posts and bookings are summed up once a week with one suggested next step.
  • Marketing around the reviews: Your website, Instagram and Facebook posts, email and texts, and delivery app orders through KitchenHub run in the same service.

Pros

  • Owner stays in control: Every reply needs your approval, which suits owners who worry about AI posting the wrong thing.
  • Published prices: Plans are listed per location, and the first 3 months cost $1 a month.
  • More than replies: Review handling sits with your website, posts and orders, so a small team manages a single service.

Cons

  • Google only: Semperi covers reviews on your Google Business Profile; Yelp, Tripadvisor and delivery app reviews aren't included.
  • No hands-off mode: Replies post only after you tap Send, so a busy week can leave reviews waiting.
  • Plan contents unclear: The pricing page doesn't say which plan includes review replies.
  • New name, short track record: The move from Nuxa to Semperi came without named customers or dated product news.

Price and plans

Semperi starts with a free scan, then costs $1 a month for the first 3 months on any plan. After that, prices are per location, per month.

  • Site: $99 per location, per month.
  • Starter: $299 per location, per month.
  • Growth: $499 per location, per month.
