9. Nuxa.ai

Nuxa.ai sells an AI operating system for restaurants made up of several named agents, each covering one area such as reviews, visibility, social, phone calls or sales. Grace is the agent that handles your Google reviews.

Notable customers: Nuxa doesn't name any restaurants using it, and the dashboard on its homepage is labeled as sample data.

What's new: There's no dated product news from Nuxa over the last couple of years.

What you can do with Nuxa.ai:

  • Let Grace answer reviews. She writes Google replies in your restaurant's voice and can post positive ones on her own.
  • Approve the tough ones. Complaints and low ratings come to you as drafts, so an owner decides how to respond.
  • Turn reviews into ops notes. Recurring themes from guest feedback surface as issues your kitchen or floor team can fix.
  • Check your local standing. Scout audits listings and visibility against nearby competitors, AI search included.
  • Watch sales shifts. Dash reads POS data from Toast, Square, Clover or Fleksa and flags changes in revenue and item mix.

Why Nuxa.ai stands out: Review replies are one agent among several, so feedback from guests connects to your listings, social posts and sales data in the same system.

Who it's best for: Independent restaurants and small groups that want reviews handled as part of a wider AI setup for marketing and operations.

Pricing: Nuxa doesn't publish a price for the full platform, so you'll need to contact the company. Its AI phone answering product has Pro plans from $49 a month.
