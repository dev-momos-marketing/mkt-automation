5. Praising.ai

Praising.ai is an inexpensive review management tool for local businesses, with a version of its site aimed at restaurants. It covers three jobs: asking guests for reviews, drafting replies with AI and tracking how your ratings move over time.

Notable customers: Praising.ai doesn't list any customers by name on its site.

What's new: Its changelog shows recent additions such as text requests, reminders, kiosk mode and an API, though none of these entries carry dates.

What you can do with Praising.ai:

  • Ask after every meal. Email and text requests go out after a visit, and guests who haven't clicked get an automatic reminder.
  • Put links where diners look. Review links and QR codes fit receipts, table tents and posters, and kiosk mode turns a counter tablet into a review station.
  • Approve AI drafts. The AI writes a reply for each review, and nothing goes live on the site until you sign off on it.
  • Keep every site together. Google, Yelp, Facebook and Tripadvisor reviews, plus a few other sources, show up in a shared view that also handles multiple locations.
  • Watch the trend lines. Charts track review volume, average rating and how many reviews you've answered, and a Visibility section shows your Maps and AI ranking.

Why Praising.ai stands out: It bundles review requests, reply drafts and rating tracking at a low per-location price, which keeps the cost manageable for an independent spot testing AI replies for the first time.

Who it's best for: Independent restaurants and small local businesses that want simple review collection with AI drafts. Its pages say it doesn't connect directly to OpenTable, Resy, Toast or Square.

Pricing: Core is $19 per location per month, Growth $29 and Pro $49. There's a free trial and no annual contract.
