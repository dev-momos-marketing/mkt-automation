5. Praising.ai

Praising.ai is a low-cost review tool for local businesses, with a version of its site aimed at restaurants. It emails guests a review request after a visit, gathers your Google and Trustpilot reviews in one inbox, and has an AI agent draft a reply to each new one for you to approve.

For an independent restaurant that still answers Google reviews by hand, its free plan is an inexpensive way to try AI replies.

Key features

  • Drafts for every new review: The AI agent writes a reply to each new Google and Trustpilot review around the clock, and nothing is posted until you approve it.
  • A tone you set once: Choose a warm, professional or apologetic voice, give 1-3 star reviews their own instructions, and get drafts in the reviewer's language.
  • Post-visit requests: Request emails go out automatically with reminders for guests who haven't clicked, and paid plans add texts you send by hand.
  • A review page with a private option: Guests arrive by link, QR code, NFC tag or a counter kiosk, then post publicly or message the owner.
  • Visibility checks: Maps Ranking shows where you appear around your address, and AI Ranking counts how often an AI assistant names you.

Pros

  • Low cost: There's a Forever Free plan, and paid plans run from $19 to $49 a month with no contract.
  • Several locations on one plan: Growth covers up to three locations and Pro up to ten.
  • You keep control: Every draft waits for your OK before it goes public.
  • Fast-moving product: The changelog has added features nearly every month in 2026.

Cons

  • Two review sources imported: Only Google and Trustpilot reviews reach the inbox; Yelp, Tripadvisor, OpenTable and Facebook are linked and aren't monitored.
  • No delivery apps: DoorDash, Uber Eats and Grubhub reviews aren't connected.
  • Manual texts and integrations: Texts go out by hand, and a POS or reservation system connects only through the API and webhooks.
  • Short track record: AI reply drafts, text requests and the API all launched in July 2026.

Price and plans

Praising.ai has a free plan, and each paid plan starts with a 14-day free trial that needs a card. Paying yearly saves 17%.

  • Forever Free: $0 for one location, with manual replies.
  • Core: $19 per month for one location.
  • Growth: $29 per month for up to 3 locations.
  • Pro: $49 per month for up to 10 locations.
