11. OvationUp

OvationUp, marketed as Ovation, is an AI-first guest experience platform for multi-unit restaurants. It starts with two-question text surveys after an order, then lets you recover unhappy guests and answer public reviews from the same dashboard.

Notable customers: Bareburger, Taziki's Mediterranean Cafe, Curry Up Now and Dave's Hot Chicken.

What's new: Ovation's spring release in May 2026 introduced Brand Voice, so the tone you set carries through review replies, automated responses and winback texts, plus a Menu Item Performance Report. In February 2026, nearly a dozen restaurant clients invested $3 million in the company.

What you can do with OvationUp:

  • Survey guests by text. Your POS or ordering system triggers a two-question SMS once the check closes, and table QR codes catch dine-in guests too.
  • Recover guests fast. Real-time alerts on negative feedback let a manager reach out while the visit is still fresh.
  • Reply with AI drafts. Suggested responses to feedback and public reviews follow your Brand Voice settings, and you can edit them before sending.
  • Filter your review inbox. Sort Google, Yelp and Facebook reviews, among others, by platform, star rating or store to find what needs a reply first.
  • Benchmark nearby rivals. With Competitive Pulse, every store is measured against as many as five local rivals, while AI tags comments by themes like speed and cleanliness.

Why OvationUp stands out: Private text feedback arrives minutes after the meal, which gives your team a chance to fix a bad experience before the guest writes about it online.

Who it's best for: Multi-unit and enterprise restaurant brands that want guest recovery and review replies in one restaurant-specific platform.

Pricing: Ovation doesn't publish prices. Quotes depend on your physical locations and ghost kitchens, with volume discounts.
