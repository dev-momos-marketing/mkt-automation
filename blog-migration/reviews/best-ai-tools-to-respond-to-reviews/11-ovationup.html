11. OvationUp

OvationUp, marketed as Ovation, is a guest experience platform for multi-unit restaurant brands. It began with text surveys and guest recovery, and since October 2025 it also answers public reviews: AI suggests a reply to each review or survey comment, and a person edits and sends it.

For review replies, the advantage is context. The team answering a one-star review on Google works in the same platform that collects private survey answers and sends recovery messages. Bareburger and Taziki's Mediterranean Cafe are among its customers.

Key features

  • AI-suggested replies: Drafts for public reviews and private feedback appear ready to edit, and teams that prefer fixed wording can use templates.
  • Brand Voice: Since May 2026, one set of tone instructions applies to review replies, automated responses and winback texts.
  • Filtered review inbox: Sort Google, Yelp and Facebook reviews, among others, by platform, star rating or store to see what needs an answer first.
  • Recovery alerts: A negative survey answer notifies the manager in real time, while the visit is still fresh.
  • Two-question SMS surveys: Your POS or ordering system triggers the text after an order, and table QR codes cover dine-in guests.

Pros

  • A person approves every reply: Drafts are suggestions, so someone on your team stays responsible for what's posted under your name.
  • Same tone everywhere: Brand Voice keeps public replies and private texts consistent across locations.
  • Order context: Integrations with over 50 POS, online ordering and loyalty systems tie feedback to real orders.
  • Restaurant focus: Comment themes such as speed, cleanliness and food quality match how restaurants run.

Cons

  • No public price list: Every account gets a custom quote, so budgeting starts with a sales conversation.
  • Recent review module: Public review replies arrived in the fall 2025 release, later than in tools built around reviews from the start.
  • Restaurant-only design: Groups that also run other kinds of businesses may need a separate tool for those.

Price and plans

Ovation doesn't publish prices; quotes depend on your physical locations and ghost kitchens, with discounts for volume.
