1. Momos

Momos answers restaurant reviews with an AI agent called Alfie, built for brands that run many stores. Alfie writes and posts a reply for each location in your house style, and it can follow up with a recovery offer when the reviewer had a poor visit.

Replies are one part of a wider guest experience platform, and the Momos team trains Alfie on your brand voice, menu and FAQs before launch. Lee's Famous Recipe Chicken, with 130 locations, raised its review response rate from 77.8% to 96.5% after moving to Momos in early 2025.

Key features

  • Fast answers: Alfie responds to each review quickly, so a regular gets thanked and an upset guest hears back while the visit is still fresh.
  • Knows the guest: Visit history, spend and loyalty data help Alfie recognize high-value guests and shape what it says to them.
  • Your brand rules: AI brand guidelines, added in 2025, keep every response within the tone, wording and escalation rules you set.
  • Make it right in the thread: A reply to a disappointed reviewer can include points, a coupon or an offer, sent through your loyalty program.
  • Human handoff: When a review needs a manager, Alfie escalates it with the history and a suggested next step already prepared.
  • Localized replies: Built-in translation answers a visitor who reviewed you in another language.

Pros

  • Publishes on its own: After you approve its answers at launch, Alfie posts replies itself, which keeps every store's response rate up.
  • Reaches past reviews: The same agent handles social DMs, surveys, delivery apps, website chat and in-app support.
  • Ongoing tuning: An onboarding specialist sets Alfie up, and customer success keeps refining its output afterward.
  • Proven at volume: Papa Murphy's has 97.7% of its review replies automated across more than 1,000 stores.

Cons

  • No list prices: You book a demo to get a quote, with no self-serve trial.
  • Sized for chains: A one-location restaurant or a business outside hospitality may find it more than it needs.
  • A week to launch: Training and sign-off with the Momos team come first, so automatic replies don't start on day one.

Price and plans

Momos doesn't publish prices; you get a custom quote after a demo.
