1. Momos

Momos answers guest reviews with AI as part of a wider guest experience platform, and it's built for restaurant chains with many stores. Every location's reviews, messages and survey answers feed one workspace, and an AI agent writes and posts the replies for each store.

Notable customers: Caribou Coffee and Condado Tacos, plus Papa Murphy's and Potbelly.

What's new: New in 2025 were integrations with Paytronix, Square and Otter, along with AI copilots, summaries of each ticket, brand guidelines the AI follows and reports designed for phones. That same year Potbelly brought all 472 of its stores onto Momos, with reviews, cases and messages side by side.

Replies to reviews are written by Alfie, the Experience Agent at the core of Momos.

Key features:

  • Answers within minutes. Alfie responds to each review fast, so a happy regular feels thanked and an upset guest hears back before the frustration sets in.
  • Knows who's writing. Each reply draws on that guest's visit history, preferences and past issues, and loyalty and POS data help Alfie spot your high-value guests.
  • Sounds like your brand. Training on your house style keeps each reply warm and recognizably yours, whether it comes from the flagship or a new suburban store.
  • Makes it right on the spot. A reply to a disappointed reviewer can include loyalty points, a coupon or an offer, sent through your loyalty program without leaving the thread.
  • Hands off with context. When a situation needs a person, Alfie escalates to your team, and the Unified Inbox routes every case to the right store with AI triage.
  • Speaks the reviewer's language. When a visitor reviews you in another language, built-in translation produces a localized reply for that location.

Alfie covers Google reviews and other review sites, social DMs, app store feedback, surveys, delivery apps, website chat and in-app support. Across more than 1,000 Papa Murphy's stores, 97.7% of review replies run automatically, and recovery offers have won back over $2M in revenue.
