3. Podium

Podium started out as SMS software for local businesses and today combines SMS, review requests and text-to-pay with an AI Employee for lead follow-up. Its newest launches are vertical AI operating systems for car dealers, home services and medspas, and restaurants sit outside the industries it targets.

Notable customers: Jackson Hewitt and Mattress Firm, along with Mountaineer Heating & Cooling and Ashley Furniture HomeStore. None of its published stories are restaurants.

What's new: Jerry 2.0, an AI employee you can customize and which runs on OpenAI models, arrived in December 2025 for auto, HVAC and medspa shops. January 2026 brought an operating system for aesthetic practices, and by October 2026 Podium reported more than $150 million in annual recurring revenue from AI agents.

What you can do with Podium:

  • Request reviews by text. A guest gets an SMS with a link that opens your Google page or another review site, which takes a few taps on a phone.
  • Handle messages in one inbox. Texts, webchat, Google messages and social DMs land in a single shared view your team can work through.
  • Let AI answer after hours. The AI Employee replies by text, phone, email and social, and a voice agent picks up calls.
  • Take payment in a thread. Text-to-pay links let you collect money inside the same conversation, handy for catering deposits.
  • Compare locations. Reporting covers reviews and conversations store by store, and bulk texts help you bring lapsed customers back.

Why Podium stands out: Everything runs through texting, so a manager on the floor can ask for reviews and answer guests from a phone without logging into a desktop dashboard.

Who it's best for: Local businesses that sell mostly over calls and texts. Restaurant teams can use the review and messaging tools, though recent launches target other industries.

Pricing: Podium no longer lists prices. Sales quotes its Core, Pro and Signature plans, and the AI Employee costs extra on top of any plan.
