12. Qualtrics

Qualtrics sells an enterprise experience management suite for researching customers, staff and brand perception. Replying to reviews is one task inside a much bigger feedback program that also runs surveys, location dashboards and AI agents.

On the reply side, its online reputation management module suggests answers written in your brand's voice, and staff post them from the same dashboards that hold survey results. That setup suits a chain whose insights team wants every public reply connected to the rest of its guest data.

Key features

  • Brand-voice drafts: Suggested responses follow the tone you set, so a location manager starts from a draft instead of an empty text box.
  • Replies inside dashboards: Reviews connected through Google Places or Facebook can be answered without leaving Qualtrics.
  • Reviews from several sites: Google, Yelp, Facebook and TripAdvisor reviews are collected in one place for reading and reporting.
  • Routing for negative reviews: Critical feedback goes to the team that can fix the issue behind it.
  • Sentiment on every review: Text analytics tags each review with themes and sentiment, which helps you decide which ones need the most careful answer.

Pros

  • Replies linked to research: Because reviews sit beside surveys, analysts can see which complaints come up in both places.
  • Consistent tone across locations: Brand-voice suggestions help dozens of managers sound like the same company.
  • Recovery beyond the reply: Experience Agents can start a real-time service recovery when a low score comes in.
  • G2 rating: Reviewers give Qualtrics Customer Experience 4.3/5 on G2 (751 reviews).

Cons

  • Limited reply sources: Answering from dashboards works for Google Places and Facebook connections, and only for users granted read and reply access to the project.
  • Heavy for reply-only needs: If AI review responses are all you want, a full research suite priced by interactions is a large purchase for one task.
  • No self-serve start: The pricing page shows no prices, no free plan and no trial.

Price and plans

Qualtrics doesn't publish prices. Its CX plans, including one for location-based industries such as restaurants, are priced by interactions, and you get a quote after a demo.
