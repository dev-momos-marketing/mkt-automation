12. Qualtrics

Qualtrics is an enterprise experience management platform covering customer, employee and brand research. Review replies are one piece of a much larger feedback program, which includes an online reputation module, location dashboards and AI Experience Agents.

Notable customers: KFC and Shake Shack.

What's new: Paying customers now use its AI Experience Agents, Qualtrics told attendees at X4 in March 2026, where it also previewed service recovery agents that work in real time. Two months later, in May 2026, the $6.75 billion Press Ganey Forsta acquisition closed; that business centers on healthcare.

What you can do with Qualtrics:

  • Collect feedback everywhere. After a purchase, guests can get surveys through email, text, your website or app, a QR code or chat.
  • Pull reviews into the program. The reputation module imports what diners post on Google and comparable review sites, so analysts can weigh it against private survey answers.
  • Give each role its view. Location dashboards show restaurant managers, market managers and executives the numbers relevant to their job.
  • Group comments by theme. Text and sentiment analytics sort open-ended comments and reviews, and predictive models flag churn risk.
  • Act through agents. Experience Agents can start a service recovery in real time and connect to Genesys, NICE or Salesforce.

Why Qualtrics stands out: It gives a large brand's insights team survey, review and operational data in one research-grade platform, with analytics that go well past replying to reviews.

Who it's best for: Restaurant and hospitality chains big enough to employ their own research or guest experience staff.

Pricing: Qualtrics doesn't list prices for its CX suite. Sales prepares a quote for each account.
