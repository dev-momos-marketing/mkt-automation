1. Momos

Category: Customer satisfaction and operations analytics

Restaurant chains with many locations use Momos as their guest experience management platform, and its analytics begin with what guests tell you. Reviews, survey answers, support chats and messages from every store land in one place, so you can measure guest feedback by location and see which issues keep costing you repeat visits.

Notable customers: Papa Murphy's, Potbelly, Condado Tacos and Caribou Coffee.

What's new: Momos spent 2025 adding to its reporting and AI: mobile-first reports, staff copilots, ticket summaries written by AI and brand guidelines the AI follows, plus connections to Square, Otter and Paytronix. That year Potbelly signed on too, and its 472 locations now share a single screen for reviews, guest messages and open cases.

Key features:

  • Every feedback source in one dataset. The Unified Inbox combines Google reviews, App Store comments, social DMs and POS data, so your satisfaction numbers cover every channel instead of a single review site.
  • Themes behind the ratings. Sentiment analysis and AI triage group comments by topic, which shows whether slow service, cold food or a rude interaction is driving a drop at a given store.
  • Survey scores you can trend. Post-visit surveys let you follow measures such as Net Promoter Score over time and compare locations on the same scale.
  • Problems routed as they appear. Smart routing sends each negative review or complaint to the right team, and Alfie, the Experience Agent, replies within minutes in your brand voice.
  • Recovery you can count. Alfie can send a guest loyalty points, a coupon or an offer after a bad visit, and you can then track how much of that revenue returns.

The numbers from Papa Murphy's show the scale: with Momos at 1,000+ stores, the brand automates 97.7% of review replies, and its recovery offers have won back $2M+ in sales.

Pricing: Custom pricing. You book a demo and Momos quotes based on your brand.
