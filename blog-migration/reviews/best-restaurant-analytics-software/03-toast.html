3. Toast

Toast is a point-of-sale system for restaurants, traded on the NYSE, whose platform also takes care of online ordering, kitchen display, payroll, marketing and loyalty. Because every order passes through the same system, its reporting gives you sales analytics without exporting data to a separate tool. It works for single restaurants as well as multi-unit and franchise groups.

Notable customers: Captain Parker's Pub, Kaldi's Coffee and Craft Lounge.

What's new: The spring 2026 update, out in May, added Toast IQ Grow, a paid tier whose centerpiece is an AI Marketing Agent; the Toast Local app for guests came back with loyalty inside it. Results published in August 2026 put Toast at about 180,000 live locations and $2.4B ARR at the end of June.

Key features:

  • Sales data from every touchpoint. Orders from counter terminals, kiosks, Toast Go handhelds and online ordering feed the same reports, so channel comparisons need no manual merging.
  • Reporting across locations. Multi-location reports let a group compare stores side by side and check how a menu or price change performed everywhere at once.
  • Central menu and pricing control. Updating menus and prices from one place keeps item names consistent, which keeps item-level sales figures clean across sites.
  • Labor next to sales. Because scheduling (through Sling by Toast), staff management and payroll run on the same platform, you can set the wage bill for a shift against what it sold.
  • Guest and marketing data. Gift card sales, loyalty activity and text or email marketing all happen inside Toast, and Toast IQ adds AI features, including the marketing agent in Toast IQ Grow.

Pricing: Plans are quoted by sales. Toasttab.com has promoted both a free Starter Kit and paid tiers over time; confirm today's figures there before you budget.
