8. TouchBistro

Category: Sales and performance reporting inside the POS

With TouchBistro, the analytics come bundled with an iPad point of sale, and extra modules cover payments, reservations, online ordering, stock and staff. Owners of independent full-service restaurants, or a handful of sites, tend to like that the reports sit in the till they already use. Since July 2026 the business has been owned by Harris, a Constellation Software operating group, and the brand name hasn't changed.

Notable customers: Café Crêpe, Uncle Tetsu, Koyo and Masseria.

What's new: Harris completed its purchase in July 2026 and committed to further product investment. Neither side shared the price; The Globe and Mail put it near $100 million.

Key features:

  • Sales you can check from anywhere. Cloud reports break takings down by hour, dish and employee while service is still running.
  • Which dishes earn their spot. Menu mix reporting ranks items by how well they sell, useful evidence when you rewrite the menu.
  • Staff and shift comparisons. Reporting by server and shift highlights strong upsellers and the services that lag behind.
  • Exceptions in their own report. Voids, refunds and discounts are listed separately, so an odd spike stands out for follow-up.
  • More data as you add modules. Tableside ordering, built-in payments, loyalty, reservations, inventory and labor tools all write into the same system.

Pricing: The POS software is $69/month for each location, and hardware is extra. On the pricing page, an Essentials package that adds payments and hardware begins at $119/month, while the homepage has advertised it at $99/month. Online ordering and other extras are charged on top, and enterprise deals are quoted.
