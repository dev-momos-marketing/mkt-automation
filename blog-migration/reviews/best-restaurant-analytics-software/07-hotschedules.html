7. HotSchedules

Category: Enterprise labor forecasting and workforce analytics

Since a 2019 merger, HotSchedules has belonged to Fourth, and the name now refers to the scheduling product inside Fourth's wider workforce and inventory suite. What it brings to an analytics stack is labor math: predicting how busy each store will be, then checking hours and wage cost against that prediction. Most users are multi-unit and enterprise restaurant groups.

Notable customers: Noodles & Company, Texas Corral, FreeRange Concepts and Snooze, an A.M. Eatery.

What's new: Starting July 2025, the AI Forecast automatically weighs ticketed happenings in each store's neighborhood, such as a concert or a play. Fourth followed in September with version 2.0 of Fourth iQ, which introduced a manager app, while release 2.2, out that November, flags likely stock shortfalls to managers ahead of time.

Key features:

  • Demand predicted per store. The labor forecast blends past sales, the weather outlook and what's on nearby, so a rainy game night gets staffed differently from a quiet Tuesday.
  • Schedules graded before publishing. Each draft rota gets a schedule score for coverage and cost, which tells a manager whether it's lean enough or short-handed.
  • Planned against worked. Budget tracking lines up scheduled hours and dollars with what staff actually clocked, and the gap shows which stores drift.
  • Compliance in the time clock. Attendance rules reflect labor law, which cuts the odds of missed breaks or overtime turning into penalties.
  • Recommendations on a phone. Through the iQ App, managers get prompts covering staffing, HR and stock, with links into Fourth's payroll modules and your POS.

Pricing: Custom pricing. Fourth keeps its rates private and quotes each group.
