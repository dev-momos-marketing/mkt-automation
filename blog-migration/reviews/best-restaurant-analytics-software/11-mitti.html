11. Mitti

Category: Operational and safety analytics

Mitti was called SafetyCulture until its rebrand in August 2026. Its app handles inspections, checklists, fixes, staff training, sensors and equipment records for teams on the floor. For a restaurant group, the analytics value lies in turning hygiene, food-safety and brand audits into comparable scores for every site.

Notable customers: Snooze, an A.M. Eatery, Fishbowl, Trippas White Group and Sodexo.

What's new: Alongside the August 2026 rename, Mitti introduced its AI Assistant, issues raised by AI, agents that watch routine checks, and 5 Star Benchmarking, one rating per location for multi-site brands. According to a pricing tracker, every plan started including a monthly allowance of AI credits in July 2026.

Key features:

  • Checks that become data. Each digital inspection records scores, photos and comments, and you can start from a big library of shared templates.
  • Location rankings. Multi-site analytics plus 5 Star Benchmarking show which kitchens hold the standard and which repeatedly slip.
  • Time to fix, measured. Corrective actions carry an owner and a deadline, so you can track how long problems stay open.
  • Early warnings from sensors. Temperature and environment monitors send alerts, catching a walk-in that's warming up before stock is lost.
  • Training and equipment in one app. Mobile lessons, maintenance logs and asset records live together, and Mitti Supply can reorder stock that an inspection flags.

Pricing: There's a free tier for up to 10 team members. Premium is roughly $24 per user per month with yearly billing and $29 with monthly billing, and staff who only complete checklists and lessons can sit on a cheaper Lite seat. Enterprise quotes are based on sites. These numbers come from outside listings that Mitti hasn't verified since the name change.
