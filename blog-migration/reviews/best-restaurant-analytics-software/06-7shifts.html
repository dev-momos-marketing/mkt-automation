6. 7Shifts

Category: Labor and workforce performance analytics

7shifts makes workforce software for restaurants and nothing else: schedules, clock-ins, tips and payroll, with a training module added in 2026. Its analytics focus on labor: how many hours you scheduled, what they cost and how that compares with the sales they served.

Notable customers: HCI Hospitality (a Freddy's Frozen Custard & Steakburgers franchisee), Boston Pizza (Regina), Conshy Girls Restaurant Group and Pitango Gelato.

What's new: Its plans were reorganized in July 2025 into four tiers, from the free Comp level up through Essentials and Pro to Premium. Employee Training followed in September 2026: managers can write courses and quizzes, hand them out by role, department or site, and see who has finished them in the app staff already open for shifts.

Key features:

  • Schedules built from forecasts. Managers drag shifts into place on top of projected sales and labor needs, so the rota matches the covers you expect.
  • Labor cost against sales. Labor budgets and cost reports measure spend against revenue, which shows which shifts ran heavy before the pay period closes.
  • Clock-ins that match the schedule. Linked to your POS and payroll, the clock holds staff to their scheduled times, so early punches and surprise overtime appear in the data.
  • Tips handled in one place. Tip pooling, tip management and payouts sit alongside hours, giving you a fuller picture of what each shift costs.
  • Staff tools on mobile. The mobile app is where crew members pick up open shifts, mark their availability and message one another; managers hand out tasks and checklists there too.

Pricing: Comp is free for a single site with 15 or fewer staff. Billed yearly, the monthly rate per site is $39.99 for Essentials (capped at 30 staff), $79.99 on Pro and $134.99 on Premium; on monthly billing, Essentials is $44.99, Pro $89.99 and Premium $149.99. Payroll costs extra, larger groups get an Enterprise quote, and a 14-day Pro trial costs nothing.
