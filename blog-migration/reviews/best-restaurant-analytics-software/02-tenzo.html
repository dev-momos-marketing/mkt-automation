2. Tenzo

Category: POS sales, labor, inventory and reservation analytics

Tenzo is a reporting and forecasting tool from London that connects to the software your restaurants use today. Your POS, labor, inventory, reservation and review data end up on a single dashboard, so a multi-site group in Britain or America works from one shared set of numbers.

Notable customers: Lettuce Entertain You, PrimoHoagies, Parker Restaurant Group and Pizza Pilgrims.

What's new: Edge VC led a Series A extension whose first close brought Tenzo $5M in April 2026. Tenzo says the funds will pay for twice as many integrations within 18 months and a bigger push into the US market, and it appointed Jane O'Riordan to chair its board.

Key features:

  • Everything on one screen. Reservations, reviews, stock, labor hours and takings sit next to each other, so you can see how a busy Friday affected labor cost and guest ratings together.
  • Forecasts that account for context. Machine learning models adjust expected sales for weather, holidays and local events, which helps you plan prep and staffing more accurately than last week's totals.
  • Forecasts sent to your rota. Predicted sales can be pushed into Deputy, Planday and similar scheduling apps, which means managers build shifts from the same numbers head office sees.
  • Site-by-site benchmarking. Location comparisons show which restaurants are pulling ahead and which are slipping, giving area managers a clear place to start.
  • Reports that arrive on their own. Daily and weekly summaries go out automatically, and an MCP connector lets your team ask AI assistants questions about Tenzo data.

Pricing: Custom pricing. There's no public price list; Tenzo's sales team prepares a quote.
