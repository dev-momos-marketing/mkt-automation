4. Apicbase

Category: Inventory and food cost analytics

Apicbase runs the kitchen side of restaurant groups, hotel F&B teams and central kitchens with several sites: recipes, menu costing, stock, ordering and production. Its analytics answer cost questions: what each dish really costs, where stock is going and how food cost compares between sites. The company now describes the platform as AI-powered.

Notable customers: Poule & Poulette, Pentahotels and The Avocado Show.

What's new: Apicbase hasn't announced dated product releases recently. The most recent dated item is an event, APX·25, which brought hospitality, foodservice and tech leaders together for the first time in November 2025.

Key features:

  • Costing from a shared recipe library. Every site works from the same ingredient and recipe library, so when a supplier raises a price, the cost of each dish and menu updates by itself.
  • Menu engineering from real recipe data. Because margins come from actual recipes, you can tell which plates are carrying your margin and which are dragging it down, with allergen details drawn from the same source.
  • Stock visibility across sites. Inventory counts and stock levels for each location help you spot variance between what was used and what should have been.
  • Purchasing tied to suppliers. Because vendors and POs are managed where recipes are costed, the prices you pay feed straight into food cost reports.
  • Forecasts and cost reports. Sales-based forecasting and multi-site food cost reporting pull in sales through connections to ordering and POS systems such as Deliverect and Square.

Pricing: Custom pricing. Apicbase keeps prices private and builds each quote around your site count and the modules you pick.
