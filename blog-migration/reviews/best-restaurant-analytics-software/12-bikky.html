12. Bikky

Category: Guest behavior analytics

Bikky builds a single guest record for multi-unit restaurant chains by combining what your POS, ordering, payment and loyalty systems know about each person. On top of those profiles it measures visit frequency, retention, order habits and campaign results. An AI analyst called Data Assistant lets you ask about the data in everyday language.

Notable customers: Mellow Mushroom, Bubbakoo's Burritos, Condado Tacos and Tiki Taco.

What's new: Bubbakoo's Burritos signed with Bikky in April 2026 to drive its personalization and data plans. Mellow Mushroom went public with its use of Bikky in September 2025, calling Data Assistant a new AI analyst. Earlier, in March 2025, Condado Tacos built a limited-time burrito around Bikky's guest findings.

Key features:

  • One record per guest. Duplicate entries from different stores and channels get merged and cleaned, so your guest counts reflect real people.
  • Loyalty over time. Frequency, retention and lifetime value reports reveal whether regulars are visiting more or drifting away.
  • LTO and channel testing. Menu and channel results are tied to who ordered, which shows whether a new item attracted fresh guests or simply moved existing orders.
  • Proof for marketing spend. Campaign and loyalty ROI reports link promotions to actual visits, and guest segments shape the next send.
  • Ask or export. Data Assistant answers plain-language questions, and you can send the data to Snowflake, an SFTP server or CSV files.

Pricing: Custom pricing. Bikky doesn't publish its rates.
