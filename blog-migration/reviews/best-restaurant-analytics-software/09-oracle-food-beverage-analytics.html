9. Oracle Food & Beverage Analytics

Category: Enterprise BI and revenue analytics for restaurants

Oracle calls its product Simphony Reporting and Analytics; it belongs to the Simphony point-of-sale family that used to carry the MICROS name. Global hospitality and restaurant companies with many brands are the typical buyers, and it makes most sense if your stores already ring sales on Simphony. Several modules, Forecasting and Budget among them, have an announced end of life, so ask Oracle what will still be supported.

Notable customers: Starbucks, Pret A Manger, Sonic and Sercotel.

What's new: On the last day of March 2026, Oracle and NetSuite introduced an AI-driven operations product for restaurants. It feeds sales from Simphony Cloud and other POS systems into one back office that handles purchasing, stock, staff scheduling, production and cash.

Key features:

  • Roll-ups by brand and region. Revenue reporting can be cut by store, concept or territory, giving leadership a like-for-like comparison of markets.
  • Push reports and alerts. Reports go out on a schedule, and urgent events trigger mobile notifications, so nobody has to log in to notice them.
  • From totals down to one check. Search finds a guest check and opens its image, handy when a single transaction needs explaining.
  • Waste logged during service. POS reason codes capture waste as it happens, giving inventory reports a cause for each loss.
  • An API for outside BI. Data can flow to Oracle Analytics Cloud or a third-party BI tool, while native links tie Simphony to Oracle's finance software.

Pricing: Custom enterprise pricing. Oracle doesn't list Simphony prices publicly.
