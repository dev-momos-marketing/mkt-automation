10. Avero

Restaurant groups use Avero to see who sold what: the software converts POS and operating data into reports on sales, labor and individual servers. Fine-dining and full-service operators with several venues are its usual fit, especially when they want managers and servers held to clear numbers each shift.

Notable customers: Wolfgang Puck Worldwide, Patina Restaurant Group, Crafted Hospitality and Four Seasons, all named on older Avero pages.

What's new: There's no dated Avero product announcement from 2025 or 2026; the newest public updates go back several years, so ask about the roadmap during a demo.

Key features:

  • Venues in a common format. Data from each restaurant's POS and operations lands in one reporting layer, so groups compare sites on equal terms.
  • Revenue traced to people. Splitting sales by shift, manager and server shows exactly who carried a busy night.
  • Coaching scorecards. Benchmarks for each server give floor managers specific figures to discuss in one-on-ones.
  • Offers measured against traffic. Guest count and revenue trends appear beside comps, discounts and promotions, so you can tell whether a deal paid for itself.
  • Numbers away from the office. Managers can check results in a mobile app, and Toast data has been available since Avero joined Toast's partner program in 2018.

Pricing: Custom pricing. Avero doesn't share a price list.
