8. Pluspoint

Headquartered in London, Pluspoint gives multi-site businesses one account for collecting and answering reviews, keeping directory profiles correct, surveying guests, sending campaigns and posting to social. Unlike most tools on this list, it shows a per-location price on its website.

Notable customers: Starfoods Group (Portugal, 60+ restaurants), Paprica Burger and SushiCafé Group.

What's new: Pluspoint opened its MCP connector to all customers around May 2026 (the month is estimated). That means a manager can ask Gemini, Claude or ChatGPT to summarize this week's reviews, spot a wrong opening time in a listing, or schedule a batch of review invitations. Social posting over MCP is announced as coming soon.

What you can do with Pluspoint:

  • Collect reviews on the guest's channel. Requests go out by SMS, email, WhatsApp or a QR code at the table, so you can match how your guests prefer to be contacted.
  • Answer from a central inbox. One queue gathers reviews from Facebook, Google and more, each with an AI-suggested reply, and an alert fires the moment negative feedback appears.
  • Run guest surveys. NPS and customer satisfaction surveys sit alongside public reviews, with per-location sentiment and keyword analysis.
  • Manage listings across directories. Google and Facebook profiles are included, and an add-on syncs details to 200+ directories, among them Apple Maps, Bing, Yelp and TripAdvisor.
  • Post and message locally. AI-generated social posts for Google, Facebook and Instagram, plus messaging campaigns, help each store stay active online.

Why Pluspoint stands out: You can see the cost before talking to sales, and every feature is in the single plan. The MCP connector also suits operators who already do much of their admin inside an AI assistant.

Who it's best for: Restaurant groups and franchisees, with a strong presence in Europe, who would rather budget a fixed monthly amount per store for review handling, directory profiles and local search.

Pricing: One plan at $29 per location monthly, or roughly $24.17 on annual billing, covers every feature for at least three locations and includes 3 users plus 50 AI credits. Each additional user is $19, each further credit $0.49, and syncing to the wider directory network (Listings Sync) adds $39 per location a month. You can test it free for 14 days, and above 30 locations or users the plan becomes custom.
