11. Podium

Podium is an AI-powered communication platform for local businesses, built around texting, reviews, payments and an AI Employee that answers leads and books appointments. Today it's sold mostly as industry-specific "AI operating systems" for automotive, home services and aesthetics, and restaurants aren't one of its focus industries.

Notable customers: Mattress Firm, Jackson Hewitt, Ashley Furniture HomeStore and Mountaineer Heating & Cooling.

What's new: Podium released Jerry 2.0, an AI employee built on OpenAI models, for automotive, HVAC and medspa businesses in December 2025. In January 2026 it launched an AI operating system for aesthetic practices, and in October 2026 it reported passing $150 million in annual recurring revenue from its AI agent business.

What you can do with Podium:

  • Answer inquiries around the clock. The AI Employee responds by text, phone, email and social, answers common questions and books appointments after hours.
  • Pick up the phone with AI. A voice agent handles incoming calls, which can help when staff are busy during a rush.
  • Invite reviews by text. Customers get a link that sends them to Google and other review sites after a purchase.
  • Keep conversations in one inbox. Texts, webchat, Google messages and social messages share a single queue for the team.
  • Take payments in a conversation. Text-to-pay links and bulk text campaigns let you collect money and run promotions from the same thread.

Why Podium stands out: It joins messaging, reviews and payments in one product, so a business can follow a customer from first text to paid invoice to public review.

Who it's best for: Local service businesses that sell through calls and texts, such as auto dealers, home services, medspas and retail. Restaurants can use the review and messaging tools, but Podium's recent launches don't target them, so compare it carefully with hospitality-focused options.

Pricing: Podium doesn't publish prices. Sales quotes its Core, Pro and Signature plans, and the AI Employee is an add-on to any of them.
