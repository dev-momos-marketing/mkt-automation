1. Momos

Category: Guest experience and reputation management software for restaurants

Momos is a guest experience platform for restaurant brands with many locations. Reviews, guest messages, survey answers and delivery app comments from every store land in one Unified Inbox, where AI agents answer guests and turn the feedback into a store-by-store view of what to fix.

Notable customers: Papa Murphy's, Potbelly, Condado Tacos and Caribou Coffee.

What's new: In December 2025, Momos recapped a year of releases: AI copilots, AI ticket summaries, brand guidelines that steer the AI, mobile-first reporting, and integrations with Paytronix, Square and Otter. Potbelly joined in 2025, so its 472 locations now share one view of reviews, support cases and guest messages.

Key features:

  • One queue for every guest signal. The Unified Inbox collects Google reviews, App Store feedback, social DMs and POS data, and AI triage passes each item to the store responsible for it.
  • Answers that know the guest. Alfie, the Experience Agent, replies in minutes in your brand's tone and takes a guest's earlier visits, preferences and past issues into account.
  • Recovery offers inside the reply. If a visit went badly, Alfie can add loyalty points, a coupon or an offer from your loyalty platform so the guest has a reason to come back.
  • Coverage beyond review sites. The agent also covers website chat, in-app help, survey comments and delivery platform feedback, and passes a case to your team with its full history when a person should step in.
  • Multilingual responses. With live translation built in, each restaurant can write back to a visiting guest in their own language, whether the comment came in as a review or a message.

On the analytics side, the Operational Insight Dashboards show where each restaurant is doing well and where guests keep reporting the same problem. Rockie, the intelligence agent, points HQ to the locations that need attention first and suggests a next step for each one.

This dashboard, for example, scores guest satisfaction for each part of the experience at every location in a franchise. Papa Murphy's, with 1,000+ locations, automates 97.7% of its review replies in Momos and credits offers to unhappy guests with $2M+ in recovered revenue.
