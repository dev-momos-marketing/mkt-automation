6. ReviewTrackers

Chains of all kinds, restaurants included, run their online reputation across many locations in ReviewTrackers, which has tools for review monitoring, replies, review requests and competitor tracking. InMoment bought it in 2022, and InMoment itself became part of Press Ganey Forsta in May 2025, though the ReviewTrackers brand and site are still live.

Notable customers: Ted's Montana Grill, PACK & SEND and Chem-Dry.

What's new: Generative AI replies arrived in July 2025, when parent company InMoment released AI Auto Responding, which drafts personal answers to reviews based on prompts your brand stores. It's offered on the Locations Starter and Locations Essential plans, the same tier names ReviewTrackers uses.

What you can do with ReviewTrackers:

  • Track every location's reviews. New reviews from Google, TripAdvisor, Facebook and similar sites land on a single screen, and an alert tells you each time one arrives.
  • Give managers approved replies. Smart Response templates let a general manager answer in a few clicks while staying within brand guidelines.
  • Request more reviews. Automated emails and texts ask recent guests to share their experience.
  • Find recurring themes. Sentiment and topic analytics show which complaints keep coming back, such as slow service at one store.
  • Mirror your franchise structure. Owners, district leaders and HQ each get reports and permissions for their own stores, while competitor tracking sets your ratings against nearby rivals.

Why ReviewTrackers stands out: Store staff get a short, easy routine for answering guests, head office gets reporting that covers every location, and brands already on InMoment can use the review data inside that wider customer experience platform.

Who it's best for: Franchise systems and multi-unit brands that need consistent, approved replies across stores, especially if InMoment or Press Ganey Forsta already runs their customer experience program.

Pricing: Pricing is set per location and isn't listed. You get a figure after a call with the sales team.
