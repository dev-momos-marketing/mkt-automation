12. Marqii

Marqii is a digital operations platform made for restaurants. From one dashboard, it keeps menus, listings, reviews and social posts current, syncs menu changes from your POS, suggests review replies with AI and helps you collect more reviews.

Notable customers: Romulus Restaurant Group (IHOP franchisee), Uccello's Hospitality Group and California Tortilla.

What's new: Marqii added Social Management in May 2025 and First Party Reviews, branded forms you share by link, QR code or website embed, in November 2025. July 2026 brought Review Generation, built with Thanx: once a guest buys, a text message asks for a rating on TripAdvisor, Facebook or Google.

What you can do with Marqii:

  • Change the menu once. When you update an item in the POS, Marqii pushes the change to Yelp, Google and similar sites, so a removed dish or new price shows up everywhere.
  • Keep listing details right. Hours, photos, offers and contact information are managed for every location from the same screen.
  • Handle reviews from restaurant channels. Reviews from delivery and catering apps like Uber Eats, Grubhub and ezCater arrive alongside OpenTable, Google, Yelp and Facebook, each with an AI-suggested reply.
  • Outsource replies if volume is high. Brands with heavy review traffic can use Marqii's managed review response service.
  • Gather more first-party feedback. Branded review forms and post-purchase SMS requests add to the review insights on your multi-location dashboard.

Why Marqii stands out: It's designed around the jobs restaurant marketers repeat every week, such as menu updates and delivery app reviews, and it publishes prices per location.

Who it's best for: Restaurant chains and franchise owners who care most about correct menus and listings online, and who want review replies handled in a tool built for their industry.

Pricing: Plans are billed monthly for each location under a yearly contract. Base, covering menus and location data, is $74.99; Pro adds reviews for $119.99; Kitchen Sync adds advanced review tools for $149.99. For each location, you can add social management for $20 or AI reply suggestions for $15, and big multi-unit groups can ask for custom terms.
