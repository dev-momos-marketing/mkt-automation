4. Eat App Review Management

Eat App is a Dubai-based reservation, table management and guest CRM platform, and its review management is an add-on with AI-powered feedback management features. You buy it as part of Eat App, so it suits restaurants that already run bookings there more than teams looking for a separate review tool.

Notable customers: Emaar Hospitality Group and The Maine Group.

What's new: January 2026 was a busy month: Eat App closed a $10M Series B extension, with PSG Equity investing via its company Zenchef, and teamed up with Swiggy to resell its growth tools in India under the GroMax name. It also disclosed buying ReserveGo, an Indian booking platform (PitchBook dates that deal to June 2025).

What you can do with Eat App:

  • Watch reviews in one dashboard. Google, Bing and Facebook reviews arrive together, so a manager doesn't need to log in to each site.
  • Answer from the same app. Staff reply to reviews inside Eat App, next to the reservations they already handle every shift.
  • Ask guests about their visit. Post-visit feedback requests go out automatically after a booking, and guests who rate the meal highly are pointed toward a public review.
  • Read sentiment by venue. Analytics break down guest mood for each restaurant in your group, so weak spots stand out.
  • Keep feedback next to guest profiles. Because the tool sits on Eat App's guest CRM, you can see a reviewer's booking history, including reservations from partner channels like Tripadvisor and Mozrest.

Why Eat App stands out: Reservations, table management, guest profiles and review replies live in one system, which cuts down on the number of logins a small team has to juggle.

Who it's best for: Restaurants that already use Eat App, or are choosing it, for bookings and guest CRM and want basic review monitoring in the same place. Before you switch on review requests, check that the routing of happy guests fits Google's policies, which discourage asking only satisfied customers for reviews.

Pricing: There's a free plan plus paid tiers, with review management charged on top. Because figures differ from country to country, ask Eat App for the current price where you operate.
