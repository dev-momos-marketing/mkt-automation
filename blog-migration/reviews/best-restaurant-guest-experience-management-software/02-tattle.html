2. Tattle

Tattle is an independent guest feedback and customer experience platform made for restaurants. Guests get a short survey once they've paid, triggered by the POS, online ordering or loyalty app you already run. Public review scores sit beside those answers, and AI picks out the change most likely to lift each store's results.

Notable customers: Hooters, Red Robin, Brinker International (Chili's) and Kung Fu Tea.

What's new: A $5 million funding round closed in August 2025, earmarked for more AI features. Kung Fu Tea announced a partnership that January 2026 to track sentiment across its channels. Tattle also released an iOS and Android app where general managers see live metrics, critical-incident pushes and AI Coach action items.

What you can do with Tattle:

  • Survey guests after every order. Its integrations with Toast, Olo, Square, Punchh, Thanx and Paytronix send the questions without anyone on staff lifting a finger.
  • Link comments to operations. Each response is tagged to an area such as speed, order accuracy, food quality or hospitality, so a store manager can see which shift habit needs work.
  • Follow reviews alongside surveys. Google and Yelp ratings appear in the same place as private feedback, which helps you compare what guests post publicly with what they tell you directly.
  • Get a ranked to-do list per store. AI recommendations put the improvement with the biggest likely impact at the top for each location.
  • Win back unhappy guests. When a survey comes back poor, a recovery workflow prompts someone to follow up, and the dashboards compare stores on metrics like recovery rate.

Why Tattle stands out: Most of its data comes from structured surveys tied to real transactions, so you hear from a wider slice of guests than the few who write public reviews. The operational tags make it easier to turn that feedback into coaching for a specific team.

Who it's best for: Multi-unit restaurant brands and franchisees in quick service, fast casual and casual dining that want feedback organized around what happens in each store.

Pricing: Tattle doesn't publish prices. You request a custom quote from the company.
