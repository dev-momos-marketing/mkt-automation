7. Birdeye

Birdeye is an agentic marketing and reputation platform for multi-location brands. Its review tools handle requests, monitoring, replies and analysis, and they share one system with listings, messaging, social, surveys and AI search visibility. A set of AI agents, now grouped into named "AI Coworkers," does much of the routine work.

Notable customers: The Saxton Group (McAlister's Deli franchisee) and Black Bear Diner.

What's new: Birdeye launched a restaurant edition of its AI marketing platform in January 2025, including Yelp review monitoring through a Yelp partnership. At View 2026 in September 2026, it introduced three AI Coworkers (Jay, Myna and Robin) and later that month added a SkyTab POS integration that pushes menu details to listings.

What you can do with Birdeye:

  • Route bad reviews to the right store. Reviews from Google, Yelp, Facebook and industry sites trigger alerts and tickets that land with the location manager responsible.
  • Build review volume. Requests go to guests by SMS and email after a visit, which helps keep your rating current at every location.
  • Let agents draft or send replies. AI writes review responses in your brand voice, and you decide how much of the reply work agents take over.
  • Keep menus and hours accurate. Its listings tools refresh business details across directories, and restaurants on SkyTab can sync menus straight from the POS.
  • Compare districts and regions. Surveys and Insights AI report sentiment at location, district and region level, with benchmarking against competitors.

Why Birdeye stands out: It covers a very wide set of local marketing jobs in one product, and its agents are designed to carry out tasks such as replying or updating listings, which matters if your marketing team is small relative to your store count.

Who it's best for: Franchise groups and chains, including restaurant brands, looking for one vendor to cover review work, directory listings, guest messaging and social posting. Because the product spans many industries and marketing tasks, check how much of it your guest experience team would actually use.

Pricing: Birdeye doesn't list prices. It quotes after a demo, based on your business.
