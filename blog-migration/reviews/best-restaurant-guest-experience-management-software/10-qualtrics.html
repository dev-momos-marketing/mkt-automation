10. Qualtrics

Qualtrics sells experience management (XM) software that big organizations rely on to study how customers, employees and the market see them. Restaurant brands get dashboards for each location's guest experience, an online reputation management module and AI Experience Agents. The company is privately held, and in May 2026 it completed its acquisition of Press Ganey Forsta, which brought the InMoment business with it.

Notable customers: KFC and Shake Shack.

What's new: At X4 2026 in March 2026, the first under CEO Jason Maynard, Qualtrics said its AI Experience Agents had moved into live use with customers who pay for them, and it previewed recovery agents that respond to guests as issues happen, plus connectors for Genesys, NICE and Salesforce. The $6.75 billion Press Ganey Forsta deal closed in May 2026, adding mostly healthcare data.

What you can do with Qualtrics:

  • Survey guests on any channel. Post-transaction surveys can go out by email, SMS, web, app, QR code or chat, depending on how the guest ordered.
  • Bring public reviews into the same program. The reputation module pulls in reviews from sites such as Google so they can be analyzed with survey results.
  • Give each role its own dashboard. Restaurant managers, market managers and executives each see the location data that matters to their job.
  • Sort comments with AI. Text and sentiment analytics cluster open-ended answers and reviews into themes, and predictive models point to the drivers of satisfaction and churn risk.
  • Act on feedback automatically. Experience Agents can start service recovery in real time, and connectors pass cases to contact-center and CRM tools.

Why Qualtrics stands out: It's built for research depth, and lets one platform also handle brand tracking, statistics and staff engagement studies, which suits brands that run formal CX programs across thousands of stores.

Who it's best for: Large multi-unit restaurant and hospitality brands with a dedicated CX or insights team that wants surveys, reviews and operational feedback in one enterprise program.

Pricing: No CX suite prices are public. You'll get a custom quote from the Qualtrics sales team.
