9. Reputation

Reputation, which used to call itself Reputation.com and still uses that domain, is an enterprise platform for reputation and customer experience at multi-location brands. One system holds its review, survey, listings and social tools, along with newer features for AI search, and the San Ramon company marked its 20th year in March 2026.

Notable customers: Tortilla and Mitchells & Butlers.

What's new: Reputation expanded its customer insights tools in January 2025 and announced a batch of AI products for brand intelligence in September 2025. In May 2026 it introduced Reputation IQ, AI Location Presence Insights and a broader AI Reputation Manager that shows how ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity describe your brand.

What you can do with Reputation:

  • Reply to reviews in one inbox. Reviews from Yelp, Tripadvisor, Google, Facebook and more land in a shared queue, so area managers can see which stores are falling behind on responses.
  • Ask for reviews at the right moment. Review invitations, sent as a text or email or shown as a QR code, can go out when a table pays, an order is delivered or the POS logs a sale.
  • Combine surveys with public feedback. Survey answers sit next to reviews, and text analytics group comments into themes such as service or cleanliness.
  • Query your data in plain English. Reputation IQ answers questions like which locations drew the most cleanliness complaints last month.
  • Track how AI search presents you. AI Reputation Manager and AI Location Presence Insights show what answer engines say about your brand and each location.

Why Reputation stands out: It brings private survey feedback and public reviews into the same analytics, and its AI search tools address a newer question for restaurants: what chatbots tell guests who ask where to eat.

It's best for: Restaurant and hospitality chains, as well as automotive and healthcare groups, whose head office wants to manage reviews, surveys and listings for every location and report on them at enterprise scale.

Pricing: Reputation doesn't publish prices. Its sales team provides a quote.
