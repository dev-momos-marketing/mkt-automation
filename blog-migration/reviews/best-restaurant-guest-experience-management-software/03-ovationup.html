3. OvationUp

Ovation (the company is sometimes called Ovation Up) is a guest experience and reputation management platform designed for multi-unit restaurants. It texts guests a two-question survey after they order, alerts managers when someone had a bad visit, and now handles public reviews and listings from the same dashboard.

Notable customers: Dave's Hot Chicken, Curry Up Now, Bareburger and Taziki's Mediterranean Cafe.

What's new: The fall 2025 release in October brought reviews, listings and social into Ovation. February 2026 brought a $3 million round put up by close to twelve of its own restaurant clients, lifting its total raised to $12 million. A May 2026 release added Brand Voice and a Menu Item Performance Report.

What you can do with Ovation:

  • Ask two quick questions by text. Your POS or online ordering system triggers the text once an order closes, while a QR code on the table covers dine-in.
  • Recover guests in real time. A low score pings the store manager right away, so the apology or callback can happen the same day.
  • Reply to reviews with AI help. One inbox holds Google, Yelp and Facebook reviews, filterable by rating and location, and AI drafts replies in your Brand Voice that staff can edit.
  • Keep listings current. Business details sync to 50+ directories, and you can publish social posts from the same place.
  • See which dishes cause complaints. AI sorts written comments into themes like food quality, speed and cleanliness, and reports connect sentiment to specific menu items and order data.

Why Ovation stands out: The text survey asks very little of the guest, which tends to raise response volume, and it reaches managers fast enough to fix a problem before it turns into a public review. Competitive Pulse also benchmarks each location against up to five nearby competitors.

Who it's best for: Restaurant groups with many units, and in particular brands where online ordering makes up much of the business, where small fulfillment mistakes can go unnoticed without quick guest feedback.

Pricing: No price list is public. Quotes depend on how many locations and ghost kitchens you run, and larger groups get volume discounts.
