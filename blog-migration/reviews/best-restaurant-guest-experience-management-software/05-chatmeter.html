5. Chatmeter

Chatmeter is a reputation, listings and local SEO platform for multi-location brands, positioned as an AI-powered restaurant intelligence platform. Since the survey company Alchemer acquired it in September 2025, the product goes by Alchemer Reputation Management, which lets a chain keep public reviews and survey results with one vendor.

Notable customers: A&W, MR MIKES Steakhouse Casual and Driven Brands.

What's new: In July 2025, Chatmeter added Pulse Ai: Competitive Intelligence, with a regional map that compares individual locations against rivals. Alchemer followed in March 2026 with AI Auto-Responder, a feature that posts on-brand replies under rules you set and sends sensitive reviews to a person first.

What you can do with Chatmeter:

  • Handle reviews from 100+ sites. One dashboard collects reviews and lets your team respond without switching between platforms.
  • Automate routine replies safely. AI Auto-Responder decides which reviews get an automatic answer based on rating, platform and wording, and flags risky ones for a manager.
  • Ask questions of your feedback. You can type something like "what are guests saying about wait times?" and Pulse Ai: Signals searches reviews, surveys, social posts and even review photos for the answer.
  • Include delivery app reviews. Ratings from Uber Eats, DoorDash and Grubhub show up beside dine-in feedback, so off-premise problems don't get missed.
  • Manage listings and local search. Business details and local SEO rankings are tracked location by location, and review generation tools help collect more reviews.

Why Chatmeter stands out: Being part of Alchemer means a chain can run guest surveys and public review management with one vendor, and the competitive tools show how each store compares with the restaurants around it.

Who it's best for: Larger chains and franchise systems that would like listings, review replies and AI analysis bundled with an enterprise survey program.

Pricing: Pricing isn't published. Alchemer's sales team quotes it for each brand.
