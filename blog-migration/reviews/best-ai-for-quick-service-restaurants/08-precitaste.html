8. PreciTaste

Category: AI kitchen management (Kitchen & BOH)

PreciTaste brings AI into the kitchen of a quick service restaurant: it forecasts demand for each location and tells the crew what to prep, what to cook and what to order. It reads POS data and uses cameras to watch ingredient levels and display cases, and Chipotle piloted it in 2022.

Notable customers: Bluemont Group (Dunkin' franchisee, 90+ locations) and Mac Haik Restaurant Group (First Watch franchisee).

What's new: In February 2026, Dunkin' franchisee Bluemont Group rolled out Do'Cast, PreciTaste's AI ordering for donuts and Munchkins, across 90+ stores. In December 2025 a PAR Technology partnership began pulling sales history from PAR POS to speed up setup, and in July 2026 PreciTaste took revenue-based financing from Round2 Capital to grow in Europe.

Key features:

  • Daily prep lists. Prep Assistant builds each morning's list from that store's own demand forecast.
  • Cook guidance on the line. Station Assistant tells the crew in real time how much to cook and when.
  • Camera monitoring. Computer vision watches pans and display cases, so a low ingredient is flagged before it runs out.
  • Forecasts with context. Predictions account for sales trends, weather, seasonality and local events.
  • Bakery ordering. Do'Cast orders donuts from the forecast plus images of the shelf.

What makes them stand out: PreciTaste covers the full kitchen day, from the prep list to production planning to the line, which helps every store cook closer to what it will actually sell.

Pricing: PreciTaste doesn't publish prices; it works with PAR and Toast POS and quotes each brand.

It's a great fit for: QSR and fast-casual chains and franchisees, including bakery-case concepts, that want fewer stockouts and less food thrown out at close.
