14. TimeForge

Category: AI labor forecasting and scheduling (Labor & Scheduling)

TimeForge handles the labor side of a quick service restaurant: who works when, who clocked in, and whether the schedule follows local labor rules. In 2024 it brought in Lineup.ai's forecasting, so its AI now reads your POS sales history and suggests how many people each part of the day needs.

Notable customers: Hard Eight BBQ and Great Harvest Bread Co. (Great Harvest Franchising, preferred vendor).

What's new: A Square integration arrived in July 2025, feeding sales from Square POS into the forecasting engine so Square restaurants get AI staffing levels too.

Key features:

  • Forecasts in 15-minute blocks. The AI looks at past POS sales to estimate demand and staffing, so the schedule tracks your lunch and dinner peaks.
  • Auto-built schedules. TimeForge drafts the week's shifts for you based on what the AI expects you to sell.
  • Time clock with alerts. Managers get a real-time warning when labor runs over target during the day.
  • Labor law guardrails. TimeForge watches overtime limits and fair workweek (predictive scheduling) requirements as you build the week.
  • Staff self-service. From the app, team members tell managers when they can work, ask for days off and trade shifts.

What makes them stand out: Lineup.ai's forecasting now sits inside the same system that builds schedules and runs the time clock, so the AI forecast feeds labor decisions without a separate tool.

Pricing: Custom pricing; contact TimeForge for a quote.

It's a great fit for: QSR and casual-dining operators, including franchise groups, who'd rather keep forecasting, the time clock and compliance checks in a single labor tool.
