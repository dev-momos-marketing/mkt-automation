5. Hi Auto

Category: Drive-thru voice AI

If your AI project starts at the speaker post, Hi Auto is a specialist worth a look. The Israeli-founded company trained its order taker for loud lanes, where it greets the car, keys the order into your point of sale and offers add-ons. In 2025 the company put its annual volume above 100 million drive-thru orders.

Notable customers: Bojangles, Checkers & Rally's and Lee's Famous Recipe Chicken.

What's new: Bojangles renewed for multiple years in 2026. The chain runs Hi Auto in roughly 500 drive-thrus and is adding 100 more by mid-2027, with upsell offers tuned to the number of cars waiting.

Key features:

  • Hears through the noise. The speech model is tuned for engines, wind and car radios at the speaker post.
  • Straight to POS and KDS. Orders appear in the POS and on the kitchen display as soon as the guest finishes.
  • Queue-aware upselling. Suggestions adjust to the length of the line, so the AI holds back when cars are stacking up.
  • Spanish ordering. Guests can order in Spanish, which Checkers & Rally's added to its drive-thrus.
  • A voice with a name. Brands can create a persona, as Bojangles did with "Bo-Linda."

What makes them stand out: Hi Auto works on the drive-thru alone and is testing Window Intelligence, a beta product that monitors the pickup window, with brands including Lee's.

Pricing: Hi Auto doesn't publish prices; pricing is custom.

It's a great fit for: QSR chains with high-volume drive-thrus that want a branded AI voice taking orders across hundreds of stores.
