6. Tattle

Category: Guest feedback & CX improvement

Tattle applies AI to the guest feedback a quick service brand already generates. A brief survey reaches the guest once they've paid, sent via the online ordering, loyalty or point-of-sale tools you use, and Tattle's models work out which single change would help each store most. A franchisee with twenty units gets a clear to-do per restaurant in place of a pile of comments about slow service.

Notable customers: Hooters, Red Robin, Brinker International (Chili's) and Kung Fu Tea.

What's new: Kung Fu Tea partnered with Tattle in January 2026 to follow sentiment across its channels. Five months before, in August 2025, the company closed $5 million to invest in its AI tools, and a newer mobile app puts alerts and coaching in front of GMs and franchisees.

Key features:

  • Triggered by the transaction. Because Tattle connects to POS, ordering and loyalty platforms (Toast, Olo and Punchh among them), each survey goes out without staff doing anything.
  • Feedback mapped to operations. Answers are grouped into speed, accuracy, food quality and hospitality, the areas a shift lead can act on.
  • AI priorities per store. Tattle ranks the improvement with the biggest impact for each location, using sentiment analysis across comments.
  • Public reviews in view. Tattle tracks what people post on Google and Yelp next to the private survey answers.
  • Alerts on a phone. The manager app flags critical incidents and lists AI Coach action items.

What makes them stand out: The AI points each GM at one concrete fix, so operations teams spend less time reading dashboards and more time on the floor.

Pricing: Tattle doesn't publish prices; you request a quote.

It's a great fit for: Multi-unit QSR, fast-casual and casual-dining brands and franchisees that want guest feedback tied to what each store does differently.
