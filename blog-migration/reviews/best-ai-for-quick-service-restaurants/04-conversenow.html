4. ConverseNow

Category: AI phone and drive-thru ordering assistants

When a pizza or wings shop is slammed, ConverseNow's AI takes the calls your crew can't get to, talks with the guest like a person would and sends the ticket to your point of sale. Its 2024 purchase of Valyant AI added drive-thru ordering to what started as a phone product.

Notable customers: Anthony's Coal Fired Pizza & Wings and Wingstop (pilot at select locations).

What's new: In April 2026, ConverseNow announced a partnership with Deliverect, so restaurants using Deliverect for order and menu management can add its voice ordering for phone and drive-thru.

Key features:

  • Answers every call. The AI picks up the phone during a dinner rush and takes the complete order, so no call rings out while staff are on the line.
  • Drive-thru ordering. The same assistant can take orders at the speaker post.
  • No re-keying. Orders go directly into the POS, which removes a common source of mistakes.
  • Large language model conversation. Generative AI lets guests change their minds and ask questions in normal back-and-forth speech.
  • Human backup. Calls can be passed to a live agent through call-center partner OrderSolutions.

What makes them stand out: It suggests an upsell consistently on each order and connects to Deliverect, which helps brands that already manage menus there.

Pricing: ConverseNow doesn't publish prices; pricing is custom.

It's a great fit for: Pizza, wings and other QSR chains that take a large share of orders by phone and want AI to handle them at every store.
