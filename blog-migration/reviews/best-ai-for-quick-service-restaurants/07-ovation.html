7. Ovation

Category: Guest feedback & reputation management

Ovation's AI starts with a very short text message: two questions sent to the guest after they order. If the answer shows a problem, such as a missing side in a drive-thru bag, the store manager hears about it immediately and can text the guest back the same day, while AI groups every written comment into restaurant themes.

Notable customers: Dave's Hot Chicken, Curry Up Now, Bareburger and Taziki's Mediterranean Cafe. Its customer stories appear on LinkedIn and in a QSR Web guide to guest feedback.

What's new: The May 2026 update added Brand Voice, so AI-written winbacks and review answers follow tone guidance you set, and a Menu Item Performance Report that ties feedback to what guests ordered. Earlier, in February 2026, roughly a dozen operators who use Ovation invested $3 million, taking total funding to $12 million.

Key features:

  • Short text surveys. Two questions arrive by SMS after an order, triggered through POS and ordering integrations or table QR codes.
  • Real-time recovery. Negative answers alert the manager right away, so a wrong order can be fixed before the guest posts about it.
  • AI reply drafts. The AI proposes answers to survey comments and online reviews in your chosen tone, and a manager can tweak each one first.
  • Reviews and listings together. Google, Yelp and Facebook reviews sit in one inbox, with listings managed across 50+ directories.
  • Competitor benchmarks. With Competitive Pulse, every store can be measured against as many as five nearby competitors.

What makes them stand out: Ovation keeps feedback as light as possible for the guest, and its AI turns those quick replies into recovery tasks and menu insights for the operator. You can compare it with other tools in our guest experience software roundup.

Pricing: Custom pricing; Ovation quotes each account by its locations and ghost kitchens, with volume discounts, and doesn't publish prices.

It's a great fit for: Quick service and fast-casual chains with many units that would like AI to flag unhappy guests by text and manage public reviews from one dashboard.
