11. MarketMan

Category: AI inventory & food cost (Inventory)

In the back of a quick service restaurant, MarketMan automates the inventory and purchasing chores that eat a manager's afternoon. Counts, recipe costs, supplier orders and invoices live in one cloud system, and each scanned invoice refreshes ingredient prices so your plate costs stay current.

Notable customers: Westville Restaurant Group, ATFC, Ltd. and Taco del Mar (multi-unit franchisee).

What's new: In April 2026 a MarketMan-powered inventory product, Square Restaurant Inventory, launched worldwide, so a restaurant on Square can manage recipes, vendors, purchase orders, scanned invoices and waste from inside its existing account.

Key features:

  • Live counts. Inventory is tracked in real time for each location.
  • Recipe costing on current prices. Plate costs update as ingredient prices change, so a menu price can be revisited quickly.
  • Supplier orders. You can build and send purchase orders to vendors automatically.
  • Invoice scanning with alerts. Scanned invoices update prices and flag items whose cost has jumped.
  • Waste and COGS reporting. You can see where product is lost and how cost of goods compares between stores.

What makes them stand out: The Square launch brings MarketMan's inventory tools to Square restaurants directly, and larger groups can add API access.

Pricing: MarketMan's prices couldn't be confirmed on its own site, and third-party trackers report changes in 2026, so ask MarketMan for a quote.

It's a great fit for: Independent restaurants, multi-unit groups and franchisees that want closer control of purchasing and food cost, including Square sellers.
