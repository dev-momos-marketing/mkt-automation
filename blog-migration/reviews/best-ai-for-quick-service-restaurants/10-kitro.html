10. Kitro

Category: AI food waste analytics (Kitchen & BOH)

KITRO is a Swiss company whose device sits over the kitchen bin, photographing and weighing each disposal and letting AI classify it. Chefs then read dashboards showing where food and money are lost and order or cook accordingly. Hotel and resort kitchens make up most of its customer list, so a typical quick service restaurant is a weaker match.

Notable customers: Marriott International, Hyatt, Pestana (Algarve hotel) and Hyatt Inclusive Collection.

What's new: In May 2026, KITRO entered Portugal with a flagship Pestana hotel in the Algarve. In February 2026 it published a Hyatt EAME case study reporting more than EUR 1.7M saved and a 31% drop in food waste, figures the vendor reports. In 2025 it became a preferred partner of Marriott International.

Key features:

  • Hands-free capture. The in-bin device records every disposal with a photo and a weight, without staff logging anything.
  • AI sorting. Waste is classified by food type and as edible or inedible.
  • Waste in money. Cost values per category show the loss in currency as well as kilos.
  • Chef dashboards. Reports guide changes to purchasing, production and buffet sizing.
  • ESG reporting. Sustainability reports help with EU food waste rules.

What makes them stand out: KITRO rents the device on a monthly subscription and offers a free assessment, which keeps the upfront cost of trying AI waste tracking low.

Pricing: The Business plan starts at CHF 349 per month for one kitchen; Enterprise is priced to match operational complexity, with discounts for contract length, property size and number of units.

It's a great fit for: Hotels, resorts, buffets and catering kitchens that report on food waste; quick service restaurants with little plate waste are a weaker match.
