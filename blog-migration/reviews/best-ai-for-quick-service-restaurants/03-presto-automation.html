3. Presto Automation

Category: Drive-thru voice AI

Presto's product, Presto Voice, is an AI that answers the drive-thru speaker and plugs into the headsets and point of sale your crew uses today. Ownership has changed: Presto Automation left Nasdaq, its assets went to a Remus Capital-led buyer group in December 2024, and the company continues privately as Presto Phoenix with its customers carried over.

Notable customers: Carl's Jr. / Hardee's and Taco John's.

What's new: In January 2026, Presto raised $10 million led by Metropolitan Partners Group to grow enterprise QSR rollouts, saying it now runs in hundreds of drive-thru lanes. In September 2025 it partnered with ElevenLabs for more natural AI voices, and in 2026 it joined the Toast Partner Ecosystem.

Key features:

  • AI at the speaker post. The voice assistant greets the car, takes the order and passes it on, freeing a crew member during the lunch peak.
  • Works with your current gear. It uses existing headsets and POS, and Toast restaurants can send voice orders straight to the kitchen.
  • Upsell on every car. The AI suggests add-ons each time, which staff often skip when the line is long.
  • Branded voices. ElevenLabs text-to-speech gives the drive-thru a lifelike voice, and brands can request a custom one.
  • Central menu control. Menu changes are managed in one place for every location, with ordering in more than one language.

What makes them stand out: Presto focuses on the drive-thru lane alone, and it's built to drop into the hardware a franchisee already owns.

Pricing: Pricing is custom; you contact Presto for a quote.

It's a great fit for: Franchisees and quick service chains running many high-traffic drive-thru lanes who'd like an AI voice handling the speaker box.
