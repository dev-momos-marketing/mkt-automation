12. xtraCHEF

Category: AI invoice processing & food cost (Inventory)

xtraCHEF is Toast's back-office tool for invoice automation, recipe costing and inventory. Toast acquired it in 2021 and still sells it as xtraCHEF by Toast, connecting supplier costs to the sales data in your Toast POS.

Notable customers: Toast doesn't name xtraCHEF customers publicly.

What's new: Toast hasn't announced any dated xtraCHEF product news in 2025 or 2026.

Key features:

  • Invoices read for you. Supplier invoices are captured and their line-item costs extracted, so managers stop typing them in after close.
  • Recipe and menu costing. The Pro tier handles prep and sub-recipes, yields and unit conversions.
  • Actual vs. theoretical. Variance reports show where food cost drifts from what the recipes predict.
  • Inventory counts. Pro also includes inventory management.
  • Vendor tools. Ordering, product catalogs and accounting integrations sit in the same place.

What makes them stand out: Cost data meets Toast POS sales directly, so you can see what each item cost against what it sold for.

Pricing: Toast doesn't publish xtraCHEF prices. It's sold through Toast sales in Essentials and Pro tiers; both include invoice automation, and recipe costing and inventory are Pro only.

It's a great fit for: QSR and other restaurants already on Toast POS that want to automate invoices and watch recipe margins.
