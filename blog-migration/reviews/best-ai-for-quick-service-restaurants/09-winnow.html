9. Winnow

Category: Restaurant AI-powered food waste management software

Winnow uses AI to measure what a kitchen throws away. Its Winnow Vision bin pairs a camera with a scale, recognizes each discarded item and weighs it, and its newer Winnow Foresight app forecasts demand so cooks produce less in the first place.

Most of its customers are hotels, buffets and contract caterers, so for quick service it suits larger groups with real prep or production waste better than a single counter-service unit.

Notable customers: Hilton, IHG Hotels & Resorts, and Accor, Marriott and Mandarin Oriental.

What's new: In September 2026, Winnow released Foresight, a mobile-first forecasting web app built with Hilton chefs that predicts demand item by item without any new hardware. In July 2026 it acquired Lumitics, a Singapore food waste AI company, to grow in Asia-Pacific and the Middle East.

Key features:

  • Throw and go. The camera recognizes whatever lands in the bin, which spares a busy line cook from typing in each item.
  • Waste by stage. Reports break waste down by item and shift, and by whether it came from prep, spoilage or plates, with a dollar value on each.
  • Screens for the crew. A kitchen display shows what's being binned and what it costs while the shift is still running.
  • Multi-site reports. Dashboards let chefs and managers compare waste between locations.
  • Item-level forecasts. Foresight plans production per item and service to cut overproduction.
  • Manual tracking option. Sites that don't need computer vision can log waste with a cheaper setup.

What makes them stand out: Winnow puts the cost of waste in front of the people producing it, and the Foresight app now adds a planning step before food is cooked.

Pricing: Winnow doesn't publish prices; pricing is custom.

It's a great fit for: High-volume kitchens and large multi-site restaurant groups with meaningful production waste; smaller QSR units with little prep may get less from it.
