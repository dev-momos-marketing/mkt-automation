16. Legion

Category: AI scheduling for large hourly teams (Labor & Scheduling)

Legion was built with AI at its core for companies that schedule thousands of hourly workers, forecasting demand and drafting schedules that stay inside budget and scheduling law, with timekeeping and team chat included. Much of Legion's recent messaging targets retailers. Restaurants are still served by a dedicated product, and its published restaurant case study covers a fast-casual brand with 60+ units and 6,000+ staff.

Notable customers: Wilson James and an unnamed fast-casual chain (60+ restaurants, 6,000+ employees).

What's new: Legion's spring 2026 release reached general availability in July 2026 and gave multi-unit leaders their own AI helper, the Upper Field Schedule Assistant. In January 2026 it shipped more than 90 new features, including AI assistants that automate scheduling decisions.

Key features:

  • Demand forecasting. AI predicts staffing needs from sales and traffic data.
  • Budget-aware schedules. Schedules are generated automatically within the labor budget you set.
  • Fair Workweek tracking. Predictive-scheduling rules and premium pay are monitored, which matters in cities with these laws.
  • AI schedule assistants. Assistants build schedules, explain them and translate them for staff.
  • Employee app. Crew pick up open shifts, swap shifts and update availability on their phones.
  • Multi-store views. District and area managers see schedules across all their stores.

What makes them stand out: Legion layers several AI assistants on top of forecasting and scheduling, giving area managers help as well as store managers.

Pricing: Legion doesn't publish prices; pricing is custom for enterprises.

It's a great fit for: Large multi-unit QSR and fast-casual operators with big hourly workforces and predictive-scheduling laws to follow.
