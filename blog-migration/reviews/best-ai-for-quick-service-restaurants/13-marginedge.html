13. MarginEdge

Category: Back-office automation and daily food cost (Inventory)

MarginEdge matches what you bought against what you sold, so a QSR owner can open a daily P&L and food cost report without waiting for the accountant. One subscription bundles the invoice handling, vendor payments, ordering and recipe costs, and the company's latest funding is going toward AI built on its restaurant purchasing data.

Notable customers: Skopos Hospitality Group, Crooked Hammock Brewery, South Block and The Tavern at Ivy City Smokehouse.

What's new: In August 2026 the company closed $80 million in Series D funding, part equity and part debt, with plans to spend it on new AI products; Schooner Capital and Ten Coves Capital led the round together. Back in April 2025, MarginEdge turned 10 and counted 10,000 restaurants as customers.

Key features:

  • Snap the invoice. A phone photo, a forwarded email or an EDI feed is enough, and MarginEdge does the data entry.
  • Yesterday's food cost today. Sales from the POS and costs from invoices combine into a daily food and beverage cost.
  • Vendor payments. You pay suppliers inside the same tool at no extra charge.
  • Cost jump warnings. MarginEdge tells you when a regular purchase like fryer oil or cheese gets more expensive.
  • Books stay in sync. Budget and P&L figures flow into accounting tools such as QuickBooks.

What makes them stand out: Because costs show up daily, a QSR operator can catch a portioning or pricing problem mid-month and correct it before the period ends.

Pricing: Pricing is a published flat fee per location each month, covering unlimited invoices and bill pay. MarginEdge's own page has listed $330; 2026 trackers put it at $350, and Freepour (bar inventory) adds $150. Check the live figure before you budget.

It's a great fit for: Independent quick service restaurants, franchisees and small-to-mid groups that want daily cost visibility linked to their books.
