15. 7Shifts

Category: Labor & Workforce Performance Analytics

7shifts is a workforce platform made only for restaurants, covering scheduling, time clocking, tip management, payroll and, since 2026, employee training. Its forecasting uses your sales history to suggest how many people each shift needs, which helps a QSR manager keep labor in line with the day's sales.

Notable customers: HCI Hospitality (Freddy's Frozen Custard & Steakburgers franchisee), Boston Pizza (Regina), Conshy Girls Restaurant Group and Pitango Gelato.

What's new: Employee Training went live in September 2026 for Premium customers: managers create lessons and quizzes, send them to a role or a store, and see who finished, all in the app crew already open for their shifts. A new set of plans took effect in July 2025.

Key features:

  • Forecast-based scheduling. You drag shifts into place while the forecast shows how many people the expected sales call for.
  • Crew app. Staff pick up swaps, change their availability and message one another from a phone.
  • Time clock rules. Schedule enforcement through POS and payroll integrations stops early clock-ins.
  • Labor vs. sales. Budgets and reports compare labor cost with sales as the week goes on.
  • Tips and payroll. Pooled tips are split and paid inside 7shifts; full payroll is available for an extra fee.

Pricing: A single store with 15 staff or fewer can use the free Comp plan. Paid per location and billed yearly, Essentials runs $39.99 a month (30 staff max), Pro $79.99 and Premium $134.99; paying month to month instead costs $149.99 for Premium, $89.99 for Pro or $44.99 for Essentials. Payroll costs extra, Enterprise pricing is quoted, and you can try Pro free for 14 days.
