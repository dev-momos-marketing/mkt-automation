3. OvationUp

Ovation, sold at ovationup.com and often called OvationUp, is an AI-first guest experience platform for multi-unit restaurants. Its AI reads private text feedback and public reviews together, sorts what guests write into restaurant themes and drafts replies your team approves.

The AI starts from private feedback. A two-question text survey follows each order, so the system also learns from complaints that guests never post publicly. Dave's Hot Chicken, Curry Up Now and Bareburger have case studies on Ovation's site.

Key features

  • Brand Voice for AI drafts: Tone instructions from the spring 2026 release guide every suggested reply, winback message and automated response, and staff can edit each draft before it goes out.
  • AI theme tagging: Written comments are filed under restaurant topics like cleanliness, speed and the quality of the food, which shows where complaints cluster across your locations.
  • Menu Item Performance Report: Guest sentiment is matched to order data, so you can see which dishes sit behind falling scores.
  • Review inbox: Reviews from Google, Yelp, Facebook and other sites land in one place, filterable by platform, rating and location.
  • Competitive Pulse: Each location is benchmarked against as many as five nearby competitors.

Pros

  • Restaurant categories built in: The AI's themes use the language of restaurant operations, so reports read the way a general manager already thinks.
  • Early warning: Real-time alerts on negative survey answers give a manager time to contact the guest before a review is posted.
  • Feedback and reviews together: Since the fall 2025 release, private surveys, public reviews, listings and social share one dashboard.
  • Customer-backed: In February 2026, nearly a dozen restaurant customers invested $3 million, taking total funding to $12 million.

Cons

  • Quote-only pricing: Ovation doesn't publish prices, so comparing costs means booking a demo or a chat first.
  • Newer review tools: Review and listings management arrived with the fall 2025 release, so that part of the product has a shorter record than its surveys.
  • Few independent ratings: G2 lists only a couple of user reviews, too few to compare it with larger platforms.

Price and plans

Ovation doesn't publish prices. It quotes each account on its physical locations and ghost kitchens, with volume discounts.
