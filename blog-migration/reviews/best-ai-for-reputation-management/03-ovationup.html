3. OvationUp

Category: Guest feedback and reputation AI for restaurant chains

Ovation, sold at ovationup.com, starts from private feedback. A two-question text survey follows each order, and AI sorts the written answers into restaurant themes such as food quality, speed and cleanliness. Since its fall 2025 release, the same platform also handles public reviews, listings and social, so the AI that reads your surveys also drafts your review replies.

Notable customers: Dave's Hot Chicken, Curry Up Now, Bareburger and Taziki's Mediterranean Cafe.

What's new: The spring 2026 release introduced Brand Voice, a set of tone instructions the AI follows in winback messages, automated responses and review replies, along with a Menu Item Performance Report that ties guest sentiment to order data. Its February 2026 round of $3 million came from close to a dozen operators that already use the product, and lifted funding to $12 million overall.

Key features:

  • AI drafts in your tone. Suggested replies to reviews and survey comments follow your Brand Voice settings, and staff can edit each one before it goes out.
  • Theme detection. Written feedback is tagged automatically, which shows whether complaints cluster around speed, cleanliness or a specific dish.
  • Instant recovery alerts. A negative survey answer notifies the manager while there's still time to contact the guest.
  • Filtered review inbox. You can sort incoming reviews from Google, Yelp and Facebook by platform, rating and location.
  • Competitive Pulse. Each location can be benchmarked against up to five nearby competitors.
  • Menu-level reporting. Sentiment is linked to menu items and orders, so you can spot the dishes behind low scores.

Pricing: Prices aren't public. Ovation quotes each account on its count of dine-in sites and ghost kitchens and discounts larger volumes.
