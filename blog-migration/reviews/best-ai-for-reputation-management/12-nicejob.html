12. NiceJob

Category: Automated review requests for small service companies

Paystone has owned NiceJob, a reputation marketing tool for small service companies, since 2021. It automates review requests, review widgets, referrals and repeat-customer campaigns from the job and invoicing tools you already use. Restaurants aren't a stated focus; it's built mainly for home services such as cleaners, movers and trades.

Notable customers: Evergreen Cleaning and You Move Me.

What's new: Through a SendJim tie-up that started in October 2025, home-service firms can print their verified 5-star NiceJob reviews on mailed postcards.

Key features:

  • Ask every customer automatically. Review requests go out by email and SMS, followed by reminders for anyone who hasn't responded.
  • Connect your job tools. Integrations include Jobber, Housecall Pro and QuickBooks, plus Zapier for everything else.
  • Answer reviews with AI. On Pro, AI replies follow the tone, length and rules you choose, with an optional auto-reply setting.
  • Show reviews on your site. Widgets display your best reviews as social proof on your website.
  • Bring customers back. Pro adds referral, repeat and rebooking campaigns plus marketing broadcasts.

Pricing: Grow starts at $75/month when you have no more than 2,500 customers a year, rising to $95/month for 2,501–5,000. Pro starts at $125/month and brings referral campaigns, repeat-business campaigns, broadcasts and AI replies to reviews. Both include a 14-day free trial and no contract.
