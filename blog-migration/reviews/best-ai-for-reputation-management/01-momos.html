1. Momos

Momos helps restaurant chains run AI-powered reputation management across every location. It gathers all of your reviews in one place, has an AI agent answer them, and turns what guests write into fixes your operations team can act on.

Notable customers: Restaurant chains such as Condado Tacos and Potbelly, plus Papa Murphy's and Caribou Coffee.

What's new: During 2025, Momos shipped AI copilots for staff, automatic recaps of every ticket, uploadable brand rules that shape AI replies, and mobile-first reporting. New integrations with Square, Otter and Paytronix arrived the same year. Potbelly, with 472 locations, also signed on in 2025 to handle its reviews, cases and guest messages in one view.

What you can do with Momos:

  • Read all feedback in a single queue. The Unified Inbox brings together Google reviews, POS data, social DMs and app store comments, then sorts and routes each item with AI triage.
  • Reply before a review goes stale. Alfie, the Experience Agent, writes answers within minutes in your brand voice and remembers each guest's earlier visits and preferences.
  • Win back an unhappy guest. From your loyalty platform, Alfie can issue a coupon, an offer or extra points, giving an unhappy guest a concrete reason to try you again.
  • Answer in the guest's language. Built-in real-time translation lets each location respond to reviewers who write in another language.
  • Pass on what needs a person. Beyond reviews, Alfie covers website chat, in-app support, surveys, delivery apps and inbound inquiries, and hands a case to your team when the situation calls for it.

Because all feedback lands in the same inbox, Momos can show you what drives good and bad ratings at each store and how satisfaction shifts over time. That makes it easier to fix a recurring problem before it spreads and to improve guest experience across the whole chain.

Papa Murphy's runs more than 1,000 locations on Momos. Its review responses are 97.7% automated, and guest recovery offers have brought back over $2M in revenue.
