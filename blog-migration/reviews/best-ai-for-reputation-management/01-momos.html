1. Momos

Momos gives restaurant chains a team of AI agents that take on the daily reputation work. Alfie, the Experience Agent, replies to reviews and guest messages in your brand voice, while Rockie, the Intelligence Agent, watches every location for incidents, risks and their root causes.

For a chain weighing AI-powered reputation management, the appeal is that these agents take action as well as report. Papa Murphy's signed on in July 2024 and runs more than 1,000 stores on Momos; within a year, its rating rose from 3.96 to 4.72.

Key features

  • Replies within minutes: Alfie answers Google, Yelp, OpenTable and TripAdvisor reviews in your house style and remembers each guest's earlier visits and preferences.
  • An agent for root causes: Rockie ranks your locations, flags incidents and risks, answers questions in chat and sends personalized reports to your phone.
  • Every signal in one place: The Unified Inbox combines reviews, App Store feedback, social DMs and POS data, tagged by AI with real-time alerts.
  • Offers inside the reply: When a guest had a bad visit, Alfie can attach loyalty points, a coupon or an offer drawn from your loyalty platform.
  • Answers in any language: Real-time translation lets each store reply to a reviewer in the language of the review.

Pros

  • Built for restaurant groups: Review replies, guest support, surveys and recovery for all your stores share one system.
  • Agents that finish the job: A complaint can end with a recovery offer, and Alfie then checks whether the guest came back.
  • Clear view by store: Analytics show which topics lift or sink ratings at each location, so you can improve guest experience chain-wide.
  • Results on record: Papa Murphy's automates 97.7% of its review replies and credits offers with more than $2M in recovered revenue.

Cons

  • Quote only: There's no price list or self-serve trial; pricing comes after a demo.
  • Restaurant focus: A brand outside hospitality, or a lone location, would use only a fraction of what it offers.
  • Guided setup: The Momos team trains Alfie for about a week before it goes live.

Price and plans

Momos doesn't publish prices; you get a custom quote after a demo, which you can book on the Momos website.
