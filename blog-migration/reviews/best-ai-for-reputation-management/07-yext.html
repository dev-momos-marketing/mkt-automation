7. Yext

Category: Listings and local search, with reviews built in

Listed on the NYSE as YEXT, Yext helps multi-location brands run their listings, local pages, reviews and visibility in AI search off a single central knowledge base. Its main strength is keeping location details accurate wherever people search, with review management alongside. A take-private proposal from its CEO was withdrawn in February 2026, and the company remains publicly traded.

Notable customers: Taco Bell, Arby's, Bojangles and Marston's.

What's new: In April 2026, Popmenu expanded its partnership with Yext letting restaurants keep menus and listings in sync and reply to reviews on 70+ platforms. In 2025, Yext bought Places Scout in February and launched Yext Scout in March, an agent that tracks your visibility and sentiment across Google, Bing and AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity.

Key features:

  • Keep one source of truth. The Knowledge Graph stores each location's hours, menus and other details in a single place.
  • Push updates everywhere. A change you make once flows to Apple Maps, Google, Facebook, Bing and many other directories and apps.
  • Answer reviews with AI help. Monitoring covers review sites, and AI drafts replies for your team to check and send.
  • Collect more reviews. AI review generation campaigns also reach third-party review sites.
  • Track AI search. Scout shows how visible you are in traditional and AI search and how you stack up against competitors.
  • Control who posts what. Permissions and approval workflows suit large teams and franchise networks.

Pricing: Custom for multi-location and enterprise brands. Third-party sites list self-serve tiers but disagree on the amounts, so ask Yext for a quote.
