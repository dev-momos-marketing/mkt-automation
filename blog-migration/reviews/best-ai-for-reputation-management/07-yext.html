7. Yext

Yext is a publicly traded (NYSE: YEXT) digital presence platform for multi-location brands. It keeps every location's details in one knowledge base and runs listings, local pages and reviews from it, with AI drafting review replies and measuring how AI assistants describe your brand.

For an AI reputation program, the standout piece is Scout, launched in March 2025, which shows how ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity talk about your restaurants compared with competitors. In April 2026, Popmenu widened its partnership with Yext so restaurants can answer reviews on 70+ platforms.

Key features

  • AI reply drafts: AI writes a response to each new review for your team to approve, and rules can let routine replies post on their own.
  • AI search tracking: Scout measures visibility and sentiment in Google, Bing and AI assistants, benchmarked against rival brands.
  • Review requests: Invitations go out by email, text or QR code, and an algorithm steers guests toward the sites that matter most for search.
  • Knowledge Graph: Hours, menus and location details live in one record that updates your listings on Google, Apple Maps, Bing and Facebook.
  • Franchise controls: Role-based access, audit trails and approval steps decide who can publish a reply for each location.

Pros

  • AI with a human check: Drafted replies wait for approval by default, so your brand voice stays in your team's hands.
  • AI answers covered: Scout reports how AI assistants describe you as well as where you appear in classic search.
  • Wide review coverage: Monitoring spans Google, Facebook, Yelp and 80+ industry sites from a single dashboard.
  • Well rated: Yext averages 4.4 stars out of 5 among G2 reviewers.

Cons

  • No price list: Yext prices each deal by location count and product, so you can't compare costs before talking to sales.
  • No free trial: Yext offers no free tier or standard trial, which means you judge the AI features in a demo.
  • Listings come first: Reviews sit alongside listings and search, so brands that mainly want AI guest messaging or surveys may need another tool.

Price and plans

Yext doesn't publish prices; you get a custom quote after a demo, based on your location count and the products you choose. Billing is typically annual, and a multi-year term costs less per year than a one-year term.
