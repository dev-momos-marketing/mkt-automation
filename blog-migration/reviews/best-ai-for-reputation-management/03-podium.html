3. Podium

Category: Texting, reviews and AI lead handling for local businesses

Podium gives local businesses one place for texting customers, collecting reviews and taking payments. Its AI Employee replies to new leads and schedules appointments on its own. It now sells mainly as industry-specific "AI operating systems" for automotive, home services and aesthetics. Restaurants aren't among its focus industries, so check how much of the product you would actually use.

Notable customers: Jackson Hewitt, Mattress Firm, Mountaineer Heating & Cooling and Ashley Furniture HomeStore.

What's new: Jerry 2.0 arrived in December 2025: an AI employee you can customize, running on OpenAI models and aimed at auto, HVAC and medspa shops. In January 2026 it launched an AI operating system for aesthetic practices, with a home services version following in August 2026. By its own count, Podium's AI agents were bringing in more than $150 million a year in recurring revenue by October 2026.

Key features:

  • Ask for reviews by text. Customers get a message with a link that opens Google or another review site in one tap.
  • Answer leads around the clock. The AI Employee responds by text, phone, email and social, handles common questions and books appointments after hours.
  • Let AI pick up the phone. A voice agent can take incoming calls while your staff is busy with customers.
  • Keep conversations together. Texts, webchat, Google messages and social messages share one inbox your whole team can see.
  • Take payment in the thread. Text-to-pay links let a customer settle a bill inside the same conversation.
  • Track results by location. Reports cover reviews and conversations for each of your sites.

Pricing: No prices are listed. Sales quotes the three plans (Core, Pro and Signature) individually, and you pay extra to add the AI Employee to whichever plan you pick.
