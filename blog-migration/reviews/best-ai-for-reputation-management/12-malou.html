12. Malou

Category: AI marketing and review platform for restaurant groups

Malou is a Paris-based platform where AI writes review replies, analyzes what guests say and helps with local SEO, social posts and visibility in AI search. Groups with three or more restaurants can run it themselves through MalouApp or add Copilot, which pairs them with a dedicated marketing expert. The interface works in English, French, Italian and Spanish.

Notable customers: Krispy Kreme, Tudor Steak House NYC, Riviera Dining Group and MELT Oberkampf.

What's new: Malou hasn't announced a dated product release in 2025 or 2026.

Key features:

  • Replies built on your guidelines. The AI adjusts tone to each review's sentiment while sticking to the voice rules your brand sets.
  • Reply scoring. Each response gets a quality score plus suggestions drawn from Google's best practices.
  • Sensitive-topic alerts. Reviews mentioning issues like hygiene or discrimination are flagged for a person to handle.
  • Semantic analysis. Recurring themes across reviews are pulled out, so you see patterns instead of reading every comment.
  • Pages for AI search. A store locator and local pages are set up to be found by search engines and AI assistants.

Malou doesn't publish a price list, so you'll need to ask its sales team for a quote.
