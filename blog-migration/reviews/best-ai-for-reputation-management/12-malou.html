12. Malou

Malou is a Paris-based AI marketing platform made only for restaurant groups. Its AI writes review replies, reads reviews for recurring themes and supports local SEO, listings, social posts and visibility in AI search.

You can run it yourself through MalouApp or add Copilot, which pairs your group with a dedicated marketing expert for search and social work. Krispy Kreme and Tudor Steak House NYC are among the brands Malou names as customers.

Key features

  • Guideline-based replies: The AI adapts each response to the review's tone and sentiment while following the voice rules your brand sets.
  • Reply scoring: Every response gets a quality score, with tips based on Google's best practices.
  • Sensitive-topic alerts: Reviews that mention hygiene, discrimination or similar issues are flagged for a person to handle.
  • Semantic analysis: Malou pulls recurring themes out of your reviews, so patterns show up without reading every comment.
  • Pages for AI search: A store locator and local pages are built to be found by search engines and AI assistants.

Pros

  • Restaurant-only design: Every part of the product is built around restaurant reviews, listings and local search.
  • Expert option: Copilot gives groups without an in-house marketer someone to run the work.
  • Four languages: The app works in English, French, Italian and Spanish.

Cons

  • Quote only: Malou doesn't publish prices; plans are priced by the number of locations.
  • Social in the top plan: Social media management comes only with the Full plan.
  • Few outside reviews: MalouApp has a single review on G2, so independent feedback is thin.

Price and plans

Malou sells three plans, Essential, Growth and Full, priced by how many locations you manage. It doesn't publish prices, so you request a quote.
