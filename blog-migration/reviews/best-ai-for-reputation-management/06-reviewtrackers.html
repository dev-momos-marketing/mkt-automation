6. ReviewTrackers

ReviewTrackers is a review monitoring and analytics platform for multi-location businesses. It collects reviews in one dashboard, lets local managers answer with wording head office has approved, and can draft replies with generative AI from prompts your brand saves.

InMoment bought ReviewTrackers in 2022, and Press Ganey Forsta acquired InMoment in May 2025. For a restaurant group weighing AI tools, its appeal is control: a person or a saved prompt shapes every answer, which suits brands with strict approval rules.

Key features

  • AI Auto Responding: Generative AI writes personalized replies from the brand prompts you store. InMoment launched it in July 2025 on its Locations Starter and Locations Essential plans.
  • Smart Response: Managers choose from replies your brand team has signed off and post one in a few clicks.
  • Sentiment and topic analytics: Review text is sorted into themes and scored for tone, so a complaint that keeps returning at several stores is easy to see.
  • Review alerts: Data from 100+ sources, Google and TripAdvisor among them, feeds one dashboard and flags each new review.
  • Review requests: Automated emails and texts ask recent customers to rate their visit.

Pros

  • AI with guardrails: Replies start from prompts and templates your brand controls, which lowers the risk of an off-brand answer going live.
  • Made for chains: Reports by location and access by role can follow the way your franchise is organized.
  • Unlimited users: The Starter plan has no cap on users, so every general manager can have a login.
  • Strong rating: G2 reviewers rate ReviewTrackers 4.6 out of 5 across 161 reviews.

Cons

  • Check AI availability: The AI Auto Responding announcement came from InMoment and never names ReviewTrackers, so ask sales whether your plan includes it.
  • Analysis costs a tier: Experience analysis and competitor insights come with the Essential plan only.
  • No public prices: A budget figure means booking a meeting with sales first.

Price and plans

ReviewTrackers doesn't publish prices; you get a quote after a meeting. Its three plans (Data Only, Starter and Essential) are priced per location, per month and paid annually.
