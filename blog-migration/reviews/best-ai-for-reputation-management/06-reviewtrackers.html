6. ReviewTrackers

Category: Review monitoring and analytics for chains

Multi-location businesses use ReviewTrackers to manage reviews and analyze their reputation. It has been owned by InMoment since 2022, and InMoment was itself sold to Press Ganey Forsta in May 2025, putting ReviewTrackers inside that group while keeping its own brand, site and plans.

Notable customers: Ted's Montana Grill, PACK & SEND and Chem-Dry.

What's new: InMoment introduced AI Auto Responding in July 2025. It writes tailored review replies based on prompts a brand stores ahead of time, and it ships with the Locations Essential and Locations Starter plans. Those are ReviewTrackers' tier names, though the announcement doesn't mention ReviewTrackers directly, so confirm availability with sales.

Key features:

  • Watch every site from one screen. Reviews from Google, Facebook, TripAdvisor and others land in one dashboard, with alerts when new ones arrive.
  • Hand managers pre-approved answers. With Smart Response templates, a store manager can respond in a few clicks while staying on brand.
  • Draft with AI. AI Auto Responding writes replies from the prompts your brand team saves.
  • Ask for more reviews. Automated requests reach customers by email and text after their visit.
  • Find recurring issues. Topic and sentiment analytics point to complaints that keep coming up, and competitor tracking shows how nearby rivals rate.
  • Mirror your franchise structure. Location reporting and role-based access can match the way your regions and owners are organized.

Pricing: ReviewTrackers charges by location and doesn't list prices; you'll need to schedule a call to get a number.
