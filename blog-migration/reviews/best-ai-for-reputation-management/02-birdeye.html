2. Birdeye

Category: Reviews, listings and AI agents in one suite

Birdeye now calls itself an agentic marketing platform built for brands with many locations. It runs review management next to listings, surveys, messaging, social and visibility in AI search, with AI agents and a newer set of "AI Coworkers" carrying out much of the routine work. Restaurant chains are an explicit focus, and in January 2025 Birdeye launched a version built on language models trained for the restaurant industry.

Notable customers: The Saxton Group (a McAlister's Deli franchisee) and Black Bear Diner.

What's new: At its View 2026 event in September, Birdeye introduced three AI Coworkers: Jay handles marketing, Myna covers operations, and Robin looks after customer experience. Later that month it added a SkyTab POS integration that copies restaurant menu details into your listings. A year earlier, at View 2025, it had unveiled its Agentic Marketing Platform with more than ten AI agents.

Key features:

  • Catch problem reviews early. Monitoring spans Yelp, Google, Facebook and industry sites, while ticketing sends each negative review to the manager of the location involved.
  • Grow review volume. Automated requests by SMS and email ask recent customers to share how their visit went.
  • Reply in your brand voice. AI drafts responses for you to approve, and Birdeye's agents can take over replies entirely once your tone is set.
  • Keep menus and hours accurate. Listings management updates your details across directories, including an automatic menu sync for restaurants on SkyTab.
  • Read sentiment by region. Surveys and Insights AI add sentiment analysis with reports by location, district and region, plus benchmarks against competitors.

Pricing: Prices aren't public. Birdeye quotes each brand individually once you've had a demo.
