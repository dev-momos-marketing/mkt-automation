2. Birdeye

Birdeye is an agentic marketing and reputation platform for brands with many locations. It runs review management alongside listings, messaging, social, surveys and AI search visibility, and its AI agents carry out much of the routine work, from drafting replies to updating directory details.

Birdeye's recent releases put AI agents at the center of reputation work. In September 2026 it introduced three AI Coworkers (Jay for marketing, Myna for operations and Robin for customer experience) that together direct 27 agents. Restaurants were an early focus: a January 2025 release added language models trained on restaurant data.

Key features

  • Agent-written replies: AI drafts responses in your brand voice for approval, or agents post them on their own once you trust the tone.
  • Review ticketing: Monitoring spans Google, Yelp, Facebook and industry sites, and a negative review becomes a ticket for the manager of that location.
  • Review requests: SMS and email invitations ask recent customers to rate their visit, which keeps fresh reviews coming in.
  • Insights AI: Surveys and reviews feed sentiment analysis with reports by location, district and region, plus competitor benchmarks.
  • Search AI: It tracks how AI answer engines present your brand and helps you improve that visibility.

Pros

  • Hands work to AI: Agents and AI Coworkers take on replies, listings updates and reporting, which helps when location managers have little time for reviews.
  • Restaurant training: The restaurant edition uses models trained for the industry and watches Yelp reviews through a partnership with Yelp.
  • Regional reporting: District and region views suit chains and franchise groups that manage reputation by area.
  • Well rated: Birdeye holds a 4.7 out of 5 rating on G2.

Cons

  • No public prices: Birdeye asks for your location count and sends a quote.
  • Cross-industry scope: Some Coworker skills, such as Myna's appointment bookings and insurance verification, are aimed at other industries.
  • Sized for chains: A single restaurant may need only a small part of the platform.

Price and plans

Birdeye doesn't publish prices. Its pricing page says you pay for what you deploy at your locations under one contract, and you request a quote with your number of locations.
