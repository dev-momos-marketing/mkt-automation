9. Sprout Social

Sprout Social is a social media management platform that also collects and answers reviews. Its AI agent, Trellis, and the AI Assist tools draft replies and posts, summarize data and suggest tags, so a marketing team can work through social comments and reviews in one queue.

It's a social tool first, best suited to restaurant groups whose marketing team already runs social media and wants AI help with replies. In May 2026, Sprout turned Trellis into a conversational layer across the product and added Trellis Studio for building custom AI workflows.

Key features

  • Trellis AI agent: Answers questions about your social and review data in conversation, and Trellis Studio runs AI workflows your team designs.
  • AI Assist: Polishes drafts of posts and replies; Enhance Reply is listed on the Advanced plan.
  • Smart Inbox: Messages, comments, mentions and reviews from connected networks arrive in one feed.
  • Reviews tab: Gathers reviews from Google Business Profile, Facebook, Yelp, Tripadvisor and Trustpilot, and replies to Google and Facebook reviews go out from Sprout.
  • Listening with news: Social listening tracks sentiment and trends, and the NewsWhip purchase in July 2025 extended it to news coverage.

Pros

  • AI included on every plan: Each seat gets 100 Trellis credits a month at no extra charge.
  • One team, one queue: Social engagement and review replies share the same inbox, tags and workflow.
  • Collaboration controls: Approvals, routing, task assignment and permissions keep several people working on one account in order.
  • Try before buying: The 30-day free trial doesn't ask for a credit card.
  • User rating: G2 reviewers rate Sprout Social 4.4 out of 5.

Cons

  • Credits for heavier AI use: Trellis Chat and Skills draw on credits, and going past 100 per seat a month means buying Trellis Plus at $28 per seat.
  • Reviews start on Standard: The entry Essentials plan doesn't list review management.
  • Listening costs extra: Social listening is an add-on, priced after a demo.
  • Per-seat pricing: Every manager who needs access adds another seat to the bill.

Price and plans

Sprout Social offers a 30-day free trial with no credit card. Prices are per seat, per month, billed annually.

  • Essentials: $79 per seat, per month ($99 billed monthly).
  • Standard: $199 per seat, per month.
  • Professional: $299 per seat, per month.
  • Advanced: $399 per seat, per month.
  • Enterprise: Custom pricing.
