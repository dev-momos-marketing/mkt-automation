9. Sprout Social

Category: Social media management that also covers reviews

Sprout Social (Nasdaq: SPT) is a social media management platform covering publishing, a unified Smart Inbox, review management, social listening and analytics, with an AI agent called Trellis. It's a social tool first, but its review features make it useful for restaurant groups whose marketing team already handles social engagement.

Notable customers: Papa Johns and Lettuce Entertain You Enterprises.

What's new: In May 2026, Sprout Social turned Trellis into a conversational layer across the product and introduced Trellis Studio for building custom AI workflows. Earlier, in February 2026, it widened its Reddit partnership and launched Recruit for managing creators. Its July 2025 purchase of NewsWhip extended listening into news coverage.

Key features:

  • Work from one inbox. Smart Inbox gathers messages, comments, mentions and reviews from your connected networks into one feed.
  • Reply to reviews in place. The Reviews tab covers Google Business Profile, Facebook, Yelp, Tripadvisor, Trustpilot and others, with direct replies on Google and Facebook; Tripadvisor replies still happen on Tripadvisor.
  • Listen beyond your own pages. Social listening tracks sentiment and trends, and NewsWhip adds news and earned media.
  • Draft faster with AI. Trellis and AI Assist write replies and posts, summarize data and suggest tags.
  • Keep a team in sync. Approvals, message routing, task assignment and role-based permissions help several people share one account.

Pricing: Per seat per month, billed annually: Standard $199, Professional $299 and Advanced $399, with Enterprise priced on request. Month-to-month billing costs more, and there's a 30-day free trial.
