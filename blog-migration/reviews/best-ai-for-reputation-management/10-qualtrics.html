10. Qualtrics

Category: Enterprise experience management and predictive CX analytics

Qualtrics is a privately held experience management (XM) platform for customer, employee and brand research. Reputation work happens through its online reputation management module, location CX dashboards and AI Experience Agents. It fits big restaurant groups that employ their own insights or customer experience staff.

Notable customers: KFC and Shake Shack.

What's new: At its X4 conference in March 2026, Qualtrics said its AI Experience Agents now have paying customers, and it previewed agents that recover service problems in real time, along with connectors to NICE, Salesforce and Genesys. Two months later, in May 2026, a $6.75 billion takeover of hospital-feedback specialist Press Ganey Forsta was wrapped up. The healthcare-heavy deal also brought InMoment under the Qualtrics umbrella.

Key features:

  • Collect feedback after each visit. After a purchase, guests can get a survey through SMS, email, a QR code, your app, the web or chat.
  • Put reviews next to surveys. Online reputation management shows public reviews from Google and similar sites beside what guests said in surveys.
  • Match dashboards to the job. A restaurant manager, a market manager and an executive each see a location view built for their role.
  • Find themes in comments. AI text analytics groups open-ended comments and reviews by theme and sentiment.
  • Recover unhappy guests quickly. Experience Agents act on feedback as it comes in, including real-time service recovery.
  • Predict what moves satisfaction. Analytics point to the drivers of satisfaction and to customers at risk of churning.

Pricing: There's no public price list for the CX suite, so you'll get a figure from the Qualtrics sales team.
