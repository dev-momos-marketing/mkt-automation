10. Qualtrics

Qualtrics is an experience management (XM) platform that large companies use to study their customers, employees and brand. Its AI reads open-ended survey answers and public reviews, sorts them by theme and sentiment, and powers Experience Agents that act on what people say.

For reputation work, the AI earns its place in three jobs: drafting review replies in your brand's voice, spotting patterns across thousands of comments, and starting service recovery while a guest is still upset. At its X4 conference in March 2026, Qualtrics said these agents were already running for paying customers.

Key features

  • AI reply suggestions: The online reputation management module drafts responses that match your brand's voice, and managers send them from Qualtrics dashboards.
  • Theme and sentiment detection: Text analytics groups written comments and reviews into topics, so a problem building at one location surfaces early.
  • Experience Agents: AI agents respond to incoming feedback, for example by opening a real-time service recovery with an unhappy guest.
  • Predictive models: Analytics estimate which issues drive satisfaction and which customers look likely to churn.
  • Review aggregation: Ratings from Google, Yelp, Facebook and TripAdvisor land in the same dataset as your survey results.

Pros

  • AI across the whole program: Surveys, public reviews and follow-up actions share one platform, so your team doesn't stitch together separate AI tools.
  • Agents already in use: Experience Agents moved past the demo stage, with Qualtrics reporting paying customers on them in early 2026.
  • Solid user rating: Qualtrics Customer Experience holds 4.3/5 on G2 across 751 reviews.

Cons

  • Quote-only pricing: Each CX plan on the Qualtrics pricing page says "Request Pricing", and the cost is tied to the number of interactions.
  • No trial: The pricing page offers only a demo request, so seeing the AI handle your own guest data starts with a sales call.
  • Sized for enterprises: A brand without a CX or insights team may pay for research modules it never opens.

Price and plans

Qualtrics doesn't publish prices. Its CX plans, including one for location-based industries such as restaurants, are priced by interactions, and you get a quote after a demo.
