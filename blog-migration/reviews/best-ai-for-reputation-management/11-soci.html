11. SOCi

Category: Local marketing run by AI agents for franchise brands

Franchise and multi-location brands use SOCi to handle marketing at the local level. It runs reviews, listings, local search and social through brand-trained AI agents called Genius Agents, while the corporate team decides the rules they follow. The company has moved its website from meetsoci.com to soci.ai.

Notable customers: Sport Clips Haircuts, WOWorks (home to six restaurant brands), Lee's Famous Recipe Chicken, and On The Border Mexican Grill & Cantina.

What's new: SOCi's agent count kept climbing through 2026: from 300,000 in May to more than 412,000 by September, together completing over 27 million local marketing tasks a year. Sport Clips put over 3,400 Genius Agents to work on reviews and local search in June 2026, covering close to 1,800 salons, with people stepping in on sensitive reviews.

Key features:

  • Reply on brand at scale. Genius Reviews writes responses that follow your guidelines and passes sensitive reviews to staff.
  • Monitor sentiment by location. Review monitoring and reporting cover every store in your network.
  • Keep listings accurate. Listings management and local search optimization cover Google, Yelp, Bing and other directories.
  • Localize social posts. Central publishing allows edits for each location, and an AI social agent helps with the posting.
  • Ask guests directly. Surveys collect first-party feedback you can read next to public reviews.
  • Set rules centrally. Role-based controls let corporate decide what local managers can change.

Pricing: SOCi doesn't publish prices; quotes depend on your location count and the products you choose.
