11. SOCi

SOCi is a localized marketing platform for franchise and multi-location brands that hands most of the local work to AI. Its Genius Agents learn each brand's guidelines and handle review replies, listings and social posts for every location, while corporate decides how far they can act on their own.

If you want AI to carry the daily reputation work across a large network, SOCi is built for that job. By its own count, more than 412,000 of its agents were deployed by September 2026, and together they complete upward of 27 million localized jobs each year. The company also rebranded and moved from meetsoci.com to soci.ai.

Key features

  • Genius Reputation Agent: Answers every review in your brand voice without anyone logging in, and routes sensitive reviews to a person.
  • Voice of Customer: Sentiment reporting across locations shows which stores guests praise and which draw repeat complaints.
  • Review solicitation: The reputation agent can also ask recent guests for reviews and run customer surveys.
  • Local search agent: A second agent keeps hours, menus and delivery links accurate on Google, Apple Maps, Yelp, Facebook and delivery apps.
  • Social agent: The Genius Social Agent writes local posts and responds to comments in your voice.
  • Corporate guardrails: Role-based permissions decide what the agents and local managers may change.

Pros

  • Hands-off replies: Scooter's Coffee says the reputation agent let its team set the tone and voice, step back and automate its replies.
  • People stay in the loop: Sport Clips runs more than 3,400 agents, and its store owners keep oversight while delicate reviews go to staff.
  • No per-seat charges: Pricing follows locations and agents, so adding local managers or an agency doesn't raise the bill.
  • Strong rating: SOCi holds a 4.5/5 rating on G2.

Cons

  • No public pricing: You need a demo and a custom quote before you know what the agents will cost.
  • No free trial: There's no way to test the AI on your own reviews before you sign.
  • Large-network focus: SOCi is designed for brands with hundreds or thousands of locations, so a single restaurant would use little of it.

Price and plans

SOCi doesn't publish prices and has no free trial. Each quote is based on your location count and the Genius Agents and skills you deploy, with no per-user or per-listing fees.
