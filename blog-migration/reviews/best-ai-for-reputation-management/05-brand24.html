5. Brand24

Category: Mention tracking and social listening

Brand24 listens for your restaurant's name wherever people talk online, from social posts and news articles to forums, podcasts, videos, blogs and review sites. AI scores the tone of each mention and warns you when volume suddenly climbs. It doesn't handle review replies, so restaurant teams usually run it next to a review platform.

Notable customers: Uber, Satoria Group (hotels) and Pasibus, a fast-casual restaurant chain in Poland.

What's new: Late in 2025, a new LLM Monitoring tab appeared in the Brand24 panel, reporting where Claude, Gemini and ChatGPT place your brand and what they say about it. During 2026 it also released an official ChatGPT app and an MCP server, so you can pull mentions, sentiment and competitor data into AI assistants.

Key features:

  • Hear about mentions fast. Coverage includes newsletters and podcasts as well as social networks, news, blogs, forums, video and review sites.
  • Read the mood. Each mention gets a positive, neutral or negative tag from AI, plus a read on the emotion behind it.
  • Spot a PR problem early. Volume and reach charts, paired with alerts, show when talk about your restaurant suddenly jumps.
  • Compare yourself with rivals. Share-of-voice reports show how often you come up next to competitors.
  • Ask questions in chat. Brand Assistant is a chat window where you type questions and get answers drawn from your mentions.
  • See what AI assistants say. LLM monitoring follows how AI tools rank and describe you; most plans need to buy it separately.

Pricing: Five published plans, cheaper when billed annually: Individual $249/month ($199 annually), Team $349 ($299), Pro $499 ($399), Business $699 ($599), and Enterprise from $1,499/month billed annually. You can test it for 14 days at no cost, though there's no permanent free tier. Data on Individual refreshes roughly twice a day, and Pro or higher plans update in real time.
