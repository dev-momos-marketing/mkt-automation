5. Brand24

Brand24 is an AI social listening tool that tracks where people mention your brand online, from social posts and news stories to forums, podcasts, videos and review sites. AI labels the sentiment and emotion of each mention, and a chat assistant answers questions about your data.

For restaurant marketers, its newest AI feature may matter most: an LLM Monitoring tab, added late in 2025, reports how ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude describe and rank your brand. Brand24 doesn't reply to reviews, so it works best next to a review management tool.

Key features

  • Mention tracking: Social networks, news sites, blogs, forums, podcasts, newsletters and video are all searched for your name.
  • AI sentiment and emotion: Every mention is tagged positive, negative or neutral, with a reading of the emotion behind it.
  • Brand Assistant: Type a plain-language question about your mentions and get a written answer drawn from them.
  • LLM Monitoring: A Brand Score, median position and AI share of voice show where AI assistants place you and which sources they cite.
  • ChatGPT app and MCP server: Pull mentions, sentiment, influencers and competitor data into ChatGPT, Claude and other assistants.

Pros

  • AI throughout: Tone scoring, a chat assistant and AI-answer tracking come in one product.
  • Fast data: Pro, Business and Enterprise update in real time, and Team refreshes every hour.
  • Easy to test: The 14-day trial needs no credit card.
  • Rated 4.6 on G2: The average comes from 332 reviews.

Cons

  • No review replies: It listens to conversations but has no way to answer reviews, so you'll still need a separate tool for that.
  • AI visibility costs extra: The pricing table marks the AI Visibility module as an add-on outside Enterprise.
  • Slower entry plan: Individual allows one user and three keywords and refreshes about every 12 hours.
  • No free plan: Once the trial ends, you need a paid subscription.

Price and plans

You can try Brand24 free for 14 days. Prices are per month, and annual billing lowers the rate.

  • Individual: $249 per month, or $199 per month billed annually.
  • Team: $349 per month, or $299 per month billed annually.
  • Pro: $499 per month, or $399 per month billed annually.
  • Business: $699 per month, or $599 per month billed annually.
  • Enterprise: From $1,499 per month, billed annually.
