8. Chatmeter

Chatmeter is a reputation, listings and local search platform for brands with many locations, now sold as Alchemer Reputation Management. Its AI publishes review replies under rules you set and answers questions about what guests are saying across reviews, social posts and surveys.

Alchemer, a survey and customer feedback company, bought Chatmeter in September 2025. The AI work since then has centered on automation with guardrails: AI Auto-Responder arrived in March 2026 and holds sensitive reviews back for a person before anything goes live.

Key features

  • AI Auto-Responder: Writes on-brand replies and publishes them for the reviews your rules allow, filtered by star rating, platform and review text.
  • Pulse Ai: Signals: You type a question in plain language and get an answer drawn from reviews, social posts, surveys and the photos guests attach.
  • Competitive Intelligence: Released in July 2025, it compares each location with nearby competitors and maps the results region by region.
  • Review management: Monitors and answers reviews from 100+ sites in a single dashboard, with bulk responses and suggested replies.
  • Listings and local pages: Keeps store details accurate across Google, Apple Maps and 70+ directories, and builds a search-friendly page for each location.

Pros

  • Automation with a safety net: Routine praise can be answered on its own, while a risky review, such as a food safety complaint, goes to your team first.
  • AI across all feedback: Pulse Ai drills into one location, a region or a single review site, which helps when you're tracing a sudden dip in ratings.
  • Surveys next to reviews: Since the Alchemer deal, public reviews and survey answers can be analyzed on the same platform.
  • Strong user rating: Alchemer Reputation Management, as G2 now lists it, holds 4.5 out of 5.

Cons

  • Quote-only pricing: Alchemer's public price list covers its survey licenses, and reputation management is priced after a demo.
  • No self-serve trial: You can't try the AI on your own reviews before a 30-minute demo with sales.
  • A brand in transition: The Chatmeter site now announces the move to Alchemer, so expect Alchemer's name on contracts and support.

Price and plans

Alchemer doesn't publish prices for Chatmeter. You book a demo and get a quote for your locations.
