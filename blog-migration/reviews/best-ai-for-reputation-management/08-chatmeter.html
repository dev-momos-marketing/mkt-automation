8. Chatmeter

Category: Reviews, listings and local SEO, now part of Alchemer

Multi-location brands use Chatmeter to handle reviews, listings and local search. Survey company Alchemer bought it in September 2025 and now markets it as Alchemer Reputation Management, part of its broader feedback platform. Restaurant chains and franchise brands remain a core audience.

Notable customers: Driven Brands, A&W and MR MIKES Steakhouse Casual.

What's new: In March 2026, Alchemer launched AI Auto-Responder, which writes and publishes replies in your brand voice. You decide which reviews qualify based on star rating, platform and wording, and anything sensitive goes to a staff member before a reply is posted. Two months before the sale, Chatmeter added Pulse Ai: Competitive Intelligence, which summarizes how competitors perform and maps regional results down to an individual store.

Key features:

  • Manage reviews from many sites. You can monitor and respond across 100+ review sites from one dashboard.
  • Automate replies with guardrails. AI Auto-Responder follows your rules on which reviews get automatic answers and flags the risky ones.
  • Ask why guests feel a certain way. Pulse Ai: Signals answers plain-language questions about reviews, social posts, surveys and the photos guests attach.
  • Include delivery feedback. Restaurant brands can bring in reviews from Uber Eats, DoorDash and Grubhub.
  • Compare against nearby competitors. Competitive Intelligence breaks the comparison down by location.
  • Track local search. Listings management and SEO tracking run for each location.

Pricing: Custom; contact Alchemer's sales team for a quote.
