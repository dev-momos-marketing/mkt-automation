4. Reputation.com

Category: Enterprise review, survey and listings platform

The company now goes by Reputation, though its site is still reputation.com. It's built for large multi-location brands and brings together reviews, surveys, listings, social and AI search visibility for restaurant, hospitality, automotive and healthcare groups that want central control with enterprise reporting.

Notable customers: Tortilla and Mitchells & Butlers.

What's new: May 2026 brought three launches: Reputation IQ, where you type questions about your feedback in everyday language; a wider version of AI Reputation Manager, which watches how Perplexity, Gemini and ChatGPT portray your restaurants; and AI Location Presence Insights. Those followed a set of AI-powered brand intelligence products announced in September 2025.

Key features:

  • Answer every site in one queue. Your team can read and reply to Tripadvisor, Google, Facebook, Yelp and other reviews without switching tools.
  • Ask right after the meal. A dine-in visit, a delivery or a POS transaction can automatically send the guest a review request by text, email or QR code.
  • Hear from guests privately too. Surveys sit next to public reviews, so you can compare what guests tell you with what they post.
  • Group feedback into themes. Text analytics files each comment under a topic like cleanliness or service, with sentiment scored per topic.
  • Check your AI search presence. Two dedicated tools report how assistants describe your brand and each location.

Pricing: Reputation doesn't publish prices, so you'll need a custom quote from its sales team.
