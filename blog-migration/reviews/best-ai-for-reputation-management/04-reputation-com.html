4. Reputation.com

Reputation (formerly Reputation.com) runs review management, surveys, listings and social for large multi-location brands, with a growing set of AI tools on top. Its entry plan already includes AI-written review responses, and higher tiers add tools that track your brand in AI search.

Its AI goes further than drafting replies. Reputation IQ, launched in May 2026, answers plain-English questions about your review and survey data, while the expanded AI Reputation Manager follows what ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini say about your restaurants.

Key features

  • AI responding: Draft replies to reviews from Google, Facebook and other connected sites in one inbox, so managers edit and approve in place of writing every answer.
  • Reputation IQ: Ask something like which locations get the most complaints about wait times, and get an answer drawn from your guest feedback.
  • AI Reputation Manager: Structures menus, hours, photos and attributes so your restaurants can appear in AI recommendations and "near me" searches.
  • Sentiment themes: Text analytics file each comment under a topic such as service or cleanliness and score how guests feel about it.
  • RepScore: An AI-driven score for every location and region shows where attention is needed first.
  • Triggered review requests: A dine-in visit, delivery or POS event can send the guest a request by SMS, email or QR code.

Pros

  • AI in the base plan: Rep Core already covers AI responding, review requests, listings and SEO.
  • Several AI tools on one dataset: Reply drafting, plain-English questions and AI search tracking all work from the same review and survey data.
  • Listed prices: The core plans show a price per location, which makes budgeting easier than with quote-only tools.
  • G2 rating: Reputation holds 4.5/5 on G2.

Cons

  • Premium sources cost extra: The pricing page puts Yelp and Tripadvisor among its Premium Sources, which is an add-on.
  • AI insights need a higher tier: AI Reputation Manager and Rep Score start on Rep Core + Pulse at $115 per location.
  • Per-location costs: At $80 to $150 per location each month, the bill grows with every restaurant you add.

Price and plans

Reputation publishes per-location monthly prices for its core plans and sells through its sales team; add-ons are quoted separately.

  • Rep Core: $80 per location, per month.
  • Rep Core + Pulse: $115 per location, per month.
  • Rep Core + Surveys: $150 per location, per month.
  • Enterprise: Custom pricing for 125+ locations.
