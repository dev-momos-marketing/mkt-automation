11. Pluspoint

Pluspoint is a London-based reputation and local marketing platform for businesses with several locations. It brings review requests and replies, listings, surveys, messaging campaigns and social posting into one tool, and it publishes a flat price per location.

Notable customers: Starfoods Group (Portugal, 60+ restaurants), Paprica Burger and SushiCafé Group.

What's new: Around May 2026, Pluspoint made its MCP connector generally available, so AI assistants such as Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini can read your reviews, audit listings and queue review requests. Social posting through the connector was announced as coming next.

Key features

  • Review requests on four channels. Ask guests for reviews by SMS, email, WhatsApp or QR code.
  • Inbox with AI suggestions. Google, Facebook and other reviews arrive in one place with suggested replies ready to edit.
  • Alerts for bad feedback. Real-time notifications let a manager respond to a negative comment quickly.
  • Listings management. Keep Google and Facebook profiles current, with a paid add-on that syncs to 200+ directories including Apple Maps, Yelp and TripAdvisor.
  • Surveys and campaigns. Run NPS and satisfaction surveys, send messaging campaigns and create AI-written posts for Google, Facebook and Instagram.
  • Analytics per location. Track sentiment and common keywords store by store.

Why businesses choose Pluspoint: Restaurant groups, including many in Europe, get reviews, listings and local marketing in one place at a price they can see before calling sales. The per-location model keeps costs predictable as you open new stores.

Pricing: $29 per location per month with every feature included, or about $24.17 when billed annually, with a minimum of 3 locations. That covers 3 users and 50 AI credits; extra users cost $19 and extra credits $0.49. The Listings Sync add-on is $39 per location per month, there's a 14-day free trial, and custom plans apply above 30 locations or users.
