11. Pluspoint

Pluspoint is a reputation and local marketing tool from London, made for brands that run several locations. Reviews, listings, surveys, messaging and social posting share one login, and the price per location is on its website.

That published price is the main reason it appears on lists of Birdeye alternatives: you can work out the cost for your whole group before talking to sales. Its newest addition, an MCP connector released on May 14, 2026, lets Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini read your reviews, audit listings and queue review requests.

Key features

  • Flexible review requests: Ask guests by SMS, WhatsApp, email or QR code, whichever fits how they ordered.
  • One review inbox: Reviews from Google, Facebook and other sites come in together, each with an AI-drafted reply ready to adjust.
  • Listings management: Google and Facebook profiles are included, and an add-on pushes details to 200+ directories including Apple Maps, Bing, Yelp and TripAdvisor.
  • Social scheduling: Posts go to Google, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn and X for every venue from one place.
  • AI assistant access: The MCP connector puts locations, reviews and review requests inside the assistant your team already uses.

Pros

  • Visible pricing: The per-location rate is public, which makes a cost comparison with your current tool straightforward.
  • No feature tiers: Every feature comes in the single plan, so smaller locations get the same tools as flagships.
  • Low-friction trial: You can test it for 14 days without entering a card.
  • Early AI-assistant support: MCP access went live in May 2026 for Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini.

Cons

  • Minimum of three locations: A single-site restaurant can't subscribe on its own.
  • Paid directory sync: The Listings Sync add-on costs $39 per location per month, billed annually.
  • Drafts only through MCP: Assistants can draft review replies through the connector but can't publish them yet.
  • Quotes for large groups: Above 30 locations or 30 users, the list price gives way to a custom plan.

Price and plans

Pluspoint has one plan with every feature, billed per location, and a 14-day free trial that needs no payment details. Annual billing charges 10 months for 12.

  • Pluspoint: $29 per location, per month ($24.17 billed annually), minimum 3 locations, with 3 users and 50 AI credits a month.
  • Listings Sync add-on: $39 per location, per month, billed annually.
  • Extra users and credits: $19 per user, per month; $0.49 per AI credit.
  • Custom: A quote above 30 locations or 30 users.
