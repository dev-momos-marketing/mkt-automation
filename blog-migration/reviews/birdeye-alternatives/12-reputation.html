12. Reputation

Reputation sells an enterprise reputation platform to large multi-location brands, covering reviews, listings, surveys, social and AI search visibility. The company was called Reputation.com until its rebrand and still runs on that domain.

It serves healthcare, automotive, real estate and retail brands as well as restaurants, which suits groups with mixed portfolios. Its per-location plans are published, which makes a first cost comparison with Birdeye easier.

Key features

  • Review inbox: Your team reads and responds to Google, Yelp, Tripadvisor and Facebook reviews without leaving the platform, with AI responding on every plan.
  • Review requests: A POS transaction, delivery order or dine-in check can trigger an invite by SMS, email or QR code.
  • Surveys: One basic template on the Pulse plan, or six advanced templates with email requests on the Surveys plan.
  • Listings: Business listings and local SEO for every location come with all three published plans.
  • AI search visibility: AI Reputation Manager reports how Perplexity, Gemini and ChatGPT describe your brand, and Reputation IQ answers typed questions about your data.

Pros

  • Mixed portfolios: One platform covers restaurant, hospitality, automotive and healthcare locations.
  • Published prices: The three standard plans are listed on the site, starting at $80 per location per month.
  • Steady AI releases: Brand intelligence products arrived in September 2025, and Reputation IQ and AI Location Presence Insights followed in May 2026.
  • High G2 score: Reputation averages 4.5 on G2 across 2,470 user reviews.

Cons

  • Per-location bill: Paying $80 to $150 per location each month gets expensive for chains with hundreds of units.
  • Survey depth costs extra: Full survey features need the $150 Rep Core + Surveys plan.
  • Add-ons priced separately: Social Suite, ticketing and competitive insights are quoted on request.
  • No trial: You request a demo to see it.

Price and plans

Reputation publishes three plans, priced per location, per month; Enterprise is custom.

  • Rep Core: $80 per location, per month.
  • Rep Core + Pulse: $115 per location, per month.
  • Rep Core + Surveys: $150 per location, per month.
  • Enterprise: Custom pricing for 125+ locations.
