12. Reputation

Reputation, the company long known as Reputation.com, sells to large chains that need one system for reviews, listings, guest surveys and social. Newer modules track what AI search assistants tell people about each of your locations.

Notable customers: Tortilla and Mitchells & Butlers.

What's new: Three AI releases arrived in May 2026. Reputation IQ answers questions you type about your feedback, the AI Reputation Manager now reports how Perplexity, Gemini and ChatGPT describe your brand, and AI Location Presence Insights rounds out the set. A batch of brand intelligence products came earlier, in September 2025.

Key features

  • One inbox for reviews. Your team can read and respond to Google, Yelp, Tripadvisor and Facebook reviews without leaving the platform.
  • Requests tied to restaurant events. A POS transaction, delivery order or dine-in check can trigger an invite sent by SMS, email or QR code.
  • Surveys beside reviews. Private feedback and public ratings sit together so you can compare them.
  • Themes from text analytics. Sentiment scoring sorts what guests write into buckets like cleanliness and service.
  • Listings and social. Manage location listings and run social listening and publishing for every store.
  • AI search visibility. See how AI assistants talk about your locations and ask your feedback data questions with Reputation IQ.

Why businesses choose Reputation: Large brands in restaurants, hospitality, automotive and healthcare use it to run reviews, surveys and listings centrally with enterprise reporting. Its newer AI search tools help if you want to know what guests see when they ask a chatbot about you.

Pricing: No public price list exists; Reputation prices each deal individually through its sales team.
