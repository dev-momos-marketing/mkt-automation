6. NiceJob

Paystone has owned NiceJob since 2021, and the software is aimed at small service companies that want more reviews and referrals with little effort. It connects to job and invoicing tools and sends review requests after each completed job, then mostly runs on its own. Restaurants aren't one of its stated markets, so it fits best if you run a single store and want simple review collection.

Notable customers: Evergreen Cleaning and You Move Me.

What's new: Since October 2025, a tie-up with the direct-mail company SendJim has let trades and other home-service firms put verified 5-star reviews on mailed postcards.

Key features

  • Automatic review requests. Customers get an email or text asking for a review, with reminders if they don't respond.
  • Connections to business tools. It plugs into Jobber, Housecall Pro, QuickBooks and Zapier to know when to send a request.
  • AI-written replies. On the Pro plan, AI drafts review responses with the tone, length and rules you choose, and can post them automatically.
  • Website review widgets. Show your latest ratings on your own site as social proof.
  • Referral and repeat campaigns. Pro also adds referral programs, rebooking campaigns and marketing broadcasts to bring past customers back.

Why businesses choose NiceJob: Owners like how quickly it's set up and how little attention it needs afterward. Since its integrations center on home-service software, a restaurant may need Zapier to trigger requests from its own systems.

Pricing: The entry plan, Grow, is $75/month when your business handles up to 2,500 customers in a year, rising to $95/month between that and 5,000. Pro, from $125/month, brings AI review replies, referral and repeat campaigns, and broadcasts. You can test it free for 14 days, and nothing locks you into a contract.
