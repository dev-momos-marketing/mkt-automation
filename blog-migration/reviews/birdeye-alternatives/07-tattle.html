7. Tattle

Tattle is an independent CX platform made only for restaurants, and it measures how guests felt about a visit. Survey invites go out on their own once a guest pays or picks up, public reviews are monitored next to them, and AI points every store manager at the change likely to move scores most.

Where Birdeye covers reviews, listings, messaging and social for multi-location brands in general, Tattle goes deep on structured guest feedback for restaurants. Its customers include Kung Fu Tea, Red Robin, Hooters and Chili's parent Brinker International.

Key features

  • Automatic invites: Surveys go out through integrations with Toast, Olo, Punchh, Paytronix, Thanx and Square, or by SMS, email and QR code.
  • Operations mapping: Each comment is linked to a part of the operation, from order accuracy and wait times to the food and how staff treated the guest.
  • Ranked recommendations: Each location gets the improvement expected to have the biggest effect on its guest scores.
  • Review monitoring: Google, Yelp, Facebook and TripAdvisor reviews appear beside your survey results.
  • Side-by-side stores: Trend charts compare locations on top box score and on how many unhappy guests were recovered.

Pros

  • Deep restaurant focus: Every survey category and integration is shaped around restaurant service.
  • A next step for each store: AI Coach turns survey data into a single action per location, with a goal for the month.
  • Prices you can see: Tattle lists its per-location rates, so you can estimate the cost before a demo.
  • Help getting started: White-glove onboarding, with integration setup and team training, comes with every plan.

Cons

  • Narrower scope than Birdeye: Its focus is guest surveys and review tracking, which leaves listings, guest messaging and social posting to other tools.
  • Reputation add-on: To reply to and build reviews on every platform, you buy Reputation Management, which is priced separately.
  • Not for single sites: Published plans begin at 5 locations.

Price and plans

Tattle publishes per-location monthly prices that fall as you add locations, with custom pricing for the largest brands.

  • 5 to 10 units: $125 per location, per month.
  • 11 to 20 units: $100 per location, per month.
  • 21 to 50 units: $85 per location, per month.
  • 51 to 200 units: $65 per location, per month.
  • 201+ units: Custom pricing.
