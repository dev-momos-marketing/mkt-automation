7. Tattle

Tattle is a restaurant-only CX platform, still independently run, that measures how guests felt about a visit. Survey invites go out automatically once a guest pays or picks up, public reviews are monitored alongside them, and AI points every store manager at the change likely to move scores the most.

Notable customers: Hooters, Red Robin, Brinker International (Chili's) and Kung Fu Tea.

What's new: Tattle closed a $5 million round in August 2025 to grow its AI features, and in January 2026 the bubble tea chain Kung Fu Tea signed on to measure guest sentiment across its channels. Tattle has also released a mobile app for GMs and franchisees, with alerts and AI Coach action items.

Key features

  • Surveys after every order. Post-transaction surveys go out automatically through integrations with Toast, Olo, Punchh, Paytronix, Thanx, Square and similar tools.
  • Feedback mapped to operations. Each comment is linked to a part of the operation, for example order accuracy, wait times, the food itself or how staff treated the guest.
  • Ranked AI recommendations. Each location gets the improvement expected to have the biggest effect on guest scores.
  • Review monitoring. Google and Yelp reviews appear beside your survey results.
  • Location comparisons. Trend charts put stores side by side on measures like top box score and how many unhappy guests were recovered.
  • Recovery workflows. Follow up with unhappy guests before they decide not to return.

Why businesses choose Tattle: Its focus on structured survey data gives managers a clear list of what to work on at each store. Keep in mind that its core strength is surveys and monitoring, so check how well review replies work for you during a demo.

Pricing: Tattle doesn't share pricing publicly; ask the company for a quote.
