4. Qualtrics

Qualtrics is an enterprise experience management (XM) platform that large companies use for customer, employee and brand research. For restaurant chains, it combines post-visit surveys, an online reputation module and location dashboards, and it now adds AI agents that act on what guests say.

It reaches well past review management, so it suits groups with a dedicated insights or CX team. If you mainly want to answer Google and Yelp reviews across a few dozen stores, expect to use a small slice of what you'd be paying for.

Notable customers: KFC and Shake Shack.

What's new: In March 2026, at its X4 conference, Qualtrics said its AI Experience Agents were live with paying customers and announced agents for real-time service recovery. In May 2026, it closed its $6.75 billion purchase of Press Ganey Forsta, which mostly expands its healthcare business and leaves the restaurant product as it was.

Key features

  • Surveys on every channel. Collect post-transaction feedback by email, SMS, web, app, QR code and chat.
  • Reviews next to survey data. The online reputation module brings in public reviews from Google and similar sites so you can read them beside private feedback.
  • Dashboards for each role. Restaurant managers, market managers and executives each get a location CX view scoped to what they run.
  • Themes from open comments. AI text and sentiment analytics group written comments and reviews into topics you can track.
  • Agents that follow up. Experience Agents take action on feedback, such as starting service recovery with an upset guest in real time.
  • Links to your other systems. Connectors feed data to and from Genesys, NICE, Salesforce and other contact-center or CRM tools.

Why businesses choose Qualtrics: Large brands can run guest surveys, brand tracking and employee research in one program, with predictive analytics on what drives satisfaction and churn. That breadth pays off when several departments share the same data; a regional restaurant group may find it heavier than it needs.

Pricing: Expect a custom quote; the CX suite has no public price list.
