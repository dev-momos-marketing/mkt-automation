4. Qualtrics

Qualtrics is a research-grade experience management (XM) platform that enterprises use for customer, employee and brand studies. For restaurant chains, it combines post-visit surveys, an online reputation module, location dashboards and AI agents that follow up on what guests say.

It makes sense as a Birdeye alternative when your priority is analyzing guest feedback in depth. If you mainly want to answer Google and Yelp reviews across a few dozen stores, expect to use a small share of what you'd pay for.

Key features

  • Surveys on every channel: Post-transaction feedback comes in by email, SMS, web, app, QR code and chat.
  • Reviews beside private feedback: The reputation module brings in Google, Yelp, Facebook and TripAdvisor reviews so you can read them next to survey answers.
  • Location CX dashboards: Restaurant managers, market managers and executives each get a view scoped to what they run.
  • Agents that follow up: Experience Agents take action on feedback, such as reaching out to an upset guest in real time.
  • Links to other systems: Connectors exchange data with Genesys, NICE, Salesforce and other contact-center or CRM tools.

Pros

  • Analysis first: Text analytics and predictive models explain why scores move, which goes further than tracking star ratings.
  • One program for several teams: Guest surveys, brand tracking and employee research can share data across departments.
  • Well rated: G2 users score Qualtrics Customer Experience 4.3/5 over 751 reviews.

Cons

  • Opaque cost: No CX plan shows a price, and quotes are based on interactions, so comparing costs with other tools takes a sales call.
  • Heavier than it looks: A regional group switching for review management alone may find the platform more complex than it needs.
  • No trial period: Qualtrics lists no free trial or free plan, so a side-by-side test means arranging a demo.

Price and plans

Qualtrics doesn't publish prices. Its CX plans, including one for location-based industries such as restaurants, are priced by interactions, and you get a quote after a demo.
