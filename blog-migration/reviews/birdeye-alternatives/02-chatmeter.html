2. Chatmeter

Chatmeter built its name helping chains manage reviews, business listings and local search rankings, and it now belongs to Alchemer, a survey and feedback company that bought it in September 2025. It's sold today as Alchemer Reputation Management, part of a multi-location business intelligence platform that pairs public reviews with survey data.

Notable customers: Driven Brands, A&W and MR MIKES Steakhouse Casual.

What's new: In March 2026, Alchemer added AI Auto-Responder, which writes and posts on-brand replies based on rules for star rating, site and review text, and holds back delicate reviews until someone on your team has looked at them. Before the sale, in July 2025, it introduced Pulse Ai: Competitive Intelligence, which maps nearby rivals region by region.

Key features

  • Manage reviews from 100+ sites. One dashboard pulls in reviews across your locations so managers can read and respond without switching tabs.
  • Automate routine replies. AI Auto-Responder follows rules you set and flags risky reviews for a human before anything is posted.
  • Ask questions in plain language. Pulse Ai: Signals runs sentiment analysis on reviews, social posts, surveys and even review photos when you type a question.
  • Benchmark against competitors. Competitive Intelligence compares each store with the restaurants around it.
  • Keep listings and local SEO in order. Update business details and track search visibility per location.
  • Include delivery app feedback. Reviews from Uber Eats, DoorDash and Grubhub sit next to Google and Yelp for restaurant brands.

Why businesses choose Chatmeter: Brands with dozens of locations like how it keeps listings and local search consistent everywhere. Since the Alchemer deal, you can also run guest surveys on the same platform, which helps if you want private feedback next to public reviews.

Pricing: Alchemer keeps Reputation Management pricing private and builds each quote around your needs.
