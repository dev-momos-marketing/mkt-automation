2. Chatmeter

Chatmeter made its name helping chains manage reviews, business listings and local search rankings. Alchemer, a survey and feedback company, bought it in September 2025, and it's now sold as Alchemer Reputation Management, part of a multi-location business intelligence platform that pairs public reviews with survey data.

If you're weighing it against Birdeye, the difference is focus. Chatmeter centers on reviews, listings, location pages and competitor benchmarks, while Birdeye also covers customer messaging and social.

Key features

  • 100+ review sites: One dashboard pulls in reviews across your locations so managers can read and respond without switching tabs.
  • AI Auto-Responder: Posts on-brand replies based on rules for star rating, site and review text, and holds back delicate reviews until someone on your team has looked at them.
  • Plain-language questions: Pulse Ai: Signals runs sentiment analysis on reviews, social posts, surveys and review photos when you type a question.
  • Competitive Intelligence: Introduced in July 2025, it maps nearby rivals and compares each store with its neighbors.
  • Listings and Local Pages: Update business details across 70+ directories and publish a landing page for every location.

Pros

  • Deep location analytics: Analytics Studio dashboards break results down by store and region, and connect to Tableau or Domo.
  • Surveys from the same vendor: Alchemer's survey tools give you private feedback next to public reviews.
  • Consistent listings: Brands with dozens of locations can keep hours and details aligned everywhere from one screen.
  • User rating: On G2, Alchemer Reputation Management scores 4.5 out of 5.

Cons

  • Pricing behind a demo: Like Birdeye, Chatmeter doesn't publish prices, so comparing costs means talking to both sales teams.
  • Narrower than an all-in-one suite: The product page lists reviews, listings, local pages and analytics, with no texting or webchat for customer messages.
  • Changing brand: The product now carries Alchemer's name, and chatmeter.com points visitors to Alchemer.

Price and plans

Alchemer keeps Reputation Management pricing private and builds each quote after a demo.
