10. Yext

Yext is a publicly traded company (NYSE: YEXT) whose platform keeps listings, local pages and reviews for multi-location brands running from one central knowledge base. Compared with Birdeye, its strength is listings accuracy and search visibility, including in AI assistants, with review monitoring and replies built in alongside.

Notable customers: Taco Bell, Arby's, Bojangles and Marston's.

What's new: Yext bought the local search intelligence firm Places Scout in February 2025 and launched Yext Scout the next month, an agent that tracks how your brand appears and is discussed on Google, Bing, ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity. In April 2026, Popmenu widened its partnership with Yext so restaurants can sync menus and listings and answer reviews on 70+ platforms.

Key features

  • Knowledge Graph. Each location's hours, menus and details live in one record that feeds everything else.
  • Listings sync. Changes go out to Google, Apple Maps, Bing, Facebook and other directories and apps.
  • Review response with AI. Monitor review sites and reply using AI-drafted responses.
  • Review generation. AI campaigns ask customers for reviews, including on third-party sites.
  • Scout visibility tracking. Compare your presence and sentiment in classic and AI search against competitors.
  • Franchise permissions. Approval workflows control who can change what across a large network.

Why businesses choose Yext: Brands with hundreds of locations pick it when the bigger risk is wrong hours or menus across dozens of directories, and they still want reviews handled in the same system.

Pricing: Yext quotes larger chains individually. Some review sites mention cheaper self-serve plans with figures that conflict, so get a quote from Yext directly.
