10. Yext

Yext is a publicly traded company (NYSE: YEXT) whose platform runs listings, local landing pages and reviews for multi-location brands from one central knowledge base. Compared with Birdeye, its center of gravity is listings accuracy and search visibility, including in AI assistants, with review monitoring and replies built in.

Yext bought the local search intelligence firm Places Scout in February 2025 and launched Yext Scout the following month. Scout follows how your brand shows up on Google, Bing, ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity, and how competitors compare.

Key features

  • Single source of truth: Each location's hours, menus and details sit in the Knowledge Graph and flow out to Google, Apple Maps, Bing, Facebook and other directories.
  • Monitoring across many sites: Google, Facebook and Yelp reviews come in with those from 80+ industry sites in a single dashboard.
  • Reply rules: AI prepares responses for approval, and you can let simple ones publish without a manual step.
  • Guest review invitations: Email, text and QR code requests, routed toward the sites with the most weight in search results.
  • Competitive intelligence: Scout compares your visibility and sentiment with competitors in traditional and AI search.
  • Yext Pages: Location landing pages designed for local search.

Pros

  • Listings depth: If wrong hours or menus across directories are your bigger risk, listings are the core of what Yext sells.
  • Competitor benchmarks: The Places Scout purchase and Scout give you a view of rivals in local and AI search.
  • Enterprise governance: Audit trails, role-based access and approvals suit large franchise networks.
  • User ratings: Yext's average rating on G2 stands at 4.4 out of 5.

Cons

  • No public pricing: Every brand gets its own quote, so budgeting starts with a sales call.
  • No standard trial: Yext offers neither a free tier nor a trial, and recommends a demo with case studies and references.
  • Reseller route for small businesses: Smaller companies are sent to Yext's reseller partners to buy.

Price and plans

Yext doesn't publish prices; larger brands get a custom quote after a demo, priced by location count and solution and usually billed annually.
