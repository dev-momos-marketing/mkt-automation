6. Malou

Malou is a restaurant-specific marketing platform from Paris that covers much of the same ground as Birdeye: AI review replies, listings and local SEO, social media and visibility in AI search. Where Birdeye serves many industries, Malou is designed around restaurant groups with three or more locations, and you can choose between running it yourself (MalouApp) or working with an assigned marketing expert (Copilot).

Notable customers: Krispy Kreme, Tudor Steak House NYC, Riviera Dining Group and MELT Oberkampf.

What's new: There's no dated Malou product update on record for 2025 or 2026.

Key features

  • AI review responses. Replies match the tone and sentiment of each review and follow your brand's voice guidelines.
  • Quality checks on replies. A score and tips based on Google's best practices help managers write better answers.
  • Alerts on serious complaints. Mentions of hygiene, discrimination and similar topics are flagged right away.
  • Listings and local SEO. Keep Google and other platforms accurate for every restaurant from one dashboard.
  • Social content. Create and schedule posts for each location.
  • Four languages. The platform runs in English, French, Italian and Spanish, which helps groups operating across Europe.

Why businesses choose Malou: Restaurant groups get a tool shaped around their own reviews and local search, with the option of a human expert doing the marketing work if the team is stretched.

Pricing: Custom quotes only; Malou has no public price list.
