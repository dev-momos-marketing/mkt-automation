6. Malou

Malou is a restaurant-specific marketing platform from Paris that covers much of the ground Birdeye does: AI review replies, listings and local SEO, social media and visibility in AI search. Birdeye serves multi-location brands in many industries, while Malou is designed around restaurant groups.

The other difference is service. Alongside the self-serve MalouApp, Malou offers Copilot, where an assigned expert plans your local SEO, produces social content and sends recommendations each month. Krispy Kreme and Riviera Dining Group are among its customers.

Key features

  • AI review responses: Replies match the sentiment of each review and stick to your brand's voice guidelines.
  • Quality checks: A score and tips based on Google's best practices help managers sharpen their answers.
  • Listings and local SEO: Presence management covers Google and 5 key local platforms on Essential, and 50+ platforms on Growth and Full.
  • Social media: The Full plan adds social media management, so posts for each location are planned in the same tool.
  • Google menu sync: Full plan customers can sync and publish their menus on Google.

Pros

  • Restaurant focus: A narrower product than Birdeye, centered on the reviews and local search that restaurants depend on.
  • Managed service option: Copilot means a marketing expert can do the work when your team is stretched.
  • European languages: The platform runs in English, French, Italian and Spanish, useful for groups operating across Europe.
  • AI connections: Growth and Full plans include MCP access, so AI assistants can connect to Malou.

Cons

  • Pricing on request: Like Birdeye, Malou doesn't publish prices.
  • Top-tier features: Social media and menu sync need the Full plan.
  • Less third-party feedback: MalouApp has one review on G2, against thousands for Birdeye.

Price and plans

Malou has no public price list. Essential, Growth and Full are quoted by the number of locations you manage.
