5. ReviewTrackers

ReviewTrackers helps chains and franchises monitor, answer and analyze reviews from one account. It concentrates on the review side of reputation: listings management and local search rank are sold as add-ons.

That focus can make it a simpler switch if reviews are the main job you need covered across locations. Through InMoment, its owner since 2022, it has been part of Press Ganey Forsta since May 2025, and Chem-Dry and PACK & SEND appear in its case studies.

Key features

  • A single review feed: Ratings from Google, Facebook, TripAdvisor and other platforms show up together, with an alert for each new one.
  • Brand-approved replies: Smart Response gives local teams ready answers they can post in a few clicks.
  • AI reply drafts: AI Auto Responding writes answers from prompts saved by your brand.
  • Requests by email and text: Recent customers are asked for a review automatically to keep ratings current.
  • Themes and rivals: The Essential plan adds experience analysis to find recurring complaints and competitor insights to benchmark nearby businesses.

Pros

  • Focused scope: You pay for review monitoring, replies and reporting, and add listings only if you need them.
  • Unlimited seats: There's no user limit on Starter, which helps when many store managers reply.
  • Fits InMoment users: Brands already on InMoment or Press Ganey Forsta can keep review data with their wider customer experience work.
  • User rating: On G2, ReviewTrackers scores 4.6 out of 5 from 161 reviews.

Cons

  • Analysis needs Essential: Starter leaves out experience analysis and competitor insights, so finding themes and benchmarking rivals means the higher plan.
  • Yearly terms: Every plan is paid annually per location.
  • No price list: Costs are shared only after a meeting.

Price and plans

ReviewTrackers prices per location, per month, paid annually, and doesn't publish its rates; you book a meeting to get a quote for Data Only, Starter or Essential.
