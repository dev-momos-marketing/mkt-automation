5. ReviewTrackers

ReviewTrackers helps chains and franchises monitor, answer and analyze their reviews from one account. Its ownership has changed twice: the CX company InMoment acquired it in 2022, then Press Ganey Forsta took over InMoment in May 2025. You can still sign up for ReviewTrackers under its own brand and plans.

Notable customers: Ted's Montana Grill, PACK & SEND and Chem-Dry.

What's new: In July 2025, InMoment launched AI Auto Responding for its review management solution, so generative AI can draft tailored answers using prompts your brand saves ahead of time, on two plans called Locations Starter and Locations Essential. The announcement doesn't name ReviewTrackers directly, although those plan names match its tiers.

Key features

  • A single review feed. Your Google, Facebook and TripAdvisor ratings, plus those from other platforms, show up together, with alerts whenever a new one posts.
  • Approved reply templates. Smart Response gives local managers brand-approved answers they can send in a few clicks.
  • Review requests by email and text. Ask recent customers for a review automatically to keep fresh ratings coming in.
  • Competitor tracking. Compare your ratings and review volume with nearby rivals.
  • Topic and sentiment analytics. Find complaints that keep recurring across stores so your operations team knows where to look.
  • Access that mirrors your franchise. Location-level reports and role-based permissions let owners, area leaders and head office each see their own slice.

Why businesses choose ReviewTrackers: Teams describe it as easy to pick up, and it covers the core review tasks without much setup. It's an especially natural fit if your company already runs InMoment or Press Ganey Forsta for wider customer experience work.

Pricing: ReviewTrackers prices per location and asks you to book a meeting to get a quote.
