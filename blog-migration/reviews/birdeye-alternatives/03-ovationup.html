3. OvationUp

Ovation, found at ovationup.com, is guest feedback and reputation software made only for multi-unit restaurants. It starts with a two-question text after each order, flags bad experiences to managers as they happen, and handles public reviews, listings and social from one dashboard.

Among Birdeye alternatives, it's the narrower choice: everything is designed around restaurant orders, POS data and restaurant feedback themes. It also appears in our guide to restaurant feedback management software.

Key features

  • Text surveys after every order: POS and ordering integrations, or a QR code on the table, trigger two quick questions to collect guest feedback.
  • Manager alerts: Negative responses notify the right location so someone can reach out and win the guest back.
  • Review replies: Google, Yelp, Facebook and other reviews arrive in one inbox, with AI-suggested responses you edit before posting.
  • Listings and social: Business details stay consistent across 50+ directories, and you can post to social from the same screen.
  • Competitor benchmarks: Competitive Pulse compares each location with up to five nearby competitors.

Pros

  • Restaurant depth: Themes such as food quality, speed and cleanliness, plus menu-level reports, come ready for restaurant teams.
  • Private feedback first: Hearing from guests by text gives you a chance to fix problems before they appear in public reviews.
  • Consistent tone: Brand Voice, added in May 2026, applies your tone instructions to winback texts and review replies.
  • Close to its customers: About a dozen restaurant brands that use Ovation invested $3 million in February 2026, bringing total funding to $12 million.

Cons

  • Restaurants only: Groups that also run non-restaurant businesses will need another platform for those locations.
  • Quote-based pricing: There's no public price list, so you'll need a demo or chat to compare it with Birdeye on cost.
  • Younger review module: Public review and listings tools joined the product in late 2025.

Price and plans

Ovation quotes each account based on its physical locations and ghost kitchens, with discounts for volume, and doesn't publish a price list.
