3. OvationUp

Ovation, sold at ovationup.com, is restaurant guest feedback and reputation software for multi-unit brands. Its center is a two-question text survey that reaches guests right after an order, so a manager hears about a bad meal while there's still time to fix it. Since late 2025 it has also handled public reviews and listings.

Notable customers: Dave's Hot Chicken, Curry Up Now, Bareburger and Taziki's Mediterranean Cafe.

What's new: In May 2026, Ovation's spring release brought Brand Voice, which applies your tone instructions to winback texts and review replies, and a report that links guest sentiment to individual menu items. In February 2026, it raised $3 million from about a dozen of its own restaurant customers, taking total funding to $12 million.

Key features

  • Two-question SMS surveys. Surveys go out automatically after an order through POS and ordering integrations, or from a QR code on the table, to collect guest feedback.
  • Instant alerts on unhappy guests. Managers get notified about negative responses so they can reach out and win the guest back.
  • One inbox for public reviews. Google, Yelp, Facebook and other reviews can be filtered by site, rating and store, with AI-suggested replies you edit before sending.
  • Listings across 50+ directories. Hours and details stay consistent, and you can post to social from the same screen.
  • Feedback sorted into restaurant themes. AI groups written comments into topics such as food quality, speed and cleanliness.
  • Competitor benchmarks. Competitive Pulse compares each location with up to five nearby competitors.

Why businesses choose Ovation: Guests answer two quick questions by text far more readily than a long email survey, so you hear from more of them. Operators running online ordering across many stores value how fast it gets a complaint in front of the right manager.

Pricing: Ovation quotes each account based on its physical locations and ghost kitchens, with discounts for volume, and doesn't publish a price list.
