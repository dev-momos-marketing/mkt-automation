10. Podium

Podium helps local businesses win and keep customers over text, using text payments, requests for reviews and an AI Employee that follows up on leads and schedules appointments. Lately it has packaged these into AI operating systems for particular industries, namely car dealers, home services and medspas. Restaurants sit outside the markets it targets today.

Notable customers: Jackson Hewitt, Mountaineer Heating & Cooling, Mattress Firm and Ashley Furniture HomeStore.

What's new: By its own count, Podium's AI agents crossed $150 million in yearly recurring revenue in October 2026. A year earlier, in December 2025, came Jerry 2.0, an AI employee that car shops, HVAC firms and medspas can tailor to their workflows.

Key features

  • AI Employee. It replies to leads by text, phone, email and social, answers questions and books appointments after hours.
  • Voice AI for calls. An agent picks up inbound phone calls when your staff can't.
  • Text review invitations. Customers get a link by text that takes them to Google or another review site.
  • Shared inbox. Texts, webchat, Google messages and social messages come into one queue for your team.
  • Payments in the conversation. Send a text-to-pay link and collect money inside the same thread.
  • Bulk text campaigns. Send promotions or win-back messages to customer lists, with reporting across locations.

Why businesses choose Podium: Businesses that sell by phone and text like having messaging, reviews and payments run by one vendor. For a restaurant, the appointment booking and lead follow-up matter less, so weigh how much of the product you'd use.

Pricing: You'll need a sales conversation to get numbers for the Core, Pro or Signature plan, with the AI Employee sold as an optional add-on to any tier.
