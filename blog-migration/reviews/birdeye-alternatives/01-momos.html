1. Momos

Momos is an AI guest experience platform made for multi-location restaurant brands, and it covers the reputation management work that most teams look to Birdeye for. Reviews, guest messages, surveys and recovery offers from every store sit in one workspace, where an AI agent drafts and sends the replies.

Notable customers: Potbelly, Caribou Coffee, Condado Tacos and Papa Murphy's.

What's new: During 2025, Momos released mobile-first reports, AI copilots for staff, automatic summaries of each ticket and AI-enforced brand guidelines, along with new integrations for Paytronix, Square and Otter. That same year, Potbelly moved reviews, cases and guest messages from its 472 locations into a single Momos view.

What you can do with Momos:

  • Bring all feedback together. Google ratings, app store comments, social DMs, survey answers and POS data for each store land in the Unified Inbox, where AI triage tags and routes them.
  • Answer guests with Alfie. Alfie, the Momos Experience Agent, writes replies within minutes, trained on your brand voice and aware of each guest's past visits and preferences.
  • Turn complaints into return visits. When a guest had a poor visit, Alfie can hand out loyalty points, a coupon or an offer pulled from the loyalty program you already run.
  • Cover chat and delivery apps too. The same agent handles customer support on website chat, in-app support, surveys and delivery apps, handing hard cases to your team.
  • Spot problems by location. Dashboards reveal which topics push ratings up or down, where incidents cluster and how satisfaction changes over time, so you can improve guest experience store by store.
  • Handle multilingual reviews. Built-in live translation means each store can answer a review in whatever language the guest used.

Every review from surveys, Yelp, Instagram and Google lands in the same inbox for your team to work through.

Alfie picks up each review and posts a personal, on-brand reply, and you can shape his tone by uploading brand guidelines or a library of approved responses.
