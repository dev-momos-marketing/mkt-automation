1. Momos

Momos is an AI guest experience platform made only for restaurant and hospitality brands with several locations. Reviews, guest messages, surveys and recovery offers for every store sit in one workspace, where an AI agent writes and sends the replies.

Where Birdeye serves multi-location businesses in many industries, Momos covers the same reputation management work with restaurant tools such as loyalty offers and delivery-app feedback. Potbelly moved its feedback and escalations from a previous vendor to Momos in 2025 and now manages reviews, cases and guest messages for 472 locations in one view.

Key features

  • All feedback together: Google ratings, app store comments, social DMs, survey answers and POS data for each store arrive in the Unified Inbox, tagged and routed by AI.
  • Replies from Alfie: Alfie, the Experience Agent, answers within minutes, trained on your brand voice and aware of each guest's past visits.
  • Loyalty-linked recovery: Alfie issues points, coupons or offers through the loyalty program you already run, and 2025 brought integrations with Paytronix, Square and Otter.
  • Support beyond reviews: The same agent provides customer support on website chat, in-app, surveys and delivery apps, and passes hard cases to your team.
  • Location dashboards: See which topics push ratings up or down and where incidents cluster, so you can improve guest experience store by store.
  • Multilingual reviews: Live translation means each store can respond in the language the guest used.

Pros

  • Specialist focus: Every workflow, from menu questions to delivery complaints, is designed around how restaurants operate.
  • Resolution included: Alfie can settle a complaint with an offer and then track whether the guest returns.
  • Reports for operators on the move: Mobile-first reports, added in 2025, let area managers check each store from a phone.
  • Hands-on team: Onboarding specialists train Alfie and roll it out to new locations as you open them.

Cons

  • Narrower scope than Birdeye: Businesses outside hospitality, or teams that want one tool across many industries, are better served by a broader platform.
  • Quote-based pricing: Like Birdeye, Momos doesn't publish prices, so a demo comes first.
  • About a week to launch: Setup and training with the Momos team happen before Alfie replies on its own.

Price and plans

Momos doesn't publish prices; you get a custom quote after a demo, which you can book on the Momos website.
