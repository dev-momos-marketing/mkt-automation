9. Marqii

Marqii is a restaurant platform built around keeping online information accurate. When your POS menu changes, Marqii sends the new items and prices to the major listing sites, and the same dashboard handles listings, reviews, social posts and location pages.

Birdeye serves many industries; Marqii is narrower and made for restaurants. It suits a restaurant group that wants menu accuracy and review replies from one vendor, with per-location prices on its website.

Key features

  • Menus from your POS: Changes made at the point of sale publish automatically, so nobody retypes a menu for each site.
  • Listings everywhere: Unlimited updates reach 70+ publishers, from opening hours and photos to preferred ordering links.
  • Restaurant review feed: Ratings from ezCater, Grubhub and DoorDash are tracked together with what guests post on Google, Yelp and Facebook.
  • Suggested or managed replies: Scale adds AI drafts for your team, and high-volume brands can buy done-for-you replies from Marqii in monthly bundles.
  • Review texts: Partner orders trigger a text inviting the guest to review you on Google, Facebook or TripAdvisor, or on a Marqii first-party form.
  • Social scheduling: Google Posts and Apple Showcase content is planned in the tool you already use for listings.

Pros

  • Made for restaurants: Menu sync and delivery-app monitoring answer needs specific to food businesses.
  • Prices you can see: Each plan has a per-location price online, and managed replies cost $0.75 to $2.00 per review depending on the bundle.
  • Less menu data entry: Deschutes Brewery's general manager told Marqii that each menu change used to take a full day of data entry and now updates from the POS.

Cons

  • Restaurants only: A company that also runs non-restaurant brands would need a second tool to cover them.
  • Replies on three platforms: Marqii can respond on Google, Yelp and Facebook; the delivery and catering apps are tracked without replies.
  • Yearly commitment: Each plan is a one-year contract per location, with no free trial.

Price and plans

Marqii doesn't offer a free trial or free plan; you can book a free demo. Plans are priced per location, per month, on an annual contract, and managed replies cost extra, from $100 a month for 50 reviews.

  • Base: $90 per location, per month (listings and menus).
  • Growth: $145 per location, per month (adds review monitoring and replies).
  • Scale: $180 per location, per month (adds AI-suggested replies, review generation and social).
  • Marqii Package: $250 per location, per month (adds Host location pages).
