9. Marqii

Marqii started out solving a problem every operator knows: a price or dish changes, and half the internet still shows the old menu. Edit an item in your POS and Marqii carries that change to Google, Yelp and other sites. Over time it has grown into a restaurant operations hub that also covers listings, review replies and social scheduling.

Notable customers: California Tortilla, Uccello's Hospitality Group and the IHOP franchisee Romulus Restaurant Group.

What's new: Working with the loyalty company Thanx, Marqii rolled out Review Generation in July 2026: shortly after paying, a guest receives a text asking for a rating on Google, Facebook or TripAdvisor. Branded First Party Reviews forms, which you can embed on your site or hand out as a link or QR code, followed a launch in November 2025.

Key features

  • POS menu sync. Menu changes made in your point of sale flow to Google, Yelp and other listings without retyping.
  • Location details everywhere. Update hours, photos, offers and contact information for every store from a single screen.
  • Restaurant-specific review inbox. Feedback left on OpenTable, ezCater, Grubhub and Uber Eats shows up next to your Google, Yelp and Facebook reviews.
  • Suggested replies or a managed service. AI drafts responses for your team, while busy brands can outsource replying altogether to a team Marqii manages.
  • More reviews after each order. First-party forms and post-purchase texts ask guests for feedback while the meal is fresh.
  • Social scheduling. Queue up Google Business Profile and Facebook posts in the same tool you use for listings.

Why businesses choose Marqii: Restaurant groups that change menus often, or sell through several delivery apps, save hours by updating everything once. It's also one of the few tools here with prices on its website, which makes budgeting simpler.

Pricing: You pay monthly per store on a one-year term, beginning with Base at $74.99 for menus and location data, Pro at $119.99 with reviews, and Kitchen Sync at $149.99 with advanced review tools. AI-suggested replies cost $15 and social management $20 per location per month, and large multi-unit accounts get custom pricing.
