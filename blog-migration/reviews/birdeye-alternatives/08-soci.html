8. SOCi

SOCi is a localized marketing platform for franchisors and chains with hundreds or thousands of locations. Like Birdeye, it covers reviews, listings and social, and its pitch centers on brand-trained AI agents that do the local work under rules set by corporate.

When you compare it with Birdeye, the pricing model is worth a look: SOCi charges by location count and by the agents you deploy, with no per-user or per-listing fees, which can suit a franchise system with many local managers. Jersey Mike's Subs, Scooter's Coffee and On The Border Mexican Grill & Cantina are among its restaurant customers.

Key features

  • Reputation agent: Replies to reviews in your voice, asks guests for new ones and escalates sensitive feedback to staff.
  • Local search agent: A separate agent handles listings and local SEO, with location details synced to Olo and Uber Eats.
  • Local pages and paid social: Build a page for each location and run localized ad campaigns from the same account.
  • Social agent: Posts and comment replies go out per location, in line with corporate guidelines.
  • Surveys: Gather private guest feedback to read alongside public ratings.

Pros

  • Agents at volume: SOCi reports over 27 million local marketing tasks a year handled by its agents.
  • Franchise-friendly controls: Corporate defines the guardrails, and each location adds its own content within them.
  • Bundling: You can start with one agent or combine several, and SOCi prices the bundle as a whole.
  • Well reviewed: SOCi's G2 rating is 4.5 out of 5.

Cons

  • No price list: You can't compare its cost with Birdeye's until SOCi scopes a quote for your locations.
  • No trial: SOCi doesn't offer a free trial, so evaluation runs through demos.
  • Sized for big networks: A restaurant with a handful of locations may find the agent workforce more than it needs.

Price and plans

SOCi doesn't publish prices and has no free trial. Each quote is based on your location count and the Genius Agents and skills you deploy, with no per-user or per-listing fees.
