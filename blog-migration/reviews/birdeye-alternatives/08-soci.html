8. SOCi

SOCi runs local marketing for franchisors and chains, and the company now lives at soci.ai after moving from its old domain. Head office sets the rules, and brand-trained AI agents called Genius Agents carry out review replies, listings updates and social posts for each location.

Notable customers: Sport Clips Haircuts, Lee's Famous Recipe Chicken, On The Border Mexican Grill & Cantina and WOWorks, which owns six restaurant brands.

What's new: SOCi's agent count keeps climbing: 300,000 by May 2026 and over 412,000 by September, which the company says now complete more than 27 million tasks yearly. Sport Clips, with close to 1,800 salons, switched on more than 3,400 of them for reviews and local search in June 2026.

Key features

  • AI review replies. Genius Reviews answers reviews in line with your brand guidelines and escalates sensitive ones to staff.
  • Review and sentiment reports. See how ratings and sentiment compare across every location.
  • Listings and local search. Keep details current on Google, Yelp, Bing and other directories.
  • Central social publishing. Corporate plans posts while each location can customize them, helped by an AI social agent.
  • Local pages and paid social. Run location pages and localized ad campaigns from the same platform.
  • Guest surveys. Collect first-party feedback to sit alongside public reviews.

Why businesses choose SOCi: Brands with hundreds or thousands of locations can keep tight corporate control while AI agents do the day-to-day local work. Role-based permissions let local managers act within the limits you set.

Pricing: SOCi quotes based on how many locations you have and which products you need.
