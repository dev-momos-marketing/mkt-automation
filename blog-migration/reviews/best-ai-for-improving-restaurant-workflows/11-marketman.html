11. MarketMan

Website: MarketMan

Category: Restaurant inventory & food cost management software

Kitchen managers use MarketMan, a cloud tool, to see what they have, what they need to buy and what each dish really costs.

Stock counts, recipe costs, orders to suppliers and incoming invoices all go through the app, which takes much of the clipboard and spreadsheet work out of the weekly inventory routine:

Notable customers: Westville Restaurant Group, ATFC, Ltd. and Taco del Mar (multi-unit franchisee).

What's new: Square sellers everywhere got a MarketMan-powered inventory module in April 2026, called Square Restaurant Inventory by MarketMan, so they can handle recipes, ingredients, purchasing, vendors, invoice scans and waste logs without leaving Square.

Key features:

  • Live counts by location. Inventory updates in real time, so each site knows what's on hand before it orders.
  • Recipe costs that follow prices. Menu and recipe costing picks up current ingredient prices.
  • Automated ordering. Purchase orders go out to suppliers automatically.
  • Invoice scanning. Scanned invoices update prices and flag when a supplier changes them.
  • Waste and loss trends. Waste tracking shows where product is lost over time.
  • Group food cost reports. Multi-location reporting covers food cost and COGS, with POS and accounting integrations including Square.

What makes them stand out: Everything in MarketMan points at food cost, from the price on the invoice to the cost of each plate, so purchasing decisions rest on current numbers.

Pricing: MarketMan doesn't confirm prices on its site. Third-party trackers list per-location Starter and Growth plans and reported increases in August 2026, so ask MarketMan for a quote.

It's a great fit for: Independent restaurants and multi-unit groups, Square sellers included, that want firmer control over inventory, purchasing and food cost.
