16. Marqii

Website: Marqii

Marqii is a digital operations platform that keeps a restaurant's menus, listings, reviews and social posts current from one dashboard. Menu changes made in the POS sync out to Google, Yelp and other sites, which takes a repetitive update job off your managers' plates.

Notable customers: Romulus Restaurant Group (IHOP franchisee), Uccello's Hospitality Group and California Tortilla.

What's new: Since July 2026, Review Generation with Thanx sends guests a text after a purchase asking them to rate the restaurant on Google, Facebook or TripAdvisor. In the months before, November 2025 brought review forms in the restaurant's own branding, and May 2025 added a scheduler for Facebook and Google Business Profile posts.

Key features:

  • Menus synced from the POS. A price or item change at the register updates Google, Yelp and other sites.
  • Listings kept current. Hours, photos, offers and contact details are managed across the web.
  • One review inbox. Ratings from search, booking, delivery and catering sites, ezCater and Uber Eats among them, land in a single feed for your managers.
  • AI reply drafts. Suggested responses give managers a starting point, and busy brands can pay Marqii's team to write the replies instead.
  • Review requests. First-party forms and post-purchase texts bring in fresh reviews.
  • Social scheduling. Posts to Facebook and Google Business Profile go out on a set schedule.

Why businesses choose Marqii: If updating menus and listings on every platform fills a good part of your team's week, Marqii handles most of that for you, and its per-location prices are public so you can cost it up front.

Pricing: Plans are listed per location per month, billed annually: Base (menus and location data) $74.99, Pro (adds reviews) $119.99 and Kitchen Sync (adds advanced reviews) $149.99. AI-suggested review responses add $15 and social management $20, and the biggest chains negotiate their own rate.
