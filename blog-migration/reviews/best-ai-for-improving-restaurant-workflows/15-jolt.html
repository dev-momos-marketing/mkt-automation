15. Jolt

Website: Jolt

Think of Jolt as the shift binder moved onto a tablet: the app lets restaurants and convenience stores swap paper checklists, food safety logs and prep labels for digital records, with scheduling and training in the same place.

Digi International closed its purchase of Jolt in August 2025 at about $145.5 million and slotted it under the SmartSense brand. The Jolt name and website remain, and in 2026 Digi sells it within SmartSense ONE.

Notable customers: A&W Restaurants and White Spot Hospitality.

What's new: At NRF in January 2026, SmartSense by Digi showed SmartSense ONE, a modular offer where operators pick from seven building blocks that mix SmartSense sensing with what Jolt does on the floor.

Key features:

  • Checklists with proof. Staff attach a picture when they finish a task and a manager signs it off, giving you a record of each shift.
  • Food safety records. Temperature logs can be filled in by staff or fed by SmartSense sensors.
  • Labels on demand. Date code and prep labels print from the app.
  • Scheduling and time clock. A drag-and-drop scheduler and time clock sit next to the task lists.
  • Training and info hub. Staff find training material and reference documents in one library.
  • Location reporting. Task completion and performance reports cover every location, with POS integration including PAR Brink.

What makes them stand out: Task accountability and food safety logging live in the same app as scheduling, and SmartSense sensors can fill in temperature logs automatically.

Pricing: Custom pricing. Jolt asks you to talk to sales for a quote.

It's a great fit for: Quick-service and franchise groups that want shift-level task accountability and food safety records, especially if SmartSense temperature sensors are on the shortlist.
