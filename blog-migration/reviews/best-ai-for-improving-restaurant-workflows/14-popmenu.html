14. Popmenu

Website: Popmenu

Popmenu gives restaurants one marketing system for the jobs that usually sprawl across several vendors: the website and its menus, online orders, guest emails and texts, phone calls, bookings and reviews.

For a stretched team, the practical gain is that guest questions, campaigns and orders run from one restaurant-specific system, with AI picking up routine calls and drafting messages.

Notable customers: La Parrilla (23-location Tex-Mex group), Steam Boys (8 locations, Tennessee), The Hampton Social and B&B Butchers & Restaurant (Berg Hospitality Group).

What's new: Popmenu's most recent launch, in October 2026, is Spotlight Menu, a new piece of menu technology. Six months earlier, in April 2026, a Yext partnership brought listings and reputation management for 70+ platforms, among them Yelp, OpenTable and Facebook, into Popmenu.

What you can do with Popmenu:

  • Answer calls with AI. The phone answering handles common guest questions, orders and reservations.
  • Take orders without commission. Each online order costs the same small fixed amount whatever its size, and guests can order through a branded app.
  • Automate campaigns. AI drafts email, SMS and social posts that go to segmented audiences.
  • Handle reviews and feedback. Guest feedback, reputation tools and AI-drafted review replies sit together.
  • Publish menus search engines can read. Websites come with interactive, SEO-friendly menus.
  • Plan across locations. A central marketing calendar and reports cover every site, with POS integrations including Square, Oracle and CAKE.

Why Popmenu stands out: It covers most of a restaurant's guest-facing digital work in one tool and lists its prices publicly, which makes it easier to budget for.

It's best for: Independent restaurants and smaller regional groups whose managers would rather not log into four tools to update a menu, send a promotion and check missed calls.

Pricing: Popmenu publishes its rates. Starter is $179 a month (or $159 when you prepay the year), Essentials is $299 ($269 prepaid) and Premier is $499 ($449 prepaid). Enterprise plans are quoted. Every online order adds $1.00, and no share of the sale goes to Popmenu.
