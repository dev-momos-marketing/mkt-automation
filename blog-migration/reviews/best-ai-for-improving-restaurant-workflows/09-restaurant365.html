9. Restaurant365

Website: Restaurant365

Category: End-to-end restaurant management software

Where many groups juggle a separate bookkeeping tool, inventory app and scheduler, Restaurant365 runs the whole restaurant back office from one cloud login, with the ledger, food cost, staff schedules and payroll sharing the same data.

Its newest layer, R365 AI, reads the full restaurant P&L to surface labor and cost insights, which helps multi-unit operators spend less time assembling reports by hand.

Notable customers: Villa Restaurant Group, Wholly Stromboli and Johnson's Landing.

What's new: R365 AI made its debut at the NRA Show in May 2026, bringing dashboards and an advisor that answer questions from your P&L, a new labor suite, and AI-built schedules that early-access customers are testing. A month later it hired Kate Hastings as Chief Customer Officer to put more into customer success.

Key features:

  • Accounting made for restaurants. Invoices are processed automatically, bank accounts reconciled and P&Ls kept for each entity.
  • Food cost visibility. Inventory and recipe costing feed reports comparing theoretical and actual food cost.
  • Labor tied to sales. Scheduling and labor management follow the sales forecast.
  • Payroll for hourly teams. Payroll and HR cover hourly staff and their tips.
  • Sales posted to the books. POS integrations bring each day's sales into accounting without manual entry.
  • Group reporting. Budgets and results for every store combine into one view, and franchisors can track royalties there too.

What makes them stand out: Accounting and operations live in the same system, so the numbers managers act on during the week are the ones that close the books.

Pricing: Restaurant365 packages its modules into bundles priced for each store, with quarterly or yearly billing, and larger organizations work out a tailored deal. The dollar amounts on Restaurant365's own pages don't agree with each other, so get a written quote for your setup.

It's a great fit for: Growing restaurant groups and franchise owners whose controllers and GMs need to work from the same set of numbers every week.
