10. TimeForge

Category: AI labor forecasting & scheduling

Around since 1998 and based in Lubbock, Texas, TimeForge helps restaurant and retail managers build schedules, track hours and stay on the right side of labor laws. In August 2024 it folded Lineup.ai into its product, which means the forecasting engine Lineup.ai was known for now drives TimeForge schedules.

Notable customers: Hard Eight BBQ and Great Harvest Bread Co. (TimeForge is a preferred vendor for Great Harvest Franchising).

What's new: In July 2025 TimeForge added a Square integration, so restaurants on Square POS can feed their sales into its labor forecasts and staffing targets.

Key features:

  • Forecasts in 15-minute blocks. Lineup.ai's models read your POS history to predict sales and labor needs through the day.
  • Auto-built schedules. Schedules are generated from forecast demand, so managers start from a draft that matches expected traffic.
  • Overtime warnings as they happen. Time and attendance tracks clock-ins and alerts managers when labor runs past target.
  • Compliance support. Overtime rules and predictive (fair workweek) scheduling requirements are handled in the scheduler.
  • Employee self-service. Staff manage availability, shift swaps and time-off requests themselves.
  • Multi-unit labor reporting. Labor cost is compared with sales across locations, with POS integrations including Toast and Square.

What makes them stand out: Forecasting, scheduling and timekeeping share one system, so the demand forecast carries straight into the schedule and then into clock-in tracking.

Pricing: Custom pricing. TimeForge quotes each operator directly.

It's a great fit for: Quick-service and casual-dining groups and franchise operators that want schedules built from AI sales forecasts, with time tracking and labor compliance in the same tool.
