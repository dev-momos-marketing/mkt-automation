7. 7Shifts

Website: 7Shifts

Category: Labor & Workforce Performance Analytics

Built only for restaurants, 7shifts is a workforce management platform that handles scheduling, time clocking, tip management and payroll for shift-based teams. In 2026 it added employee training, so the same app staff open to check their shifts can also deliver courses.

Notable customers: HCI Hospitality (Freddy's Frozen Custard & Steakburgers franchisee), Boston Pizza (Regina), Conshy Girls Restaurant Group and Pitango Gelato.

What's new: In September 2026 7shifts launched Employee Training, where you build courses and quizzes, assign them by role, department or location and see who has finished; it comes with the Premium plan. That followed a July 2025 move to a new lineup of plans.

Key features:

  • Forecast-based schedules. A drag-and-drop builder uses sales and labor forecasts to show how many people each shift needs.
  • Staff self-service. The mobile app lets employees swap shifts, set availability and message the team without texting the manager.
  • A time clock tied to the schedule. POS and payroll integrations help keep clock-ins in line with the hours you planned.
  • Labor cost next to sales. Budgets and reports put labor spend beside sales, so you can trim a shift while the week is still running.
  • Tips handled for you. The app calculates tip pools and pays them out, and you can add restaurant payroll for an extra fee.
  • Checklists and training. Managers assign shift tasks and checklists, and new hires get onboarding packages and courses.

Pricing: The free Comp plan covers one location with up to 15 employees. Billed annually, per location per month, Essentials costs $39.99 (up to 30 employees), Pro $79.99 and Premium $134.99; on month-to-month billing the same three tiers cost $44.99, $89.99 and $149.99. Enterprise is quoted, payroll is billed separately and Pro comes with a 14-day free trial.
