5. Loman

Website: Loman

Category: Conversational AI phone answering for orders & reservations

Loman is an AI phone agent made for restaurants. It answers calls day and night, takes orders and reservations, and passes them to your POS, and the company says the agent was live in more than 1,500 restaurants by July 2026. It raised a $3.5M seed round led by Next Coast Ventures in 2025.

Notable customers: Big Apple Pizza, Taqueria El Arquito and Brick Oven Pizza.

What's new: Loman spent 2026 adding POS partners: SpotOn in January, Shift4 in June and the Toast Partner Ecosystem in July, where phone orders drop into the Toast workflows a restaurant already uses.

Key features:

  • Every call picked up. The agent answers 24/7 and handles several callers at once during a rush, so nobody on the line has to leave a ticket to grab the phone.
  • Orders into the POS. Phone orders go into Toast, Square, Clover, SpotOn, NCR, Shift4 and other systems.
  • Card payments by phone. Guests can pay during the call, so the order reaches the kitchen already settled.
  • Table bookings. It takes reservations, including through OpenTable.
  • Routine questions handled. Callers asking about the menu, hours and similar basics get answers without a staff member stepping away.
  • Menu training at setup. Loman trains the agent on your own menu before it goes live.

What makes them stand out: It connects to a long and growing list of restaurant POS systems, which helps if your locations don't all run the same one.

Pricing: Loman no longer lists prices. Its Starter, Premium and Enterprise tiers are quoted by sales and charged per location.

It's a great fit for: Independent and multi-unit restaurants, especially busy pizza and takeout concepts, that lose orders when nobody can get to the phone.
