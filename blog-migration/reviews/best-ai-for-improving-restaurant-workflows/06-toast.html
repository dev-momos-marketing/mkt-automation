6. Toast

Website: Toast

Category: Restaurant point of sale (POS) & operations software

Toast (NYSE: TOST) is a POS and management platform made only for restaurants, covering counter terminals, handhelds, kiosks, kitchen screens, online ordering, payroll and guest marketing.

By the end of June 2026 the company counted roughly 180,000 restaurants live on its system, and its Toast IQ features are starting to take routine work like campaign planning off owners' desks.

Notable customers: Captain Parker's Pub, Kaldi's Coffee and Craft Lounge.

What's new: The May 2026 spring release brought Toast IQ Grow, a premium upgrade that gives you both AI help with campaigns, through its AI Marketing Agent, and a person on Toast's side as your marketing success manager, plus a refreshed Toast Local app for guests that now carries loyalty rewards. In July 2026 it picked independent operators for its 2026-2027 Customer Advisory Board.

Key features:

  • POS across the floor. Counter terminals, kiosks and Toast Go handhelds let servers take orders and payments at the table.
  • Ordering and delivery together. Online orders, delivery integrations and the Toast Local app feed the same system as dine-in tickets.
  • Kitchen display. Tickets go to screens on the line, which removes printed chits from the kitchen.
  • Menus managed centrally. Items and prices for every location are updated from one place.
  • Staff and pay in one place. Sling by Toast handles the schedule, and payroll runs on the same platform that rings up sales.
  • AI for marketing. The AI marketing agent in Toast IQ Grow works alongside loyalty, gift cards and email and SMS campaigns.

What makes them stand out: Front of house, kitchen, payroll and marketing all come from one restaurant-only vendor, so data moves between them without exports or extra integrations to maintain.

Pricing: Toast quotes plans through sales. Its website has promoted a no-cost Starter Kit alongside monthly plans, but the numbers change, so confirm today's figures with Toast before you budget.

It's a great fit for: Operators of any size, from one dining room to a franchise system, who would rather run the counter, the kitchen, the payroll and the guest marketing through one company.
