12. clearCOGS

Website: ClearCOGS

Category: AI inventory & waste reduction

ClearCOGS uses AI to forecast restaurant demand from POS sales data, then turns those forecasts into daily prep lists, ordering suggestions and alerts aimed at cutting food waste. It raised a $3.8 million seed round led by Closed Loop Partners in April 2025.

Notable customers: ClearCOGS doesn't name its customers publicly.

What's new: In September 2025 ClearCOGS released an open API, so its forecasts and recommendations can flow into the tools your team already works in.

Key features:

  • Item-level forecasts. Demand is predicted for each menu item from your POS sales history.
  • Prep lists and pars. Each day starts with recommended prep quantities and par levels for the kitchen.
  • Order quantities. Purchasing suggestions tell you how much to buy.
  • Exception alerts. Warnings flag likely stock-outs, price changes and unusual sales.
  • Labor input. The same forecasts can inform labor planning.
  • Toast and open API. An integration in the Toast marketplace and the open API carry forecasts into other systems.

What makes them stand out: It swaps the morning guess about how much to prep for a list built from your own sales, which goes straight at overproduction and spoilage.

Pricing: Custom pricing. ClearCOGS doesn't publish prices.

It's a great fit for: Restaurants and multi-unit groups, Toast users in particular, that lose margin to overproduction, spoilage and guesswork in daily prep and ordering.
