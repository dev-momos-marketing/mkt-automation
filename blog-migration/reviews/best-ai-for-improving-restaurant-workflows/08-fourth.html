8. Fourth

Website: Fourth

Category: Labor forecasting & workforce management

Fourth is a back-office suite for restaurant and hospitality groups that combines HotSchedules scheduling, time and attendance, HR and payroll with inventory and purchasing. Its Fourth iQ layer uses AI to forecast sales and labor and to give store managers recommendations. The company is backed by Marlin Equity Partners and appointed Scott Collison as CEO in March 2026.

Notable customers: Bar Louie.

What's new: Fourth iQ 2.3, released in January 2026, alerts managers to spikes in early employee exits, low schedule-compliance scores, the job boards bringing in the most hires and gaps between AI and manager sales forecasts. Fourth iQ 3.0 followed in early 2026 with above-store AI views for senior leaders, building on the inventory shortfall warnings in iQ 2.2 from November 2025.

Key features:

  • AI demand forecasts. Fourth iQ predicts sales and labor needs so schedules follow expected traffic.
  • Next-best actions. The iQ mobile app suggests what a store manager should tackle next.
  • Above-store views. Area and senior leaders see AI summaries across their locations in iQ 3.0.
  • HotSchedules for the team. Staff get a scheduling app, backed by time and attendance, HR, recruiting and payroll services.
  • Compliance alerts. Warnings flag labor-law and schedule-compliance issues before they turn into a problem.
  • Inventory with AI alerts. Purchasing, recipe costing and inventory tools warn managers early about shortfalls.

What makes them stand out: Labor and inventory share the same AI layer, so a manager gets staffing and stock recommendations in one app.

Pricing: Custom pricing. Fourth doesn't publish prices.

It's a great fit for: Multi-unit and enterprise groups that want workforce management, inventory and AI forecasting handled by one back-office vendor.
