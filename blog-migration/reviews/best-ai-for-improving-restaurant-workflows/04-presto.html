4. Presto

Website: Presto Automation

Category: AI-driven voice ordering for drive-thru

Built for quick-service drive-thrus, Presto Voice takes orders through the headsets and POS a restaurant already owns. Presto Automation, the formerly Nasdaq-listed company that built it, sold its assets in December 2024 to a group led by Remus Capital; the business now runs privately as Presto Phoenix, and existing customers carried over.

Notable customers: Carl's Jr. / Hardee's and Taco John's.

What's new: In January 2026 Presto raised $10 million in a round led by Metropolitan Partners Group to expand enterprise QSR rollouts, saying the product had moved beyond pilots into hundreds of drive-thru lanes. It also joined the Toast Partner Ecosystem in 2026, so drive-thru orders taken by voice reach Toast kitchens directly.

Key features:

  • Speaker-post ordering. The AI greets guests and takes their order, so the window crew can concentrate on payment and handing out food.
  • Existing hardware. It works with your current headsets and POS, plus a Toast integration, which keeps installation light.
  • Suggestive selling. Upsell prompts are offered during each order at the speaker.
  • Several languages. Guests can place their order in more than one language.
  • Central menu control. A menu change is set once and pushed to the drive-thru at every location.

What makes them stand out: Presto Voice now speaks with lifelike ElevenLabs voices, custom brand voices included, following its September 2025 partnership with ElevenLabs, which makes the greeting at the speaker sound less robotic.

Pricing: Custom pricing. Contact Presto for a quote.

It's a great fit for: Multi-unit QSR chains and franchise groups with busy drive-thrus that want order taking handled by AI.
