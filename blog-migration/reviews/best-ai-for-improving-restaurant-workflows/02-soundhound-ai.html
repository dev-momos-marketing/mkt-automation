2. Soundhound AI

Website: SoundHound AI

Category: Voice AI for ordering & drive-thru

Listed on Nasdaq as SOUN, SoundHound AI builds restaurant voice agents that pick up phone calls, take drive-thru orders and run voice kiosks, so your crew spends less of each shift repeating the menu. It has bought Amelia, SYNQ3 Restaurant Solutions and Interactions over the past two years, and its restaurant products now run on its own OASYS agent platform.

Notable customers: Five Guys, Chipotle, Red Lobster, Applebee's / IHOP and Church's Texas Chicken.

What's new: In May 2026 SoundHound introduced OASYS, a self-learning system where you build an agent once and run it on phones, drive-thrus, kiosks, web chat and cars. In January 2026 Five Guys renewed its deal after more than one million AI-handled phone ordering interactions and let franchisees switch the phone agent on at their own stores.

Key features:

  • Phone line coverage. The agent answers calls, takes the order and fields questions about the menu, opening hours and allergens, which keeps managers off the phone during a rush.
  • Drive-thru order taking. Voice ordering runs at the speaker post, including on Acrelec hardware, and brings up upsells and promotions while guests order.
  • Orders straight to the kitchen. POS integration sends each finished order to the line without anyone typing it in a second time.
  • Kiosk and in-car channels. The same voice ordering reaches self-service kiosks and vehicles, and it understands several languages.
  • Voice Insights. Drive-thru conversations are turned into operational data your area managers can review across locations.

What makes them stand out: With OASYS a chain can manage one ordering agent across phone, drive-thru and kiosk, instead of running a separate tool for each channel.

Pricing: Custom pricing. SoundHound doesn't publish rates, so expect a quote based on your locations and channels.

It's a great fit for: High-volume QSR and casual-dining chains that want phone and drive-thru order taking automated across many stores, with help from the vendor on an enterprise rollout.
