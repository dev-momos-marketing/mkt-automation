13. SevenRooms

Website: SevenRooms

Category: Restaurant guest experience & retention software

SevenRooms handles reservations, table management, guest profiles and marketing for restaurants and hospitality venues. Each profile stores preferences, allergies, occasions and visit history, so hosts and servers have that context before a guest sits down.

DoorDash completed its acquisition of SevenRooms in June 2025, at about $1.2 billion. The product is still sold under the SevenRooms brand, and its leadership stayed on to run it.

Notable customers: Union Square Hospitality Group, Wolfgang Puck, Nobu Restaurants and Dishoom.

What's new: The headline of SevenRooms' July 2026 release is its phone agent, which runs on ElevenLabs voices, picks up calls, books tables and knows a regular when one rings; it can also pitch group dining, take payments and report on how the agent performs. Earlier, in March 2025, AI started drafting review replies, summarizing feedback and tidying staff notes.

Key features:

  • Reservations and waitlist. Guests book directly, and walk-ins join a waitlist in the same system.
  • AI table management. Seating, pacing and cover forecasts help hosts plan the floor for the night.
  • Automated email and SMS. Campaigns go to segments built from guest data.
  • Voice AI on the phone. Calls are answered and reservations booked without a host leaving the stand.
  • Feedback tools. AI drafts review replies and summarizes what guests say.
  • Deposits and group payments. Secure payment collection covers deposits and group bookings.

What makes them stand out: Reservations, visits, phone calls and feedback all add to one guest profile, so the front of house works from the same history at every visit.

Pricing: Custom pricing. SevenRooms doesn't publish prices.

It's a great fit for: Full-service restaurants, hotel F&B, nightlife venues and hospitality groups that want reservations, guest profiles and retention marketing in one system.
