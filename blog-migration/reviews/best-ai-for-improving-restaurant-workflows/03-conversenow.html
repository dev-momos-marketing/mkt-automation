3. ConverseNow

Website: ConverseNow

Category: Conversational voice AI for phone & drive-thru

Restaurant chains use ConverseNow's voice assistants to answer restaurant phone lines and drive-thru lanes, talk with guests through large language models and drop finished orders into your POS. Buying Valyant AI in 2024 strengthened its drive-thru side, and when a call needs a person, the guest can be passed to call-center agents at its partner OrderSolutions.

Notable customers: Anthony's Coal Fired Pizza & Wings and Wingstop (a pilot at select locations).

What's new: In April 2026 ConverseNow partnered with Deliverect, so operators who already run orders and menus through Deliverect can add its phone and drive-thru voice ordering from there.

Key features:

  • Complete phone orders. The assistant picks up calls and takes whole orders, which frees the team member who would otherwise be stuck at the phone by the counter.
  • Drive-thru lanes. Voice ordering at the speaker post takes the headset load off your window crew during peaks.
  • An upsell on every order. Suggestions come up automatically on each call, so add-on sales don't depend on who happens to be working.
  • Direct POS entry. Orders arrive in the POS ready for the kitchen, removing the step of staff keying them in again.
  • Human backup. Calls the AI can't finish can go to a live agent through OrderSolutions.

What makes them stand out: Guests can talk the way they normally would thanks to the generative AI conversation, and the Deliverect integration makes it easier to fit into an order and menu setup you already have.

Pricing: Custom pricing. ConverseNow doesn't list prices publicly.

It's a great fit for: Pizza, wings and other QSR or fast-casual chains where phone and drive-thru orders make up a large part of daily volume.
