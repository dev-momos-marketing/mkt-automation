4. Popmenu

Popmenu gives restaurants a marketing system made for the industry: a website with an interactive menu, direct ordering, email and text campaigns, reservations, review tools and an AI that answers the phone. It fits best when an owner or small brand team wants one platform to handle guest-facing marketing for each of its stores.

Notable customers: La Parrilla (23-location Tex-Mex group), Steam Boys, The Hampton Social and B&B Butchers & Restaurant.

What's new: Oracle Simphony operators could buy Popmenu through Oracle Cloud Marketplace starting in April 2025. A year later, in April 2026, a tie-up with Yext brought listings and reputation for 70+ sites such as Yelp and OpenTable into the Popmenu dashboard. October 2026 brought the announcement of Spotlight Menu, a fresh release on the menu side.

What you can do with Popmenu:

  • Let AI pick up the phone. An AI voice answers calls about common questions, orders and reservations, keeping staff on the floor during a rush.
  • Publish a site for each location. Websites come with interactive menus that search engines can read.
  • Keep delivery margins in house. Direct orders cost a set amount each instead of a cut of the ticket, and guests can reorder through a branded app.
  • Automate guest campaigns. Email, SMS and social posts can be drafted by AI and sent to segmented audiences.
  • Manage reviews and feedback. Reputation tools collect guest feedback and suggest AI-drafted replies to reviews.
  • Plan marketing centrally. A shared calendar and performance reports let you compare results across locations.

Why Popmenu stands out: Website, ordering, phone answering and marketing all come from one vendor that only serves restaurants. POS integrations with Square, Oracle and CAKE connect it to systems many groups already use.

It's best for: Independents and growing restaurant groups that would rather have one vendor run their website, ordering, phones and guest marketing.

Pricing: Popmenu lists Starter ($179 monthly), Essentials ($299) and Premier ($499), dropping to $159, $269 and $449 when you prepay for the year. Enterprise deals are quoted. Every online order adds a flat $1.00, and Popmenu takes no share of sales.
