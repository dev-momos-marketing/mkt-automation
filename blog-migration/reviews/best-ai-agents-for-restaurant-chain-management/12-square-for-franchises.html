12. Square for Franchises

Square for Franchises adds franchisor tools to the Square point of sale. Head office sees reporting across the system, controls menus and calculates royalties and fees, and every franchisee still runs its own Square account. Some of Square's 2026 press material uses a different name for it, Square Franchise Suite.

Notable customers: Whit's Frozen Custard, Steak Escape, Cascadia Pizza Co. and Groucho's Deli.

What's new: Three restaurant brands announced moves to Square in 2026. Steak Escape switched all 23 of its stores in March and uses the franchise tools behind the scenes. Cascadia Pizza Co. returned as a customer in August. In September, Whit's Frozen Custard signed up about 100 shops spread over 11 states.

Key features:

  • One view of sales. Reporting covers corporate and franchise locations side by side.
  • Menus pushed brand-wide. Build a menu once and send it to every location.
  • Flexible royalty rules. A royalty or fee can be a fixed charge or a share of each store's sales, measured gross or net.
  • Royalty reports and collection. Reports break down by location, location group and date, and payments are collected through Square Invoices.
  • Franchisees keep control of their money. Every operator has a separate Square login, and payouts go straight to them.
  • The rest of the Square toolkit. Registers, kitchen screens, kiosks, web ordering, loyalty and marketing come from Square for Restaurants, and partners like Restaurant365 plug in.

What makes them stand out: POS, payments and franchisor controls come from a single vendor, so royalties are calculated from the same sales data the registers record.

Pricing: Ask Square's sales team for the franchise layer's price. On top of that, every store needs a Square for Restaurants subscription, chosen from the Free, Plus and Premium tiers on Square's pricing page, and pays card processing fees.

It's a great fit for: Emerging and growing restaurant franchise brands in QSR, cafe, fast casual and dessert that want POS, payments and franchisor tools from one provider.
