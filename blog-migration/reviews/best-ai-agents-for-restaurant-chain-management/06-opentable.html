6. OpenTable

OpenTable, part of Booking Holdings, pairs a diner-facing reservation network with the host stand software restaurants use to manage tables. Its AI push began in July 2025 with Concierge, a chat assistant inside each restaurant's profile that fields diners' questions ahead of booking.

Notable customers: Piano Piano (Toronto) and HMSHost (U.S. airport restaurants).

What's new: By July 2026, Concierge had become a fuller dining assistant that lets people search for restaurants in plain language. The following month OpenTable released its biggest bundle yet, 20+ new or reworked features. Among them: AI drafts of each restaurant's profile, Guest AI recommendations for diners, voice AI partner connections, and bookable listings inside AI assistants and search. The US and Canada got it first, with more countries due in Q4 2026.

What you can do with OpenTable:

  • Answer diners ahead of the reservation. Concierge handles questions on your listing, so guests learn about your menu or space while your hosts stay focused on service.
  • Remember regulars. Guest profiles store visit history and preferences, which helps hosts at any location personalize the next visit.
  • Automate guest messages. Confirmations and reminders go out on their own, and email marketing is included on higher plans.
  • Plan each service. Floor plans and table assignments give hosts a live picture of the room as covers arrive.
  • Get found through AI search. Bookings can arrive through assistants and search tools such as Google, ChatGPT and Perplexity.
  • Plug in your other systems. AI phone agents and point-of-sale tools, Square included, can pass data to and from OpenTable.

Why OpenTable stands out: Its AI assistant lives on the same profile diners already use to reserve, so a full-service group gets AI answers without adding a separate chatbot. Dashboards on covers, no-shows and guest feedback make it easier to compare locations.

It's best for: Full-service and fine-dining groups that take reservations and want AI-assisted guest communication inside the booking platform they already use.

Pricing: OpenTable sells three tiers, Basic, Core and Pro, each combining a monthly subscription with a per-cover charge on bookings from its network. Its website doesn't list today's amounts, so request a quote for your locations.
