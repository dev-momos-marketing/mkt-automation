13. BrandWide

BrandWide is franchise software from Soffront Corporation, which has made CRM products since 1992. One login takes a franchisor from recruiting and signing new owners through training, store audits, royalty billing and local marketing. It serves franchise systems in many industries, so a restaurant brand gets general franchise tooling with no kitchen-specific features.

Notable customers: BrandWide doesn't name its customers publicly.

What's new: BrandWide hasn't released dated product news in the past two years.

Key features:

  • Franchise lead pipeline. A development CRM tracks candidates and nurtures them toward signing.
  • Digital onboarding. Documents and e-signatures for new franchisees run through a DocuSign integration.
  • Training records. A learning management system trains franchisees and their staff.
  • Field audits. Compliance checklists guide each location visit.
  • Royalties without spreadsheets. BrandWide works out what each franchisee owes, sends the invoice and takes payment, syncing with QuickBooks Online and Stripe.
  • Franchisee helpdesk. A helpdesk and intranet handle support requests and shared documents.

What makes them stand out: BrandWide puts the whole franchise lifecycle, from first lead to royalty collection, in one product aimed at emerging and mid-sized brands. Local marketing tools and system-wide dashboards round it out.

Pricing: Custom. BrandWide doesn't publish prices, so contact the vendor for a quote.

It's a great fit for: Younger and mid-market restaurant franchise systems looking for a single tool to sell, open, support and bill their franchisees.
