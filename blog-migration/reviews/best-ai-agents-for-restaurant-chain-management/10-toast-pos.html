10. Toast POS

Toast (NYSE: TOST) started as a point of sale for restaurants and now also handles online ordering, kitchen screens, payroll, guest marketing, loyalty and head-office controls for multi-unit brands. By the end of June 2026, around 180,000 locations were live on it. Its AI work is grouped under the Toast IQ name, which now includes a marketing agent.

Notable customers: Captain Parker's Pub, Kaldi's Coffee and Craft Lounge.

What's new: Toast's seasonal launch in May 2026 introduced a premium option called Toast IQ Grow. Buyers get Marketing Essentials, a person assigned as their marketing success manager, and Toast's AI Marketing Agent. The same release brought back the Toast Local app for guests, now with loyalty inside. Second-quarter 2026 results showed roughly 9,500 locations added in three months and annual recurring revenue of $2.4B.

Key features:

  • Hardware for every station. Counter terminals, kiosks and Toast Go handhelds all run the same POS.
  • Menus managed from head office. Menu and price changes are made centrally and pushed to each location.
  • Orders from every channel. Online ordering, delivery integrations and the Toast Local app send tickets to the kitchen display.
  • Staff admin in one place. Sling by Toast handles scheduling, and payroll runs off the same records as the POS.
  • Guest marketing with AI. Loyalty, gift cards and email or SMS campaigns are included, and Toast IQ Grow adds an AI marketing agent.
  • Reporting across stores. Multi-location reports let you compare sales and performance by unit.

What makes them stand out: For franchise and multi-unit groups, central menu and pricing control lets head office update every store in one place, while the same vendor handles POS, payroll and guest marketing.

Pricing: Plans are quoted by Toast's sales reps. A no-cost Starter Kit has been promoted alongside paid tiers in the past, so confirm what's on offer before you budget.

It's a great fit for: Anything from one dining room to a franchise network, as long as you want registers, ordering, payroll and marketing under one contract.
