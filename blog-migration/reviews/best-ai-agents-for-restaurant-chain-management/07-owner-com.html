7. Owner.com

Owner.com bundles what an independent restaurant needs to sell direct: a website its AI builds for you, online ordering without commissions, a branded app, and marketing and loyalty that run themselves. A point-of-sale system and AI phone ordering have since joined the lineup. Because it targets independents and small groups, a larger chain should check how well it handles many units.

Notable customers: Saffron Indian Restaurant, Goi Cuon, Doo-Dah Diner and Township Line Pizza.

What's new: Goldman Sachs Alternatives led Owner's $240M Series D in August 2026, valuing the company at $2.3B. Owner plans to use the money to stretch beyond restaurants and build AI software for local businesses in general. Reporting on the deal noted that the POS and the AI phone line, which places live orders, are already part of the product.

What you can do with Owner.com:

  • Let AI take phone orders. Calls are answered by an AI that places orders directly, so the phone stops pulling staff off the line.
  • Bring past guests back. SMS and email campaigns fire automatically based on each guest's order history.
  • Shift orders to your own channel. Commission-free online ordering and catering give guests a reason to skip third-party delivery apps.
  • Launch a site and an app. You get an AI-built website optimized for search and a branded mobile ordering app.
  • Reward repeat visits. A loyalty and rewards program runs on its own across online and app orders.
  • Ring up orders in store. Owner now sells its own restaurant POS alongside the ordering tools.

Why Owner.com stands out: Owner's pitch is that a neighborhood restaurant deserves the direct-ordering setup the big pizza and chicken chains built for themselves. Plans are published and flat, and Owner's team handles support questions from guests.

It's best for: Single-site restaurants and owners with a few locations who want guests ordering from them directly, with fewer orders lost to delivery apps.

Pricing: Two plans with identical features and no long-term contract. Flex is $249 a month, and the restaurant pays 5% on each order. Flat Rate is $499 a month with no per-order charge to the restaurant. On top of either, guests see a 5% support fee at checkout, and cards cost 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction.
