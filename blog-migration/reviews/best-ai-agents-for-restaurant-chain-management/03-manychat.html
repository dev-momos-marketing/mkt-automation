3. ManyChat

Manychat is a chat marketing platform that automates conversations on Instagram, Facebook Messenger, WhatsApp, TikTok and SMS. For a restaurant chain it sits on the social marketing side, replying to DMs and comments, pushing promotions and collecting sign-ups. It has no tools for running stores.

Notable customers: Nike, The New York Times, and a restaurant chain client of Astral Restaurant Systems featured in a Manychat case study.

What's new: In April 2025, Manychat closed a $140M Series B led by Summit Partners to build AI agents for social and messaging channels. In October 2025 it added Follow to DM, which messages each new Instagram follower automatically, and on March 2, 2026 it moved to pricing based on active contacts.

What you can do with ManyChat:

  • Reply to Instagram on autopilot. DMs, story replies and post comments get answered automatically, so questions about hours or the menu don't sit unread through a busy weekend.
  • Run several channels from one account. Messenger, WhatsApp, TikTok, SMS and email share a workspace with Instagram.
  • Design flows visually. Templates and a drag-and-drop builder let a marketing coordinator create guest journeys without writing code.
  • Add AI to conversations. AI steps and intents handle free-text questions and capture details such as a guest's email address.
  • Send offers to followers. Broadcasts and sequences deliver discount codes, promotions and news about a new menu item.
  • Take over a chat. Staff can jump in from the live chat inbox when a conversation needs a real person.

Why ManyChat stands out: Everything is designed around social messaging, which suits brands with big Instagram or Facebook audiences. Keyword triggers and ad-to-DM entry points pull people into flows that end in a sign-up or an order.

It's best for: Restaurant brands with an active social following that want to automate DMs, comment replies and promotions, paired with separate tools for operations.

Pricing: Plans are priced by active contacts since March 2, 2026, and the free plan covers up to 25. Pro (2,500 contacts) costs $29/month billed annually or $39 monthly, and Business (7,500 contacts) costs $69/month annually or $99 monthly. Essential and Advanced plans are also listed, WhatsApp conversation fees from Meta are extra, and prices vary by region.
