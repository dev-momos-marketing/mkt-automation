14. Zenput

Zenput, now called Crunchtime Ops Execution, is operations execution software for multi-unit restaurants. Crunchtime bought the company in 2022, and the product dropped the Zenput name in February 2026 as part of folding it completely into Crunchtime. It handles checklists, audits, food safety and temperature monitoring, corrective actions and rollout tracking across locations.

Notable customers: Chipotle, Domino's and Freddy's Frozen Custard & Steakburgers.

What's new: Besides the February 2026 rebrand, Crunchtime announced fresh AI features in July 2026 that span inventory, analytics and the former Zenput product.

Key features:

  • Checklists sent to every store. Head office pushes digital checklists and tasks to all locations at once.
  • Brand standards audits. Inspections, including area manager field visits, score each location against your standards.
  • Automated temperature logs. Food safety records include temperature monitoring that runs without manual readings.
  • Fixes that get tracked. When a store misses an item, a corrective action is opened and followed until someone closes it.
  • Rollout tracking. You can see which stores have completed a new initiative and which are behind.
  • Part of a larger suite. It connects with Crunchtime's inventory, labor and learning products.

What makes them stand out: It's built to confirm that every location follows the same procedures on every shift, with live dashboards at head office and a phone-first app that crew members and district leaders carry around the store.

Pricing: Custom. Crunchtime doesn't publish prices.

It's a great fit for: Multi-unit restaurant brands and franchise systems that need consistent procedures, audits and food safety checks at every location.
