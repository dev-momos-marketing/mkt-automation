11. Jolt

Jolt replaces the clipboard in restaurants and convenience stores with an app for shift checklists, food safety records, label printing, schedules and staff training. Digi International bought it in August 2025, a deal worth about $145.5 million, and runs it inside SmartSense. The Jolt name and website remain in use.

Notable customers: A&W Restaurants and White Spot Hospitality.

What's new: January 2026 saw SmartSense ONE go on show at the NRF retail conference: a mix-and-match offer that splits the Jolt and SmartSense products into seven core modules.

Key features:

  • Proof that tasks happened. A checklist item can demand a photo and a manager's approval before it counts as done.
  • Food safety logs. Temperature records can come from SmartSense sensors instead of handwritten entries.
  • Labels at the prep station. Staff print date codes and prep labels whenever a batch is made.
  • Schedules and punches. Managers arrange shifts visually, and the built-in time clock records when people start and finish.
  • Training hub. A training library and information hub keep reference material in one spot for staff.
  • Location reporting. Reports on task completion and performance cover every store, and POS integrations include PAR Brink.

What makes them stand out: Jolt focuses on accountability at the shift level, and its link to SmartSense hardware means temperature checks can be recorded automatically. Text notifications keep team members aware of what's due.

Pricing: Custom. Jolt shares rates through its sales team.

It's a great fit for: Quick-service and franchise restaurant groups that want task accountability and food safety records, especially those also weighing SmartSense temperature sensors.
