8. FranConnect

FranConnect is a franchise and multi-location management platform covering franchise development, onboarding, training, field operations, royalties and franchisee support. Its Frannie AI suite adds generative AI agents for franchise sales, operations, finance and training. FranConnect says it works with nearly 1,500 multi-location brands.

Notable customers: Tropical Smoothie Cafe, Capriotti's, Papa Johns and Subway.

What's new: Frannie AI launched in January 2025. The July 2026 release added a Candidate Coach AI assistant for franchise development, standardized project tracking and Training & Development (World Manager) inside the app. A mobile version of the franchisee support agent shipped in May 2026, and the October 2026 release notes list an upcoming Franchisee Support Agent that cites the brand's approved Hub content in its answers.

What you can do with FranConnect:

  • Work the franchise pipeline. A development CRM tracks candidate leads and deals, and an AI coach helps candidates through the process.
  • Track new openings. Onboarding and opening tasks for each new unit are managed as projects.
  • Close audit gaps. Field audits and inspections create corrective tasks linked to the exact questions a location failed.
  • Follow the money. Royalty reporting sits next to AR aging and collections dashboards.
  • Answer franchisees faster. A communication hub and AI support agent respond to franchisee questions with cited sources.
  • Train inside the app. World Manager learning content is available without leaving FranConnect.

Why it stands out: One platform covers the franchisor-franchisee relationship from candidate to open store to ongoing audits and royalties, and it connects to HubSpot, Salesforce, QuickBooks and POS systems. Frannie AI adds agents for lead nurturing, franchisee questions and analytics on top.

Best for: Franchisors and multi-unit restaurant brands that want a single system for recruiting, opening, auditing and supporting franchisees.

Pricing: Custom. FranConnect doesn't publish prices, and it's aimed at brands with significant scale and budget.
