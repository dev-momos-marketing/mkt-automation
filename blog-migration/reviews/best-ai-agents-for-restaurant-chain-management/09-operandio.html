9. Operandio

Operandio is an Australian-built operations, checklist, training and compliance platform for franchise and multi-unit hospitality operators. On top of digital SOPs it adds AI search, AI course building and translation, which helps staff at every location find and follow the same procedures.

Notable customers: Flagship Restaurant Group (Omaha, 50+ locations), Cold Rock Ice Creamery (70+ franchise locations), Riverland Group and Abacus Dining Group.

What's new: In February 2026, Operandio bought FranchiseLab, the recruitment tool co-founded by Greg Nathan, adding franchisee recruitment to its platform. FranchiseLab keeps its own brand.

Key features:

  • SOPs staff can query. AI search lets a team member ask a question and get the answer from your knowledge base.
  • Shift checklists. Opening, closing and food-safety checklists are digital and assigned as tasks.
  • Audits that trigger fixes. Inspections generate corrective actions automatically when an item fails.
  • AI course builder. Upload an SOP document and it becomes a training module with a quiz.
  • Labels and sensors. Prep labels print from the app, and sensors record fridge and freezer temperatures automatically.
  • Multilingual content. SOPs, checklists and training can be translated for teams that speak different languages.

What makes them stand out: The app is mobile-first and works on kiosks, so floor staff use it during the shift. LaunchPad playbooks and pre-opening checklists help when you open new sites, and announcements record who has read them.

Pricing: Custom, based on locations and modules. Operandio doesn't publish prices.

It's a great fit for: Multi-unit restaurant groups and franchisors that need SOPs, HACCP food safety logs and training kept consistent across every location.
