2. Tidio

Tidio sells live chat, an AI agent called Lyro, no-code Flows and a help desk to smaller businesses, with restaurants among its users. In a restaurant group's stack it covers the guest-facing website layer, answering questions about menus, hours and bookings before anyone on staff has to step in. It doesn't handle store operations.

Notable customers: Suitor, Gecko Hospitality and Pastreez.

What's new: In October 2026, Tidio announced that Lyro placed first under the default sort of G2's AI CX Agent Evaluations and scored highest on policy compliance among the agents compared. The ranking comes from Tidio's own announcement.

What you can do with Tidio:

  • Put chat on every location page. Guests start a conversation on your site, and staff reply from the mobile or desktop app wherever they happen to be.
  • Let Lyro take routine questions. The AI agent answers from your knowledge base and website content, so questions about opening times or dietary options get handled around the clock.
  • Hand tricky chats to a person. When Lyro can't resolve a request, it passes the guest to a team member instead of looping.
  • Build Flows by dragging steps. Greetings, lead capture and FAQ answers are set up visually, with no developer involved.
  • Answer email in the same tool. A ticketing help desk handles email, and Lyro can work those tickets too.
  • Check how the bot performs. Analytics show conversation volume and how many questions the AI resolved on its own.

Why Tidio stands out: Setup needs no code, and the reporting on AI resolutions tells you quickly whether the agent is actually taking load off your hosts and managers.

It's best for: Small and midsize restaurant groups that want an AI chat agent on their website with an easy route to a human.

Pricing: A free plan is available. Starter costs $24.17 per month billed annually ($29 billed monthly), and Growth starts at $49.17 per month billed annually. Plus is custom. Lyro costs extra, and that charge scales with how many conversations the AI handles.
