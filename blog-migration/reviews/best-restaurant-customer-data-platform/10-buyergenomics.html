10. BuyerGenomics

Category: Predictive CDP

BuyerGenomics is a predictive customer data platform with marketing automation built in. It combines POS, Olo, loyalty and website data, then estimates when each guest is likely to spend again so campaigns can go out at the right moment. Brands already ordering through Olo get a native connection.

Notable customers: BuyerGenomics doesn't publicly name restaurant customers.

What's new: There's no dated product news from BuyerGenomics in 2025 or 2026, so ask about its current roadmap before you commit.

Key features:

  • Olo and POS inputs. A native Olo integration sits alongside connections to most POS, ordering and reservation systems.
  • Combined profiles. Each guest record merges transaction, loyalty, website, mobile and demographic data.
  • Next-purchase predictions. Machine learning forecasts when a guest will probably buy again.
  • Automatic guest types. Guests are sorted into types and value tiers, and you can build your own segments too.
  • Behavior triggers. Follow-ups fire on actions such as browsing a menu or abandoning an online cart.
  • Revenue reporting. A drag-and-drop campaign builder is paired with reports on incremental revenue.

Pricing: Custom pricing. BuyerGenomics doesn't publish prices but advertises a free trial.
