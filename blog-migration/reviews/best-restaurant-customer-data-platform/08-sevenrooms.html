8. SevenRooms

Category: Guest experience & CDP

SevenRooms is a reservations, table management, guest CRM and marketing platform for restaurants and hospitality venues. DoorDash completed its purchase of the company in June 2025, and SevenRooms is still sold under its own brand. Its guest profiles come mainly from bookings and visits, so they're richest for full-service dining rooms.

Notable customers: Union Square Hospitality Group, Wolfgang Puck, Nobu Restaurants and Dishoom.

What's new: In March 2025, SevenRooms added AI Responses for reviews, AI Feedback Summary and AI Note Polish. DoorDash closed the acquisition in June 2025, and in July 2026 SevenRooms launched guest intelligence tools led by a Voice AI that books tables and recognizes returning callers.

Key features:

  • Detailed guest profiles. The CRM stores preferences, allergies, occasions and visit history for each diner.
  • Reservation data capture. Direct booking and waitlist tools collect guest details before they arrive.
  • Table and pacing tools. AI table management handles seating, pacing and cover forecasts.
  • Segmented marketing. Email and SMS campaigns run automatically for segments built from profile data.
  • Phone answering. Voice AI picks up calls, takes reservations and knows repeat guests.
  • Feedback in profiles. Review and feedback tools draft replies with AI and summarize what guests are saying.

Pricing: Custom pricing. SevenRooms doesn't publish prices.
