7. Paytronix

Category: Loyalty, ordering & guest analytics

Paytronix sells restaurant and c-store brands a set of guest tools: rewards programs, web ordering, gift cards, a branded app and messaging. The Access Group announced its acquisition of Paytronix in November 2024, and the product keeps its name as an Access company. All of those modules write to one guest database, which is where its value as a data platform comes from.

Notable customers: Peet's Coffee, Las Palapas and Bruster's Real Ice Cream.

What's new: In October 2025, Paytronix gave every client Journey Builder, a no-code AI tool for multi-step campaigns. Rewards Hub followed in April 2026 for self-service reward changes, and in September 2026 messaging with Journey Builder came natively to Access Evo.

Key features:

  • One guest database. Loyalty, ordering, gift cards and the app share guest records, so purchases from every module land on the same profile.
  • Multi-step journeys. Journey Builder automates sequences across email, SMS and push based on what each guest does.
  • Self-service rewards. Rewards Hub lets marketers adjust rewards, earning ratios and promotions without vendor help.
  • Gift cards tied to loyalty. Stored-value and e-gift cards are redeemable alongside loyalty, adding another spend signal per guest.
  • Segments and insights. You group guests by behavior and review how programs perform.
  • POS connections. Integrations include Qu, Revel and many other POS and ordering systems.

Pricing: Custom pricing. Paytronix doesn't publish prices.
