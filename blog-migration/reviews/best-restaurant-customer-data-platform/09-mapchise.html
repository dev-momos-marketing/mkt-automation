9. Mapchise

Category: Guest data & revenue attribution

Mapchise is a restaurant data analytics platform that mixes your POS, loyalty and marketing data with pricing, competitor and mobility data. Its customer data platform is one module within a product that leans toward location intelligence, trade areas and site selection. That combination fits groups that want to know who their guests are and where the next unit should go.

Notable customers: Mapchise doesn't name customers publicly, and its case studies describe anonymous brands such as a pizza chain and a regional sandwich shop.

What's new: Mapchise hasn't announced any dated product releases recently.

Key features:

  • Guest profiles from your systems. POS, online ordering and loyalty data are unified into individual customer profiles.
  • Where guests come from. Customer Explorer and customer maps plot your guests' locations around each restaurant.
  • Foot traffic signals. Mobility data shows how diners move and where demand is shifting.
  • Site selection. White-space analysis supports expansion, including radius exclusion zones that protect franchise territories.
  • Menu and price analysis. You can spot combos that pull guests away from higher-margin items and compare competitor pricing.
  • Attribution for complex ownership. Campaign analysis works across multi-group and franchisor structures.

Pricing: Custom pricing. Mapchise doesn't publish prices.
