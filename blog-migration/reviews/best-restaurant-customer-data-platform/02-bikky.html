2. Bikky

Category: Guest data & behavioral analytics

Bikky is a customer data platform made for multi-unit restaurant brands, from regional chains up to several hundred locations. It pulls POS, online ordering, payment and loyalty records together, cleans out duplicates and gives you one profile per guest. On top of that sit guest analytics, campaign measurement and Data Assistant, an AI analyst you can question directly.

Notable customers: Mellow Mushroom, Bubbakoo's Burritos, Condado Tacos and Tiki Taco.

What's new: In March 2025, Condado Tacos built a burrito limited-time offer around insights from its Bikky data. In September 2025, Mellow Mushroom said it runs marketing and expansion decisions on Bikky, and in April 2026 Bubbakoo's Burritos picked it to drive personalization.

Key features:

  • Ready-made connectors. Prebuilt integrations bring in data from your POS, ordering, payment and loyalty vendors without a custom data project.
  • Clean guest records. Profiles are standardized and de-duplicated across every location and channel, so one regular counts as one person.
  • Behavior metrics. You track how often guests return, how many you keep and what each one is worth over time.
  • Menu and LTO testing. Item and channel performance is tied to guest behavior, which shows whether a new dish brings people back.
  • An AI analyst on call. Type something like which locations lost the most repeat guests, and Data Assistant replies within seconds, so you skip waiting on a custom report.
  • Data out. Guest data can be shared to Snowflake or delivered over SFTP and CSV for your own warehouse.

Pricing: Custom pricing. Bikky doesn't publish prices.
