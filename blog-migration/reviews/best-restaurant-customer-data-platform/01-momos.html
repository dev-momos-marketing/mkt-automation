1. Momos

For restaurant groups running many locations, Momos adds the voice of the guest to customer data, using AI agents to handle feedback and support. Reviews, survey answers, chat and social messages land next to POS records, so the feedback side of a guest's history sits in the same place as their orders. Across the 20,000+ locations and 600+ brands on the platform, you can see which guests had a bad visit, what they said and what was done about it.

Notable customers: Papa Murphy's, Potbelly, Condado Tacos and Caribou Coffee.

What's new: During 2025 the team shipped AI copilots, automatic summaries of each ticket, a place to store brand guidelines for AI, phone-friendly reports, and integrations for Paytronix, Square and Otter. After partnering with Momos that year, Potbelly sees reviews, open cases and messages for all 472 of its shops side by side.

Key features:

  • Feedback joined to POS data. The Unified Inbox pulls POS records in with Google and App Store reviews plus social direct messages, then triages and routes each item with AI.
  • Guest memory. Alfie, the Experience Agent, recalls a guest's past visits and preferences when replying, and it writes in your brand voice within minutes.
  • Recovery through loyalty. When a visit goes wrong, Alfie can hand out loyalty points, coupons or offers through your existing rewards program to win the guest back.
  • Every inbound channel. Guests can reach it through surveys, delivery apps, inbound enquiries, chat on your website or support inside your app, and tricky cases escalate to your staff.
  • Multilingual replies. Messages are translated in real time and answered in the guest's language.
  • Measured results. Papa Murphy's reports $2M+ in revenue recovered through offers and 97.7% of review replies automated across 1,000+ locations.
