12. Como

Category: All-in-one loyalty, CRM & CDP

Como is a loyalty, CRM and guest engagement platform, sold as Como Sense, for restaurant and retail chains. Global Payments acquired Como in 2023, and it now operates as Como, a Global Payments company. Its guest profiles are built mainly from loyalty membership linked to POS purchases, so it works best as a data platform when your program has strong sign-up.

Notable customers: Villa Italian Kitchen, Rush Bowls and Creams Cafe.

What's new: In March 2026, Quantic POS announced an integration with Como Sense, according to a third-party company profile. A September 2026 release reportedly added AI-written campaign summaries in beta and a redesigned member import tool.

Key features:

  • Purchases linked to members. Guest profiles connect each POS transaction to an individual loyalty member.
  • Configurable programs. You set up points, rewards and member benefits to match your brand.
  • Branded app. A mobile app and digital membership give guests a reason to identify themselves.
  • Automated campaigns. Segments drive email, SMS and in-app messages without manual sends.
  • Easy migration. Member import tools bring over guests from an older club or mailing list.
  • POS coverage. Integrations include Revel, Oracle MICROS, PAR and Quantic, and Como Sense Express offers a lighter version for smaller operators.

Pricing: Custom pricing. Como doesn't publish prices.
