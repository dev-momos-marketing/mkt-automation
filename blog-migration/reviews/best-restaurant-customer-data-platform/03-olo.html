3. Olo

Category: Enterprise guest data platform

Olo is a restaurant commerce platform covering digital ordering, marketplace integration, payments, guest data and, since late 2025, loyalty. Thoma Bravo took the company private in September 2025, and it still operates under the Olo name. Its Guest Data Platform builds profiles from the ordering data already flowing through Olo, which suits brands that run most digital sales on it.

Notable customers: Ben & Jerry's, Gong Cha, Pilot Travel Centers and Jason's Deli.

What's new: In September 2025, Thoma Bravo closed its roughly $2 billion acquisition and Olo left the NYSE. In December 2025, Olo bought Spendgo and launched Olo Loyalty, and in March 2026 it unveiled the Olo App, a cross-brand consumer ordering app charging a flat per-order fee.

Key features:

  • Profiles from ordering. The Guest Data Platform and marketing tools use every web, app and catering order as a source of guest data.
  • Marketplace orders in the POS. Rails sends DoorDash, Uber Eats, Grubhub and other marketplace orders directly to the POS.
  • Loyalty on the same data. Olo Loyalty, from the Spendgo deal, lets in-store guests enroll with a phone number.
  • Shared guest login. Olo Accounts, formerly Borderless, gives guests one passwordless sign-in across Olo-powered restaurants.
  • Payments. Olo Pay handles card-not-present and in-store payments, linking spend to the guest who placed the order.
  • Menu control. You manage menus centrally across locations and channels.

Pricing: Custom pricing. You contact Olo for a quote.
