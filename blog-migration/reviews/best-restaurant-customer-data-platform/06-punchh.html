6. Punchh

Category: Enterprise loyalty & CDP suite

Punchh is an enterprise loyalty, offers and guest engagement platform for restaurant chains. PAR Technology acquired it in 2021, and it's now sold as PAR Punchh inside the PAR Engagement cloud, next to PAR Ordering and PAR's guest data. For a data-focused buyer, the draw is loyalty records sitting alongside POS and ordering data from the same vendor.

Notable customers: Krystal, Torchy's Tacos, Church's Texas Chicken and Salsarita's Fresh Mexican Grill.

What's new: In June 2025, PAR introduced PAR Engagement, bundling Punchh loyalty and offers with rebuilt ordering and guest data, and added membership tiers and rules that AI can switch on. Six months later, in December 2025, PAR announced Smart Passes, loyalty cards that live in Apple Wallet and Google Wallet.

Key features:

  • Program types. You can run points, visit-based or tiered membership programs.
  • Offers engine. Campaigns use segmentation and AI-driven rules to decide which guests get which offer.
  • Wallet enrollment. Smart Passes let guests sign up with one tap and track points and offers without downloading an app.
  • Connected channels. Integrations cover POS, online ordering, mobile apps and kiosks, so each channel adds to the guest record.
  • Cross-channel reporting. Guest data and reports span channels as part of PAR Engagement.
  • Gamified loyalty. PAR Games adds game-style experiences that run inside Punchh.

Pricing: Custom pricing. PAR doesn't publish prices for Punchh.
