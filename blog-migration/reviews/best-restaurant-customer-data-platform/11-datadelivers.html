11. DataDelivers

Category: First-party data enrichment CDP

DataDelivers is a restaurant-specific customer data platform that unifies POS, loyalty, online ordering and reservation data. Its Guest Connect product matches anonymous card transactions to marketable guest profiles. That's useful when most of your guests never join the loyalty program and you still want to reach and measure them.

Notable customers: TGI Fridays, Primanti Bros. and Kahala Brands (Cold Stone Creamery).

What's new: In 2025, DataDelivers released its Restaurant Guest Engagement Report on retention, drawing on data from tens of millions of guests at 2,000+ restaurant locations. It hasn't published dated product launches in 2026.

Key features:

  • Single guest view. POS, loyalty, e-club, ordering and reservation data are merged into one record per guest.
  • Non-loyalty guests. Guest Connect compares anonymous card and POS transactions with a proprietary consumer dataset to profile guests outside your program.
  • Record enrichment. Appended data fills gaps in thin profiles that only hold a name or email.
  • Audience building. Segments feed targeted email and digital campaigns.
  • New-opening prospects. You can target likely guests around a restaurant before it opens.
  • Visit and sales measurement. Campaign results are tied to visits and sales, and integrations include an Olo partnership.

Pricing: Custom pricing. DataDelivers doesn't publish prices.
