5. Thanx

Category: Loyalty-led CDP

Thanx is a guest engagement platform for restaurant brands that grows its guest database through loyalty. Card-linked and app-based programs, a CRM, marketing automation, first-party ordering and AI agents named ThanxAI, SegmentAI and RecoveryAI all write to the same guest record. Card-linked earning matters for data capture, because guests are identified by the card they pay with even if they never open an app.

Notable customers: Velvet Taco, Taco Bueno, Salt & Straw and The Big Greek Cafe.

What's new: In March 2026, Deliverect and Thanx partnered on first-party ordering paired with loyalty. In June 2026, a Kea integration let phone-order guests join loyalty, and in July 2026 Thanx released RecoveryAI, which brands pay for only when a lost guest returns.

Key features:

  • Payment card recognition. Guests are matched to their profile by payment card in store and online, which widens the pool of guests you can identify.
  • Segments in plain words. SegmentAI turns a description, such as weekday regulars who skipped last month, into an audience.
  • Lifecycle automation. Campaigns run on their own for new, loyal and lapsed guests, including winback offers.
  • Bad-order follow-up. RecoveryAI contacts guests after a poor digital ordering experience and tries to earn the next visit.
  • Owned ordering data. A branded app and Thanx Web ordering keep online orders tied to known guests.
  • Rewards beyond discounts. Access Pass offers experiences, secret menu items and early access alongside points and tiers.

Pricing: Custom pricing. Thanx doesn't publish prices, and a quote reflects your locations, unit volume and chosen modules.
