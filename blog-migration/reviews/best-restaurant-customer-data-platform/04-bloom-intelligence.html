4. Bloom Intelligence

Category: Guest data & marketing automation

Bloom Intelligence is a restaurant guest data and marketing platform that assembles one profile from WiFi logins, POS checks, online orders, reservations, reviews, surveys and ad data. It then puts that profile to work in automated campaigns, review replies and lapsed-guest recovery. The company now describes itself as a revenue operating system for restaurants.

Notable customers: Roka Akor, Corky's Kitchen & Bakery, Beachside Hospitality Group and Divieto Ristorante.

What's new: The company hasn't put out dated product news in 2025 or 2026, so check recent changes with its sales team during a demo.

Key features:

  • Wide data capture. Guest WiFi, POS, ordering, reservations, website visits, reviews, surveys and Google and Meta ads all feed the same guest record.
  • Restaurant integrations. It connects with platforms such as Toast, Olo, OpenTable and Cisco Meraki.
  • Lapsed-guest campaigns. Automated email and SMS flows target guests who have stopped coming or look likely to drift away.
  • Ads matched to checks. Ad attribution links paid campaigns to completed POS checks, so you see which spend produced visits.
  • Review replies. AI drafts responses on Google, Yelp, TripAdvisor and Facebook.
  • Bloom AI. An assistant answers plain-language questions about your guests, segments and lifetime value.

Pricing: Custom pricing. Bloom Intelligence quotes each restaurant group individually and lists no prices.
