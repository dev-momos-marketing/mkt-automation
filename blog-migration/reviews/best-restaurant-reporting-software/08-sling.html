8. Sling

Category: Free-First Employee Scheduling & Team Communication

Owned by Toast since 2021, Sling is a scheduling, time tracking and team messaging app for shift-based businesses, and sold both on its own and inside Toast as Sling by Toast. Its reporting is lighter than the enterprise tools on this list: on paid plans you get labor cost and overtime figures next to the schedule, which is often enough for an independent restaurant or a small chain.

Notable customers: Sling doesn't list restaurant customers by name on its site.

What's new: Sling hasn't shared dated product announcements for 2025 or 2026; recent Toast help articles mainly cover how it works with Toast POS.

Key features:

  • Template-based schedules. You build weeks by dragging shifts and reusing templates, giving you a consistent plan to compare actual hours against.
  • Labor cost while you schedule. Paid plans show what a draft schedule will cost and flag overtime, so budget problems surface before the week starts.
  • Clock-in records. The time clock on paid plans logs when staff actually start and stop, which feeds the hours side of your labor reports.
  • Toast sales in the schedule. The Toast integration brings over employees, wages, positions and POS sales, letting you set staffing against real revenue.
  • Team messaging and tasks. Chat threads, a newsfeed and to-do lists hold handover notes for each shift, with mobile alerts for open shifts and swaps.

Pricing: Scheduling is free, with a 30-user cap for accounts not set up through Toast. Paid tiers are charged per user each month: $2.00 for Premium or $4.00 for Business, or $1.70 and $3.40 if you pay yearly, after a 15-day trial. Every Toast POS package comes with Sling Lite, and Toast restaurants can upgrade to Sling Pro in Toast Shop.
