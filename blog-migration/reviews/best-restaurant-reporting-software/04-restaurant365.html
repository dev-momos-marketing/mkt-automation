4. Restaurant365

Category: Accounting & operations reporting for multi-unit groups

Restaurant365 is a cloud back-office system for restaurant management that puts restaurant accounting, inventory, food costing, scheduling and payroll under one login. For reporting, the appeal is that every number traces back to the general ledger.

Food cost, labor and sales reports pull from the same books your accountant closes, so the P&L you review on Monday matches what operations saw during the week. Since 2026, an AI layer called R365 AI sits on top of that P&L data.

Notable customers: Villa Restaurant Group, Wholly Stromboli and Johnson's Landing.

What's new: Restaurant365 used the May 2026 National Restaurant Association Show to unveil R365 AI, an engine trained on the complete restaurant P&L. It drives new AI dashboards and an AI advisor, plus a labor suite and AI-built schedules (scheduling is in early access for now). A month later, in June 2026, Kate Hastings joined as Chief Customer Officer.

Key features:

  • Restaurant accounting built in. Supplier invoices flow in without manual keying, bank reconciliation happens in the same tool, and groups that own several legal entities get a P&L for each.
  • Theoretical vs. actual food cost. Recipe costing and inventory counts feed a variance report that shows how far real usage drifted from what recipes predicted.
  • Daily sales in the books. Your POS sends every day's sales totals straight to the ledger, so nobody rekeys totals before a report can run.
  • Labor tied to forecasts. Scheduling, payroll and tip handling for hourly teams connect to sales forecasts, which puts labor percentage in the same report as revenue.
  • Portfolio budgeting. Multi-location reports compare budget with actual results and can track franchise royalties across your units.
  • AI advisor. R365 AI dashboards flag P&L, labor and cost changes and answer questions about them in plain language.

What makes them stand out: Accounting and operations live in one system, so the reports your controller and your area managers read come from the same records.

Pricing: Plans are bundles priced per location, paid each quarter or each year, with bigger groups getting a custom quote. Figures differ across Restaurant365's own pages, so get a written number from sales.

It's a great fit for: Multi-location groups and franchisees that want reporting held to accounting standards inside one platform.
