14. TouchBistro

Category: POS-Based Sales & Performance Reporting

TouchBistro is an iPad POS and management system whose reporting and analytics come built in, with paid add-ons for payments, online ordering, reservations, inventory and labor. Harris, an operating group of Constellation Software, acquired the company in July 2026, and the product is still sold as TouchBistro. Its reports suit independent full-service restaurants and small groups that want sales, menu and staff numbers without buying a separate BI tool.

Notable customers: Café Crêpe (multi-location group with central commissary), Uncle Tetsu (multi-unit QSR, North America), Koyo (sushi, Queens NY) and Masseria.

What's new: Harris announced its purchase of TouchBistro in July 2026, said it would keep investing in the product and brought the TouchBistro team into Harris. Terms weren't disclosed, though The Globe and Mail put the price at about $100 million. No new reporting features have been dated since.

Key features:

  • Sales by hour, item and server. Live reports split revenue by daypart, by dish and by the employee who rang it in, and owners can review them remotely via cloud access.
  • Menu mix. Item performance analysis shows which dishes sell and which only take up space on the menu.
  • Shift and server results. Performance reports by shift and server help you coach staff and staff the busiest periods.
  • Discounts, voids and refunds. Exception tracking makes unusual comps or voids easy to spot in the daily numbers.
  • Inventory and labor modules. Optional modules add stock and labor data, so cost reports can sit next to sales in the same system.

Pricing: Point of Sale software starts at $69 per location per month, with hardware sold separately. The Essentials bundle with payments and hardware is listed from $119 per month on the pricing page (the homepage has shown $99). Online ordering, kitchen display and other add-ons cost extra, and enterprise is quoted.
