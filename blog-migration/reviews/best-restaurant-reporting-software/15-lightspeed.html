15. Lightspeed

Category: Restaurant cloud POS & multi-location management software

Made by Lightspeed Commerce (listed on the NYSE and TSX), Lightspeed Restaurant is a cloud POS that combines floor and order handling, built-in payments, stock tracking and roll-up reporting across locations. In Europe it runs as the K-Series platform.

For reporting, two recent additions stand out: Tempo, which measures how tables and orders flow through a dining room, and Lightspeed AI, which lets you ask questions about sales and operations in plain language instead of digging through report menus.

Notable customers: Five Guys (Koeppel Companies franchise), Dean & David and Cafe Lola (Johnson City, Tenn.).

What's new: In November 2025, Lightspeed introduced Tempo, pacing intelligence built on live dining-flow data for table pacing, order flow and occupancy, starting with flagship customers. Lightspeed AI arrived in January 2026 with conversational assistants inside Lightspeed Restaurant, and a March 2026 update added Lightspeed Tasks digital checklists plus table pacing and reservation improvements.

Here's what the platform covers:

Key features:

  • Consolidated group reports. Menus are managed centrally and sales roll up across locations, so you compare stores in one report.
  • Menu performance. Inventory and recipe tracking feed item reports that support menu engineering decisions.
  • Service pacing data. Tempo shows how long tables wait at each course and how orders move, which helps full-service rooms find slow spots.
  • Answers from Lightspeed AI. Built-in assistants respond to questions about sales and operations, cutting the time spent building reports by hand.
  • Payments in the same data. Lightspeed Payments, with tap-to-pay on mobile and payment at the table, keeps tender data alongside orders for cleaner reconciliation.

What makes them stand out: It works for one restaurant on day one and keeps the same reports as you add locations, with pacing data that most POS reporting lacks.

Pricing: Lightspeed's published US plans run from $189/month (Essential) to $399/month (Premium). Processing fees, reservations, KDS screens and other add-ons are billed on top, and bigger groups receive custom quotes.

It's a great fit for: Bars, full-service dining rooms and expanding groups looking for detailed sales, menu and pacing reports from their cloud POS.
