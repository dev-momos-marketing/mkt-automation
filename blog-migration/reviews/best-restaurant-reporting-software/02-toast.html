2. Toast

Category: Restaurant POS with built-in reporting & analytics

Toast is a restaurant-specific POS and management platform, publicly traded on the NYSE, whose modules also cover online orders, the kitchen screens, payroll, guest marketing and loyalty. Its reports draw on all of those modules, so sales, labor and guest data sit in the same place without a separate connector.

That scale matters when you compare restaurants against each other. Toast said it had roughly 180,000 live locations as of June 30, 2026, after adding about 9,500 net new locations in its second quarter.

Notable customers: Captain Parker's Pub, Kaldi's Coffee and Craft Lounge.

What's new: Toast IQ Grow debuted with the spring 2026 release in May: a premium tier where an AI Marketing Agent works alongside a human success manager for marketing; the same release brought back Toast Local as a guest app that now carries loyalty. Two months later, in July 2026, Toast announced a 2026-2027 advisory board of independent restaurant customers.

Key features:

  • Sales reporting at the source. Every check rung on Toast terminals, kiosks or Toast Go handhelds lands in the same reports, so daily sales figures need no reconciling.
  • Labor next to sales. Because scheduling (Sling by Toast) and payroll run in the same system as the POS, you can read each shift's labor cost against the revenue it brought in.
  • Menu and price control. Central menu and pricing management keeps items consistent across sites, so item-level reports compare like with like.
  • Guest and loyalty data. Gift card, loyalty and campaign results (email or SMS) feed the same account, showing which guests come back and what they order.
  • Multi-location views. Group operators can review performance by store or across the whole portfolio, with Toast IQ adding AI features on top.

What makes them stand out: Because Toast runs the POS itself, its reports start from first-party transaction data and skip the sync delays third-party tools can introduce.

Pricing: Rates come from a Toast sales rep. The company has promoted a free Starter Kit as well as paid tiers in the past, and you should check toasttab.com for today's figures before budgeting.

It's a great fit for: Restaurants already on Toast POS that want sales, labor and guest reporting without buying another tool.
