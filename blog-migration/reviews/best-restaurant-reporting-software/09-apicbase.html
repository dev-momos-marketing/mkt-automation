9. ApicBase

Category: Inventory and Food Cost analytics

Built for foodservice businesses with several sites, Apicbase covers the back of house: recipes, menu costing, inventory, purchasing and production, and the company now markets it as AI-powered. For reporting, its strength is a single recipe library: food cost reports for every outlet start from the same standardized recipe data, which makes site-to-site comparisons fair.

Notable customers: Poule & Poulette, Pentahotels and The Avocado Show.

What's new: No dated product launch from Apicbase turned up for this year or last. In November 2025 it held APX·25, its Apicbase Partnerships Experience, a new event for hospitality, foodservice and technology leaders.

Key features:

  • Standard recipe costs. A central ingredient and recipe library calculates dish and menu costs automatically, so every site reports against identical specs.
  • Menu engineering from recipe data. Cost and allergen details flow from recipes into menu analysis, so you can rank dishes by the margin they return.
  • Stock across sites. Inventory counts give you current stock levels per location, the starting point for usage and variance reports.
  • Purchasing and suppliers. Purchase orders and vendor details share the platform with recipes, linking what you buy to what recipes say you should use.
  • Central kitchen planning. Production planning and sales-based forecasts help commissaries and multi-outlet groups report on output and food cost together.

Pricing: Custom. Apicbase prices each deal by location count and the modules you choose, and it doesn't list rates publicly.
