11. SynergySuite

Category: Enterprise Back-of-House Management Suite

Aimed at multi-unit restaurant chains, SynergySuite is a modular cloud platform for the back of house. Its modules span purchasing, stock, labor, recipe costs, food safety and reporting, and AI handles forecasting and ordering. Chains choose it to hold every location to the same cost targets and report on food and labor variance from one place.

Notable customers: Playa Bowls, Church's Chicken, Friendly's Restaurants and Shipley Do-Nuts.

What's new: SynergySuite closed $12 million of new funding in August 2025, earmarked for expanding its AI tools for chains and franchisees. In September 2025, Playa Bowls (200+ locations) signed on, using it to control food and labor costs and to automate purchasing and stock management.

Key features:

  • Theoretical vs. actual food cost. Recipe costing compares what each location should have used with what it did use, pinpointing the stores with the widest gap.
  • AI ordering. Demand forecasts drive suggested orders, and the resulting purchases flow into cost reports automatically.
  • Live stock by location. Counts and stock tracking across sites keep inventory values current for weekly reporting.
  • Labor with time and attendance. AI-assisted scheduling sits beside punch data, putting planned and actual labor in one report.
  • Cash and compliance controls. Void and refund controls, cash management and food safety checklists add loss prevention and audit data to the same system.
  • Chain-wide reporting. Group-level reports compare every store, fed by POS connections such as PAR Brink and Toast.

Pricing: Custom; SynergySuite quotes each chain on request.
