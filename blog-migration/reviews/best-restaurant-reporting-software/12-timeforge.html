12. TimeForge

Category: AI Demand Forecasting & Auto-Scheduling

TimeForge, a Lubbock, Texas, company dating back to 1998, sells labor software to restaurant and retail operators: shift schedules, a time clock with attendance records, and tools for staying within labor laws. Since August 2024, the AI forecasting engine from Lineup.ai has been part of the TimeForge platform, predicting both sales and staffing needs. For reporting, that pairing gives you a forecast, a schedule built from it and the actual hours worked, all in one place to compare.

Notable customers: Hard Eight BBQ and Great Harvest Bread Co. (Great Harvest Franchising, preferred vendor).

What's new: TimeForge became part of Square's app ecosystem in July 2025. The integration reads sales from Square POS and uses them to forecast labor needs and set staffing levels.

Key features:

  • Forecasts in 15-minute steps. Lineup.ai models read your POS history and predict sales and labor needs for each quarter hour, a fine-grained baseline for labor reports.
  • Schedules generated from demand. TimeForge drafts employee schedules that follow the forecast, so variance reports measure performance against a realistic plan.
  • Over-target alerts. Managers get a live warning from the attendance module once hours climb above the labor target, before the overspend reaches the weekly report.
  • Compliance records. Overtime rules and predictive (fair workweek) scheduling support keep the documentation regulated cities and states ask for.
  • Labor against sales by unit. Multi-unit reports set labor cost beside revenue for every location, using POS integrations including Toast and Square.
