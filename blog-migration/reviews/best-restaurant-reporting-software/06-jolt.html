6. Jolt

Category: Task & compliance performance reporting

Jolt gives restaurant and c-store crews a phone or tablet in place of the clipboard, so checklists, temperature sheets and prep labels become digital records. Its owner is now Digi International: the August 2025 deal moved Jolt under Digi's SmartSense division, while the product kept its own name and website.

For reporting, Jolt turns what happens on each shift into data you can review: which tasks got done, who signed them off, and which temperature readings fell outside range.

Notable customers: A&W Restaurants and White Spot Hospitality.

What's new: Digi paid about $145.5 million, and the purchase was completed in August 2025. Five months later, at NRF 2026 in January, the combined business showed SmartSense ONE: seven core modules that mix Jolt's task tools with SmartSense monitoring.

Key features:

  • Proof that tasks happened. Digital checklists capture photos and a manager's sign-off, so completion reports show evidence along with the checkmarks.
  • Food safety records. Staff enter temperature readings on the device, while optional SmartSense sensors watch coolers around the clock, for an audit trail that needs no clipboard.
  • Labels and date codes. Prep and date code labels print from the app, which helps keep expiry records consistent between shifts.
  • Scheduling and time clock. Shift schedules and punch records live next to checklist results, so you can link missed tasks to staffing gaps.
  • Location comparisons. Multi-location reports rank stores on task completion and performance, with POS integrations such as PAR Brink adding sales context.

What makes them stand out: Jolt reports on frontline execution shift by shift, a layer of accountability that sales and labor reports rarely capture.

Pricing: Jolt doesn't list prices; you get a custom quote after talking with sales.

It's a great fit for: Franchise and quick-service groups that want shift-level compliance reporting, particularly those weighing SmartSense temperature sensors too.
