10. MarginEdge

Category: Back-Office Automation & Real-Time Food Cost

Every morning, MarginEdge turns the previous day's invoices and POS sales into food cost, inventory and P&L numbers for restaurant operators. One subscription bundles bill pay, supplier ordering, recipe costing and invoice handling, so independents and small groups can watch margins daily rather than waiting for the books to close.

Notable customers: Skopos Hospitality Group, Crooked Hammock Brewery, South Block and The Tavern at Ivy City Smokehouse.

What's new: April 2025 marked a decade of MarginEdge, which said it had reached 10,000 restaurant customers. Then in August 2026 it closed $80 million, part Series D equity and part debt, in a round Schooner Capital shared the lead on with Ten Coves Capital. The company plans to spend it on new AI tools built from the purchasing data it handles.

Key features:

  • Invoices without typing. Invoices arrive by phone photo, email forward or EDI feed, and MarginEdge processes each one so costs reach your reports within days.
  • Daily food and beverage cost. Sales from your POS are matched with invoice spend to produce a cost percentage each day.
  • Price-change alerts. You choose items to watch and get notified when a supplier raises the price.
  • Recipe and menu-item costing. Inventory counts and costed recipes show the margin on each dish using current prices.
  • Budgets and P&L. Budget tracking and P&L views sync with accounting software such as QuickBooks, and the platform connects to dozens of POS systems.

Pricing: A single flat monthly fee per location includes unlimited invoices and bill pay. The vendor's site has shown $330; independent pricing trackers in 2026 say it's now $350, with Freepour (bar inventory) at an extra $150. Confirm before you sign.
