3. 7Shifts

Category: Labor reporting & workforce analytics

7shifts is a workforce platform used only by restaurants, with shift schedules, a time clock, tip pooling and payroll in one app, plus staff training added in 2026. Its reports focus on labor, the cost you can adjust fastest from one week to the next.

You see labor spend against sales, scheduled versus actual hours and attendance patterns per location, which turns a schedule into something you can measure.

Notable customers: HCI Hospitality (Freddy's Frozen Custard & Steakburgers franchisee), Boston Pizza (Regina), Conshy Girls Restaurant Group and Pitango Gelato.

What's new: Employee Training arrived in September 2026: managers create courses with quizzes, give them to staff by role, department or site, and see completion rates in the scheduling app. Since July 2025, the plans have been called Comp (free), Essentials, Pro and Premium.

Key features:

  • Labor budgets against sales. Labor-cost reports set spend beside revenue for each location, so you can see which days ran heavy before payroll closes.
  • Forecast-based schedules. Managers drag shifts into place with projected sales and labor beside them, giving every schedule a target you can report against later.
  • Time clock tied to the schedule. Schedule enforcement works through POS and payroll integrations, keeping early clock-ins and unplanned overtime out of your numbers.
  • Tip records. Tip pooling, management and payouts are tracked in the app, which makes tip reporting easier to audit.
  • Training completion. Onboarding packages and in-app courses show which team members have completed required training at each site.

What makes them stand out: Its reports are designed around shift-based, high-turnover hospitality teams, so store managers can act on labor numbers the same week.

Pricing: A single location with 15 or fewer staff can use Comp at no cost. Billed annually, each location pays $39.99 a month for Essentials (capped at 30 employees), $79.99 for Pro or $134.99 for Premium. Enterprise deals are quoted, and payroll costs extra.

It's a great fit for: Operators who want close, location-level reporting on labor cost and schedule performance.
