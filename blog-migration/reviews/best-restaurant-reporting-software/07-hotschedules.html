7. HotSchedules

Category: Enterprise Labor Forecasting & Workforce Analytics

Since its 2019 merger with Fourth, HotSchedules has been the scheduling and labor piece of Fourth's platform, which also offers inventory, HR and payroll; the Fourth name now leads, while HotSchedules survives as the product name. Multi-unit and enterprise groups use it to forecast labor demand and report on labor cost against budget, with the Fourth iQ app surfacing alerts for managers.

Notable customers: Noodles & Company, Texas Corral, FreeRange Concepts and Snooze, an A.M. Eatery.

What's new: Starting in July 2025, AI Forecast began adjusting for ticketed shows close to a store, a concert or a play for example, with the setting switched on by default. Fourth iQ 2.0 followed in September 2025 with the iQ App for manager alerts on mobile, tablet or web, and iQ 2.2 in November 2025 added warnings about likely inventory shortfalls.

Key features:

  • AI labor forecasts. Forecasts combine sales history, weather and nearby events, so labor targets in your reports reflect expected traffic.
  • Schedule score. Demand-based schedules get a score for coverage and cost, which lets you compare schedule quality across stores.
  • Scheduled vs. actual. Labor budget tracking reports the gap between planned and worked hours, so overspend is visible before payroll runs.
  • Compliance controls. Time and attendance tools enforce labor law rules and keep the punch records you need for audits.
  • Manager alerts. The Fourth iQ App pushes recommendations across scheduling, HR and inventory to managers on the floor.
  • Connected modules. It links to Fourth inventory, HR and payroll and to restaurant POS systems, giving labor reports actual sales to measure against.

Pricing: Custom; Fourth doesn't publish prices, so you'll need a quote.
