16. Tenzo

Category: POS sales, labor, inventory, reservation analytics

Tenzo, based in London, is a reporting and forecasting platform that sits on top of the systems you already use, pulling POS, labor, inventory, reservation and review data into one dashboard. Of all the tools here, it's the one built purely for analysis: it doesn't count stock or cost recipes, and it focuses on telling multi-site operators in the UK and US how each location is performing.

Notable customers: Lettuce Entertain You, PrimoHoagies, Parker Restaurant Group and Pizza Pilgrims.

What's new: In April 2026, Tenzo raised a $5M first close of a Series A extension led by Edge VC, with plans to grow its US business and, over 18 months, offer twice as many integrations. The same announcement named Jane O'Riordan as board chair.

Key features:

  • Single performance dashboard. Reviews, reservations, labor, inventory and sales share one screen, so you stop exporting data from five systems into a spreadsheet.
  • Forecasts that account for context. Machine learning predicts sales using weather, holidays and local events, then sends forecasts to scheduling tools such as Planday and Deputy.
  • Site benchmarking. Location comparisons highlight your best and weakest stores on the same measures.
  • Scheduled summaries. Tenzo sends daily and weekly performance reports automatically to the people who need them.
  • Query from AI assistants. An MCP connector lets your team ask questions of Tenzo data from AI assistants.

Pricing: Custom; Tenzo doesn't publish its prices.
