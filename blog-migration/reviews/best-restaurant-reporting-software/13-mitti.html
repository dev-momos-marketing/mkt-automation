13. Mitti

Mitti, formerly SafetyCulture, is a frontline operations platform for inspections, checklists, corrective actions, training, sensors and asset tracking across many sites; the company took the new name in August 2026. Restaurant groups use it to turn food safety, hygiene and brand-standard audits into scores and trends they can compare from one location to the next.

Notable customers: Snooze, an A.M. Eatery, Fishbowl, Trippas White Group and Sodexo.

What's new: In August 2026, SafetyCulture relaunched as Mitti, pitching a single system to run frontline teams that spans software, sensors, supply ordering and insurance. The rebrand came with an AI Assistant, AI issue creation, monitoring agents and 5 Star Benchmarking, which gives each location of a multi-site operator a single score. Since July 2026, every plan, including Free, also comes with monthly AI credits, according to a pricing tracker.

Key features:

  • Scored inspections. Digital checklists record photos, notes and scores, and a large public template library helps you start audits quickly.
  • One number per site. Cross-location analytics, including 5 Star Benchmarking, roll each restaurant's results into a score you can rank and track.
  • Issues with owners. Corrective actions get assigned to a named person with a due date, so reports show what was fixed and what is overdue.
  • Sensor alerts. Temperature and environmental sensors send alerts and add continuous readings to your compliance history.
  • AI monitoring. An AI assistant, AI-created issues and agents that watch routine checks reduce the manual work of spotting problems in audit data.

Pricing: Third-party listings show a free plan for teams of up to 10 and Premium at about $24 per seat per month on annual billing, or $29 month to month, with cheaper Lite seats for staff who only complete inspections and training. Enterprise is quoted by site. Pricing hadn't been confirmed on Mitti's own site after the rebrand.
