6. 7Shifts

Category: Labor and workforce performance intelligence

7shifts focuses solely on restaurant teams: schedules, clock-ins, tip distribution, payroll and, as of 2026, staff training. From a BI point of view, its main contribution is labor spend set against each location's sales, often the second figure operators look at after revenue.

Notable customers: HCI Hospitality (a Freddy's Frozen Custard & Steakburgers franchisee), Boston Pizza (Regina), Conshy Girls Restaurant Group and Pitango Gelato.

What's new: Premium subscribers gained Employee Training in September 2026. With it, managers create lessons and quizzes, target them to particular roles, departments or sites, and watch completion rates inside the scheduling app. Earlier, in July 2025, the plans were reorganized into Comp, Essentials, Pro and Premium.

Key features:

  • Demand-led schedules. Managers drag shifts into place while projected sales and labor needs sit alongside, so staffing tracks expected traffic.
  • Labor cost against sales. Labor budgeting and reporting compare what you spend on staff with what each location sells.
  • Punches held to plan. Linked to your POS and payroll, the time clock enforces the published schedule, keeping worked hours near what was budgeted.
  • Tips handled in the app. Tip pooling, tip management and payouts run alongside scheduling, with restaurant payroll as an add-on.
  • Mobile self-service for staff. From a phone, an employee can pick up a colleague's shift, mark the days they're free or send a group message.

Pricing: Single-site teams of 15 or fewer can stay on Comp for free. Essentials, limited to 30 staff, is $39.99 per site monthly when you pay for the year; Pro is $79.99 and Premium $134.99. Paying monthly instead costs $44.99 (Essentials), $89.99 (Pro) or $149.99 (Premium). You can trial Pro for 14 days, payroll is billed on top, and Enterprise needs a quote.
