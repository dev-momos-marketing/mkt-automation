10. Avero

Avero reads the transactions in your POS and other operating systems and reshapes them into reports on revenue, labor and individual servers. Its focus on shift-, manager- and server-level accountability makes it a fit for full-service and fine-dining groups running several venues.

Notable customers: Wolfgang Puck Worldwide, Patina Restaurant Group, Crafted Hospitality (Tom Colicchio) and Four Seasons.

What's new: Recent dated announcements from Avero are hard to find, so ask the sales team about its current roadmap before you commit.

Key features:

  • A reporting layer over the POS. POS and operational data from multiple venues land in one place for group-wide reporting.
  • Accountability by shift and person. Sales break down by shift, manager and server, so you can see who drives results on a given night.
  • Server scorecards. Scorecards and benchmarking give front-of-house managers a factual basis for coaching.
  • Trends across venues. Revenue and guest count trends can be compared between locations over time.
  • Discount and comp tracking. Promotions, comps and discounts are tracked, so you can see what they cost.
  • Mobile access for managers. A mobile app lets managers check performance when they're away from the office.

Pricing: Avero lists no prices publicly, so each group is quoted individually.
