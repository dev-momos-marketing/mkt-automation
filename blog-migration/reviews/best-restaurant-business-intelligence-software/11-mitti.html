11. Mitti

Category: Operations, food safety and brand-standard intelligence

Mitti is the name SafetyCulture has used since August 2026. The mobile app covers audits, checklists, follow-up actions, staff training, sensor monitoring and equipment records for teams spread over many sites. In a restaurant BI stack, it supplies the compliance and standards data your POS never sees, scored so you can rank locations against each other.

Notable customers: Snooze, an A.M. Eatery, Fishbowl, Trippas White Group and Sodexo.

What's new: The August 2026 rebrand came with new AI tools: an assistant, automatic issue logging and agents that watch routine checks. It also introduced 5 Star Benchmarking, which rolls each location's results into one score. Before that, third-party price monitoring recorded AI credits landing in the Free, Premium and Enterprise tiers in July 2026.

Key features:

  • Audits done on a phone. Staff fill in digital checklists with photos, comments and scoring, starting from one of many public templates if you don't want to build your own.
  • One score per site. Analytics across locations, including 5 Star Benchmarking, let multi-site operators rank stores on standards.
  • Issues with owners and deadlines. Corrective actions are assigned to staff with due dates, so problems found in an audit get followed up.
  • Sensor alerts. Temperature and environmental sensors trigger alerts when readings go out of range.
  • AI for routine checks. An AI assistant, AI-created issues and monitoring agents take on repetitive oversight work.

Pricing: Up to 10 users pay nothing on the free tier. Outside listings quote Premium near $24 per user monthly when you commit for a year, $29 if you go month by month, and Lite seats cost less for people who only do inspections and training. Enterprise pricing is site-based and quoted. Mitti has not confirmed these figures on its own site since the rebrand, so check with the vendor.
