9. Oracle Food & Beverage Analytics

Category: Enterprise BI and revenue reporting for large restaurant groups

Oracle now sells this product as Simphony Reporting and Analytics, a module of the Simphony POS platform that grew out of MICROS. Head-office teams at groups with several brands and regions use it to report on everything Simphony captures, and to pipe that data into the wider BI tools their analysts already know.

Notable customers: Starbucks, Pret A Manger, Sonic and Sercotel.

What's new: At the end of March 2026, Oracle and NetSuite announced a joint AI-powered back office for restaurants. It takes sales from Simphony Cloud and other point-of-sale systems and uses them to run purchasing, stock, staff scheduling, production and cash handling in one place.

Key features:

  • Reporting by brand and region. Sales and revenue reports roll up across locations, brands and regions for head-office teams.
  • A BI API for your own tools. The Business Intelligence API sends data to Oracle Analytics Cloud or third-party BI platforms, so analysts can work in the tool they prefer.
  • Drill-down to the guest check. Check search lets you go from a report figure down to the image of the individual check.
  • Scheduled reports and alerts. Reports go out automatically, and mobile alerts flag critical events during service.
  • Waste captured at the POS. Staff record waste with reason codes during the business day, which feeds inventory tracking.

Pricing: Oracle doesn't publish Simphony prices; enterprise pricing is quoted by sales.
