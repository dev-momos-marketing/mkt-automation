1. Momos

Category: Guest experience and customer satisfaction intelligence

Momos runs an AI-driven guest experience hub for restaurant brands with many locations. Most BI tools in this list read your sales, labor and food cost numbers; Momos covers the layer they tend to miss, which is what guests say about each visit. Reviews, survey answers and support messages from every location flow into one place, where AI agents answer guests and the insights show your operations team which stores need attention.

Notable customers: Papa Murphy's, Potbelly, Condado Tacos and Caribou Coffee.

What's new: The 2025 release cycle brought staff-facing AI copilots, auto-generated ticket recaps, configurable AI brand rules and reports designed for phones first. Paytronix, Square and Otter joined the integration list that year. Potbelly signed on in 2025 too, consolidating guest messages, cases and reviews from 472 shops into one screen.

Key features:

  • A single feed of guest signals. The Unified Inbox combines POS data with Google reviews, direct messages on social channels and App Store comments, then uses AI triage and routing so each item reaches the right person.
  • Themes behind the ratings. Sentiment analysis groups what guests write by topic, so a recurring complaint about one store shows up as a trend instead of scattered comments.
  • Survey scores by location. Guest surveys feed metrics such as Net Promoter Score, giving you a satisfaction figure to put next to each store's sales.
  • Fast replies in your voice. Alfie, the Experience Agent, responds to guests within minutes, recalls their past visits and preferences, and passes tricky cases to your team.
  • Recovery you can measure. Through your rewards program, Alfie can credit points or issue a coupon or offer, which lets you count how many unhappy guests return.
  • Coverage across languages. Real-time translation lets each location read and answer feedback written in other languages.

Pricing: Pricing is custom, and you get a quote by booking a demo.
