7. HotSchedules

Category: Enterprise labor forecasting and workforce intelligence

HotSchedules and Fourth combined in 2019; the company now trades as Fourth, and HotSchedules lives on as the name of its scheduling and labor module. It combines AI labor forecasting with the Fourth iQ manager app, which suits multi-unit and enterprise groups that want labor decisions backed by demand data.

Notable customers: Noodles & Company, Texas Corral, FreeRange Concepts and Snooze, an A.M. Eatery.

What's new: From July 2025, AI Forecast switched on a default adjustment for nearby ticketed shows, so a concert or play down the street now shapes that site's projection. Fourth released Fourth iQ 2.0 with the iQ App in September 2025, and version 2.2 in November 2025 added alerts that warn managers of inventory shortfalls.

Key features:

  • Headcount forecasts. Past sales, the weather outlook and what's happening locally all feed an AI model that estimates the staff each shift requires.
  • Scored schedules. Schedules are built from forecast demand and given a score for coverage and cost, so managers can see the trade-off before publishing.
  • Budget vs. actual tracking. Labor budget reports compare scheduled hours with actual hours across locations.
  • Compliant punches. Time and attendance tracking comes with controls built around labor law requirements.
  • Recommendations on mobile. The Fourth iQ App sends managers alerts and suggestions covering workforce, HR and inventory.
  • Links to the wider Fourth suite. HotSchedules connects to Fourth's inventory, HR and payroll modules as well as restaurant POS systems.

Pricing: Fourth doesn't publish prices; pricing is quoted by sales.
