8. TouchBistro

Category: Built-in POS reporting for sales and staff performance

TouchBistro runs on iPads as a restaurant point of sale, and its reporting screens come included, with optional modules for payments, ordering, bookings, stock and labor. Since July 2026 it has belonged to Harris, part of Constellation Software, though the brand hasn't changed. For an independent full-service restaurant or a small group, it means sales intelligence lives in the POS itself, with no extra BI subscription to manage.

Notable customers: Café Crêpe, Uncle Tetsu, Koyo and Masseria.

What's new: The July 2026 sale to Harris closed with the TouchBistro team staying on and Harris pledging further product investment. Neither side released the deal terms.

Key features:

  • Live sales reports from the cloud. Sales by time period, menu item and staff member are available as they happen, from anywhere.
  • Menu mix analysis. Item performance reports show which dishes sell and which ones take up space on the menu.
  • Server and shift results. Performance reporting by server and shift helps you spot coaching needs on the floor.
  • Exceptions in plain view. Discounts, voids and refunds are tracked, so unusual patterns stand out.
  • Modules that extend the data. Inventory and labor modules, along with add-ons for ordering, reservations and loyalty, bring more of the operation into the same reports.

Pricing: The core Point of Sale software is $69/month per location before hardware. The pricing page shows an Essentials bundle, which adds payments and devices, from $119/month. Extras such as online ordering and kitchen display are charged separately, and enterprise deals are quoted.
