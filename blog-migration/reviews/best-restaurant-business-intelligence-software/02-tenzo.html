2. Tenzo

Category: POS sales, labor, inventory and reservation intelligence

Tenzo, headquartered in London, works as an intelligence layer above your existing POS, scheduling, stock, booking and review tools. Because it reconciles those feeds into a single dashboard, a group with sites on both sides of the Atlantic can compare branches and plan staffing off one agreed set of figures.

Notable customers: Lettuce Entertain You, PrimoHoagies, Parker Restaurant Group and Pizza Pilgrims.

What's new: April 2026 brought a $5M first close on Tenzo's extended Series A, with Edge VC leading. The funding is meant to double the number of connected systems within 18 months and grow the American business; Jane O'Riordan took over as chair of the board.

Key features:

  • Cross-system reporting. Sales, labor, stock, bookings and reviews sit on one screen, which saves your managers from exporting and merging spreadsheets every Monday.
  • Forecasts with local context. Its machine learning model adjusts expected sales for the weather, holidays and events in the area, and those projections can flow into Planday or Deputy for shift planning.
  • Site benchmarking. Ranking locations side by side makes it clear which sites lead and which ones need a closer look.
  • Scheduled digests. Performance summaries are emailed every day and every week without anyone building them, so area managers start the day with the figures already in hand.
  • Chat-style lookups. Tenzo offers an MCP connector, so someone on your team can ask an AI assistant about last week's numbers and get answers drawn from Tenzo.

Pricing: Tenzo keeps its prices private, so expect a tailored quote after a sales call.
