4. Apicbase

Category: Inventory and food cost intelligence

Apicbase handles the kitchen side of multi-site foodservice, and the company now markets the platform as AI-powered. Because recipes, menu costing, stock, purchasing and production share one database, the food cost figures you report across sites come from the very records your chefs work with daily.

Notable customers: Poule & Poulette, Pentahotels and The Avocado Show.

What's new: There's no recently dated product launch on record from Apicbase. Its latest public milestone was APX·25 in November 2025, an inaugural partnerships event that gathered executives from hospitality, foodservice and tech.

Key features:

  • Costing from one recipe library. Each dish is costed automatically from a central ingredient list, so a supplier price change flows through to every menu item.
  • Menu engineering on real data. Recipe data powers menu engineering and allergen details, which helps you judge which dishes earn their place.
  • Stock visibility by site. Inventory counts and stock levels are tracked across locations, giving you a basis for comparing usage between kitchens.
  • Purchasing and production planning. Supplier ordering and production plans for central kitchens and multiple outlets run from the same system.
  • Food cost reporting with forecasts. Sales-based forecasting and cross-site food cost reports draw on POS and ordering integrations such as Square and Deliverect.

Pricing: No list prices are published. The quote you receive scales with your site count and the modules you switch on.
