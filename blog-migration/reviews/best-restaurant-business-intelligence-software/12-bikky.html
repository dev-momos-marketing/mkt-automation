12. Bikky

Category: Guest data and marketing intelligence

Bikky is a customer data platform aimed at restaurant chains. It stitches transactions from POS, digital ordering, payments and loyalty into one profile per guest, then adds guest analytics, campaign measurement and an AI analyst called Data Assistant. That gives your BI a guest-level view of visits and spend to sit beside store-level sales.

Notable customers: Mellow Mushroom, Bubbakoo's Burritos, Condado Tacos and Tiki Taco.

What's new: Bubbakoo's Burritos became a customer in April 2026, using the platform as the base for personalized marketing and wider data planning. Mellow Mushroom's announcement from September 2025 credits Bikky with guiding its marketing and growth choices, and described Data Assistant as newly released.

Key features:

  • One record per person. Bikky tidies and merges duplicates across stores and channels, so a regular who orders online and dines in shows up once.
  • Retention and lifetime value. Analytics on visit frequency, retention and lifetime value show how loyal your guests really are.
  • Menu tests tied to behavior. Menu and channel performance link back to guest behavior, which helps when you judge a limited-time offer.
  • Proof of marketing ROI. Campaign and loyalty results are measured, so you can see which promotions paid off.
  • Plain-language questions. Data Assistant answers questions about your guest data without a query or a request to an analyst.
  • Exports to your warehouse. Data can be shared to Snowflake, over SFTP or as CSV files for your own BI tools.

Pricing: Prices aren't public; Bikky quotes each brand separately.
