3. Toast

Category: POS-driven sales and revenue intelligence

Toast, which trades publicly under the ticker TOST, runs point of sale for restaurants alongside ordering, kitchen screens, payroll, marketing and loyalty. For business intelligence, its value is that every transaction already lives in the same system, so multi-location reports come straight from the source data without a separate integration project.

Notable customers: Captain Parker's Pub, Kaldi's Coffee and Craft Lounge.

What's new: The spring 2026 update, shipped in May, added a paid tier called Toast IQ Grow that bundles an AI Marketing Agent, and Toast Local came back as a guest app that now includes loyalty. Its Q2 2026 results, reported in August, put live locations at roughly 180,000 as of June 30.

Key features:

  • Reports across every store. Multi-location reporting rolls sales from all your sites into one view, so owners can compare performance without collecting files from each manager.
  • Menus and prices set centrally. Managing menus and pricing from one place keeps item data consistent, which makes item-level comparisons between locations reliable.
  • Labor data next to sales. Sling by Toast scheduling and payroll sit in the same platform as the POS, so labor cost and revenue come from one record.
  • Every channel in one dataset. In-store terminals, kiosks, Toast Go handhelds, online ordering and delivery integrations all feed the same sales history.
  • AI features in Toast IQ. Toast IQ adds AI tools to the platform, including an AI marketing agent for operators on the Toast IQ Grow package.

Pricing: Toast quotes plans through its sales team; check its pricing page for current options before you budget.
