8. Owner.com

Category: Restaurant direct ordering & customer retention software

Owner.com aims to move more of an independent restaurant's takeout away from delivery marketplaces and onto channels the restaurant controls. It builds your website with AI, takes commission-free orders through it and a branded app, and follows up with automated marketing and loyalty. In 2026 its product grew to include a POS and an AI phone line that takes orders.

Notable customers: Saffron Indian Restaurant, Goi Cuon, Doo-Dah Diner and Township Line Pizza.

What's new: Owner closed a $240M Series D in August 2026 with Goldman Sachs Alternatives as lead investor, putting its worth at $2.3B. The plan for that cash is to reach beyond restaurants toward AI software for many kinds of local shops, and reporting on the round confirmed that a POS and AI phone ordering are now part of the restaurant product.

Key features:

  • A website that ranks. AI drafts a restaurant site set up for search, so diners looking for your cuisine nearby can find you and order.
  • Direct ordering and catering. Pickup, delivery and catering orders come through your own site and branded app without a per-order commission to a marketplace.
  • Marketing on autopilot. Owner watches order history and sends a text or email to nudge guests who haven't been back in a while.
  • Loyalty built in. A rewards program gives regulars a reason to come back to your channel.
  • Phone orders handled by AI. An AI voice agent answers calls and puts orders straight into the system during a rush.
  • POS and guest support. Owner now offers a restaurant POS and handles customer support for your guests.

What makes them stand out: Website, ordering, app, loyalty and follow-up marketing come from one company and share the same order data, which suits owners without a marketing hire.

Pricing: Two published plans, identical in features and free of long contracts: Flex at $249/month, where you also give up 5% of every order, and Flat Rate at $499/month, where you keep the whole order. On the guest side, a separate 5% support fee shows up at checkout; according to Owner's blog, card processing costs 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction.

Things to consider: Owner is built around independents. Larger chains and operators who need reservations, back-office accounting or deep inventory will still pair it with other systems.

It's a great fit for: Neighborhood restaurants and owners with a handful of locations who want a larger share of orders to come direct and less dependence on third-party delivery apps.
