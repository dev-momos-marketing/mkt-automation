4. Marketman

Category: Restaurant inventory & food cost management software

MarketMan covers what happens between your suppliers and your kitchen: counting stock, costing recipes, placing orders and checking invoices. It runs in the cloud for single restaurants and multi-unit groups, and since 2026 a version of it is also sold inside Square for restaurants on that POS.

Notable customers: Westville Restaurant Group, ATFC, Ltd. and Taco del Mar (multi-unit franchisee).

What's new: Since April 2026, restaurants using Square in any market can add Square Restaurant Inventory by MarketMan, which brings ingredient and recipe management, supplier ordering, vendor records, scanned invoices and a waste log into Square's own dashboard.

Key features:

  • Counts by location. Your team walks the walk-in with a mobile device, and stock levels for that store refresh during the count.
  • Recipe costs that follow prices. Each menu item's cost is tied to the latest ingredient prices, so a jump in produce or protein shows up in your margins right away.
  • Ordering from par levels. The system drafts orders from par levels and emails them to vendors, replacing the clipboard order sheet.
  • Invoice scanning. Snap a photo of a delivery invoice and MarketMan records the new prices and highlights anything that moved, so you catch a supplier increase before the next order.
  • Waste logging. Recording waste over time shows which items and stations lose you the most product.
  • Food cost across sites. COGS and food cost reports compare locations, with POS and accounting integrations feeding the numbers.

What makes them stand out: The whole product centers on purchasing and food cost, so the path from a supplier invoice to a plate cost is short and easy to follow.

Pricing: No price list appears on MarketMan's website right now, and third-party trackers report recent increases, so ask MarketMan for a quote.

It's a great fit for: Chef-owners and purchasing managers, from one kitchen to a regional group, who feel food cost slipping and want ordering under control, plus Square restaurants that prefer an inventory add-on inside their POS.
