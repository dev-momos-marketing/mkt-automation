7. TripeSeat

Category: Restaurant event & private dining management software

When a rehearsal dinner or a large office holiday party comes in, Tripleseat is where the inquiry, the proposal, the signed contract, the banquet event order and the deposit all live. Restaurants, restaurant groups and hotels use it to run the private dining side of the business with the same discipline they apply to the main floor.

Here's a look at some of Tripleseat's tools:

Notable customers: Bentley's Oyster Bar & Grill (Corrigan Collection), McCormick & Kuleto's and Hakkasan Mayfair (TAO Group).

What's new: In July 2025, Tripleseat connected to Reserve with Google, so a planner searching on Google Search or Maps can send an event request straight to your sales inbox. That summer PartyPay card payments also went live outside the US, in the UK, Canada, Australia and Europe, and managers gained sales goals per user or per location.

Key features:

  • Leads from several doors. Inquiries arrive from your website form, TripleseatDirect and Google, and each becomes a trackable lead for your events manager.
  • Proposals to signatures. You build the proposal, turn it into a contract and collect an e-signature without printing anything.
  • BEOs the kitchen can use. Banquet event orders and event sheets pull menu, timing and headcount from the booking, so the chef and floor work from one document.
  • Room availability at a glance. One calendar shows which spaces are held or booked across every room and restaurant you manage.
  • Deposits and payments. PartyPay takes card deposits and final balances online, with processing charged as you go.
  • Revenue tracking. Guest history, sales goals and event revenue reports show which planners and locations bring in the most business.

What makes them stand out: Event sales usually sprawl across inboxes, spreadsheets and paper BEOs, and Tripleseat gathers that work into one record per event that sales, kitchen and finance can all open.

Pricing: Tripleseat doesn't publish software prices, so you'll need a quote. PartyPay processing is billed per transaction.

It's a great fit for: Restaurants, groups and hotels where private dining, large-party bookings or catering bring in a meaningful share of revenue.
