11. Sevenrooms

Category: Restaurant guest experience & retention software

SevenRooms combines reservations, table management, a guest database and marketing, so the host stand knows a guest's allergies, anniversaries and last few visits before they arrive. DoorDash completed its purchase of SevenRooms in June 2025, and the product is still sold under its own name with its leadership in place.

Recent releases lean on AI, from drafting review replies to answering the restaurant's phone.

Notable customers: Union Square Hospitality Group, Wolfgang Puck, Nobu Restaurants and Dishoom.

What's new: In July 2026, SevenRooms released guest intelligence tools led by Voice AI, built on ElevenLabs, which picks up calls, books tables and recognizes repeat guests, and now also cross-sells group bookings, collects payments and reports on performance. Earlier, in March 2025, it added AI review replies, feedback summaries and note polishing.

Key features:

  • Direct reservations and waitlist. Guests book through your own channels, and walk-ins join a managed waitlist.
  • AI-assisted seating. Table management suggests seating and pacing and forecasts covers for the night.
  • Profiles your team can act on. Each guest record holds preferences, allergies, occasions and visit history.
  • Segmented campaigns. Email and SMS go to groups you define, such as lapsed regulars or birthday guests this month.
  • An AI phone line. Voice AI answers calls and takes reservations when the host is busy.
  • Feedback, reviews and deposits. AI drafts review replies and summarizes feedback, and secure payments cover deposits for groups.

What makes them stand out: SevenRooms treats each reservation as part of an ongoing guest record, so service notes and marketing both draw on what you know about the person at the table.

Pricing: SevenRooms doesn't publish prices; it quotes each venue or group.

It's a great fit for: Table-service restaurants, hotel dining outlets, bars, clubs and multi-concept groups where repeat visits drive revenue and the team wants booking data and guest marketing under one roof.
