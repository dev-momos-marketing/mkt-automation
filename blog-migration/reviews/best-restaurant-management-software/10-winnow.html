10. Winnow

Category: Restaurant AI-powered food waste management software

Every plate scraped into the bin was paid for twice, once at purchase and again in the labor to prep it, yet most kitchens never measure it. Winnow puts a camera and a scale over the waste bin so its Winnow Vision system can recognize and weigh what gets thrown out, while a companion app called Foresight estimates tomorrow's demand so cooks can trim batch sizes before anything is wasted.

Its named customers are mostly hotel groups and other high-volume kitchens, where production and buffet waste add up quickly.

Notable customers: Hilton, IHG Hotels & Resorts, Accor, Marriott and Mandarin Oriental.

What's new: In September 2026, Winnow launched Winnow Foresight, which Hilton chefs helped design; it runs on a phone, uses hotel occupancy and service history to estimate how many portions of each dish you'll need, and requires no extra equipment. Two months earlier it acquired Lumitics of Singapore, another AI food waste specialist, to widen its reach in Asia-Pacific markets and the Middle East.

Key features:

  • Automatic waste capture. The Vision bin identifies and weighs each discarded item, so staff just throw and walk away.
  • Waste broken down by cause. Data is split by item, shift and stage, such as prep trim, spoilage or plate waste, with a cost attached.
  • Feedback at the bin. A screen in the kitchen shows cooks what they just threw away and what it cost.
  • Reports for chefs and managers. Dashboards compare sites, so a group can see which kitchens waste the most and why.
  • Forecasts to prep against. Foresight suggests how much of each item to make for a given service.
  • A manual option. Smaller kitchens can skip the camera and record waste by hand on a cheaper package.

What makes them stand out: Winnow turns waste into a number cooks see on the spot, which gives a kitchen team something concrete to change at the next prep.

Pricing: Winnow quotes each kitchen or group individually and lists no prices online.

It's a great fit for: High-volume kitchens with heavy production waste, such as hotel restaurants, buffets, contract caterers and large multi-site groups. Small quick-service units with little prep waste will get less out of it.
