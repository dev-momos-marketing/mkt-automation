9. Lightspeed

Category: Restaurant cloud POS & multi-location management software

Built by Lightspeed Commerce (LSPD on the NYSE and TSX), which also serves shops and retailers, Lightspeed Restaurant is the company's food-and-beverage POS. On an iPad it runs tables and orders, takes payments, tracks inventory and reports across locations, and in Europe the same platform is known as K-Series.

Lightspeed added AI assistants and service-pacing tools in 2025 and 2026, aimed mainly at full-service dining rooms.

Here's the range of tools in the Lightspeed restaurant platform:

Notable customers: Five Guys (Koeppel Companies franchise), Dean & David and Cafe Lola (Johnson City, Tenn.).

What's new: January 2026 brought Lightspeed AI, conversational helpers inside the restaurant POS that answer questions about your sales and operations. Two months later it shipped digital checklists through Lightspeed Tasks plus table pacing and reservation updates. Before that, in November 2025, it introduced Tempo, which turns what's happening on the floor right now into views of how fast tables move, how orders stack up and how full the room is.

Key features:

  • Floor and coursing on iPad. Servers work from a floor plan, fire courses in order and move tables around as the night changes.
  • Payments at the table. With Lightspeed Payments, guests can close the check at the table or tap a card on the server's phone, freeing up turns.
  • Kitchen routing. Screens on the line split each ticket so grill, fry and pass only see their own items.
  • Inventory and menu insight. Recipe-level stock tracking and menu performance reports show what sells and what it costs you.
  • Control for groups. Menus are managed centrally and reports roll up across locations, with online ordering, reservations and loyalty as add-ons.
  • Pacing and AI help. Tempo flags slow tables and kitchen backups, and Lightspeed AI lets managers ask about performance in plain language.

What makes them stand out: Lightspeed puts unusual attention on how service flows through a full dining room, with Tempo and table pacing built for operators who measure turns and course timing.

Pricing: American restaurants start on Essential for $189/month, while the Premium tier is priced from $399/month. Budget separately for card processing and extras like kitchen screens or the reservations module; big groups negotiate their own rates, and UK or Australian prices aren't the same.

It's a great fit for: Full-service restaurants, bars and growing multi-location groups who care about table turns and course timing as much as they care about the sales report.
