2. Restaurant365

Category: End-to-end restaurant management software

For a group that closes its books every month, Restaurant365 pulls the general ledger, inventory and food cost, staff schedules, payroll and reports into one cloud product organized around a shared P&L. It's designed for operators with several locations or franchise units, where finance and operations teams need to see the same numbers. A newer AI layer, R365 AI, reads that P&L data to point managers toward labor and cost problems.

Notable customers: Villa Restaurant Group, Wholly Stromboli and Johnson's Landing.

What's new: On the floor of the National Restaurant Association's yearly trade show in May 2026, Restaurant365 first demoed R365 AI: dashboards and an advisor powered by P&L data, a new labor management suite, and AI scheduling that early-access users get before everyone else. A month later it named Kate Hastings as Chief Customer Officer.

Key features:

  • Accounting made for restaurants. Your bookkeeper stops keying in produce and liquor bills by hand, reconciles the bank right where the ledger lives, and each entity in your group gets its own P&L.
  • Food cost you can trust. Inventory counts and recipe costs feed a report comparing what your menu should have used with what the kitchen actually went through.
  • Schedules built on sales. Managers plan shifts against forecast sales, so labor stays in line with the covers you expect.
  • Payroll for hourly teams. Pay runs and HR handle tipped staff, which saves your office from moving hours and tips between separate tools.
  • Sales posted from the POS. Integrations pull each day's sales into the ledger, so nobody keys in totals by hand at month end.
  • Group-level reporting. Budgets, multi-location reports and franchise royalty tracking give owners one view across every unit.

What makes them stand out: Accounting and day-to-day operations share one database, so a change in food cost or overtime shows up on the P&L without exports or reconciliation between systems.

Pricing: You pay per location for a bundle, invoiced every quarter or once a year, while bigger groups negotiate a custom quote. Restaurant365's various web pages quote different dollar figures, so get today's number directly from its team.

It's a great fit for: Franchisees and multi-unit operators whose controllers want accounting, stock and staffing on one P&L and fewer back-office tools to maintain.
