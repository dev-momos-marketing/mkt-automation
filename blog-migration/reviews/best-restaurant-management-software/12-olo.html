12. Olo

Category: Restaurant digital ordering & delivery infrastructure software

Olo sits behind the ordering buttons of many chain restaurant apps and websites. It runs branded ordering, feeds marketplace orders into the POS, handles payments and builds guest data and loyalty on top of all those transactions.

Thoma Bravo took Olo private in September 2025, and Olo continues to operate and sell under its own name.

Notable customers: Ben & Jerry's (Olo Ordering, Rails and Olo Pay), Gong Cha (Olo Rails), Pilot Travel Centers (Olo Rails) and Jason's Deli (Order suite, Olo Pay and Catering+).

What's new: In December 2025, Olo closed its acquisition of Spendgo and launched Olo Loyalty on top of its guest data tools. Then in March 2026, at Beyond4, Olo previewed the Olo App, its first product built for diners: one app where guests can order from any participating Olo brand, with restaurants paying a fixed amount per order in place of a commission. Industry outlets said it went live in October 2026.

Key features:

  • Your own ordering channels. Guests order from a site and app carrying your brand, choosing pickup, curbside, delivery or a catering tray.
  • Marketplace orders in the POS. With Rails, an order placed on DoorDash, Uber Eats or Grubhub prints in the kitchen like any other ticket, so staff don't re-enter tickets from separate tablets.
  • Delivery without a marketplace. When a guest orders delivery on your site, Dispatch hands the run to an outside courier network.
  • Payments. Olo Pay processes online and in-store card payments.
  • Guest data and loyalty. The Guest Data Platform, marketing tools and Olo Loyalty use ordering history, and guests can join loyalty with a phone number at the counter.
  • One menu everywhere. Menus are managed centrally across locations and channels, while Olo Accounts, once called Borderless, lets a diner sign in without a password at any brand on Olo.

What makes them stand out: Olo is built for brands whose digital orders run into high volumes across many stores, where uptime and POS integration matter as much as the ordering screen itself.

Pricing: Olo prices each deal individually; contact its sales team for a quote.

It's a great fit for: Chains, from regional groups to national brands, that want their own ordering channels, marketplace orders flowing into the POS, and guest data and loyalty built on that ordering history.
