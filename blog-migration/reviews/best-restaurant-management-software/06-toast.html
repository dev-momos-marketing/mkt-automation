6. Toast

Category: Restaurant point of sale (POS) & operations software

Toast is a publicly traded (NYSE: TOST) POS and management platform built only for restaurants. Around the point of sale it adds online ordering, kitchen screens, payroll and team tools, marketing and loyalty, and controls for groups with many locations. It reported roughly 180,000 live locations in mid-2026.

Notable customers: Captain Parker's Pub, Kaldi's Coffee and Craft Lounge.

What's new: The Spring 2026 update (May) added a paid marketing tier called Toast IQ Grow, which bundles an AI Marketing Agent, a dedicated success manager and Marketing Essentials. The same update brought back Toast Local, the consumer app, now carrying loyalty. Its Q2 2026 earnings, reported in August, counted roughly 9,500 restaurants added on a net basis during those three months and $2.4B in annualized recurring revenue as of June 30, 2026.

Key features:

  • POS hardware for every station. Front counters get fixed terminals, guests can self-order at kiosks, and servers punch in tableside on Toast Go.
  • Ordering beyond the dining room. Web orders, delivery partners and Toast Local, the guest-facing app, all drop takeout tickets into one queue.
  • Kitchen and menu control. A kitchen display system routes tickets, and menus and prices can be changed centrally across locations.
  • Payroll and scheduling. Payroll and team management sit alongside scheduling through Sling by Toast.
  • Guest marketing. Points programs, gift cards and text or email promotions draw on what guests bought at the register, and Toast IQ Grow adds an AI agent for marketing.
  • Reports for groups. Multi-location reporting lets owners compare sales and performance across stores.

What makes them stand out: Toast covers the POS, ordering, payroll and marketing from a single vendor, so a general manager has one login and one contract for most of the front and back of house.

Pricing: Expect a sales conversation: Toast's site has promoted a no-cost Starter Kit next to paid subscriptions, and terminals add to the bill, so request a written quote before you set a budget.

It's a great fit for: Operators of every size, whether one corner bistro or a franchise system with dozens of stores, who are looking to run tickets, takeout, wages and loyalty through a single vendor.
