3. 7Shifts

Category: Restaurant workforce & employee management software

On the labor side of the business, 7shifts answers four daily questions: who works when, how they clock in, how tips get split and how everyone gets paid. It serves only restaurants, from a single cafe to franchise groups, and in 2026 it added staff training to the same app your team already opens to check shifts.

Notable customers: HCI Hospitality (a Freddy's Frozen Custard & Steakburgers franchisee), Boston Pizza (Regina), Conshy Girls Restaurant Group and Pitango Gelato.

What's new: In September 2026, 7shifts launched Employee Training, so a manager can write a short course with a quiz, send it only to line cooks or only to one store, and track completion; Premium subscribers get it at no extra charge. A year earlier, in July 2025, 7shifts had replaced its old tiers with a free Comp plan plus three paid ones.

Key features:

  • Forecast-driven scheduling. While you drag shifts onto the grid, forecast sales and wage totals update beside them, so you can see the cost of a schedule before you post it.
  • A mobile app for staff. Your crew can pick up an open shift, mark days off or ping the team from a phone, which cuts down on group texts to the manager.
  • Clock-ins that follow the schedule. The time clock connects with your POS and payroll and can stop early punches that create unplanned overtime.
  • Labor cost against sales. Set a labor target per store, then watch wages measured against each day's takings.
  • Tips and payroll together. Tip pooling and payouts run in the app, and restaurant payroll is available as a paid add-on.
  • Checklists and onboarding. Managers assign shift tasks, send new hires their paperwork and track training in one place.

What makes them stand out: Every screen is designed for shift work in restaurants, with tips, split shifts and frequent swaps treated as normal cases from day one.

Pricing: Comp costs nothing if you run one location and employ up to 15 people. On annual billing, the cheapest paid tier, Essentials, comes to $39.99 per location monthly for teams of up to 30. Pro lands at $79.99 and Premium, which includes the new training module, at $134.99. Paying monthly instead of yearly costs more: Essentials $44.99, Pro $89.99, Premium $149.99. Payroll is an extra charge, enterprise deals are priced individually, and you can test Pro for 14 days first. Plans changed recently, so check 7shifts' pricing page before you buy.

It's a great fit for: Independent restaurants through multi-unit and franchise groups that want scheduling, labor control, tips and payroll handled by one restaurant-specific tool.
