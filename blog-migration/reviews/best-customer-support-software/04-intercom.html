4. Intercom

Intercom is a customer service platform built around Fin, an AI agent that answers customers from your help content, plus a helpdesk where your team takes over what Fin can't resolve. It covers chat, email, phone, WhatsApp, SMS and social messages.

The company behind it renamed itself Fin in May 2026, and Salesforce completed its acquisition in September 2026. The helpdesk keeps the Intercom name, and customers include Anthropic, Lightspeed and Synthesia.

Key features

  • Fin AI agent: Answers questions across chat, email, WhatsApp, SMS and social from your help articles, and passes harder cases to a person.
  • Fin Voice: Picks up phone calls and handles common questions, such as opening hours, before routing callers to your team.
  • Shared inbox and tickets: Agents work every channel from one inbox, with Copilot drafting suggested answers.
  • Help center: Customers search answers themselves, and the same articles feed Fin.
  • Proactive messages: Messages trigger on what customers do on your site or in your app, such as stalling at checkout.
  • Reporting: Dashboards track how Fin and your human team perform.

Pros

  • AI and people in one place: Fin, the inbox and the help center share context, so customers don't repeat themselves after a handoff.
  • Published pricing: Seat and Fin prices are on Intercom's website, and the free trial needs no credit card.
  • Pay per outcome: Fin is charged once per conversation it resolves, however many questions it answers along the way.
  • User rating: Fin by Intercom has a 4.5 out of 5 rating on G2.

Cons

  • Variable bill: Seats plus a fee per Fin outcome mean costs rise with conversation volume.
  • Copilot costs extra: The assistant for human agents is an add-on priced per agent.
  • Fin minimum elsewhere: Running Fin on top of another helpdesk carries a minimum monthly commitment.
  • General purpose: Intercom serves every industry, so restaurant work such as replying to Google or Yelp reviews needs another tool.

Price and plans

You can try Intercom free for 14 days. Seat prices are per month, billed annually, and Fin is billed by usage.

  • Essential: $29 per seat, per month (new customers currently see $19).
  • Advanced: $85 per seat, per month.
  • Expert: $132 per seat, per month.
  • Fin AI agent: From $0.99 per outcome.
  • Copilot add-on: $29 per agent, per month billed annually, or $35 billed monthly.
