4. Intercom

Intercom is a customer service platform built around Fin, an AI agent that answers customers using your help content, with a helpdesk where your team picks up whatever Fin hands over. The company behind it renamed itself Fin in 2026 and is now part of Salesforce, while the helpdesk keeps the Intercom name.

Notable customers: Anthropic, Lightspeed and Synthesia.

What's new: In May 2026, Intercom changed its company name to Fin to put its AI agent at the center. In September 2026, Salesforce completed its acquisition of Fin, which keeps serving existing customers as part of Salesforce AI Labs.

What you can do with Intercom:

  • Let AI answer first. Fin replies to customers over chat, email, WhatsApp, SMS and social channels, drawing on your help articles, and passes harder cases to a person.
  • Answer the phone with AI. Fin Voice picks up calls and handles common questions, such as opening hours or order status, before routing a caller to your team.
  • Work from one inbox. Agents handle every channel and ticket in a shared inbox, with Copilot drafting suggested replies from past conversations and documentation.
  • Publish a help center. Customers can search answers themselves, and the same articles feed Fin's responses.
  • Reach out proactively. Trigger messages based on what customers do on your site or app, like a tip when someone stalls during checkout.

Why Intercom stands out: The whole product is designed around Fin, and Intercom bills it per outcome, so you pay when it actually resolves a conversation. Running the AI agent, the human inbox and the help center on one platform also keeps context intact when a customer moves from bot to agent.

It's best for: Businesses with high volumes of repetitive questions that want an AI agent to handle them across channels, with a helpdesk for everything else.

Pricing: Seats start at $29 per seat per month on the Essential plan, billed annually, and Fin is billed separately at $0.99 per outcome.
