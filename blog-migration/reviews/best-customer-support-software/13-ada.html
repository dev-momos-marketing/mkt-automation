13. Ada

Ada is an AI customer service platform built around an AI agent that answers customers on your behalf across chat, messaging, email and voice. Teams use it to resolve routine questions automatically and pass the rest to people with the full conversation attached.

Notable customers: Betsson, Cebu Pacific, monday.com and Malaysia Airlines.

What's new: In February 2026, Ada introduced its Unified Reasoning Engine, which gives its agents one intelligence layer across voice, chat, messaging and email. In July 2026, it added native AI generation in 60 languages and images in automated conversations.

What you can do with Ada:

  • Run one agent on every channel. The same AI agent handles chat, messaging apps, email and phone calls, so answers stay consistent wherever a guest reaches you.
  • Follow your playbooks. Playbooks guide the agent through multi-step requests in the order your team would handle them, on voice as well as chat.
  • Answer in the guest's language. Native generation in 60 languages lets one agent serve guests across regions without separate scripts.
  • Act inside your systems. APIs connect Ada to the tools behind your business, so the agent can look up details and complete tasks instead of only replying.
  • Hand off to your team. Integrations with helpdesks such as Dixa pass conversations to human agents when a guest needs a person.

Why Ada stands out: Ada was built for automated customer service from the start, and its agent works the same way across text and voice. That suits brands whose volume is too high for people to answer every routine question.

It's best for: Larger brands with high support volume across several channels that want an AI agent to take the first response.

Pricing: Ada doesn't publish prices. Plans are quoted by its sales team and are mainly priced by conversations.
