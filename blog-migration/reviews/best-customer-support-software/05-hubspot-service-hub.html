5. HubSpot Service Hub

HubSpot Service Hub is the customer service product built on the HubSpot CRM. It combines a help desk, tickets, a knowledge base, a customer portal and feedback surveys with Breeze Customer Agent, HubSpot's AI agent for answering customer questions.

Notable customers: Twelve, Sesame HR and Unific.

What's new: In June 2025, HubSpot opened Breeze Customer Agent to all Pro and Enterprise customers, billed through HubSpot Credits. In June 2026, the help desk added workflow-based ticket routing, such as sending VIP or language-specific requests to the right agent.

What you can do with HubSpot's Service Hub:

  • Answer every channel in one place. The help desk workspace brings email, chat, WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger and phone calls into one queue for your team.
  • Let AI handle common questions. Breeze Customer Agent replies from your knowledge base around the clock and passes harder conversations to a person with context.
  • Offer self-service. Publish help articles and a customer portal where guests can find answers or check the status of a request.
  • Route and track tickets. Set rules, workflows and SLAs so each request reaches the right person and nothing waits too long.
  • Measure satisfaction. Send NPS, CSAT and CES surveys and see results next to the customer's full history.

Why HubSpot Service Hub stands out: Support conversations sit on the same CRM record as marketing emails and sales deals, so your agents see the whole relationship before replying. For teams already on HubSpot, that shared record saves a lot of setup and switching between tools.

It's best for: Growing businesses already using HubSpot for marketing or sales that want customer service on the same platform.

Pricing: Free tools are available. Paid plans are charged per seat, with Professional listed at $90/seat/month and Enterprise at $150/seat/month; Starter pricing changes with promotions, so check HubSpot's page. AI agent usage draws on HubSpot Credits.
