5. HubSpot Service Hub

HubSpot Service Hub is customer service software built on the HubSpot CRM. It brings together a help desk with ticketing, a knowledge base, a customer portal and feedback surveys, plus Breeze Customer Agent, HubSpot's AI agent for answering customer questions.

For a restaurant brand that already runs marketing or catering sales in HubSpot, every support conversation lands on the same contact record as those emails and deals. In April 2026, HubSpot said Customer Agent resolves 65% of conversations and switched it to pricing per resolved conversation.

Key features

  • Help desk workspace: Email, chat, WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger and calls arrive in one queue, with routing and custom views.
  • Breeze Customer Agent: The AI agent answers email and chat questions around the clock from your knowledge base and hands harder cases to a person.
  • Self-service: A knowledge base and a secure customer portal let people find answers and check on their requests.
  • Routing and SLAs: Assignment workflows added in June 2026 send VIP or language-specific tickets to the right agent, and SLA rules flag anything waiting too long.
  • Feedback surveys: NPS, CSAT and CES results appear next to each customer's history.

Pros

  • One customer record: Agents see marketing emails, sales deals and past tickets before they reply.
  • Low-cost start: Free tools cover up to 2 users, and paid plans come with a 14-day free trial.
  • Pay per result: Customer Agent costs $0.50 per resolved conversation, so you pay when it closes a question.
  • Well rated: On G2, users give HubSpot Service Hub 4.4 out of 5 across 3,016 reviews.

Cons

  • Onboarding fees: Professional carries a required one-time $1,500 onboarding fee, and Enterprise one of $3,500.
  • Key tools on higher plans: The knowledge base and Customer Agent need Professional or Enterprise.
  • Best inside HubSpot: If your guest data lives in another CRM, you get less from the shared record.
  • No review replies: As a general help desk, it doesn't answer public reviews on Google or Yelp, so restaurants need another tool for that.

Price and plans

Free tools cover up to 2 users, and paid plans have a 14-day free trial. Paid plans are priced per seat, and AI agent use draws on HubSpot Credits.

  • Starter: $20 per seat, per month (HubSpot runs discounts for new customers).
  • Professional: $90 per seat, per month, billed annually, plus a one-time $1,500 onboarding fee.
  • Enterprise: $150 per seat, per month, plus a one-time $3,500 onboarding fee.
