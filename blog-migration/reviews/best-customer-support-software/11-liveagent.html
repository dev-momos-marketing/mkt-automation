11. LiveAgent

LiveAgent is an all-in-one help desk made by Quality Unit for small and growing support teams. Live chat, email tickets, phone calls and social messages share one inbox, and an AI chatbot answers routine questions before a person steps in.

Its prices are low for a tool with a built-in call center, which suits a restaurant group that wants reservation and catering calls in the same queue as website chats. Huawei and Slido are among its customers.

Key features

  • Universal inbox: Email, chat, calls and messages from Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp and other channels land in one queue.
  • Call center: IVR menus, call routing and call recording run inside the help desk, with no separate phone system.
  • AI chatbot: Rolled out in early 2025, it works from your existing chat button, answers from your content in the customer's language and hands off to an agent.
  • AI help for agents: The AI Answer Assistant drafts replies from the ticket and your help articles, and AI summaries shorten long threads.
  • Rules and reports: Automation rules, ticket distribution and SLAs assign work, and real-time reports track agent performance.

Pros

  • Affordable entry: Starter is $15 per agent, per month on annual billing.
  • Phone in the same inbox: From the Team plan, calls sit next to chats and emails.
  • AI without a markup: AI comes on every plan, and LiveAgent adds no fee on top of your AI provider's price.
  • Strong ratings: LiveAgent has a 4.5/5 rating on G2 from more than 1,500 reviews.

Cons

  • No free plan: After the trial, you need a paid plan.
  • Starter is limited: Social channels, the call center and reporting start on Team.
  • Separate usage bills: AI usage is billed by your OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini or FlowHunt account, and calls by your phone provider.
  • General help desk: There are no restaurant-specific tools such as review replies or loyalty offers.

Price and plans

LiveAgent has a 30-day free trial with no credit card. These are regular prices billed annually (monthly billing costs more); lower offer prices apply to trials started by November 22, 2026.

  • Starter: $15 per agent, per month, for up to 5 agents.
  • Team: $29 per agent, per month, for up to 15 agents.
  • Business: $49 per agent, per month.
  • Enterprise: $3,199 per month with 80 agents included.
