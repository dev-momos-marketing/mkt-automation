11. LiveAgent

LiveAgent is an all-in-one help desk made by Quality Unit that brings live chat, email tickets, phone calls and social messages into one inbox. It now includes an AI chatbot, so you can answer routine questions automatically while your team handles the rest.

Notable customers: Huawei, Slido and FrëschKëscht.

What's new: In March 2025, LiveAgent finished rolling out its AI Chatbot, which runs from the chat button already on your site. In August 2025, it added an AI Answer Generator to help agents write replies.

What you can do with LiveAgent:

  • Keep every channel in one place. Emails, chats, calls and messages from social platforms all land in the same queue, so a guest's complaint never sits in a separate app.
  • Take calls without extra tools. The built-in call center handles IVR menus, routing and call recording for reservation or catering lines.
  • Automate common answers. The AI Chatbot replies to questions about hours, menus or bookings in the customer's own language and hands off to an agent when needed.
  • Write better replies. AI writing tools polish agents' drafts and suggest answers based on your saved content.
  • Publish self-service help. Build a knowledge base and customer portal so guests can find answers on their own.
  • Route and measure work. Set rules that assign tickets automatically, then track response times on real-time reports.

Why LiveAgent stands out: You get chat, a phone system, social inboxes and AI in a single subscription that's priced for smaller teams. That makes it easy to cover every way guests contact you without buying several tools.

It's best for: Small and mid-size businesses, including restaurant groups, that want chat, phone and social support plus AI in one affordable help desk.

Pricing: Plans start at $15/agent/month billed annually, with a free trial. Larger teams can choose an Enterprise plan at $3,199/month, which includes 80 agents.
