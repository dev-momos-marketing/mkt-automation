10. Zoho Desk

Zoho Desk is the help desk in Zoho's business software suite. It turns emails, social messages, web forms, phone calls and chats into tickets in one view, and adds self-service portals, automation and Zia, Zoho's AI assistant.

For a restaurant group, Zoho Desk makes most sense if you already run Zoho CRM or other Zoho apps, or if you want a low-cost desk for guest questions about orders, bookings and gift cards. In July 2026, Zoho extended Zia features such as sentiment analysis and auto tags to every paid edition from Standard up.

Key features

  • Multichannel tickets: Email, web forms, phone, social media and live chat requests arrive in a single queue, and Standard adds WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook Messenger.
  • Zia AI: The assistant reads the tone of each message, tags tickets, suggests replies and flags unusual spikes in volume.
  • Self-service: A knowledge base, community forum and Answer Bot help guests find answers before they open a ticket.
  • Automation: Assignment rules, SLAs, multi-level escalations and Blueprint workflows move each ticket through the right steps.
  • Service reports: Customer happiness ratings and custom dashboards show how quickly and how well your team answers.
  • Zoho integrations: Ticket data connects with Zoho CRM and Zoho Analytics, so guest history and reporting stay in one suite.

Pros

  • Low cost: A free plan covers 3 users, and paid plans start at $7 per user, per month, billed annually.
  • Strong user rating: Zoho Desk has a 4.4 out of 5 rating on G2.
  • Zoho fit: Teams on Zoho CRM get tickets and customer records in the same system.
  • Many channels: Messaging apps such as WhatsApp and Instagram sit next to email and phone in one ticket view.

Cons

  • Limited free plan: The free edition covers 3 users and email ticketing with limited capabilities.
  • Key tools on Professional: Blueprints, telephony, multi-department support and the full Zia set start at $23 per user, per month, billed annually.
  • Monthly billing costs more: Standard is $20 per user on monthly billing, against $14 billed annually.
  • General help desk: Replying to reviews across locations and other restaurant-specific work need a separate tool.

Price and plans

Zoho Desk has a free plan for up to 3 users and a 15-day free trial of any paid plan. Annual billing prices are as follows; monthly billing costs more.

  • Express: $7 per user, per month.
  • Standard: $14 per user, per month.
  • Professional: $23 per user, per month.
  • Enterprise: $40 per user, per month.
