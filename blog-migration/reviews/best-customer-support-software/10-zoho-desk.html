10. Zoho Desk

Zoho Desk is the help desk in Zoho's business software suite, with multichannel ticketing, self-service portals, automation and Zia, Zoho's AI assistant. It connects closely with Zoho CRM and Zoho Analytics, and it can also run on its own.

Notable customers: Summerfield Resources, RJG and Mercedes-Benz India.

What's new: In July 2025, Zoho launched its own Zia LLM and a no-code Agent Studio for building AI agents across its apps. In July 2026, Zia features like sentiment analysis and auto tags became part of every paid Zoho Desk edition.

What you can do with Zoho Desk:

  • Collect requests from every channel. Messages sent by email, through your website form, over live chat, on social media or by phone all become tickets in a single view.
  • Get help from Zia. The AI assistant reads the tone of each message, tags tickets, suggests replies and flags unusual spikes in volume.
  • Deflect simple questions. A branded help center and Answer Bot point customers to the right article before they open a ticket.
  • Automate the process. Assignment rules, SLAs, escalations and Blueprint workflows keep each ticket moving through the right steps.
  • Track service quality. CSAT surveys and agent reports show how quickly and how well each request was handled.

Why Zoho Desk stands out: It packs AI, automation and multichannel support into plans priced for small teams, including a free tier. If you already use Zoho apps, ticket data flows straight into your CRM and reports.

It's best for: Budget-conscious support teams of any size, especially businesses already working in the Zoho suite.

Pricing: A free plan covers up to 3 agents. Express costs $7/agent/month for teams of up to 5 agents, and Standard costs $14/agent/month, both billed annually.
