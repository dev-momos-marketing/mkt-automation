16. Front

Front is a customer operations platform that looks like email and works like a helpdesk. Shared inboxes bring together email, SMS, chat and social messages, each conversation gets an owner, and AI agents can take on routine requests.

It suits a restaurant group whose guest relations team handles catering inquiries, event bookings and supplier threads by email as much as by chat. Front launched its Autopilot AI agent in September 2025, and in June 2026 it opened a beta that lets outside AI agents work inside Front as teammates.

Key features

  • Shared inboxes: A catering email, a text from a regular and a website chat can sit in one view, with a clear owner for each.
  • Internal comments: Teammates discuss a conversation with comments and @mentions that the guest never sees.
  • Rules and SLAs: Route messages by location or topic, schedule follow-ups and escalate anything close to missing its SLA.
  • Autopilot and Copilot: Autopilot resolves common requests on its own, and Copilot drafts replies and suggests next steps for agents.
  • AI Topics and analytics: See the most common reasons guests get in touch, along with response times and workload by person or inbox.
  • Knowledge base: Publish answers to frequent questions, such as allergen policies or catering minimums.

Pros

  • Familiar workflow: Teams used to email get assignment, SLAs and AI without moving to a rigid ticket queue.
  • Flexible AI: Use Front's own agents or connect outside agents built with tools such as Claude or Sierra.
  • Free trial without a card: The 14-day trial includes every Professional feature.
  • Highly rated: Front has a 4.7/5 rating on G2.

Cons

  • One channel on Starter: Support across email, SMS, social and chat starts on Professional at $65 per seat each month.
  • AI add-ons: Below Enterprise, Copilot is $20 per seat per month, and Autopilot is billed per conversation on every plan.
  • Seat-based cost: Every agent needs a paid seat, so a growing team raises the bill.
  • Onboarding fee for large contracts: Front requires an onboarding package for contracts over $25k.

Price and plans

You can try Front's Professional features free for 14 days without a credit card. Annual prices per seat are below.

  • Starter: $25 per seat, per month, up to 10 seats.
  • Professional: $65 per seat, per month, up to 50 seats.
  • Enterprise: $105 per seat, per month.
  • Autopilot: From $0.05 per conversation, as an add-on.
