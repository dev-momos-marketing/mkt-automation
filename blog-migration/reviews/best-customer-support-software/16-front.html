16. Front

Front is a customer operations platform that feels like email but works like a helpdesk. Your team shares inboxes for email, SMS, chat and social messages, assigns each conversation to an owner, and can now hand routine requests to an AI agent.

Notable customers: sennder, Hootsuite, Cisco Meraki and Lydia.

What's new: In September 2025, Front launched Autopilot, an AI agent that resolves conversations across channels within limits your admins set. In June 2026, it opened a beta that lets outside AI agents work inside Front alongside your team.

What you can do with Front:

  • Share inboxes across channels. Pull a catering email, a text from a regular and a chat message into one view where everyone can see who owns what.
  • Collaborate out of sight. Comment and @mention teammates inside a conversation so the guest only sees the final reply.
  • Automate the routing. Build rules that assign messages by location or topic, chase follow-ups and escalate anything nearing its SLA.
  • Let AI take the first pass. Autopilot answers common questions on its own, while AI drafting helps agents reply to the rest faster.
  • Measure your team. Analytics show response times, SLA adherence and workload by person or inbox.

Why Front stands out: Teams that live in email get assignment, SLAs and AI without switching to a rigid ticket queue. It works well when the same conversations involve guests, suppliers and event clients.

It's best for: Operations and guest-relations teams, including multi-location restaurant groups, that handle a high volume of email and messages.

Pricing: Starter costs $25/seat/month billed annually for up to 10 seats, Professional $65, and Enterprise $105. Autopilot is an add-on billed at $0.89 per resolution.
