3. Freshdesk

Freshdesk is the helpdesk from Freshworks, built for teams that answer customers across email, chat, phone and social. Its Freddy AI layer adds AI agents that resolve routine questions, a copilot for human agents and insights on why people get in touch.

For a restaurant group with a central guest services desk, refund requests, catering questions and gift card problems become tickets with an owner and a deadline. In November 2025, Freshworks added the Freshdesk Command Center, one workspace for every channel with Freddy AI Copilot built in.

Key features

  • Omnichannel ticketing: Email, chat, phone and social messages arrive as tickets in one inbox, so a complaint never sits unseen in a separate app.
  • Freddy AI Agent: Answers common questions on chat and email around the clock, and you build and test it in the no-code AI Agent Studio.
  • Freddy AI Copilot: Summarizes long threads, drafts replies and suggests the next step for your agents.
  • Automations: Rules assign tickets, set SLA timers and send follow-ups so nothing waits too long.
  • Self-service portal: A knowledge base lets guests find opening hours, refund steps or allergen details on their own.
  • Order lookups: Integrations such as Shopify, Stripe and FedEx bring order details into the ticket.

Pros

  • Low entry price: The Growth plan costs $19 per agent per month on annual billing.
  • AI sessions included: Each paid plan comes with 500 Freddy AI Agent sessions, once per account, so you can test automation before buying more.
  • Proven at scale: Decathlon and Bridgestone are among the brands with Freshworks customer stories.
  • Strong user rating: Freshdesk has 4.4/5 on G2 from 3,754 reviews.

Cons

  • No free plan: New accounts get a 14-day Enterprise trial, and the account is suspended if you don't continue on a paid plan.
  • Copilot costs extra: Freddy AI Copilot is a $29 per agent per month add-on, offered on Pro and Enterprise only.
  • Metered AI agent: After the included 500 sessions, more cost $49 per 100.
  • General-purpose helpdesk: Public review replies and guest surveys aren't part of it, so restaurants need another tool for those.

Price and plans

New accounts get a 14-day trial of the Enterprise plan. Prices are per agent, per month, billed annually; Freddy AI Copilot is a $29 add-on on Pro and Enterprise, and extra AI Agent sessions cost $49 per 100.

  • Growth: $19 per agent, per month.
  • Pro: $55 per agent, per month.
  • Enterprise: $89 per agent, per month.
