3. Freshdesk

Freshdesk is the helpdesk from Freshworks, built for teams that answer customers across email, chat, phone and social. Its Freddy AI layer now includes AI agents that resolve routine questions, a copilot for human agents and analytics on what customers contact you about.

Notable customers: Decathlon, Bridgestone, Body & Fit and Dineout.

What's new: In November 2025, Freshworks added the Freshdesk Command Center, a single workspace for every channel with Freddy AI Copilot inside, plus Vertical AI Agents with more than 50 prebuilt workflows and Freddy AI Insights for spotting service trends.

What you can do with Freshdesk:

  • One shared inbox. Email, chat, phone and social messages arrive as tickets in one place, so a guest complaint never sits unseen in a separate app.
  • AI agents for routine questions. Freddy AI Agent answers common questions on chat and email around the clock, and you build and test it in Agent Studio without code.
  • Copilot for your team. Freddy AI Copilot summarizes long threads, drafts replies and suggests the next step on Pro and Enterprise plans.
  • Automatic assignment. Rules route tickets by topic or location, set SLA timers and send follow-ups so nothing waits too long.
  • Self-service help center. A knowledge base lets customers find opening hours, refund steps or allergen details on their own.
  • Trend alerts. Freddy AI Insights shows the root causes behind contact spikes and flags unusual volume early.

Why Freshdesk stands out: You get multichannel ticketing and AI self-service at a lower entry price than most enterprise suites. Setup is quick, and you can add AI agents and the copilot once your volume justifies them.

It's best for: Small and mid-size teams, including restaurant groups with a central guest services desk, that want an affordable helpdesk with room to grow.

Pricing: Growth costs $19, Pro $55 and Enterprise $89 per agent per month, billed annually. Freddy AI Copilot and AI Agent sessions cost extra.
