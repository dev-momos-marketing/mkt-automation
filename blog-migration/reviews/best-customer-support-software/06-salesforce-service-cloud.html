6. Salesforce Service Cloud

Salesforce Service Cloud, now sold under the name Agentforce Service, is Salesforce's customer service platform. It puts every case, channel and customer record in one console on top of the Salesforce CRM, with AI agents that can answer customers before a person steps in.

Notable customers: OpenTable, NightOwl Entertainment, Finnair and Pandora.

What's new: In March 2026, Salesforce launched Agentforce Contact Center, an add-on that brings voice, digital channels, CRM data and AI agents into the same platform. It's generally available to Agentforce Service customers in the US and Canada.

What you can do with Salesforce Service Cloud:

  • Work every channel in one console. Agents see calls, emails, chats, messaging apps and social posts as cases next to the guest's full history.
  • Answer around the clock. Agentforce AI agents handle common questions such as booking changes or order status at any hour and pass harder ones to your team.
  • Help agents reply faster. AI drafts responses, summarizes long cases and classifies incoming requests so they reach the right queue.
  • Run voice natively. The Agentforce Contact Center add-on handles phone support inside Salesforce, so you can skip a separate telephony tool.
  • Offer self-service. Publish a help center and knowledge base that customers and AI agents both draw answers from.
  • Track service levels. Set SLAs and routing rules, then monitor performance on dashboards by team or location.

Why Salesforce Service Cloud stands out: Support agents and AI agents work from the same customer data your sales and marketing teams use. That shared record lets a large brand personalize every reply and automate more of its routine requests.

It's best for: Large restaurant groups, hospitality companies and enterprises already on Salesforce that want a highly configurable service desk with AI agents.

Pricing: The Starter Suite starts at $25/user/month, billed annually. Higher editions and the Agentforce for Service add-on ($125/user/month) cost more.
