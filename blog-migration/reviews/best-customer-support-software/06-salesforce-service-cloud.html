6. Salesforce Service Cloud

Salesforce Service Cloud is Salesforce's customer service platform, also sold under the name Agentforce Service. It brings cases from every channel into one console on top of the Salesforce CRM, with AI agents that can answer customers before a person steps in.

For a large restaurant or hospitality group, the draw is shared data: support reps and AI agents work from the same guest record your sales and marketing teams use. OpenTable runs customer service on it, and in March 2026 Salesforce launched Agentforce Contact Center, adding native voice and digital channels in the US and Canada.

Key features

  • Agent console: Calls, emails, chats, messaging apps and social posts arrive as cases next to the customer's full history, in one workspace.
  • AI agents: Agentforce agents handle common questions such as booking changes or order status at any hour and pass harder ones to your team.
  • Help for reps: AI drafts responses, summarizes long cases and classifies incoming requests so they reach the right queue.
  • Service levels: Entitlements, SLAs, routing and omni-channel queues, plus a self-service help center and dashboards on team performance.

Pros

  • One customer record: Service history sits beside sales and marketing data, which makes personal replies and automation easier.
  • Highly configurable: Routing, queues and SLA rules can follow the way each brand or location works.
  • Trial without a card: You can test it free for 30 days with no credit card.
  • Well rated: Its G2 average is 4.4 stars out of 5.

Cons

  • AI agents cost more: The AI agent for customer service comes with the Advanced edition at $395 per user per month, or as a paid add-on.
  • Metered AI: AI use draws on Flex Credits, with 500K per org on Core and 1 million on Advanced; extra credits are paid.
  • Annual commitments: Pro Suite and every higher edition are billed annually, and Pro Suite requires a contract.
  • A big platform: It's built for brands already on Salesforce, so a small team without it takes on a lot of setup.

Price and plans

Salesforce offers a 30-day free trial with no credit card, and prices are per user, per month.

  • Starter Suite: $25 per user, per month, billed monthly or annually; transaction fees apply.
  • Pro Suite: $100 per user, per month, billed annually, with a contract.
  • Core: $195 per user, per month, billed annually.
  • Advanced: $395 per user, per month, billed annually.
  • Max: $550 per user, per month, billed annually.
