7. Gorgias

Gorgias is a helpdesk and AI Agent platform for online stores, built around Shopify order data. Email, chat, social, SMS and voice land in one inbox, and agents can see and change a customer's orders without leaving the ticket.

For a restaurant, it fits the part of the business that ships: packaged sauces, merch, gift cards or meal kits sold through a Shopify store. BrüMate, Tommy John and Try The World use it, and the public roadmap for 2026 focuses on tighter control over what AI Agent knows and faster responses.

Key features

  • Shared inbox: Messages from email, chat, social, SMS and phone calls arrive in a single queue your team works together.
  • Order actions: Edit, refund or cancel a Shopify order from the ticket sidebar.
  • AI Agent: Resolves routine questions such as order status and returns using your policies, and passes the rest to your team.
  • Gorgias Voice: Adds a phone menu, call routing and AI summaries of calls inside the helpdesk.
  • Revenue statistics: Show which support conversations ended in a sale.

Pros

  • Priced by volume: Plans follow ticket count and are never priced per agent, so adding seasonal staff doesn't change the bill.
  • Quick order fixes: With the order inside the ticket, a question about a damaged box can become a refund or replacement without switching tools.
  • AI paid on results: You pay for AI Agent only when it fully resolves a request.
  • Rated well: Gorgias has a 4.6/5 rating on G2.

Cons

  • Ecommerce focus: Most of the tooling assumes an online store, so a restaurant without one gets little from the order features.
  • Overage fees: Tickets beyond your plan cost $0.40 each on Starter and Basic, and $0.36 on Pro and Advanced.
  • AI counts twice: Each AI Agent interaction is charged and also counts toward your helpdesk tickets.
  • Revenue reports on higher plans: Revenue attribution starts on Pro, at $300 a month billed annually.

Price and plans

Gorgias offers a 7-day free trial with no credit card. Plans are priced by monthly ticket volume, and annual billing lowers the price on Basic and above.

  • Starter: $10 per month for 50 tickets, billed monthly.
  • Basic: $50 per month billed annually ($60 monthly) for 300 tickets.
  • Pro: $300 per month billed annually ($360 monthly) for 2,000 tickets.
  • Advanced: $750 per month billed annually ($900 monthly) for 5,000 tickets.
  • AI Agent: $0.90 per automated interaction on annual plans, $1.00 on monthly plans.
