7. Gorgias

Gorgias is a helpdesk and AI Agent platform made for online stores, with Shopify order data built into every conversation. If your restaurant sells packaged sauces, merch or gift cards online, it lets your team answer order questions and change orders from the same screen.

Notable customers: BrüMate, Tommy John and Try The World.

What's new: In April 2025, Gorgias introduced Shopping Assistant, an AI feature that recommends products and suggests add-ons inside chat. Its public roadmap names 2026 the year its AI Agent matures, with tighter control over what the bot knows.

What you can do with Gorgias:

  • Work from one inbox. Email, chat, social comments and DMs, SMS and phone calls all arrive in a shared queue.
  • Fix orders inside the ticket. Agents can see a customer's Shopify orders and edit, refund or cancel them without opening another tab.
  • Hand routine questions to AI Agent. It answers order-status, shipping and return requests using your policies, and passes anything unusual to a person.
  • Recommend products in chat. Shopping Assistant suggests items and bundles to shoppers who are still deciding.
  • Take calls in the helpdesk. Gorgias Voice adds a phone menu, call routing and AI summaries of calls and voicemails.
  • Measure support revenue. Revenue statistics show which conversations led to a sale.

Why Gorgias stands out: Its whole workflow is built around the order, which shortens the path from a customer question to a refund or replacement. Billing AI Agent per resolved conversation also keeps costs tied to the work the bot actually completes.

It's best for: Ecommerce brands on Shopify, including restaurants and food brands with an online shop, that want order actions and AI replies in one helpdesk.

Pricing: The Starter plan is $10 per month for 50 billable tickets, and AI Agent costs $0.90 per resolved conversation on most plans billed annually.
