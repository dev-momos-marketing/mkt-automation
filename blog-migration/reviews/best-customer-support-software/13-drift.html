13. Drift

Drift is a conversational marketing and sales chat platform, now owned by Clari + Salesloft after the two companies merged in December 2025. Its chatbots greet website visitors, qualify them and book meetings, and they can also answer common support questions before passing the chat to a person.

Notable customers: 1Password, RapidMiner and Park Place Technologies.

What's new: In March 2026, Clari + Salesloft announced a gradual sunset of Drift and named 1mind as its exclusive AI successor, so existing Drift clients are being referred to 1mind.

What you can do with Drift:

  • Chat with visitors right away. AI-powered chatbots open a conversation when someone lands on your site and ask a few qualifying questions.
  • Book meetings in the chat. Bots offer open slots from a rep's calendar, so qualified buyers can schedule without email back-and-forth.
  • Route high-intent visitors. Conversations from valuable prospects go straight to the right rep or a live agent.
  • Target named accounts. Account-based playbooks show tailored messages to visitors from companies on your target list.
  • Answer support questions. Bots cover frequent questions and hand off to a person when a chat needs one.
  • Sync your CRM. Conversation and lead data flow into Salesforce and HubSpot.

Why Drift stands out: Drift tied website chat directly to pipeline, which made it a long-time choice for B2B revenue teams. 1Password's case study describes using it to answer common questions with AI and route the rest to human agents.

It's best for: Current Drift customers on the Clari + Salesloft platform who are planning their move to a successor.

Pricing: No public pricing. Drift was sold on annual quotes, and with the sunset, existing clients are being referred to 1mind.
