14. Aircall

Aircall is a cloud phone system for sales and support teams, now positioned as an AI-powered communications platform. Alongside business numbers, routing and call recording, it offers an AI Voice Agent that picks up routine calls and AI tools that transcribe and summarize every conversation.

Notable customers: Baguette Atelier, Birdies and Doxo.

What's new: In May 2026, Aircall bought Vogent, a San Francisco voice AI startup, to make its AI Voice Agent sound more natural on live calls. In September 2026, it added ready-made voice agent templates and campaigns that let an AI agent call through an uploaded contact list.

What you can do with Aircall:

  • Get numbers fast. Add local, toll-free or international numbers from the dashboard and route calls with IVR menus, queues and opening hours.
  • Answer calls around the clock. The AI Voice Agent learns from your FAQs and handles questions like opening hours, directions or booking policies when no one is free.
  • Skip the note-taking. AI Assist transcribes calls and writes summaries, action items and sentiment, then logs them to your CRM.
  • Coach in real time. Supervisors can listen in, whisper to an agent or join a call, and review recordings afterward.
  • Keep context in your other tools. Calls and texts sync with Salesforce, HubSpot, Zendesk, Intercom and other CRMs and helpdesks.

Why Aircall stands out: Its large library of integrations means every call shows up in the system your team already works in, with the caller's history on screen before anyone says hello. The AI Voice Agent adds coverage for busy periods and after hours without new phone hardware.

It's best for: Teams that take a high volume of calls and want their phone system tied to their CRM or helpdesk, with AI to answer and summarize calls.

Pricing: Essentials costs $30 and Professional $50 per license per month, billed annually, with a minimum of 3 licenses; Custom plans are quoted.
