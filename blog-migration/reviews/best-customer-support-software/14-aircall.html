14. Aircall

Aircall is a cloud phone system for sales and support teams, now positioned as an AI-powered communications platform. Besides business numbers, routing and call recording, it offers an AI Voice Agent for routine calls and AI Assist to transcribe and summarize conversations. Aircall says more than 22,000 businesses use it.

Restaurant support teams still take calls about hours, bookings, catering and order problems, and Aircall connects those calls to the helpdesk or CRM that holds the rest of the conversation. In May 2026 it bought Vogent, a San Francisco voice AI company, to improve how its agent handles live calls.

Key features

  • Numbers and call flows: Local, toll-free and international numbers, with IVR menus, queues, routing and business hours.
  • AI Voice Agent: Answers routine questions such as opening hours or directions from your FAQs, day and night, and since September 2026 it can also run outbound call campaigns.
  • AI Assist: Produces call transcripts, summaries, action items and sentiment, and syncs them to your CRM.
  • Supervisor controls: Managers can listen in live, whisper to an agent, join a call and review recordings.
  • Messaging: Text guests by SMS or MMS from a shared inbox, with WhatsApp available too.
  • Integrations: 250+ connections, including Salesforce, HubSpot, Zendesk and Intercom, put the caller's history on screen.

Pros

  • Works with your helpdesk: Calls and texts sync into the CRM or helpdesk your team already uses.
  • After-hours coverage: Every plan includes 50 minutes of AI Voice Agent calls each month.
  • Clear entry price: Published plans start at $30 per license per month.
  • Well rated: G2 reviewers rate Aircall 4.4/5, based on 1,682 reviews.

Cons

  • Three-license minimum: Essentials and Professional need at least 3 licenses, and Enterprise starts at 25.
  • Summaries cost more on Essentials: AI call summaries are included in Professional; on Essentials they need the AI Assist add-on.
  • Usage-based AI minutes: Voice agent calls beyond the included minutes cost $0.29 a minute pay as you go, or come in bundles.
  • Phone-first: Email tickets and live chat need a separate helpdesk, which Aircall connects to.

Price and plans

Aircall has a 7-day free trial. Plans are priced per license, per month, billed annually (annual billing saves 25%), with a minimum of 3 licenses.

  • Essentials: $30 per license, per month.
  • Professional: $50 per license, per month.
  • Enterprise: Custom pricing, from 25 licenses.
