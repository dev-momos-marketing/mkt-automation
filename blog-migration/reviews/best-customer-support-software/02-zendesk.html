2. Zendesk

Zendesk is a customer service platform, sold today as the Zendesk Resolution Platform, that combines ticketing, messaging, voice, a help center and AI agents. It's built for mid-size and large businesses that field many questions on many channels, and food brands such as Grubhub, Grill'd and Impossible Foods run their support on it.

In March 2026, Zendesk completed its purchase of Forethought, whose self-improving AI agents work across chat, email and voice.

Key features

  • Single agent workspace: Email, messaging, live chat, social messages and phone calls arrive in one view, with the customer's history beside each ticket.
  • AI agents: Bots answer routine order, refund or opening-hours questions across chat, email and voice and pass harder cases to a person.
  • Routing and triggers: Omnichannel routing and automated triggers send each request to the right team; Suite Professional adds skills-based routing and an IVR phone tree.
  • Copilot: This add-on suggests replies and next steps while your team works a conversation.
  • Help center: A self-service knowledge base lets guests find allergen or booking answers without contacting you.

Pros

  • Every channel together: Your team handles email, chat, social and calls without switching apps.
  • Room to grow: A large app marketplace and optional add-ons let you add channels and automation without changing tools.
  • Clear entry prices: Per-agent prices are published, and the trial gives you two weeks on Suite Professional.
  • Well reviewed: Zendesk has a 4.3/5 rating on G2 from more than 7,000 reviews.

Cons

  • Copilot costs extra: The agent assistant is a $50 per agent, per month add-on (billed annually) for Professional and higher.
  • AI billed by resolution: Plans include 5 or 10 automated resolutions per agent each month; extra ones cost $1.50 committed or $2.00 pay-as-you-go.
  • Basic entry plan: Support Team covers email and ticketing; AI agents and telephony start at Suite Team.
  • General-purpose: Restaurant work such as public review replies or loyalty offers may need another tool.

Price and plans

Zendesk offers a 14-day free trial of Suite Professional. Prices are per agent, per month, billed annually; monthly billing costs more.

  • Support Team: $19 per agent, per month ($25 billed monthly).
  • Suite Team: $55 per agent, per month ($69 billed monthly).
  • Suite Professional: $115 per agent, per month ($149 billed monthly).
  • Suite Enterprise + Copilot: Custom pricing from sales.
