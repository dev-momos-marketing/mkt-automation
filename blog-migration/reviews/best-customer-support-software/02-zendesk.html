2. Zendesk

Zendesk is a customer service platform that brings ticketing, messaging, voice, a help center and AI agents into one system it now calls the Resolution Platform. Large support teams use it to manage questions from many channels in a single workspace, and food brands from delivery apps to burger chains run their guest support on it.

Notable customers: Grubhub, Grill'd and Impossible Foods.

What's new: In March 2026, Zendesk completed its purchase of Forethought, adding AI agents for chat, email and voice that refine their own procedures over time.

What you can do with Zendesk:

  • Answer every channel in one place. Email, web messaging, live chat, social messages and phone calls land in the same agent workspace with the guest's history attached.
  • Let AI agents handle routine questions. Bots answer common requests like order status or opening hours across chat, email and voice, then pass harder cases to a person.
  • Give agents an AI assistant. The Copilot add-on drafts replies and suggests next steps while your team works a conversation.
  • Route conversations automatically. Triggers and omnichannel routing send each request to the right team based on channel, topic or location.
  • Publish a help center. Build a self-service knowledge base so guests can find refund, allergen or booking answers without contacting you.
  • Track team performance. Dashboards report on volume, response times and satisfaction across channels.

Why Zendesk stands out: Its range of channels, automation rules and marketplace apps lets a growing support operation add complexity without switching tools. The Forethought deal also gives you AI agents that work across text and voice from the same vendor.

It's best for: Mid-size and large businesses, including multi-location restaurant groups, that field a high volume of guest questions across several channels.

Pricing: Support Team starts at $19 per agent per month billed annually, and Suite Team, which adds messaging, voice and AI agents, is $55 per agent per month billed annually.
