17. Gladly

Gladly is a customer service platform organized around people instead of ticket numbers, and it now describes itself as an agentic commerce platform. Each customer has one ongoing conversation that spans every channel, and Gladly AI works inside that thread to answer questions or help your agents.

Notable customers: JOANN, Rothy's and Ulta Beauty.

What's new: In May 2026, at its Gladly Connect Live conference, Gladly announced agentic commerce features that let a single AI handle both buying questions and service requests such as exchanges.

What you can do with Gladly:

  • Keep one thread per guest. Phone calls, email, chat, texts and social messages from the same person appear as a single running conversation.
  • See the full customer picture. Agents get past interactions and order details in the profile, so guests don't have to repeat themselves.
  • Resolve requests with Gladly AI. The AI answers and completes common requests across channels and brings in a person when needed.
  • Support agents during live conversations. AI assistance helps your team with context and replies while they talk to a customer.
  • Route by person. Conversations go to the right agent based on the customer and their history.
  • Report on service quality. Dashboards show managers how quickly agents respond and how satisfied customers feel afterward.

Why Gladly stands out: Because the record follows the person, a regular who calls one week and texts the next reaches an agent who already knows their history. Adding AI to that same thread gives automated answers the same context your staff sees.

It's best for: Larger consumer and hospitality brands that treat personal, relationship-based service as part of the guest experience.

Pricing: Custom pricing; Gladly shares quotes through its sales team.
