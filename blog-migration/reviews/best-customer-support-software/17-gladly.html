17. Gladly

Gladly is a customer service platform organized around the customer: each person has one lifelong conversation that spans voice, email, chat, SMS and social. It now calls itself an agentic commerce platform, with Gladly AI resolving requests and Team Assist helping human agents.

Most of its named customers are retailers, such as Ulta Beauty, Rothy's and Nordstrom, and Breeze Airways is its reference in travel and hospitality. In May 2026, at Gladly Connect Live, it announced agentic commerce features that let one AI handle buying questions and service requests such as exchanges.

Key features

  • Lifelong conversation: Calls, emails, chats, texts and social messages from one person appear as a single thread.
  • Customer profile: Agents see past interactions and order data beside the conversation.
  • Gladly AI: AI answers and resolves common requests on every channel, then hands off to a person with the context attached.
  • Team Assist: Human agents get AI help with context and replies during live conversations.
  • People-based routing: Conversations go to agents based on who the customer is and their history.
  • Service reporting: Managers track team performance and customer satisfaction.

Pros

  • History follows the guest: A regular who calls one week and texts the next reaches an agent who can see both.
  • Shared record for AI and staff: Handoffs from AI to a person keep the conversation intact.
  • Hospitality use cases: Its travel and hospitality offering covers booking changes, cancellations and service based on loyalty status.
  • Highly rated: G2 users give Gladly an average of 4.7 out of 5, from 1,113 reviews.

Cons

  • Retail focus: Its homepage pitches a "retail-trained" AI commerce solution, and its hospitality page names no restaurants.
  • No trial or price list: The pricing page leads to a demo and a sales conversation.
  • Enterprise scale: It's built for mid-market and enterprise consumer brands, so a small restaurant group may need less.

Price and plans

Gladly doesn't publish prices or offer a listed free trial; you book a demo and get a quote from its sales team.
