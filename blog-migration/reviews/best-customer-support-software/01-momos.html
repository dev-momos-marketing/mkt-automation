1. Momos

Momos is an AI-powered guest experience platform built for restaurants. It brings guest messages, reviews and support cases from every location into one place, and its AI agent answers guests on behalf of each store.

Notable customers: Papa Murphy's, Potbelly, Condado Tacos and Caribou Coffee.

What's new: In 2025, Momos added AI copilots, AI ticket summaries and AI brand guidelines, plus integrations with Paytronix, Square and Otter. Potbelly brought its reviews, cases and guest messages from 472 locations into one Momos view that year.

The customer support side of Momos is Alfie, the Experience Agent.

What you can do with Alfie:

  • Reply in minutes. Alfie answers guests quickly in your brand voice, drawing on each guest's visit history, preferences and past issues.
  • Resolve on the spot. It can issue points, coupons and offers straight from your loyalty platform, so a complaint ends with a reason to come back.
  • Cover every channel. One agent works across website chat, in-app support, post-visit surveys and delivery app feedback, and escalates to your team when a person is needed.
  • Work from one inbox. The Unified Inbox gathers Google reviews, app feedback, social messages and POS data, with AI triage that routes each case to the right location.
  • Answer in the guest's language. Real-time translation lets each location reply in the language the guest wrote in.

At Papa Murphy's, which runs more than 1,000 locations, Momos automated 97.7% of review replies and recovered over $2M in revenue through recovery offers.
