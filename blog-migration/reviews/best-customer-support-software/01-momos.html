1. Momos

Momos runs guest support for restaurant brands through Alfie, an AI Experience Agent that sits inside its wider guest platform. Alfie answers guests around the clock on chat, email, phone, social and delivery apps, resolves what it can by itself, and passes the rest to your team with the whole conversation attached.

Think of it as an AI chatbot built for restaurants that can also settle a complaint on the spot with loyalty points, a coupon or an offer. Just Salad, which has 120+ locations, reports 1,200+ hours saved each month and 99% faster responses since adopting Momos.

Key features

  • One agent on every channel: Alfie covers website chat, your mobile app, email and contact forms, phone, Instagram and WhatsApp messages, and delivery apps such as DoorDash and Grubhub.
  • Escalation with context: When a case needs a person, Alfie flags it along with the conversation history and a suggested next step.
  • Guest details beside each ticket: Agents see spend, order history and ratings, and AI summaries condense long threads into a few lines.
  • Recovery in the conversation: Points, coupons and offers come straight from your loyalty and POS platforms without switching tools.
  • Return tracking: Alfie notes if an unhappy guest visits again, so you can see which recoveries actually worked.
  • Routing by location: AI triage in the Unified Inbox sends every case to the store it belongs to.

Pros

  • Trained on your restaurants: Momos teaches Alfie your menu, FAQs and recovery playbooks, so answers reflect how your stores actually work.
  • Fewer tools: Support tickets, reviews and survey feedback share one inbox and one guest history.
  • Multilingual support: Real-time translation lets each location help guests in their own language.
  • Managed rollout: Momos handles setup and new-location rollouts, with customer success after launch.

Cons

  • Pricing by quote: Prices aren't published, and you start with a demo.
  • Hospitality only: It's built for restaurant and hospitality brands, so a support team in another industry would need a general help desk.
  • Setup takes a week: Training Alfie and approving its answers with the Momos team comes before launch.

Price and plans

Momos doesn't publish prices; you get a custom quote after a demo.
