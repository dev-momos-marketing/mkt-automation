12. Tidio

Tidio is a customer service platform for small and midsize businesses that pairs a website chat widget with Lyro, its AI agent, plus no-code automations and a help desk. For a restaurant, that means a bot on your site can answer questions about hours, menus or bookings while your team handles the rest from the same inbox.

Notable customers: Suitor, Gecko Hospitality and Pastreez.

What's new: In October 2026, Tidio reported that Lyro ranked first in the default view of G2's AI CX Agent Evaluations, with the top policy-compliance score among the agents tested.

What you can do with Tidio:

  • Add live chat to your website. Install the widget and reply to guests from a browser or the mobile app while you're on the floor.
  • Let Lyro answer common questions. The AI agent learns from your website and FAQ content, then replies to questions about opening hours, menus or availability.
  • Hand off to your team. When a guest needs a person, Lyro passes the chat to a staff member with the conversation so far.
  • Build automations visually. Flows lets you set up greetings, lead capture or booking prompts by dragging blocks, with no code.
  • Manage email in a help desk. Tickets from email sit next to chats, and Lyro can answer email tickets too.

Why Tidio stands out: The setup is guided and code-free, so a manager can launch a chat bot without a developer. Pricing that starts with a free plan also lets a small venue try chat before committing to the AI add-on.

It's best for: Small and midsize restaurants that want a website chat with an AI agent they can set up themselves.

Pricing: Tidio has a free plan, and the Starter plan costs $24.17 per month billed annually, with Lyro sold as an add-on priced by AI conversations.
