12. Tidio

Tidio is a customer service platform for small and midsize businesses that combines a website chat widget, the Lyro AI agent, no-code Flows automations and an email help desk. For a restaurant, a bot on your site can answer questions about hours, menus or bookings while staff handle the rest from the same inbox.

In October 2026, Tidio reported that Lyro ranked first in the default view of G2's AI CX Agent Evaluations, passing 40 of 46 tasks with the highest policy-compliance score (92%) among nine agents.

Key features

  • Live chat widget: Install it on your website and reply to guests from a browser or the mobile and desktop apps while you're on the floor.
  • Lyro AI agent: It learns from your website and FAQ content, then answers questions about opening hours, menus or availability.
  • Handoff to staff: When a guest needs a person, Lyro passes the chat on with the conversation so far.
  • Flows: Drag blocks to set up greetings, lead capture or booking prompts without code.
  • Email help desk: Email tickets sit next to chats, and Lyro can answer those too.

Pros

  • Free to start: The free plan includes 10 seats, 50 billable conversations a month and 50 Lyro conversations for life.
  • No developer needed: Setup is guided, so a manager can launch a chat bot alone.
  • Low paid entry: Starter costs $24.17 a month on annual billing.
  • Well reviewed: Tidio holds 4.6/5 on G2 from 1,879 reviews.

Cons

  • Lyro is an add-on: After the first 50 free conversations, Lyro starts at $32.50 a month for 50 Lyro conversations.
  • Conversation caps: Plans are metered by billable conversations, with 50 a month on Free and 100 on Starter.
  • Limited Flows reach: Automations reach 100 visitors a month on Free and Starter; more needs the Flows add-on.

Price and plans

Tidio has a free plan and a 7-day free trial of its paid plans and add-ons, with no credit card. Prices below are per month, billed annually (2 months free).

  • Free: $0, with 50 billable conversations a month.
  • Starter: $24.17 per month, with 100 billable conversations.
  • Growth: From $49.17 per month, from 250 billable conversations.
  • Plus: From $300 per month, plus usage.
  • Premium: Price on request.
  • Lyro AI Agent add-on: From $32.50 per month for 50 Lyro conversations.
