8. Kustomer

Kustomer is an AI-native customer service platform and CRM that puts every conversation, order and past issue on one customer timeline. Its AI comes in three parts: Concierge answers customers, Envoy assists human reps, and Architect helps you build and improve AI agents and workflows.

Meta divested Kustomer in 2023, and it raised a $30M Series B led by Norwest in August 2025. Its customers include sweetgreen, a useful reference for restaurant brands, along with Everlane and HexClad. In March 2026, it launched Kustomer AI, a standalone platform that adds its AI to an existing helpdesk, starting with Zendesk.

Key features

  • Customer timeline: Orders, chats, emails and calls sit in one record, so agents and AI see the full history before answering.
  • Omnichannel inbox: Email, chat, voice, SMS, WhatsApp and social messages arrive in the same workspace.
  • Concierge: The customer-facing AI agent resolves requests across channels using what it knows about that customer.
  • Envoy: Reps see order history, open issues and sentiment, with suggested replies and next steps.
  • Routing and workflows: AI-based routing and a workflow builder prioritize conversations and send them to the right team.
  • Kustomer Voice: Phone support with calls, SMS and transcription, billed by usage.

Pros

  • Context for automation: Because AI works from the same timeline as your agents, automated answers can draw on orders and earlier conversations.
  • Keeps Zendesk if you want: Kustomer AI can run on top of an existing Zendesk setup.
  • Restaurant reference: sweetgreen appears on its customer list.
  • G2 rating: Across 558 G2 reviews, Kustomer averages 4.4 out of 5.

Cons

  • Quote only: Enterprise and Ultimate show "Talk to Sales" in place of a price.
  • AI billed on top: Concierge and Envoy are add-ons with their own per-conversation or per-user fees.
  • Implementation fee: Some configurations need a statement of work and a separate implementation charge.
  • Sized for high volume: It's built for consumer brands with large contact volumes, so a single restaurant may not need this much platform.

Price and plans

Kustomer doesn't publish prices for its Enterprise and Ultimate plans; you talk to sales for a quote. Its pricing details page lists these add-on rates.

  • Concierge (AI agent for customers): $0.75 per engaged conversation.
  • Envoy (AI for reps): $50 per user, per month.
  • Kustomer Voice: From $0.02 per minute.
