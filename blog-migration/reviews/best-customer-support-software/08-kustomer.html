8. Kustomer

Kustomer is an AI-native customer service platform and CRM that shows every conversation, order and past issue on one customer timeline. It has run as an independent company again since Meta divested it in 2023, and its AI Agents now serve customers, support reps and team leaders.

Notable customers: Everlane, Jerome's Furniture, Daily Harvest and HexClad.

What's new: In March 2026, Kustomer launched Kustomer AI, a standalone enterprise AI platform that runs on top of an existing helpdesk, starting with Zendesk. In December 2025, it added AI Assistants for building automations and monitoring how workflows and AI agents behave.

What you can do with Kustomer:

  • See the whole customer. Orders, past chats and emails sit on one timeline, so agents answer with full context instead of asking customers to repeat themselves.
  • Cover every channel. Email, chat, voice, SMS and social messages come into the same workspace.
  • Automate common requests. AI Agents answer questions and take actions like order lookups at any hour, then hand off to a person when needed.
  • Help reps reply faster. AI suggests responses, summarizes conversations and points reps to the next step.
  • Route by priority. Workflows and AI sort incoming conversations by intent, value and urgency and send them to the right team.
  • Watch performance. Leaders get analytics and observability on queues, agents and AI behavior.

Why Kustomer stands out: The timeline gives both people and AI the customer's full history, which suits brands whose questions revolve around orders, deliveries and subscriptions. You can run it as your full helpdesk or add Kustomer AI to the system you already use.

It's best for: Consumer brands with high contact volumes, including food and meal-delivery brands, that want support built around the customer record.

Pricing: Custom pricing. Kustomer quotes per seat or per conversation, so you'll need a quote for your team size and volume.
