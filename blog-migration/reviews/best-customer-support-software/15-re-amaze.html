15. Re:amaze

Re:amaze is a helpdesk and AI customer service platform for ecommerce and online businesses, sold under the GoDaddy umbrella. It gives you a shared inbox, live chat, bots and an AI agent, with room to run several brands from one account.

Notable customers: Built Bar, Todd Snyder and Beer Cartel.

What's new: In August 2025, Re:amaze moved its Intents feature, which sorts incoming messages, onto AI with no extra training needed. In September 2025, it extended that AI to response templates, quick replies, shopper bots and its FAQ bot.

What you can do with Re:amaze:

  • Work from one inbox. Email, live chat, social media, SMS and voice conversations land in a shared queue your whole team can see.
  • Add an AI agent. The AI Agent, still in beta, learns from your help content and answers questions on chat, email and SMS at any hour.
  • See orders in context. Shopify, BigCommerce and WooCommerce order details appear inside the conversation, so agents can answer delivery questions quickly.
  • Start conversations. Cues send proactive chat messages when a visitor lingers on a page or meets a rule you set.
  • Run several brands. Each brand gets its own inbox, help center and look while your team works from a single login.

Why Re:amaze stands out: Multi-brand support is built in, which suits companies selling several product lines or storefronts online. Its ecommerce integrations put order history beside every message, so replies need fewer lookups.

It's best for: Online stores and DTC brands, including food and drink brands selling online, that manage one or more storefronts on Shopify, BigCommerce or WooCommerce.

Pricing: Basic is $29, Pro $49 and Plus $69 per user/month, or a Starter plan at $59/month for up to 500 responded conversations. Annual billing is discounted, and a free trial is available.
