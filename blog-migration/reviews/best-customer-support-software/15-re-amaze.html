15. Re:amaze

Re:amaze is a helpdesk and AI customer service platform for ecommerce and online businesses, sold as Re:amaze by GoDaddy. It brings email, live chat, social, SMS and voice into one shared inbox, adds chatbots and an AI agent, and lets you run several brands from a single account.

It fits food and drink brands that sell online, where most support questions are about orders. In 2025, Re:amaze moved its message sorting (Intents) onto AI and extended the same AI context to response templates, quick replies and its shopper and FAQ bots.

Key features

  • Shared inbox: Email, live chat, social media, SMS, VoIP and video calls land in one queue your whole team can see.
  • AI Agent: Still in beta, it learns from your help content and answers questions on chat, email and SMS.
  • Order context: Shopify and BigCommerce order details appear inside each conversation.
  • Cues and chatbots: Proactive chat messages fire when a visitor meets a rule you set, and bots handle routine questions.
  • Multi-brand support: Each brand keeps its own inbox, help center and look under one login.

Pros

  • Faster order answers: Agents see purchase details in the conversation, so delivery and refund questions need fewer lookups.
  • Several storefronts, one team: Pro and Plus include unlimited brands.
  • Flexible published pricing: Per-user plans sit beside a flat-rate Starter plan with unlimited team members.
  • Easy to try: The 14-day trial includes every Plus feature and needs no credit card.
  • Well rated: Re:amaze averages 4.6 out of 5 in G2 reviews.

Cons

  • AI limits: The AI Agent is still in beta, and each user gets 5, 10 or 20 AI resolutions a month, depending on the plan.
  • Basic is single-brand: Running more than one brand requires Pro or Plus.
  • Ecommerce focus: Its integrations center on online stores, so a restaurant group that needs POS or reservation data in support may need another tool.

Price and plans

Re:amaze offers a 14-day free trial with all Plus features and no credit card. Prices are per team member, per month on monthly billing; annual billing costs less.

  • Basic: $29 per team member, per month ($26.10 billed annually); one brand.
  • Pro: $49 per team member, per month ($44.10 billed annually); unlimited brands.
  • Plus: $69 per team member, per month ($62.10 billed annually); unlimited brands.
  • Starter: $59 per month, flat, for unlimited team members and up to 500 responded conversations a month.
