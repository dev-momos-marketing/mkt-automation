9. Help Scout

Help Scout is a help desk for small and mid-size teams that puts email, live chat, messaging and a help center into one shared inbox. An AI agent, AI Answers, replies to common questions from your knowledge base, and WhatsApp and SMS sit next to email in the same view.

For a restaurant group, it fits a guest relations team that handles catering requests, gift card questions and complaints by email and chat. In 2026, Help Scout added WhatsApp in its Winter release and native two-way SMS in August.

Key features

  • Shared inbox: Your team sees who is replying to what, assigns conversations and leaves private notes, so two people never answer the same guest.
  • Beacon live chat: A website widget offers chat and suggests help articles before a visitor sends a message.
  • AI Answers: Responds to common questions from your Docs knowledge base and hands the conversation to a person when it can't help.
  • WhatsApp and SMS: WhatsApp messages and texts through your Twilio number arrive in the same inbox as email.
  • AI drafts and summaries: On Plus and Pro, AI suggests replies and condenses long threads before you jump in.

Pros

  • Free plan: Up to 5 users can share one inbox and one Docs site at no cost.
  • Pay-per-result AI: AI Answers is billed only when it resolves a conversation, and you can set a monthly spending cap.
  • Easy trial: Standard and Plus come with a 15-day free trial and no credit card.
  • Well reviewed: Help Scout holds 4.4 out of 5 on G2 across 428 reviews.

Cons

  • AI drafts need a higher plan: Standard includes only the AI Inbox assistant; AI drafts start on Plus.
  • Extra AI fees: Each AI Answers resolution costs $0.75 on top of your seats.
  • User caps: Standard stops at 25 users and Plus at 50, while Pro requires at least 10 users and a demo.
  • Bring your own Twilio: SMS runs through a Twilio account you connect, and phone calls need the Aircall add-on.

Price and plans

Help Scout has a free plan for up to 5 users and a 15-day free trial on Standard and Plus. Prices below are per user, per month, billed annually; monthly billing costs about 16% more.

  • Standard: $25 per user, per month.
  • Plus: $45 per user, per month.
  • Pro: $75 per user, per month (minimum 10 users).
  • AI Answers: $0.75 per resolution, as an add-on.
