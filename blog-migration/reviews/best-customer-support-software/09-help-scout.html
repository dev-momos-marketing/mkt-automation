9. Help Scout

Help Scout is a help desk for small and mid-size teams that puts email, chat, messaging and a help center into one shared inbox. It now adds an AI agent, AI Answers, that replies from your knowledge base, and newer channels like WhatsApp and SMS sit alongside email in the same view.

Notable customers: OnePageCRM, Litmus and Mabel's Labels.

What's new: In March 2026, Help Scout's Winter release added WhatsApp as a channel, conversation routing and an Aircall integration. In July 2026, it added native two-way SMS, so a team can connect its own Twilio number and answer texts from the inbox.

What you can do with Help Scout:

  • Share one inbox. Your team sees who is replying to what, assigns conversations and leaves private notes, so two people never answer the same guest.
  • Chat on your website. The Beacon widget offers live chat and suggests help articles before a visitor sends a message.
  • Answer with AI. AI Answers responds to common questions from your Docs knowledge base and hands the conversation to a person when it can't help.
  • Cover more channels. WhatsApp, SMS through your Twilio number and social messages arrive in the same inbox as email.
  • Write faster. On Plus and above, AI drafts suggest replies and AI summaries condense long threads before you jump in.

Why Help Scout stands out: Its interface stays close to a familiar email client, which makes it quick for new teammates to pick up even with chat, AI and a help center added. Conversations read like personal emails without ticket numbers, which suits brands that care about a friendly tone.

It's best for: Small and mid-size teams that want an easy shared inbox with chat, a help center and optional AI answers.

Pricing: A free plan covers up to 5 users; paid plans cost $25, $45 or $75 per user per month billed annually, and AI Answers costs $0.75 per resolution.
