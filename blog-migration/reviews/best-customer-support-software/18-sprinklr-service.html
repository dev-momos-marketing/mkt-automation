18. Sprinklr Service

Sprinklr Service is the AI contact center product in Sprinklr's customer experience suite. It brings voice, social, messaging, email and chat, which Sprinklr counts as 30+ channels, into one agent console with AI agents, an agent copilot and quality tools.

Its customers include Deutsche Telekom, 3M, Telefonica and Gulf Bank. That's the scale it's built for: global brands with large contact centers, including international restaurant and hospitality chains that handle service across social, messaging and voice.

Key features

  • One console for every channel: Calls, WhatsApp, LINE, WeChat, social comments, review sites, email and chat reach agents in the same place, with the customer's history attached.
  • Routing by intent and sentiment: AI routing matches each case to an agent by skill and capacity, and sentiment analysis helps upset customers reach someone sooner.
  • AI agents and voice bots: Customer-facing AI agents handle routine requests and FAQs in your brand voice.
  • Agent Copilot: Suggests responses, pulls relevant articles and summarizes each conversation; the April 2026 release added proactive nudges during live conversations.
  • AI+ Studio: Build AI agents and test them against simulated conversations before launch, then keep scoring them once live.
  • Contact center operations: Workforce management, quality management and analytics on 100% of conversations are part of the suite.

Pros

  • Broad channel coverage: Social care across 25+ social channels runs next to voice and messaging, on one set of customer data.
  • AI tested before launch: Autonomous Evaluation puts AI agents through simulated scenarios before customers ever see them.
  • Feedback built in: Omnichannel surveys and AI-predicted CSAT bring customer feedback into the service data.
  • G2 rating: Sprinklr Service scores 4.3 out of 5 on G2 from 774 reviews.

Cons

  • Custom pricing only: Sprinklr doesn't publish prices, and you get a quote after a demo.
  • Enterprise scope: It's sized for large, multi-country contact centers, so a restaurant group with a small guest relations team may need far less.
  • Learning curve: G2 reviewers most often name the learning curve and cost as drawbacks.

Price and plans

Sprinklr doesn't publish prices for Sprinklr Service; you get a custom quote after a demo.
