18. Sprinklr Service

Sprinklr Service is the AI-native contact center product in Sprinklr's customer experience suite. It brings voice, social, messaging, email and chat, which Sprinklr counts as 30+ channels, into one agent console with AI agents, copilots and quality tools.

Notable customers: Deutsche Telekom, 3M, Telefonica and Gulf Bank.

What's new: In April 2026, the Spring '26 release added Autonomous Evaluation, which tests AI agents against simulated conversations before launch and keeps scoring them once live. The same release made Agent Copilot proactive, with real-time nudges for human agents.

What you can do with Sprinklr Service:

  • Answer every channel in one console. Calls, WhatsApp, LINE, WeChat, social comments, review sites, email and chat reach agents in the same place, with the customer's history attached.
  • Route by intent and sentiment. Unified routing matches each case to an agent by skill and capacity, and sentiment analysis helps upset customers reach someone sooner.
  • Automate with AI agents. Customer-facing AI agents and voice bots resolve common requests and deflect calls.
  • Coach agents live. Agent Copilot suggests replies and next steps during each conversation.
  • Test AI before launch. AI+ Studio lets you build, simulate and evaluate AI agents in a no-code workspace.
  • Run the contact center. Workforce management, quality management and analytics are part of the suite.

Why Sprinklr Service stands out: Few platforms cover social, messaging and voice service this broadly in a single system. Global brands can run support, social care and customer insight from one data set across many countries.

It's best for: Large, multi-country brands with big contact centers, such as global restaurant and hospitality chains, that handle service across social, messaging and voice.

Pricing: Custom enterprise pricing, available on request.
