6. Qualtrics

Website: Qualtrics

Qualtrics sells an enterprise research suite built on an idea it calls Experience Management (XM), spanning customers, employees and brand perception. Restaurant chains typically use its location dashboards, its reputation module for public reviews, and newer AI Experience Agents that respond to feedback. Silver Lake took the company private in 2023.

Notable customers: Shake Shack and KFC both appear in Qualtrics' customer stories.

What's new: Qualtrics used its X4 event in March 2026 to say paying customers now run its Experience Agents, and it previewed agents that handle service recovery in real time. It also finished buying Press Ganey Forsta for $6.75 billion in May 2026; that business is largely healthcare, so the restaurant product is unchanged.

What you can do with Qualtrics:

  • Reach guests wherever they are. Send post-visit surveys through text, email, your app, a website intercept, chat or a QR code on the receipt.
  • Put public reviews in the program. Its reputation module imports Google and other review sites so surveys and star ratings sit in one data set.
  • Match dashboards to roles. A store manager sees one restaurant, an area leader sees their market, and the C-suite sees every brand.
  • Let AI read the comments. Text and sentiment analysis clusters thousands of open-ended answers into themes your team can act on.
  • Start recovery automatically. An Experience Agent can react to a low score the moment it comes in and begin making things right with that guest.
  • Feed your CRM and call center. Connectors for Salesforce, Genesys and NICE carry guest feedback into the tools your service teams already use.

Why Qualtrics stands out: Few platforms match its research depth, from predictive models of what drives satisfaction and churn to brand tracking and employee surveys on the same system as guest feedback.

Who it’s best for: Big restaurant and hospitality groups with in-house CX or insights analysts who want reviews, surveys and operations data managed as one enterprise program.

Pricing: Not published for the CX suite; Qualtrics quotes each deal through its sales team.
