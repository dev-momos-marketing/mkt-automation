6. Qualtrics

Qualtrics builds feedback programs for large brands around an idea it calls experience management (XM), covering guests, employees and brand perception. Restaurant chains use it to collect post-visit surveys, read public reviews beside them, and send the results to each location.

KFC and Shake Shack both appear in its customer stories. The newest piece is Experience Agents, AI that reacts to feedback as it comes in; Qualtrics told its X4 audience in March 2026 that paying customers now use them.

Key features

  • Post-visit surveys: Guests can answer by text, email, your app, a website intercept, chat or a QR code.
  • Closed-loop follow-up: An Experience Agent can pick up a low score the moment it lands and begin making things right with that guest.
  • Dashboards by role: A store manager sees one restaurant, an area leader sees a market, and executives see the whole brand.
  • Comment analysis: AI clusters open-ended answers into themes with sentiment, so managers read patterns rather than every response.
  • Driver analysis: Predictive models show which parts of the visit move satisfaction most.
  • CRM and call-center feeds: Connectors for Salesforce, Genesys and NICE carry guest feedback into the systems your service team already uses.

Pros

  • Research depth: Survey design, text analytics and predictive modeling are built for professional researchers, so an insights team rarely outgrows them.
  • Staff and guests together: Employee experience surveys run on the same platform, so team and guest feedback can be read side by side.
  • Proven at chain scale: Restaurant brands the size of KFC run their guest feedback on it.

Cons

  • Interaction-based quotes: CX plans are priced by interactions, which Qualtrics defines as data records it collects or processes, and no prices are listed.
  • Demo before access: The pricing page offers no free trial or free plan.
  • Needs analysts: The platform pays off when an insights or CX team runs it; a lean operations team may find it more than it can manage.

Price and plans

Qualtrics doesn't publish prices. Its CX plans, including one for location-based industries such as restaurants, are priced by interactions, and you get a quote after a demo.
