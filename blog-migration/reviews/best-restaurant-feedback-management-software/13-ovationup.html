13. OvationUp

Website: OvationUp

Ovation, found at ovationup.com, is a guest experience platform for multi-unit restaurants that starts with a two-question text survey sent right after the meal. Unhappy answers reach a manager in real time so they can recover the guest, and since late 2025 the platform also handles public reviews and listings.

Notable customers: Taziki's Mediterranean Cafe, Bareburger, Curry Up Now and Dave's Hot Chicken.

What's new: The spring 2026 release, out in May, introduced Brand Voice settings that shape winback texts, automated responses and review replies, plus a report tying guest sentiment to individual menu items. Three months before that, nearly a dozen restaurant brands that use Ovation put in $3 million between them, lifting the company's total raised to $12 million.

What you can do with Ovation:

  • Text guests a quick survey. Your POS or online ordering system fires off the two questions once a guest pays, and dine-in guests can also scan a table QR code.
  • Save the visit while it's fresh. Negative answers trigger an alert, giving a manager the chance to reach out before the guest posts a public review.
  • Reply in your brand's voice. AI suggests responses to private feedback and public reviews, which you can edit before they go out.
  • Manage reviews and listings. Google, Yelp and Facebook reviews can be filtered by rating, platform and location, and listings stay updated across 50+ directories.
  • Learn what guests complain about. Written comments are tagged into restaurant themes such as speed, cleanliness and food quality.
  • See how dishes perform. Menu item reports and Competitive Pulse benchmarking against up to five nearby rivals show where you're ahead or behind.

Why Ovation stands out: A two-question text takes a guest seconds to answer, so more guests are likely to answer than with a long emailed survey, and the speed of the alerts gives managers a real window to fix a bad experience.

It's best fit for: Multi-unit and enterprise restaurant brands, especially those with busy online ordering, that want to catch problems fast through text feedback and answer public reviews in the same dashboard.

Pricing: Not listed publicly. Quotes are built for each account, counting both brick-and-mortar sites and ghost kitchens, and larger groups get a volume discount.
