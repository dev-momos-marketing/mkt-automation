13. OvationUp

Ovation collects restaurant guest feedback with a two-question text message sent right after an order. Built for multi-unit brands, it alerts a manager to unhappy answers in real time, groups written comments by theme, and since late 2025 also covers public reviews and listings.

Short surveys suit busy guests, and Ovation says its texts bring in far more responses than long-form surveys. Taziki's Mediterranean Cafe, Dave's Hot Chicken and Curry Up Now are among the brands with published case studies.

Key features

  • Post-order text surveys: Your POS or online ordering system sends two questions once the guest pays, and dine-in guests can scan a QR code at the table.
  • Real-time recovery alerts: A poor score reaches the manager straight away, with AI-suggested responses to help win the guest back.
  • Feedback categories: Comments are sorted into 35+ categories such as food quality, speed and cleanliness, with heat maps and custom dashboards.
  • Menu item reports: Sentiment linked to order data shows which dishes draw the most complaints.
  • Reviews and listings: Public reviews from Google, Yelp and Facebook sit beside survey answers, and listings stay current across 50+ directories.
  • Winback texts: Text marketing campaigns invite guests back, written in the tone you set with Brand Voice.

Pros

  • Light for guests: Two questions by text ask very little, and longer follow-up surveys tied to the order are there when you need more detail.
  • Tied to real orders: Over 50 POS, ordering and loyalty integrations connect responses to the orders behind them.
  • Built for restaurant groups: Location filters, theme categories and menu reports reflect how multi-unit operators review performance.
  • Funded by its users: Nearly a dozen restaurant customers put $3 million into the company in February 2026.

Cons

  • Custom pricing only: You need a quote before you can compare costs with other feedback tools.
  • No self-serve trial: The pricing page offers a demo and a quote, with no free trial to test it on your own.
  • Few independent reviews: G2 shows only a couple of user reviews, so there's little outside feedback to compare.

Price and plans

Not listed publicly. Ovation builds a quote for each account, counting brick-and-mortar sites and ghost kitchens, and larger groups get a volume discount.
