12. Malou

Website: Malou

Malou, based in Paris, helps restaurant groups turn online reviews into something they can act on. Its AI reads reviews for recurring themes, flags sensitive complaints, and drafts replies in your brand's voice, while the same platform also handles local SEO, listings and social posts. You can use the self-serve MalouApp or add Copilot for support from a dedicated marketing expert.

Notable customers: Krispy Kreme, Tudor Steak House NYC, Riviera Dining Group and MELT Oberkampf.

What's new: Malou's recent changes aren't dated publicly, so no 2025 or 2026 release can be pinned down.

What you can do with Malou:

  • Find patterns in feedback. Semantic analysis groups what guests write into recurring themes, so a slow-service problem shows up across many reviews.
  • Catch the reviews that need a person. Mentions of hygiene, discrimination and other sensitive subjects trigger an alert.
  • Reply in a consistent voice. AI drafts follow your brand guidelines and adapt to whether a guest is happy or upset.
  • Improve each reply. A quality score with tips from Google's best practices shows managers where an answer falls short.
  • Compare restaurants. A multi-location dashboard shows feedback for every site, in English, French, Italian or Spanish.

Why Malou stands out: It focuses entirely on restaurants and offers an expert-led option, which helps groups without an in-house marketer act on guest feedback.

It's best for: Groups running at least three restaurants who want review analysis, replies and local marketing in one place.

Pricing: Malou quotes on request and doesn't publish its prices.
