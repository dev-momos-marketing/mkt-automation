12. Malou

Malou helps restaurant groups turn online reviews into feedback they can act on. Based in Paris, it reads reviews for recurring themes, flags sensitive complaints and drafts replies, and the same platform handles local SEO, listings and social posts.

Its analysis works on the reviews your restaurants receive across platforms. Groups that want hands-on help can add Copilot, a dedicated expert who sends monthly recommendations, on top of the self-serve MalouApp.

Key features

  • Recurring themes: Semantic analysis groups what guests write, so a slow-service problem appears as a pattern across many reviews.
  • Sensitive complaint alerts: Reviews about hygiene, discrimination and similar subjects are flagged for a manager.
  • Consistent replies: AI drafts follow your brand guidelines and adapt to whether the guest was happy or unhappy.
  • Customer insights: Every plan includes insights reporting, with advanced performance insights on Growth and Full.
  • Multi-location dashboard: Compare feedback across restaurants in English, French, Italian or Spanish.

Pros

  • Made for restaurants: Malou works only with restaurants, so you don't sort through features built for other industries.
  • Reply coaching: A quality score with tips from Google's best practices shows where an answer falls short.
  • Expert help: Copilot suits groups without a marketer to act on what the reviews say.

Cons

  • No published pricing: Quotes depend on how many locations you manage.
  • Full plan for social: Social media management and Google menu sync are only in the top plan.
  • Limited independent reviews: G2 lists one MalouApp review, too few to give it a rating.

Price and plans

Malou quotes on request. Its three plans (Essential, Growth and Full) are priced by location count, with Store Locator and Boosters as add-ons.
