1. Momos

Momos helps restaurant brands with many locations collect, answer and learn from guest feedback in one platform. Post-visit surveys, reviews, app comments, social messages and delivery-app feedback flow into a shared inbox, where an AI agent replies and analytics group complaints by type and store.

It also counts among the AI-powered reputation management platforms for restaurants, since public reviews run through the same workflow as private feedback. Papa Murphy's collected 571,946 survey submissions in its first year on Momos and saw its incident rate drop 45%.

Key features

  • Surveys that resolve issues: Alfie sends customizable post-visit surveys in real time and follows up right away when a guest reports a problem.
  • Feedback from every source: The Unified Inbox takes in survey answers, Google reviews, social DMs and App Store comments, and AI triage routes each item to the right location.
  • Complaints sorted by type: AI tags each complaint, such as order accuracy, wait time or service quality, so a pattern across stores shows up quickly.
  • A reply for every guest: Alfie writes back in your brand's tone with each guest's visit history in view, and hands cases to your team when a person should step in.
  • Delivery and app feedback: Comments from delivery apps such as Uber Eats and DoorDash, and from your own app, land in the same place.

Pros

  • One loop from survey to fix: Feedback, replies, recovery offers and incident analytics share one system, so nothing falls between tools.
  • Time back for managers: Because Alfie works as an AI agent for customer support, store managers can focus on cases that need a call or a visit.
  • Insight by location: Topic and incident analytics show what matters most to guests at each store, so you can work on the parts of the guest experience that count.
  • Translation built in: A store can answer a survey comment or review in the guest's own language.

Cons

  • Pricing on request: Quotes follow a demo, and there's no free trial.
  • Chain-sized: A single restaurant collecting a few surveys a week may not need a platform of AI agents.
  • Onboarding first: Your first automated replies go out after a guided setup that runs about a week.

Price and plans

Momos doesn't publish prices; you get a custom quote after a demo, which you can book on the Momos website.
