1. Momos

Momos is an AI guest experience platform for multi-location restaurant brands, and it's also one of the AI-powered reputation management platforms built specifically for restaurants. It collects guest feedback from reviews, surveys, apps, social and delivery platforms, has an AI agent write back to guests, and shows each location what its feedback says about operations.

Notable customers: Caribou Coffee, Papa Murphy's, Potbelly and Condado Tacos.

What's new: Momos shipped AI copilots, AI ticket summaries, AI brand guidelines and mobile-first reports in 2025, along with integrations for Square, Otter and Paytronix. Also in 2025, Potbelly brought its guest messages, support cases and reviews together in Momos, giving it one view of all 472 restaurants.

What you can do with Momos:

  • Bring all feedback into one inbox. The Unified Inbox pulls in POS data, social DMs, App Store comments and Google reviews, then applies AI triage to route each item to the right location.
  • Reply to every guest quickly. Alfie, the Experience Agent, writes back within minutes using the tone your brand has trained it on, with each guest's visit history, preferences and past problems in view.
  • Turn complaints into return visits. An apology can come with loyalty points, a coupon or an offer, which Alfie issues directly through your loyalty system.
  • Cover feedback on every channel. The same agent works across post-visit surveys, delivery app feedback, website chat and in-app support, and hands cases to your team when a person should step in.
  • Respond in the guest's language. Built-in live translation means a store can reply to a review or survey comment in the language the guest used.

Because Alfie handles routine replies as an AI agent for customer support, your managers can spend their time on the feedback that needs a call or a visit.

The analytics side shows which topics drive positive and negative feedback at each store, so you can work on the parts of the guest experience that matter most to your guests.

Across its 1,000+ restaurants, Papa Murphy's uses Momos to automate 97.7% of its review replies and has recovered over $2M in revenue through offers to unhappy guests.
