8. Reputation

Reputation is an enterprise feedback and reputation platform for multi-location companies; it dropped the ".com" from its brand but kept the reputation.com address. It puts private surveys beside public reviews, listings and social, then analyzes all of that feedback by theme and sentiment.

For feedback management, the plan matters: surveys come in steps, from one basic template on Rep Core + Pulse to six advanced templates and email survey requests on Rep Core + Surveys. The company, based in San Ramon, California, marked 20 years in business in March 2026.

Key features

  • Surveys next to reviews: Private answers and public star ratings sit side by side, so you can check whether both point to the same problem.
  • Sentiment and text analytics: Comments from reviews and surveys are grouped into themes such as service and cleanliness across all your sites.
  • Rep Score: Every reputation signal is rolled into one number, with journey insights on the Pulse plan.
  • Outreach campaigns: The Surveys plan adds targeted outreach alongside survey invitations by email.
  • Reputation IQ: You ask questions of your feedback in plain English and get an answer without building a report.

Pros

  • Private and public in one view: Head office sees survey results and review trends in the same system.
  • Clear survey tiers: The published price list shows which survey features each plan includes before you talk to sales.
  • Order-based review requests: Dine-in, delivery or POS events can trigger an invitation by SMS, email or QR code.
  • Well reviewed: G2 users rate it 4.5 out of 5 from 2,470 reviews.

Cons

  • Full surveys on the top plan: Rep Core has no surveys, so a complete feedback program needs Rep Core + Surveys at $150 per location per month.
  • Many industries: Healthcare, automotive, real estate, financial services and retail share the platform with food and beverage, so it's less tailored to restaurants than specialist tools.
  • Add-ons on request: Ticketing, social and competitive insights cost extra, and their prices aren't listed.

Price and plans

Prices are listed per location, per month. Enterprise plans are custom.

  • Rep Core: $80 per location, per month.
  • Rep Core + Pulse: $115 per location, per month.
  • Rep Core + Surveys: $150 per location, per month.
  • Enterprise: Custom pricing for 125+ locations.
