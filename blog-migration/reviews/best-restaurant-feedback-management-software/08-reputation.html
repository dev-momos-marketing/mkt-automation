8. Reputation

Website: Reputation.com

Reputation, which dropped the ".com" from its brand name but still uses the reputation.com domain, is an enterprise platform for multi-location companies. It combines reviews, surveys, listings, social and, more recently, tracking of how AI search tools describe your brand. The company, based in San Ramon, California, marked 20 years in business in March 2026.

Notable customers: Tortilla, plus Mitchells & Butlers in a case study from 2018.

What's new: May 2026 brought Reputation IQ, which lets you put questions to all of your feedback data in plain English, and expanded its AI Reputation Manager, a view into the way Perplexity, Gemini or ChatGPT describe your restaurants to people searching.

What you can do with Reputation.com:

  • Respond to reviews centrally. Google, Yelp, Facebook, Tripadvisor and other sites feed one inbox, so area managers can clear every location's queue in one sitting.
  • Request reviews at the right moment. Invitations by SMS, email or QR code can fire from dine-in, delivery or POS events.
  • Run surveys beside reviews. Private feedback from guests sits next to what they post publicly.
  • Spot themes in comments. Text analytics group feedback into topics such as service and cleanliness, showing which issue keeps coming back.
  • Check your AI search presence. AI Location Presence Insights and AI Reputation Manager show how your locations appear in answer engines.
  • Handle listings and social. Listings management, social listening and publishing are part of the same platform.

Why Reputation stands out: It covers a wide span of feedback sources for large brands and has moved early into AI search visibility, which matters as more guests ask chatbots where to eat.

It's best for: Restaurant, hospitality, automotive and healthcare groups with many sites that need reviews, surveys and listings run centrally with enterprise reporting.

Pricing: Prices are custom and not listed on the site, so expect a sales conversation first.
