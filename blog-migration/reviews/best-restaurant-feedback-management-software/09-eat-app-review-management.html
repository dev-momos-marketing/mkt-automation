9. Eat App Review Management

Eat App is a Dubai-based restaurant reservation, table management and guest CRM platform, and its review management tools sit inside it. Reviews from Google, Facebook and other sites collect in one searchable dashboard, where staff can reply, track response times and read AI sentiment analysis.

Because reviews live next to bookings and guest profiles, you can see a regular's visit history beside what they wrote. That makes it a practical choice if you already use Eat App as your restaurant guest experience platform, or plan to.

Key features

  • Searchable review feed: Filter reviews by keyword, platform or star rating, with a time-stamped activity feed that shows when each one arrived and when your team replied.
  • AI sentiment and summaries: Eat App's AI highlights trends in guest satisfaction and summarizes reviews so managers can focus on what changed.
  • Smart rules: Set rules that automate responses, flag important feedback and alert the team when someone needs to act.
  • Post-dining surveys: Custom surveys go out after a reservation to collect private feedback, starting on the Core plan.
  • Review at table: On Pro, a QR code on tables or receipts lets guests leave a review, see your offers, book again or fill in a survey.

Pros

  • Feedback meets guest data: Reviews sit in the same CRM as reservations, so each guest's booking history is in the same system.
  • Easy to test: Prices are published, and you can try any paid plan free for 14 days.
  • Well rated: G2 users give the Eat App platform 4.7 out of 5, based on 40 reviews.

Cons

  • Sold with reservations: Review management comes with the Core plan ($199 per month, or $159 billed yearly) and higher.
  • Google-centered replies: The plan comparison describes replying to Google reviews; other sites appear only as "Facebook and more."
  • QR reviews need Pro: Review at table and the loyalty suite are part of the Pro plan at $389 per month, or $299 billed yearly.
  • Two systems if you book elsewhere: If your reservations run on another platform, you'd be running Eat App alongside it.

Price and plans

Review management comes with Eat App's Core, Pro and Enterprise plans. A free plan covers up to 100 guests a month without review tools, and you can try any paid plan free for 14 days. Prices below are in US dollars.

  • Core: $199 per month, or $159 per month billed yearly.
  • Pro: $389 per month, or $299 per month billed yearly.
  • Enterprise: Custom pricing for groups with 8 or more venues.
