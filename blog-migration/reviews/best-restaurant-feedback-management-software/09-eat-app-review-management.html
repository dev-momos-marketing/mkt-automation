9. Eat App Review Management

Website: EatApp

Eat App Review Management is an add-on inside Eat App, a Dubai-based restaurant reservation and guest experience platform. It pulls your reviews into one view, lets staff answer them without leaving the app, and asks guests for a review after they dine.

Keep in mind that it isn't sold as a standalone reputation tool. You get it by running Eat App for bookings, table management and guest CRM, so it makes the most sense if you're already on that system or choosing one.

Notable customers: Emaar Hospitality Group and The Maine Group, both named in Eat App press materials from 2021.

What's new: In January 2026, Eat App announced a $10M Series B extension led by PSG Equity through Zenchef, a partnership with Swiggy in India, and its purchase of ReserveGo, an Indian reservations platform used by more than 1,000 restaurants.

What you can do with Eat App:

  • See reviews in one dashboard. Feedback from Google, Facebook and Bing collects in a single screen for each venue.
  • Reply from inside Eat App. Hosts and managers can answer reviews in the same tool they use to run the floor.
  • Follow up after the visit. Guests get a feedback request once they've dined, and those with a good experience are pointed toward a public review site.
  • Read sentiment by venue. Review analytics show how guest mood differs from one location to the next.
  • Connect reviews to guest profiles. Because feedback sits in the same CRM as bookings, you can see a regular's history next to what they wrote.
  • Capture partner bookings. Reservations from channels such as Tripadvisor and Mozrest feed into the same guest database.

Why Eat App stands out: You get reservations, guest data and basic review handling from a single vendor, which keeps the tech stack small for an independent restaurant or a small group. If you use the post-visit flow, check Google's rules: Google discourages sending only happy guests to leave reviews.

It's best for: Restaurants that use Eat App, or plan to, for reservations and guest CRM and want simple review monitoring and replies in the same place.

Pricing: Eat App offers a free plan and paid tiers, with review management sold as an add-on. Prices vary by region, so check the pricing page for your market.
