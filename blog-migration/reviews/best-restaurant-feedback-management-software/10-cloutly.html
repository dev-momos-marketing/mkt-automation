10. Cloutly

Cloutly is a review and messaging platform for businesses with several locations, from salons and clinics to restaurants. It asks customers for reviews by text and email, collects reviews and messages in one inbox with AI reply drafts, and counts what reviewers keep raising at each location.

For restaurants, a request can go out when the bill closes in Square or Clover, so each review is credited to the location where the guest ate. Cloutly names Merivale, Sorsi e Morsi and Francesca's among its restaurant users.

Key features

  • Requests from the till: Texts and emails go out after service on a delay you choose, follow-ups stop once a review lands, and recent reviewers are skipped.
  • QR codes for walk-ins: A code on the bill folder or table reaches guests whose number you don't have.
  • Shared inbox: Google and Facebook reviews, plus 39 review sites from Essentials, sit beside Messenger, Instagram DMs and texts, filtered by location.
  • Draft with AI: Replies are written from the review itself in a Friendly, Professional or Playful tone, and you decide when to send.
  • Feedback counts: Ask, on the Growth plan, answers plain-English questions with exact counts, and Signals tallies negative mentions against ten hospitality themes per restaurant.

Pros

  • Transparent pricing: Per-location prices are published, and you can try it for two weeks without entering a card.
  • Location-level setup: Each location gets its own campaigns, review page and QR codes, and head office sees them side by side.
  • Works with common systems: Square and Clover trigger requests, and the REST API is on every plan.
  • Answers with evidence: Ask shows the reviews behind each number, which helps you decide what to fix first.

Cons

  • Starter covers two sites: Only Google and Facebook reviews are included until Essentials.
  • Insight tools cost more: Ask, competitor tracking and listings management are only on Growth.
  • SMS caps: Each location gets 50, 100 or 200 SMS credits a month; more need a pay-as-you-go pack.
  • Signals on request: The Cloutly team switches on the restaurant themes, since the platform serves many kinds of service business.

Price and plans

Cloutly has a 14-day free trial with no credit card. Prices are per location, per month, billed monthly; yearly billing is paid upfront with a month free.

  • Starter: $39 per location, per month.
  • Essentials: $55 per location, per month.
  • Growth: $69 per location, per month.
  • Enterprise: Priced after a pilot; ten or more locations book a rollout call.
