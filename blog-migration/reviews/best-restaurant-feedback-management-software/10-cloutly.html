10. Cloutly

Website: Cloutly

Cloutly is a review management tool for businesses with several locations. It asks customers for reviews by text, email or QR code, gathers incoming reviews into one inbox, and lets you display your best ones on your website. Most of its published customers come from retail and services, so restaurants are one audience among several.

Notable customers: Testimonials on Cloutly's site come from Swimart and Swyftx; it doesn't name a restaurant customer.

What's new: Cloutly hasn't announced dated product updates in 2025 or 2026.

What you can do with Cloutly:

  • Run review campaigns. Automated SMS and email requests go out per location, and guests can post a review without creating a login.
  • Collect reviews at the counter. A printed QR code lets walk-in guests leave a review even when you don't have their phone number or email.
  • Monitor sites in one inbox. Google and Facebook are covered on every plan, and 40+ other review sites open up from the Growth tier.
  • Draft replies with AI. Magic Response writes an answer based on what the guest said, and you can post it straight back to the review site.
  • Show reviews on your site. Website widgets display recent reviews to people deciding where to eat.
  • Track competitors. Higher plans add competitor monitoring for each location.

Why Cloutly stands out: Its pricing is published, billed month to month per location, so a small group can compare plans and get started without waiting on a sales call.

It's best for: Small multi-location service and hospitality businesses that want easy review requests and a shared inbox at a predictable price.

Pricing: Per location per month with a 14-day free trial: Starter is $27 (Google and Facebook only), Growth runs $63 to $69 and Power $73 to $79 depending on billing, with custom pricing for 15+ locations.
