5. Tattle

Website: Tattle

Tattle is a guest feedback and customer experience platform designed around how restaurants operate. Short surveys reach guests right after they order, triggered by the ordering, loyalty or POS system you already run, and Tattle's AI then ranks which change would lift each store's scores most. Public reviews are tracked in the same place, and the company remains independent.

Notable customers: Hooters, Red Robin, Chili's parent Brinker International and Kung Fu Tea.

What's new: Kung Fu Tea picked Tattle in January 2026 to measure how guests feel across its channels. That followed a $5 million raise in August 2025 aimed at its AI features, and a newer iOS and Android app now puts alerts in front of GMs and franchisees.

What you can do with Tattle:

  • Survey guests after every order. Surveys trigger automatically from tools such as Toast, Olo, Punchh, Paytronix, Thanx and Square, so you hear from a steady share of real guests.
  • Tie comments to operations. Feedback is scored against areas like speed, accuracy, food quality and hospitality, which tells a GM where the shift went wrong.
  • Get a ranked to-do list. AI recommendations point each location at the improvement likely to lift scores the most.
  • Track reviews beside surveys. Google and Yelp reviews appear alongside private survey answers, giving you public and private feedback in one picture.
  • Compare locations over time. Trend dashboards cover metrics such as top box score and recovery rate for each store.
  • Act from a phone. The manager app sends push alerts for serious incidents and lists AI Coach action items, and existing subscribers get it at no extra charge.

Why Tattle stands out: It's built around structured surveys linked to restaurant operations, so feedback arrives organized by the parts of service a manager can coach, along with service recovery workflows for guests who had a bad visit.

Who it’s best for: Multi-unit restaurant brands and franchisees in QSR, fast casual and casual dining that want guest feedback tied to what happens in each location.

Pricing: Prices aren't published, so you'll need to ask Tattle for a quote.
