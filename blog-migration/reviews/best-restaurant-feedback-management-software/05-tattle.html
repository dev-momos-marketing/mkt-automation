5. Tattle

Tattle is a restaurant guest feedback platform that collects short post-order surveys through the ordering, loyalty and POS tools you already run. Its AI reads the answers location by location and ranks the change that would raise each store's scores most, while public reviews are tracked in the same place.

Among restaurant feedback management software, Tattle is built around structured surveys: every answer is tied to a part of service a manager can coach. Kung Fu Tea chose it in January 2026 to follow guest sentiment across its channels.

Key features

  • Surveys after every order: Toast, Olo, Punchh, Paytronix, Thanx and Square trigger a survey automatically, so you hear from a steady share of everyday guests.
  • Feedback by operating area: Answers are scored on speed, accuracy, food quality and hospitality, and item-level ratings flag specific dishes.
  • Ranked to-do list: AI Coach names the improvement with the most expected impact at each location, with monthly goals.
  • Store comparisons: Trend dashboards track top box score and recovery rate for every location over time.
  • Guest recovery: Workflows route unhappy guests to a quick follow-up.
  • Manager app: Surveys and reviews share one inbox on iOS and Android, with push alerts for serious incidents.

Pros

  • Steady feedback flow: Automatic triggers bring in answers from regular guests with no comment cards or staff prompts.
  • Structure a GM can use: Because answers are tagged to operations, a manager can see which part of the shift needs attention.
  • Guided onboarding: Every plan includes a setup specialist and custom survey design, and Tattle says most customers collect feedback within 1 to 2 weeks.
  • Published prices: Per-location rates are listed on Tattle's site, so you can budget before a call.

Cons

  • Review management costs extra: Responding to and growing reviews across platforms comes with a Reputation Management add-on whose price isn't listed.
  • Built for chains: Pricing tiers begin at 5 locations, so single-unit operators sit outside the published plans.
  • Survey-first scope: Tattle centers on surveys and reviews, so listings or guest messaging would need a separate tool.

Price and plans

Tattle publishes per-location pricing, billed per month, and the rate falls as you add locations. There's no free trial listed.

  • 5 to 10 units: $125 per location, per month.
  • 11 to 20 units: $100 per location, per month.
  • 21 to 50 units: $85 per location, per month.
  • 51 to 200 units: $65 per location, per month.
  • 201+ units: Custom pricing.
