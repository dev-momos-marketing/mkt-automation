7. Marqii

Marqii is a restaurant platform for menus, listings, reviews and social posts. Its feedback tools gather public reviews from search and delivery sites, add sentiment analysis and AI summaries, and ask guests for reviews through branded forms and texts.

It fits best when most of your guest feedback arrives as online reviews and you want to read it alongside the menu and listing details guests see before they visit.

Key features

  • Review insights: Sentiment analysis and AI summaries surface recurring praise and complaints, location by location.
  • First Party Reviews: Branded forms reach guests by link, QR code or an embed on your website, and their answers feed Marqii's insights.
  • Review Generation: After a guest orders through a Marqii partner, a templated text asks about the experience and links to the review site you choose.
  • Custom notifications: Alerts let your team know when new reviews land, and the managed team flags urgent ones to you directly.
  • Monthly check-ins: Managed Review Response customers go through sentiment, response trends and keywords with Marqii's team each month.

Pros

  • Every channel in one place: Feedback from DoorDash, Uber Eats and ezCater sits beside your Google, Yelp and Facebook reviews, which makes channel comparisons easier.
  • Owned and public feedback: Branded forms collect comments under your own name, while texts point guests toward public review sites.
  • Accurate menus: POS menu sync keeps dishes and prices current online, so guests see what you actually serve.

Cons

  • Texts need a partner order: Review requests by text go only to guests who order through a Marqii partner, so other guests need the form or QR code.
  • Add-on costs: Below Scale, Review Generation carries a $100 monthly platform fee per brand, plus $15 per 1,000 texts.
  • Yearly terms: Each location is billed under an annual contract, and there's no free trial.

Price and plans

Marqii doesn't offer a free trial or free plan; you can book a free demo. Plans are priced per location, per month, on an annual contract, and managed replies cost extra, from $100 a month for 50 reviews.

  • Base: $90 per location, per month (listings and menus).
  • Growth: $145 per location, per month (adds review monitoring and replies).
  • Scale: $180 per location, per month (adds AI-suggested replies, review generation and social).
  • Marqii Package: $250 per location, per month (adds Host location pages).
