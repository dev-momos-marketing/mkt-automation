7. Marqii

Website: Marqii

Built specifically for restaurant operators, Marqii handles the digital upkeep that eats into a manager's week: menus, listings, review replies and social posts. Menu edits made in your POS flow out to search and review sites, and AI drafts responses to guest feedback. Recent releases have added ways to ask guests for reviews directly.

Notable customers: IHOP franchisee Romulus Restaurant Group, California Tortilla and Uccello's Hospitality Group.

What's new: A July 2026 launch with Thanx sends guests a text after they buy, asking for a review on Google, Facebook or TripAdvisor. Before that, November 2025 brought First Party Reviews, a branded form you can share as a link, a QR code or an embed on your site.

What you can do with Marqii:

  • Edit your menu in one place. A price change entered in the POS reaches Google, Yelp and other sites automatically, so a guest never orders from last season's menu.
  • Manage location details. Opening hours, photos, specials and phone numbers stay aligned for every restaurant you run.
  • Work reviews from a single queue. Feedback from Google, Yelp, OpenTable, Uber Eats, ezCater and other platforms lands in the same Marqii inbox.
  • Reply with AI help. Suggested responses speed up manager replies, and a managed service can take over response work for busy brands.
  • Bring in fresh reviews. Branded first-party forms and texts sent after a purchase increase how many guests leave feedback.
  • Plan social content. Facebook and Google Business Profile posts can be scheduled alongside your listings work.

Why Marqii stands out: Every feature targets a restaurant problem, especially menu accuracy, and the company lists its prices openly, with the Base plan at $74.99 a month for each location (billed annually) and Pro, which adds reviews, at $119.99.

It’s best for: Restaurant groups and franchisees with several locations that would rather run menus, listings and review replies from one restaurant-only tool.
