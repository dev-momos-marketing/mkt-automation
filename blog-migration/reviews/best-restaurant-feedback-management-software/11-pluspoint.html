11. Pluspoint

Website: Pluspoint

Headquartered in London, Pluspoint gives brands with many locations one place to handle their online reputation and local marketing. One subscription covers review requests and replies, listings, NPS surveys, messaging campaigns and social posts, and the company publishes a flat price per location. Several of its case studies come from European restaurant groups.

Notable customers: Portugal's Starfoods Group, which runs 60+ restaurants, along with Paprica Burger and SushiCafé Group.

What's new: Pluspoint's MCP connector became generally available around May 2026. With it, a manager working in Gemini, ChatGPT or Claude can pull up recent reviews, check listings for errors and line up review requests; social posting through the same route is promised next.

What you can do with Pluspoint:

  • Ask for reviews on many channels. Requests can go out by WhatsApp, SMS, email or a table QR code, which helps in markets where guests prefer messaging apps.
  • Reply from a central inbox. Google, Facebook and other reviews arrive together, with AI-suggested answers your team can edit.
  • Get warned about bad feedback. Real-time alerts flag negative reviews so a manager can step in quickly.
  • Measure satisfaction directly. NPS and CSAT surveys give you private feedback to compare with public ratings.
  • Keep listings in sync. Google and Facebook listings are included, and an add-on pushes details to 200+ directories such as Apple Maps, Yelp and TripAdvisor.
  • Compare locations. Per-location analytics break down sentiment and the keywords guests use most.

Why Pluspoint stands out: It bundles reviews, listings, surveys and social posting at one transparent price, which makes budgeting simple for a group adding new sites, and its AI assistant connector is an early move for this category.

It's best for: Restaurant groups and franchises, including those in Europe, that want reviews, listings and local SEO in a single tool with a published per-location price.

Pricing: Each location costs $29 a month, or roughly $24.17 on annual billing, with all features and a minimum of 3 locations. The base includes 3 users plus 50 AI credits, the Listings Sync add-on is $39 a month per location, you can trial it free for 14 days, and groups over 30 locations or users move to a custom plan.
