11. Pluspoint

Pluspoint is a London-based platform for multi-location businesses that covers review requests and replies, listings, surveys, messaging campaigns and social posts in one subscription. It publishes a flat price per location, and several of its case studies come from European restaurant groups.

For feedback management, the useful part is the private channel: QR code surveys collect NPS and satisfaction scores, so a kitchen or service issue reaches the manager before it becomes a public review. Portugal's Starfoods Group, with 60+ restaurants, is one of its customers.

Key features

  • NPS and CSAT surveys: Guests rate their visit through a QR code survey, and results are benchmarked venue by venue.
  • Negative feedback alerts: Real-time notifications flag a poor score or review so a manager can follow up quickly.
  • Review requests on four channels: Invitations go out by SMS, email, WhatsApp or a QR code on the table or receipt.
  • Shared inbox with AI drafts: Google, Facebook and other reviews arrive together, with AI-written replies your team can edit and publish.
  • Sentiment by location: Analytics break down sentiment and the keywords guests use most for each restaurant.
  • Win-back campaigns: Messaging campaigns and coupons reach guests whose contacts came in through surveys and reviews.

Pros

  • Clear budgeting: One published price per location includes surveys, reviews, listings and social posting, so adding a site has a known cost.
  • Messaging-app reach: WhatsApp requests help with guests who answer chat messages faster than email.
  • Easy to try: The 14-day free trial starts without payment details.
  • Restaurant references: European groups such as Starfoods Group, Paprica Burger and SushiCafé Group have published case studies with Pluspoint.

Cons

  • Three-location minimum: The smallest subscription is $87 a month on monthly billing, which rules out a single restaurant.
  • Capped AI credits: The account includes 50 AI credits a month, and extra credits cost $0.49 each.
  • Directory sync costs extra: Pushing listings beyond Google and Facebook needs the Listings Sync add-on.

Price and plans

Pluspoint has one plan with every feature, billed per location, and a 14-day free trial that needs no payment details. Annual billing charges 10 months for 12.

  • Pluspoint: $29 per location, per month ($24.17 billed annually), minimum 3 locations, with 3 users and 50 AI credits a month.
  • Listings Sync add-on: $39 per location, per month, billed annually.
  • Extra users and credits: $19 per user, per month; $0.49 per AI credit.
  • Custom: A quote above 30 locations or 30 users.
