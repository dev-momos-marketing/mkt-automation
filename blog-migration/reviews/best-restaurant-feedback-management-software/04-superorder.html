4. Superorder

Superorder is an AI agent platform for restaurant chains that covers restaurant reputation management, post-order surveys, delivery profitability and location scorecards. Known as Forward Kitchens until 2023, it now centers on agents that collect feedback, answer reviews and win back unhappy guests.

For feedback management, its strength is timing: a survey reaches the guest right after an order, while the details are fresh. Turning Point Restaurants partnered with Superorder in June 2025 to gather post-order feedback across its 32 East Coast locations, and our guide explains why answering reviews pays off for restaurants.

Key features

  • Surveys & Feedback Agent: AI sends a survey after each order, so problems reach you privately before they turn into public reviews.
  • Recovery offers: Guests who report a poor experience can receive tailored compensation automatically, which gives them a reason to order again.
  • Insights and alerts: AI reads feedback and operating data together to flag trends, sentiment shifts and guest issues in real time.
  • AI scorecards: Locations are compared side by side, so you can see which stores need attention first.
  • Review Management Agent: Reviews from Google, Yelp and delivery platforms land in one dashboard, with AI-written replies that reference each guest's order.

Pros

  • Feedback from off-premise orders: Post-order surveys reach delivery and takeout guests, who never see your dining room or your servers.
  • A closed loop: Surveys, recovery offers and review replies run in one system, so an unhappy guest can be heard and compensated without manual steps.
  • Enterprise support: The plan includes unlimited users, a dedicated account manager and a 24-hour support response SLA.

Cons

  • Quote only: Superorder doesn't publish prices, so you'll need a demo to learn the cost.
  • No free trial: A 30-day money-back guarantee stands in for a trial period.
  • Delivery-first design: Much of the platform targets delivery and takeout, so a dine-in independent may never use the delivery agents.

Price and plans

Superorder doesn't publish prices; you get a quote for its Enterprise plan after a demo. Pricing is store-based and billed monthly, and a 30-day money-back guarantee takes the place of a free trial.
