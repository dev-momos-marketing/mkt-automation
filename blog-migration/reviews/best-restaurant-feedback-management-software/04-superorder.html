4. Superorder

Website: Superorder

Superorder is an AI agent platform for restaurant chains that handles restaurant reputation management, post-order surveys, delivery profitability and location scorecards. The company was called Forward Kitchens until 2023, when it rebranded and moved from delivery operations software toward AI agents that write personalized review replies and win back unhappy guests.

Notable customers: Tasty Restaurant Group, L&L Hawaiian Barbecue, Wow Bao and Marco's Pizza.

What's new: In July 2025, L&L Hawaiian Barbecue began working with Superorder on guest engagement and feedback collection across its 230+ restaurants. Superorder hasn't announced any product releases in 2026.

What you can do with Superorder:

  • Pull reviews into one view. The Review Management Agent collects reviews from Google, Yelp and the delivery apps so off-premise complaints don't get lost.
  • Reply with order context. AI-written responses reference what the guest actually ordered, which reads far more personal than a generic apology.
  • Hear from guests privately. The Surveys & Feedback Agent asks for feedback after each order, giving unhappy guests a direct line to you before they post publicly.
  • Send recovery offers automatically. Dissatisfied guests receive an offer to come back, without a manager having to chase each case.
  • Protect delivery margins. Separate agents focus on the profitability of your third-party delivery business.
  • Compare stores on a scorecard. AI scorecards and real-time alerts show which locations are slipping and which guest issues need attention today.

Why Superorder stands out: Its feedback and recovery flows are designed around delivery and takeout orders, so they reach guests whose problems often never surface in dine-in feedback.

Best for: Multi-unit restaurant brands with heavy delivery and takeout volume that want AI replies across Google, Yelp and delivery apps plus a way to recover unhappy guests.

Pricing: Superorder doesn't publish prices; you'll need to request a custom quote.
