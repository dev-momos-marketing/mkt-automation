2. ReviewTrackers

ReviewTrackers turns public reviews into feedback a restaurant group can act on. It gathers what guests write across review sites, sorts comments by topic and sentiment, and gives store managers approved replies so each guest hears back.

At heart it's a reputation management platform, owned by InMoment since 2022 and part of the Press Ganey Forsta group since May 2025. Ted's Montana Grill is one of the restaurant brands featured in its case studies.

Key features

  • Topic and sentiment analysis: Comments are grouped into themes, so you can tell whether slow service or food temperature drives more complaints at a given store.
  • Feedback requests: Automated emails and texts invite guests to share how their visit went, which adds fresh comments for quieter locations.
  • One feed for every site: Google, Facebook, TripAdvisor and many smaller platforms land in a single dashboard, with alerts for anything new.
  • Replies in your voice: Smart Response templates and AI-drafted answers help managers close the loop quickly.
  • Store-level reporting: Each owner and area manager sees the feedback for the restaurants they run.

Pros

  • Feedback in one place: Review data can sit inside InMoment's customer experience platform next to your survey results.
  • Restaurant references: Its case studies include a restaurant chain, Ted's Montana Grill.
  • Light work for store teams: Templates let a general manager answer in a few clicks while head office keeps control of wording.
  • G2 rating: Users give it 4.6 out of 5 on G2, from 161 reviews.

Cons

  • Theme analysis on Essential: Experience analysis and competitor insights aren't part of the Starter plan.
  • Changing ownership: Two acquisitions since 2022 mean some new features, such as AI replies, are announced under the InMoment name.
  • Unlisted prices: You need a meeting to find out what your locations would cost.

Price and plans

ReviewTrackers doesn't publish prices; the plans page asks you to book a meeting for a quote. Pricing is per location, per month and paid annually, across Data Only, Starter and Essential.
