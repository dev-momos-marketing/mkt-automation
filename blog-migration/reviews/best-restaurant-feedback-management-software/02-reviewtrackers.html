2. ReviewTrackers

Website: ReviewTrackers

ReviewTrackers is a reputation management platform for multi-location brands that gathers public reviews, helps managers answer them and reports on what guests keep mentioning. Its ownership has changed twice: InMoment acquired the company in 2022, then Press Ganey Forsta acquired InMoment in May 2025. You can still find ReviewTrackers sold under its original name.

Notable customers: Ted's Montana Grill on the restaurant side, along with PACK & SEND and Chem-Dry.

What's new: In July 2025, InMoment added AI Auto Responding to its reputation product, which writes on-brand review replies from prompts you save; it ships on the Locations Starter and Locations Essential tiers that ReviewTrackers also uses.

What you can do with ReviewTrackers:

  • Read every review in one feed. Google, Facebook, TripAdvisor and other sites flow into a single dashboard, and you get an alert whenever a new review lands for any location.
  • Give managers approved replies. Smart Response templates written by your brand team let a general manager answer a guest in a few clicks without going off script.
  • Ask guests for reviews. Automated requests go out by email and text after a visit, which helps quieter locations build up fresh reviews.
  • Find recurring complaints. Sentiment and topic analytics group feedback so you can see whether slow service or cold food is the bigger problem at a given store.
  • Benchmark nearby rivals. Competitor tracking compares your ratings and review counts with restaurants around each location.
  • Mirror your franchise structure. Location-level reports and role-based access let owners, area managers and head office each see the stores they run.

Why ReviewTrackers stands out: It keeps review work simple for store teams while giving head office the reporting it needs, and its data can also feed InMoment's wider customer experience platform if your brand runs surveys there.

Best for: Multi-location and franchise restaurant brands that want central control over review replies and reputation reporting, especially those already working with InMoment or Press Ganey Forsta.

Pricing: Custom and priced per location; the plans page asks you to book a meeting for a quote.
