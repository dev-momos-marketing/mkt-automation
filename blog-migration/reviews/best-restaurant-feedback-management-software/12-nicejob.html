12. NiceJob

Website: NiceJob

NiceJob is reputation marketing software for small service businesses, owned by Paystone since 2021. It sends review requests automatically from the job and invoicing tools a business already uses, then reuses good reviews on your website and in referral and repeat-customer campaigns. Its customers are mostly home-service companies, so a restaurant would be using it outside its main focus.

Notable customers: You Move Me and Evergreen Cleaning, both home-service firms with published case studies.

What's new: In October 2025, NiceJob teamed up with SendJim so home-service businesses can print verified 5-star NiceJob reviews on direct-mail postcards.

What you can do with NiceJob:

  • Request reviews on autopilot. Email and text invitations go out after each sale, with reminders for customers who haven't responded yet.
  • Trigger requests from your tools. Connections to Jobber, Housecall Pro, QuickBooks and Zapier start the process without staff remembering to send anything.
  • Answer reviews with AI. Pro subscribers can shape AI-written answers by setting a preferred length, tone and ground rules, and optionally switch on fully automatic replies.
  • Display reviews on your website. Widgets turn recent praise into social proof for people comparing businesses.
  • Bring customers back. Pro also adds referral campaigns, rebooking reminders and broadcast messages to past customers.

Why NiceJob stands out: Once it's connected to your invoicing or booking software, review collection runs quietly in the background, which suits owners who have little time for marketing tasks.

It’s best for: Small local service businesses, such as cleaners, movers and trades, that want hands-off review collection. Restaurants can use it, but the integrations are built around job-based workflows.

Pricing: The Grow plan starts at $75/month for up to 2,500 customers a year ($95/month for 2,501–5,000), and Pro starts at $125/month. You can try either free for 14 days, and there are no long-term contracts.
