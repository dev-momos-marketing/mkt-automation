16. SOCi

Website: SoCi

Franchise systems use SOCi to run marketing for each location from head office, and the company recently rebranded and switched its domain from meetsoci.com to soci.ai. Day-to-day work on review replies, listings, local search and social posts is done by Genius Agents, which learn your brand's rules, and corporate decides how much freedom they get.

Notable customers: On The Border Mexican Grill & Cantina, Lee's Famous Recipe Chicken, Sport Clips Haircuts and WOWorks, which owns six restaurant brands.

What's new: By September 2026, SOCi counted more than 412,000 deployed agents that together handle upward of 27 million localized tasks annually, up from 300,000 agents in May. In June 2026, Sport Clips rolled out more than 3,400 Genius Agents for reviews and local search across nearly 1,800 salons, escalating sensitive reviews to people.

What you can do with SOCi:

  • Automate review replies within guardrails. Genius Reviews writes answers that follow your brand guidelines and passes delicate cases, like a complaint about illness, to staff.
  • Watch sentiment across stores. Review monitoring and reporting show which locations are trending up or down.
  • Manage local listings. Store hours and details are kept up to date across directories including Bing, Yelp and Google, which helps guests nearby find you.
  • Publish social centrally. Head office plans posts that each location can tailor, with an AI social agent helping produce them.
  • Survey guests directly. Built-in surveys gather first-party feedback to sit beside public reviews.
  • Set rules from the top. Role-based controls let corporate define policies while local managers work inside them.

Why SOCi stands out: It runs at very large scale, with agents deployed across thousands of locations, so a big franchise system can keep review replies, listings and social consistent without adding local marketing staff.

It's best for: Franchise and multi-location brands running hundreds to thousands of sites that want corporate oversight of local reviews, listings and social, carried out mostly by AI agents.

Pricing: Custom. SOCi prices each deal on location count and the mix of products you add.
