16. SOCi

SOCi is a local marketing platform for franchise systems and restaurant chains, run largely by AI agents that learn each brand's rules. On the feedback side, its reputation agent brings public reviews, review requests and customer surveys together, and it can answer reviews for every location within limits set by head office.

It fits when your feedback program has to cover hundreds of locations at once. WOWorks, which owns six restaurant brands, and Lee's Famous Recipe Chicken are customers, and a Punchh integration puts ratings from loyalty feedback next to public reviews.

Key features

  • Customer surveys: Built-in surveys collect first-party feedback that you can read against what guests post in public.
  • Voice of Customer: Sentiment reporting turns review text from every store into trends, so you can see which locations are improving and which need a visit.
  • Review solicitation: Requests to recent guests keep a steady flow of fresh reviews coming in.
  • Loyalty and delivery feedback: The Punchh connection brings loyalty ratings into SOCi, and Grubhub review ratings can be monitored there too.
  • Automated responses: The Genius Reputation Agent replies on brand and hands delicate cases to staff.

Pros

  • Private and public feedback side by side: Survey answers and reviews sit in the same platform, which helps you catch a problem before it reaches your star rating.
  • Human review of sensitive cases: Complaints that need care are flagged for a person instead of getting an automatic answer.
  • Access for every manager: With no per-user fees, each general manager can read their own location's feedback.
  • G2 score: Reviewers on G2 rate SOCi 4.5/5.

Cons

  • Pricing on request: SOCi lists no prices, so budgeting starts with a sales conversation.
  • No trial period: Every deployment is scoped through a demo, so you can't run a pilot on your own first.
  • Made for large systems: Brands with hundreds or thousands of sites are the target, and an independent restaurant would pay for reach it doesn't need.

Price and plans

SOCi doesn't publish prices and has no free trial. Each quote is based on your location count and the Genius Agents and skills you deploy, with no per-user or per-listing fees.
