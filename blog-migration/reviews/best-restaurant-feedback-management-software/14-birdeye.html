14. Birdeye

Website: Birdeye

Birdeye describes itself as an agentic marketing platform for multi-location brands. Reviews are one module among several, next to listings, messaging, social, surveys and visibility in AI search, and a growing set of AI agents now carries out much of that work. In 2025 it added a version trained specifically on restaurant data.

Notable customers: Black Bear Diner and The Saxton Group, which franchises McAlister's Deli.

What's new: At its View 2026 event in September 2026, Birdeye introduced three AI Coworkers named Jay, Myna and Robin, covering marketing, operations and customer experience. Later that month came a link with the SkyTab point-of-sale system, so restaurants on SkyTab can push menu updates straight to their listings.

What you can do with Birdeye:

  • Route bad reviews to the right store. Monitoring across Google, Yelp, Facebook and industry sites comes with alerts and tickets assigned to each location's manager.
  • Let AI handle replies. Responses can be drafted by AI or handled by agents, written to match your brand voice.
  • Grow review counts. Text and email invitations ask recent guests to share their experience.
  • Keep menus and details accurate. Listings management updates directories, and SkyTab users can sync menus automatically.
  • Answer messages in one inbox. Texts, webchat, social DMs and email arrive together for your team.
  • Analyze sentiment by region. Surveys and Insights AI report on how guests feel at the location, district and region level, with competitor benchmarks.

Why Birdeye stands out: It goes furthest toward handing day-to-day reputation and marketing tasks to AI agents, which appeals to brands whose local managers have little time for review work.

It's best for: Multi-location brands and franchise groups, restaurant chains among them, that want reviews, listings, messaging and social on one platform with AI doing much of the execution.

Pricing: Birdeye doesn't publish prices; you receive a custom quote after a demo.
