14. Birdeye

Birdeye is a platform for multi-location brands that gathers guest feedback from reviews and surveys, measures sentiment and acts on it with AI agents. It also manages listings, messaging and social, so feedback sits next to the rest of your local marketing.

For feedback management, the most relevant piece is Robin, the customer experience Coworker launched in September 2026, which runs agents for surveys, sentiment analysis, ticketing and insights. Restaurant brands using Birdeye include Black Bear Diner and The Saxton Group, a McAlister's Deli franchisee.

Key features

  • Guest surveys: Run surveys and read the results beside public reviews from Google, Yelp, Facebook and industry sites.
  • Insights AI: Sentiment is scored across reviews and surveys and reported at the location, district and region level.
  • Ticketing: Each negative review becomes a ticket assigned to the manager of that location, so follow-up has a clear owner.
  • Feedback requests: SMS and email invitations ask recent guests to share their experience, giving you more responses to learn from.
  • Competitor benchmarks: See how your locations compare with nearby competitors.

Pros

  • Feedback and action together: Surveys, review monitoring, ticketing and replies run in one place, which shortens the path from a complaint to a fix.
  • Area roll-ups: District and region reports help multi-unit operators spot where guest sentiment is slipping.
  • Agent support: AI agents draft or post review replies, which frees managers to deal with the issue behind a complaint.
  • G2 rating: Birdeye scores 4.7 out of 5 on G2.

Cons

  • Custom quotes: Birdeye publishes no prices and quotes based on your locations.
  • Broad platform: It's built for multi-location brands across industries, so a group that mainly wants surveys may find it more than it needs.

Price and plans

Birdeye doesn't publish prices; you get a custom quote after sharing your number of locations.
