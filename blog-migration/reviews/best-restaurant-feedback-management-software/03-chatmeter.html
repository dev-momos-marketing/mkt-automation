3. Chatmeter

Website: Chatmeter

Chatmeter is a reputation, listings and local search platform for multi-location brands, with AI that reads reviews and turns them into brand intelligence by location. Alchemer, a survey and customer feedback company, acquired it in September 2025, and it's now sold as Alchemer Reputation Management. For restaurant groups, that puts public reviews and survey answers in the same enterprise platform.

Notable customers: Restaurant brands A&W and MR MIKES Steakhouse Casual, as well as Driven Brands.

What's new: In March 2026, Alchemer released AI Auto-Responder, which drafts and posts review replies under rules you set by rating, platform and wording, and holds sensitive reviews back for a person to handle first.

What you can do with Chatmeter:

  • Watch 100+ review sites. Reviews from across the web land in one dashboard, so a regional manager can scan every store's new feedback in a single pass.
  • Automate routine replies. AI Auto-Responder answers the reviews your rules allow and flags risky ones, such as a food safety complaint, for a human.
  • Query your feedback in everyday words. The Pulse Ai: Signals tool responds to questions about reviews, social posts and surveys, and it can read the photos guests attach to reviews.
  • Compare against local competitors. Competitive Intelligence shows how each location stacks up against the restaurants around it, down to a regional map.
  • Include delivery feedback. Review data from Uber Eats, DoorDash and Grubhub sits next to Google and Yelp, which matters if off-premise is a large share of sales.
  • Keep listings accurate. Listings management and local SEO tracking per location help guests find correct hours and details.

Why Chatmeter stands out: Since the Alchemer deal, you can run guest surveys and review management from one vendor, and July 2025's competitor tools give operators a location-by-location view of how they compare with nearby rivals.

Best for: Restaurant chains and franchise brands that want reviews, listings, AI review analysis and survey feedback together in one enterprise system.

Pricing: Custom; contact sales for a quote.
