3. Chatmeter

Chatmeter is a review, listings and local search platform for multi-location brands, with AI that turns guest reviews into brand intelligence for each location. Since Alchemer acquired it in September 2025, it has been sold as Alchemer Reputation Management, next to Alchemer's survey software.

That pairing matters for feedback management, because what guests write in public and what they tell you in a survey can be analyzed together. If you're building a wider feedback program, our guide to restaurant intelligence explains how the pieces fit.

Key features

  • Feedback from 100+ review sites: New reviews for every store arrive in one dashboard, so a regional manager can scan them in a single pass.
  • Pulse Ai: Signals: Ask about reviews, social posts or survey answers in everyday words, then narrow the answer to a location, region or provider.
  • Surveys through Alchemer: Collect direct feedback with Alchemer's survey tools and set it beside public ratings.
  • Delivery app reviews: Ratings from Uber Eats, DoorDash and Grubhub appear with Google and Yelp, useful when off-premise orders are a large share of sales.
  • AI Auto-Responder: Routine reviews get an on-brand reply under rules you write, while sensitive ones wait for a person.

Pros

  • Public and private feedback together: Reviews and survey answers come from one vendor, which makes it easier to spot a problem both channels mention.
  • Questions in plain language: Operators can query their feedback without building a report first.
  • Reads review photos: Pulse Ai includes the pictures guests attach, so comments about how a dish or dining room looked become part of the analysis.
  • Rated 4.5 on G2: Reviewers on G2 score Alchemer Reputation Management 4.5 out of 5.

Cons

  • Price on request: There's no published price for reputation management; Alchemer's price list covers survey licenses only.
  • Demo before testing: You see the product in a 30-minute personalized demo, with no self-serve trial.
  • Sized for chains: A single restaurant may find the platform larger than its feedback program needs.

Price and plans

Alchemer doesn't list Chatmeter prices; contact its sales team for a quote.
