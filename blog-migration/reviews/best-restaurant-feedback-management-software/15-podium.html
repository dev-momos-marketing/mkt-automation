15. Podium

Website: Podium

Podium is an AI-powered communication platform for local businesses, centered on texting, reviews, payments and an AI Employee that answers leads and books appointments. Its recent launches bundle those pieces into vertical "AI operating systems" for car dealers, medspas and home-service firms. Food service sits outside that list, so a restaurant operator would mostly be buying it for review invitations and messaging.

Notable customers: Mattress Firm, Jackson Hewitt, Ashley Furniture HomeStore and Mountaineer Heating & Cooling; none of its published stories are restaurants.

What's new: In October 2026, Podium reported that its AI agent business had passed $150 million in annual recurring revenue roughly two and a half years after launch. That followed Jerry 2.0, a customizable AI employee on OpenAI models released in December 2025 for automotive, HVAC and medspa businesses.

What you can do with Podium:

  • Invite reviews by text. After a purchase, Podium texts the customer a short message whose link opens your Google profile or a different review platform.
  • Answer leads after hours. The AI Employee replies over text, phone, email and social, handles questions and books appointments when your team is off.
  • Pick up phone calls with AI. A voice agent answers incoming calls so fewer go to voicemail.
  • Reply from a shared inbox. Your staff see SMS threads, website chats, social DMs and Google messages in a single list.
  • Take payment in a chat. A payment link sent inside the conversation means someone can pay a deposit or invoice on their phone.
  • Send text campaigns. Bulk messages promote offers or win back past customers, with reporting on reviews and conversations by location.

Why Podium stands out: It links the first message, the payment and the follow-up review in one system, which works well for businesses that sell over calls and texts.

It's best for: Local service businesses such as auto dealers, repair shops, home services, medspas and retailers. Restaurants that mainly need guest feedback management may find much of the platform unused.

Pricing: Podium doesn't publish prices. Its three tiers (Core, Pro, Signature) are quoted by a sales rep, with the AI Employee available as an extra on top of whichever tier you choose.
