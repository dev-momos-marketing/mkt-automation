15. Yext

Yext is a digital presence platform for multi-location brands, listed on the NYSE as YEXT. In a feedback program, it handles the public side: it gathers reviews from many sites, helps you answer them, invites more guests to rate their visit and keeps hours and menus accurate so guests aren't caught out by wrong details.

Because Yext centers on listings and public reviews, brands that want structured post-visit surveys usually pair it with a survey tool. Popmenu expanded its Yext partnership in April 2026, so restaurants can sync menus and respond to reviews on 70+ platforms.

Key features

  • Reviews in one feed: Feedback from Google, Facebook, Yelp and 80+ industry sites arrives in one dashboard, with alerts so a complaint at one location doesn't slip by.
  • Response drafts: AI suggests a reply to each review, and a manager approves it or sets rules for posting automatically.
  • More feedback where it counts: Requests by email, text or QR code reach guests when they're likely to respond, spread across the review sites that weigh most in search.
  • Sentiment beyond review sites: Scout tracks how Google, Bing and AI assistants describe your brand next to competitors.
  • Correct facts at the source: The Knowledge Graph sends updated hours and menus to Google, Apple Maps, Bing and Facebook.

Pros

  • Many review sources: With 80+ sites covered, you see public feedback from far beyond Google and Yelp.
  • Consistent details: Fixing hours or a menu once updates every listing, so guests read the same information wherever they look.
  • Head office approvals: Approval workflows let a central team check replies from franchise locations before they go live.
  • G2 rating: Yext scores 4.4 out of 5 with G2 reviewers.

Cons

  • Surveys may need another tool: Private guest surveys aren't Yext's focus, so a full feedback program likely needs a second platform.
  • Custom quotes: Yext sets a price for each brand based on locations and products, with no public figures to compare.
  • No trial period: There's no free tier or standard trial, so evaluation happens in a demo.

Price and plans

Yext doesn't publish prices; you get a quote after a demo. Pricing depends on location count and the solution you choose, and billing is typically annual.
