15. Yext

Website: Yext

Yext, listed on the NYSE as YEXT, manages the digital presence of multi-location brands from a single knowledge base. For restaurant feedback, its role is collecting and answering public reviews across many sites and keeping the information guests complain about, such as hours and menus, correct everywhere they search.

Notable customers: Taco Bell, Arby's, Bojangles and Marston's.

What's new: Popmenu expanded its Yext partnership in April 2026, letting restaurants sync menus and listings and respond to reviews on 70+ platforms. Earlier, in March 2025, Yext introduced Scout, an agent that follows brand visibility and sentiment on Google, Bing and AI assistants, a month after acquiring Places Scout.

What you can do with Yext:

  • Monitor and answer reviews. Reviews from many sites can be tracked and answered, with AI drafting the replies.
  • Ask for more reviews. AI-run campaigns invite customers to leave feedback, on third-party review sites too.
  • Read sentiment in AI search. Scout shows how ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity describe you compared with competitors.
  • Keep details accurate. The Knowledge Graph pushes hours and menus to Google, Apple Maps, Bing and Facebook.
  • Control franchise access. Permissions and approvals decide which teams can reply or edit for each location.

Why Yext stands out: It connects review handling to listings and AI search visibility at enterprise scale, which suits brands where inaccurate information drives a share of negative feedback.

It's best for: Multi-location and franchise restaurant brands whose main need is listings accuracy and local search, with review management alongside.

Pricing: Custom for multi-location and enterprise brands; self-serve tiers quoted on third-party sites don't agree with one another, so confirm with Yext.
