10. Freshchat

Freshchat is the messaging and chatbot product from Freshworks, collecting web chat, WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook Messenger, SMS and email into one shared inbox. Its Freddy AI Agent answers guest questions automatically, and it is also sold inside the Freshdesk Omni suite for teams that want support and messaging together.

Notable customers: Orderin, Fantastic Services and FinAccel.

What's new: With its November 2025 product launch, Freshworks rebuilt Freshdesk Omni, which includes Freshchat, around a Command Center workspace and introduced its agentic Freddy AI platform at the same time.

What you can do with Freshchat:

  • Answer guests with Freddy. The AI agent learns from your knowledge base and replies on every connected channel, from WhatsApp to web chat.
  • Build agents without code. Freddy AI Agent Studio lets you set up AI agents and workflows, for example a gift card balance check.
  • Send chats to the right person. Conversations are assigned automatically, so a catering request reaches the events team.
  • Reach past guests. Proactive campaigns over WhatsApp and web chat share promotions or reopening news.
  • Help staff reply faster. Freddy AI Copilot, a paid add-on, assists agents while they answer.
  • Measure bots and people. Analytics track how the AI and your team are performing.

Why Freshchat stands out: It covers most messaging channels a restaurant group uses in one inbox, with an AI agent on top. If you already run Freshdesk or Freshsales, Freshchat slots into the same Freshworks account.

It's best for: Restaurant groups that want omnichannel guest messaging with an AI agent inside a general support platform, especially those already using Freshworks.

Pricing: A free plan covers up to 10 agents. Growth is $19, Pro $49 and Enterprise $79 per agent per month, billed annually, according to third-party summaries of the pricing page. Freddy AI Agent is charged per session (reported at $49 per 100 sessions), and Copilot costs extra.
