2. Tidio

Tidio gives smaller businesses a chat widget for their website, with Lyro, its AI agent, drafting replies to guests from whatever content you feed it. It also includes no-code automations called Flows and an email help desk, so a small restaurant team can handle questions about hours, menus and bookings from one place.

Notable customers: Suitor, Gecko Hospitality and Pastreez.

What's new: Tidio said in October 2026 that Lyro placed first in G2's AI CX Agent Evaluations under the default ranking, scoring highest for sticking to policy. Keep in mind that Tidio is the one reporting this.

What you can do with Tidio:

  • Put chat on your site. A widget on your restaurant website takes guest messages, and your team can reply from the desktop or mobile app during a shift.
  • Let Lyro handle repeat questions. The AI agent reads your website and knowledge base to answer things like parking, gluten-free options or whether you take large parties.
  • Pass tricky chats to staff. When Lyro can't help, it hands the guest to a person so a complaint or special request still gets a human reply.
  • Automate greetings and capture leads. Flows, the visual builder, can welcome visitors, collect catering contact details and answer set FAQs without code.
  • Manage email in the same tool. Guest emails become tickets in a queue next to your chats, and Lyro can answer those as well.
  • See what the AI resolves. Analytics show conversation volume and how many chats Lyro closed on its own.

Why Tidio stands out: You can get a working AI chat agent on a restaurant website in an afternoon, and the free plan lets you test it before paying anything. Because live chat, automation and email sit together, a lean front-of-house team doesn't have to juggle separate tools.

It's best for: Independent restaurants and small groups that want a no-code website chat with an AI agent answering everyday guest questions.

Pricing: There's a free plan. Starter costs $24.17 a month billed annually ($29 month to month), Growth begins at $49.17 a month billed annually, and Plus is priced on request. Lyro is an add-on charged by the number of AI conversations.
