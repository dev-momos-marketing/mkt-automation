14. Zendesk

Zendesk, now marketed as the Zendesk Resolution Platform, puts AI agents, a ticket queue, messaging, voice and a guest-facing knowledge base in one product. Restaurant and food brands use it when guest contacts arrive in volume across many channels and need a structured process behind the AI that answers them.

Notable customers: Grubhub, Grill'd and Impossible Foods.

What's new: Zendesk announced a deal for the AI agent company Forethought on March 11, 2026 and closed it on March 26. The resulting Forethought AI Agents by Zendesk work over chat, email and voice and improve themselves over time.

What you can do with Zendesk:

  • Deploy AI agents on text and voice. Agents take common guest questions over chat, email and the phone, then escalate what they can't solve.
  • Bring channels together. Email, messaging, live chat, social and voice arrive in one workspace for your support staff.
  • Route complaints automatically. Triggers and omnichannel routing can send a cold-food complaint to the right location manager.
  • Support staff with Copilot. The add-on proposes replies and next steps during a conversation.
  • Let guests help themselves. A help center answers questions on allergens, catering or refunds without a ticket.
  • Report across locations. Dashboards show contact volume and service trends, and the app marketplace adds integrations.

Why Zendesk stands out: AI agents, ticketing and reporting live in one system, so an automated answer and a manager's follow-up share the same record. That auditable trail matters once guest support spans dozens of locations.

It's best for: Large restaurant groups and franchise operators that handle a high volume of guest contacts and want AI agents backed by a full helpdesk.

Pricing: Support Team is $19 per agent a month billed annually ($25 monthly), Suite Team $55 billed annually ($69 monthly) and Suite Professional $115 billed annually. Copilot is an add-on.
