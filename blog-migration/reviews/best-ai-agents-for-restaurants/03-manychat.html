3. ManyChat

ManyChat automates the messages a restaurant gets on social media, covering Instagram DMs and comments as well as Messenger, WhatsApp, TikTok and SMS. It replies to guests where they already scroll, hands out promo codes and grows a contact list you can market to. Because it focuses on social messaging, it works next to your booking and ordering tools rather than replacing them.

ManyChat's blog features restaurant examples, including a La Catrina campaign and an Astral Restaurant Systems client chain. ManyChat credits the second with 7,000 visits plus $52,000 of added revenue; read those as the vendor's own numbers.

Notable customers: Nike and The New York Times.

What's new: Since March 2026, ManyChat bills by active contacts, and its free tier has shrunk to 25 of them. A year earlier, in April 2025, Summit Partners led a $140M Series B that the company is spending on AI agents for social channels and global growth.

What you can do with ManyChat:

  • Reply to DMs and comments automatically. A guest who types a keyword under your post, or answers a story, gets the menu link or an answer in their inbox within seconds.
  • Answer free-text questions with AI. AI steps pick up what a guest is asking and collect details such as an email address for your list.
  • Send promotions to followers. Opted-in followers can receive a weekend discount code, a new seasonal dish or an event reminder in a broadcast or timed sequence.
  • Build flows visually. A drag-and-drop builder with templates lets a marketing manager set up automations without a developer.
  • Step in when needed. A live chat inbox lets staff take over a conversation the bot shouldn't handle alone.
  • Connect your other tools. Integrations with Google Sheets, HubSpot, Zapier and Make move captured contacts where you need them.

Why ManyChat stands out: Many guests find a restaurant on Instagram first, and ManyChat answers them right there in the app. Ads that open a DM and keyword-triggered replies turn a busy social account into a steady flow of contacts for later campaigns.

It's best for: Restaurants and small groups with an active Instagram or Facebook following that want to automate social messages and simple promotions.

Pricing: Plans scale with active contacts. Free covers 25. Pro, for 2,500 contacts, runs $39 on monthly billing or $29 per month on an annual plan; Business, for 7,500 contacts, runs $99 monthly or $69 per month annually. WhatsApp conversation fees from Meta come on top, and prices differ by region.
