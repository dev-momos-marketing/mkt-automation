8. Botpress

With Botpress, your team designs its own AI agents in a visual studio and drops into code wherever the logic gets specific. It isn't restaurant-specific, which gives you more freedom and more setup work than a ready-made restaurant tool.

The trade-off suits groups with developers or an agency: you decide exactly how the agent handles bookings, orders or feedback and which systems it talks to.

If that build effort is realistic for your team, Botpress lets the agent follow your own policies and menu logic instead of a vendor's defaults.

Notable customers: Windstream, Able and Ruby Labs.

What's new: Botpress introduced a new pricing structure for workspaces created after May 14, 2026; older workspaces keep the previous setup of a plan fee plus AI spend. Back in June 2025, Framework Venture Partners led its $25M Series B, which Deloitte Ventures and HubSpot Ventures also joined.

What you can do with Botpress:

  • Design custom guest agents. Build flows for reservations, FAQs, ordering or post-visit feedback around your own rules, as restaurant chatbot roundups such as Hostie describe.
  • Let agents take actions. An agent can reach out to your systems through APIs mid-conversation, for example to look up open tables, and finish the job itself.
  • Ground answers in your content. Knowledge bases built from your documents and website keep replies tied to your real menu and policies.
  • Publish once to many channels. The same agent can run on web chat, WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram and SMS.
  • Connect your systems. An integration hub and APIs link the agent to a POS, CRM or booking system.
  • Hand off to people. Botpress Desk adds a helpdesk with human handoff and can connect to Zendesk, Intercom or Freshdesk.

Why Botpress stands out: You own the logic, the integrations and the channels, so the agent can match how your restaurant group actually operates. Conversation logs and analytics help a technical team keep refining it after launch.

It's best for: Groups that employ developers, or work with an agency, and want an agent built around their exact POS, booking and CRM setup.

Pricing: There's a free tier. According to third-party sites, Plus is roughly $89 monthly and Team roughly $495 monthly, with AI usage billed on top; Enterprise is quoted. Since Botpress reworked pricing for new accounts in May 2026, check the official page before budgeting.
