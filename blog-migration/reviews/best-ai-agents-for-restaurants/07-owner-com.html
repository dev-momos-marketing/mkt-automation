7. Owner.com

Owner.com is aimed at independent restaurants trying to win back direct orders. You get an AI-generated site, a commission-free ordering page, an app under your restaurant's name, plus automated campaigns and a rewards program.

Lately it has added a point-of-sale system and an AI phone agent that takes real orders over the phone, so its AI now answers calls as well as sending campaigns.

Notable customers: Saffron Indian Restaurant, Goi Cuon, Doo-Dah Diner and Township Line Pizza.

What's new: Owner.com picked up $240M of fresh funding in August 2026, a Series D whose lead backer was Goldman Sachs Alternatives, and the round priced the business at $2.3B. The company said it would use the money to move past restaurant ordering and marketing toward software that runs local businesses with AI, and reports on the round listed the POS and phone ordering among its products.

What you can do with Owner.com:

  • Take phone orders with AI. The phone agent places orders directly, which helps when the line rings during a rush.
  • Launch a search-friendly site. Owner.com builds your restaurant website with AI and tunes it for search.
  • Sell direct. Online ordering, catering and a branded app let regulars order from you without a delivery marketplace in between.
  • Bring guests back automatically. Texts and emails are triggered by each guest's order history, for instance a nudge to someone who hasn't ordered in a month.
  • Reward repeat orders. A loyalty program runs across web and app ordering.
  • Ring up orders in-house. The restaurant POS keeps counter and online sales in one system.

Why Owner.com stands out: It gives a single-location owner the kind of direct ordering and marketing setup that big chains build in-house. Owner.com also handles guest support for orders, which takes some phone and email work off your staff.

It's best for: Independents and owners of a few locations who are tired of paying delivery-app commissions and want regulars ordering from them directly.

Pricing: Feature sets match across the two plans, and you can leave either one whenever you like. Flex is $249 a month with a 5% cut of every order going to Owner.com. Flat Rate is $499 a month and skips that per-order charge entirely. On top of either, guests are charged a 5% order support fee, and card payments cost 2.9% + $0.30 each.
