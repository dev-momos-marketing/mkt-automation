5. Slang AI

Slang AI is a voice agent that works as a phone host for restaurants, picking up every call so your hosts can focus on guests in the room. It answers questions, books and changes reservations, and sends private-event leads to the right person. Its newer Superhost product adds guest memory for full-service dining rooms.

Notable customers: Gusto54 Restaurant Group.

What's new: Slang AI raised $36M in a February 2026 Series B, with US Venture Partners leading, and said it worked with 2,000+ restaurant locations at the time. Then, in October 2026, came Superhost, which recalls earlier calls, preferences and visit history and speaks nearly 100 languages.

What you can do with Slang AI:

  • Cover the phone around the clock. Every inbound call gets answered 24/7 in a natural voice, including during the dinner rush.
  • Book through your reservation system. Integrations with OpenTable, SevenRooms and Yelp let the agent take and change bookings directly.
  • Route event inquiries. Private-dining calls flow into Tripleseat, and the agent hands off to staff when a caller needs a person.
  • Text links for takeout. Callers who want takeout get a text with your ordering link, so the order happens online.
  • Recognize regulars. Superhost remembers returning guests and their preferences, within policies the restaurant sets.
  • Review every call. Summaries and analytics show what guests ask about most.

Why Slang AI stands out: It was designed around the restaurant phone line from day one, so the reservation and event integrations are built in. Superhost's guest memory brings a little of a seasoned host's familiarity to calls your team would otherwise miss.

It's best for: Full-service and reservation-driven restaurants that lose bookings to unanswered calls during service. It sends ordering links by text and doesn't take orders by voice.

Pricing: Slang AI keeps its prices private, so expect a custom quote after a sales call.
