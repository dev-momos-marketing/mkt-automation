9. Hyperleap AI

Hyperleap AI runs one AI assistant across a restaurant's web chat and its WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger inboxes, all drawing on a single knowledge base.

For restaurants it offers an "AI front desk" setup that handles menu, opening-hours and dietary questions and hands out the reservation link, which suits places where guests would rather message on WhatsApp than call.

Its main published example is Jungle Lodges & Resorts, a state tourism group in Karnataka, India, that runs Hyperleap across web chat and WhatsApp. Hyperleap's own pages report the results inconsistently, so judge it on a trial.

Notable customers: Jungle Lodges & Resorts.

What's new: Hyperleap AI hasn't put out any dated product announcements in the last couple of years that we could find.

What you can do with Hyperleap AI:

  • Answer on every messaging app. One agent covers website chat, WhatsApp through the Business API, Instagram DMs and Messenger.
  • Reply from your own menus. Upload your menu PDFs, FAQ sheet and house policies, and the assistant bases its replies on them.
  • Share booking links. Front-desk flows handle hours and menu questions, then send guests your reservation link.
  • Send allergy questions to staff. The agent routes allergy questions to a person instead of guessing.
  • Capture event leads. Requests for large parties and private events are collected as leads your team can follow up.
  • Serve several locations. A shared knowledge base can answer for multiple sites, with replies in several languages.

Why Hyperleap stands out: It treats WhatsApp as a main channel and pairs it with a low entry price, which is rare among the tools here. The cautious handling of allergy questions is a sensible default for anything guest-facing in food service.

It's best for: Budget-conscious independents and small groups whose guests mostly message on WhatsApp and who want one assistant for web and social inboxes.

Pricing: Three plans: Plus at $40, Pro at $100 and Max at $200 per month. Every plan starts with a 7-day trial, and you'll need to enter a card to begin it. Hyperleap's pages don't agree on how many AI responses each plan includes, so confirm on its pricing page.
