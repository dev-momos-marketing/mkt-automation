4. Popmenu

Popmenu is a marketing platform made for restaurants that bundles your website, interactive menu, online ordering, guest email and SMS campaigns, and an AI agent that answers the phone. It also covers reservations, a waitlist and review replies, which makes it one of the more complete restaurant-specific options in this list.

Notable customers: La Parrilla, Steam Boys, The Hampton Social and B&B Butchers & Restaurant.

What's new: In April 2026, a Yext partnership began letting Popmenu users manage listings and reputation across 70+ sites, including Yelp and OpenTable, without leaving the dashboard. In October 2026, it announced a new menu product called Spotlight Menu.

What you can do with Popmenu:

  • Let AI pick up calls. The phone agent answers routine questions and helps callers with orders and reservations while your staff stay on the floor.
  • Take orders without commission. Online ordering and a branded app charge a flat fee per order instead of a share of the sale.
  • Run guest campaigns. Email, SMS and social messages can be automated or drafted with AI and sent to segments such as lapsed regulars.
  • Handle reviews and feedback. AI drafts replies to reviews, and guest feedback sits beside your other marketing data.
  • Manage seating. A digital waitlist and reservations keep walk-ins and bookings organized.
  • Connect your POS. Integrations include Square, Oracle and CAKE, with reporting across locations.

Why Popmenu stands out: Few platforms put an AI phone agent, online ordering and guest marketing behind a single login built only for restaurants. A caller, a web visitor and an email subscriber all feed the same guest records, so your marketing reflects how people actually order.

It's best for: Independents and growing groups with a handful of locations that would rather get their site, online orders, guest marketing and call handling from one restaurant-focused company.

Pricing: On month-to-month billing, Starter costs $179, Essentials $299 and Premier $499. Prepay a full year and those drop to $159, $269 and $449 respectively. Enterprise is quoted by sales. Each online order costs you $1.00 regardless of its size. Popmenu doesn't publicly say which plan includes AI phone answering, so ask during the demo.
