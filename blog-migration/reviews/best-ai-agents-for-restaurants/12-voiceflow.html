12. VoiceFlow

Out of Toronto, Voiceflow offers a workspace where teams design, test and operate their own chat and voice agents, combining drawn-out workflows with playbooks the AI interprets. Like Botpress, it's a toolkit you build on, with no restaurant templates or POS connectors out of the box, but its canvas is approachable for a digital or CX team without engineers.

Notable customers: Trilogy, Turo and Roam.

What's new: Voiceflow unveiled V4 in February 2026 and opened it to every user by March 2026. Agents now combine autonomous playbooks, step-by-step visual workflows and tools, routing between them, and you can assign different AI models to different parts of one agent.

What you can do with Voiceflow:

  • Map fixed processes visually. The canvas lays out steps, branches and conditions for flows like taking a reservation request.
  • Describe skills in plain language. Playbooks let the agent handle open-ended questions, such as menu recommendations, from instructions you write.
  • Build for chat and phone. The same platform produces website and messaging agents as well as voice agents for your phone line.
  • Answer from your documents. A knowledge base grounds replies in your menus, policies and web pages.
  • Pick models per task. A quick model can route requests while a reasoning model handles complicated ones.
  • Test and refine together. Shared workspaces, transcripts and analytics let several editors review how the agent performs.

Why Voiceflow stands out: V4 gives you both predictable scripted paths and flexible AI behavior in one agent, which matters when a booking flow must follow rules but guests still ask anything. It fits between plug-and-play chat widgets and code-first frameworks.

It's best for: Groups whose own digital or guest-experience staff want to design a chat or phone agent themselves and hook it up to existing systems.

Pricing: There's a free plan. Reviewers outside the company put Pro near $60 for each editor monthly and Business near $150 monthly. More seats and usage credits cost extra, and Enterprise requires a quote. Check current pricing with Voiceflow, since credit allowances changed during 2026.
