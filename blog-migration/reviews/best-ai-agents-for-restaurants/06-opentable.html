6. OpenTable

OpenTable, part of Booking Holdings, pairs a network where diners find and reserve tables with the reservation book and floor tools your hosts work from. Over the past year it has added AI on both sides of the booking: Concierge answers diners' questions on your restaurant profile, and new tools help restaurants appear in AI assistants and search.

If your restaurant is already listed, Concierge draws on your OpenTable profile to answer what a guest wants to know before reserving, such as whether you have a patio or how the tasting menu works.

Notable customers: Piano Piano and HMSHost.

What's new: Concierge first appeared in July 2025, and a year later, in July 2026, it grew into a broader assistant that lets diners search for restaurants in plain language. August 2026 brought OpenTable's largest batch of releases yet: profiles drafted by AI, Guest AI suggestions, hooks for voice AI partners, and bookable listings inside ChatGPT, Alexa+, Perplexity, Copilot and Google. The US and Canada got these first.

What you can do with OpenTable:

  • Field pre-booking questions. A guest wondering at midnight whether there's a kids' menu can ask Concierge on your listing and reserve right away.
  • Get found in AI assistants. A diner asking an AI assistant for a dinner spot can land on your availability and book it there.
  • Manage the dining room. Hosts see the floor plan, seat parties and keep track of covers and turn times as service runs.
  • Remember your guests. Each regular's file stores past visits and likes, which helps you personalize the next visit and act on guest feedback.
  • Automate guest messages. Confirmations, reminders and, on higher plans, email marketing go out without manual work.
  • Connect phone and POS tools. OpenTable works with third-party AI phone agents and with point-of-sale systems like Square.

Why OpenTable stands out: It pairs a large diner network with AI that works on your listing, so you don't need a separate chatbot for pre-booking questions. The August 2026 release also puts your availability inside the AI tools diners increasingly use to choose where to eat.

It's best for: Full-service and fine-dining restaurants that take reservations and want AI help with diner questions alongside table and guest management.

Pricing: The Basic, Core and Pro tiers each carry a subscription, and diners booked via OpenTable's own network cost an extra per-cover charge. OpenTable's site doesn't confirm today's amounts, so ask sales for a quote.
