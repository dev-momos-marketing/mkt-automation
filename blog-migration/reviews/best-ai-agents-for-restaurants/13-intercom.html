13. Intercom

Intercom combines Fin, an AI agent built to resolve questions by itself, with a helpdesk where your people work: shared inbox, ticketing, a help center and phone support.

The parent company rebranded as Fin in May 2026 (the helpdesk kept its Intercom label); Salesforce then finished buying it in September 2026. It isn't restaurant-specific, though large brands can use Fin to answer a big share of routine guest messages.

Notable customers: Anthropic, Lightspeed and Synthesia.

What's new: On May 12, 2026, Intercom announced its new company name, Fin. Salesforce signed a deal to buy Fin in June 2026 and closed it on September 10, 2026, with Fin now part of Salesforce AI Labs.

What you can do with Intercom:

  • Let Fin answer first. Drawing on your help articles, Fin handles guests over WhatsApp, SMS, social, email, phone and chat.
  • Answer calls with AI. Fin Voice handles phone questions, such as delivery issues or gift card balances.
  • Work the rest in one inbox. A shared inbox and tickets let your team pick up what Fin passes along.
  • Draft replies faster. Copilot suggests answers to agents while they handle a conversation.
  • Offer self-service. A searchable help center covers common questions about allergens, refunds or loyalty points.
  • Track AI and team results. Reports compare what Fin resolves with what your staff handle.

Why Intercom stands out: Fin is one of the more mature AI support agents available, and outcome-based billing means you pay for resolutions instead of attempts. For a chain fielding thousands of similar questions, that can take real pressure off a guest-support team.

It's best for: Multi-location restaurant brands that want an AI agent handling most routine guest questions across channels, backed by a helpdesk for the rest.

Pricing: Essential is $29 per seat a month billed annually, Advanced $85 and Expert $132. Fin carries its own charge of $0.99 for each outcome it delivers.
