15. Landbot

Landbot is a no-code chatbot builder for websites, WhatsApp and Messenger, with a drag-and-drop flow editor and an AI Agent block for open-ended questions. It works best for structured conversations like table bookings and feedback surveys, and it includes a ready-made WhatsApp restaurant reservation template to start from.

Notable customers: FASTA, Choices, CASFID and Convosight.

What's new: Landbot 4 arrived in December 2025 with an AI Agent block, an AI Copilot that helps you build bots, Hybrid AI components that mix buttons into AI chats, and a two-way n8n integration.

What you can do with Landbot:

  • Start from a reservation template. The WhatsApp table-booking template gives you a working flow to adapt to your hours and party sizes.
  • Build flows by dragging blocks. The visual editor lets a manager design FAQ, ordering or feedback bots without code.
  • Add AI for open questions. On paid plans, the AI Agent answers free-text questions from your own content.
  • Keep choices tidy. Hybrid AI puts buttons, selectors and autocomplete inside AI conversations so guests pick a date or time quickly.
  • Broadcast on WhatsApp. Campaigns announce specials or events to guests who opted in.
  • Sync guest data. Integrations with Google Sheets, Zapier, Make, n8n, webhooks and CRMs move bookings and survey answers automatically.

Why Landbot stands out: It gives you more control over conversation design than a basic widget while staying usable without developers. The rule-based flows keep bookings predictable, and the AI block covers the questions your script didn't anticipate.

It's best for: Independent restaurants and small groups, especially in WhatsApp-heavy markets, that want to build their own reservation, FAQ or feedback bots.

Pricing: The free Sandbox plan includes 100 chats a month, Starter is about €40 a month and Pro about €100 a month. WhatsApp plans are priced separately, Business starts around €400 a month, and AI chats are metered.
