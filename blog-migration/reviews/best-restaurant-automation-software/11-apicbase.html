11. Apicbase

Website: Apicbase

Category: Recipe, stock and food cost automation

Groups running several kitchens use Apicbase to run the back of house from one place: recipes and their costs, stock, supplier orders and production. Because every module draws on the same recipe records, a supplier raising the price of butter changes the cost of each dish containing it automatically. The company currently describes its software as AI-powered.

Notable customers: Poule & Poulette, Pentahotels and The Avocado Show.

What's new: There's been no dated product launch from Apicbase lately. Its most recent milestone was APX·25 in November 2025, a first-time gathering it organized for executives from hospitality, foodservice and tech.

Key features:

  • Shared recipe database. Ingredients and dishes are defined once for the whole group, with plate and menu costs worked out for you.
  • Allergens and menu engineering. Allergen information and menu analysis come straight from the recipe data.
  • Multi-site counts. Each restaurant records its counts, and head office sees what's on the shelves everywhere.
  • Commissary production. A central kitchen can schedule what to make for each outlet it sends food to.
  • Connected ordering. Supplier purchase orders, plus integrations with POS and ordering systems such as Square and Deliverect.

Pricing: Apicbase keeps prices private, and what you pay scales with how many kitchens you run and which parts of the platform you switch on.
