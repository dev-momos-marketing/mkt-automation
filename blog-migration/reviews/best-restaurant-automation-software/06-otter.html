6. Otter

Website: Otter

Category: Order consolidation and POS automation

Otter is built for restaurants that live on delivery: orders from Uber Eats, DoorDash, Grubhub and direct channels arrive in one queue on one device. It also manages menus across those platforms and sells its own POS, kiosk, online and QR ordering. Reporting pulls sales from every channel together.

Notable customers: Telly's Charburgers.

What's new: In May 2025, DoorDash named Otter one of the first partners in its Preferred Integrations Program, and Otter added notification controls for each channel. In January 2025 it shipped a DoorDash plugin for tracking Dashers and contacting customers from the orders screen.

Key features:

  • A single order queue. Delivery and direct orders show up together, so staff stop juggling a separate tablet for each app.
  • Menus pushed everywhere. You edit the menu once and Otter sends it to each delivery platform.
  • Pause from your phone. Stores can be paused and items marked unavailable on every channel, including from the Otter Go mobile app.
  • Uptime monitoring. Otter watches for stores that drop offline and for order issues, so lost sales get caught sooner.
  • Cross-channel reports. Sales, menu items and channels are compared across platforms in one view.

What makes them stand out: Otter covers both the delivery aggregation layer and the front-of-house hardware, so a delivery-heavy or multi-brand kitchen can run orders, menus and its POS from one vendor.

Pricing: Otter doesn't publish a price list; plans are quoted by its sales team.

Great fit for: Restaurants, virtual brands and multi-brand kitchens where third-party delivery makes up a large share of orders.
