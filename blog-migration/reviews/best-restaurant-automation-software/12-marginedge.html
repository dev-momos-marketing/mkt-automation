12. MarginEdge

Website: MarginEdge

Category: Automated invoice entry and daily food cost

MarginEdge takes the invoice pile off your desk: you snap a photo or forward an email, and the invoice is processed and matched to your POS sales. From that data it produces daily food cost, inventory and P&L numbers, so you see problems during the month instead of after it closes. The one subscription also covers paying vendors, placing orders and costing recipes.

Notable customers: Skopos Hospitality Group, Crooked Hammock Brewery, South Block and The Tavern at Ivy City Smokehouse.

What's new: An $80 million Series D package of equity and debt closed in August 2026, with Schooner Capital and Ten Coves Capital as co-leads, aimed at new AI tools that draw on the purchasing data passing through MarginEdge. Back in April 2025, the company reached its tenth anniversary with 10,000 restaurants signed up.

Key features:

  • Hands-off invoices. Invoices arrive by photo, email or EDI and are processed without anyone keying line items.
  • Food cost by the day. Matching what you sold against what you bought gives a running food and beverage percentage, refreshed daily.
  • Bill pay included. Vendors are paid from the same platform at no extra charge.
  • Price-change alerts. You're told when the cost of an item you buy goes up.
  • Ordering and costing. Order guides, inventory counts and recipe costing sit alongside budgets and an accounting sync with tools such as QuickBooks.

What makes them stand out: One flat subscription covers unlimited invoices and bill pay, and MarginEdge connects to dozens of POS systems.

Pricing: MarginEdge posts one flat monthly fee per location. Its own pricing page has listed $330; 2026 third-party trackers put it at $350, with Freepour bar inventory adding $150. Check marginedge.com before you budget.

It's a great fit for: Independents and growing restaurant groups tired of typing invoices into spreadsheets who want to watch food cost move in near real time.
