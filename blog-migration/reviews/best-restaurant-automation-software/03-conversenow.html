3. ConverseNow

Website: ConverseNow

Category: Voice AI that automates phone and drive-thru orders

ConverseNow puts virtual assistants on a restaurant's phone lines and drive-thru, where they hold a natural back-and-forth with the guest and place the finished order in the POS. Buying Valyant AI in July 2024 gave the company a stronger drive-thru product alongside its phone assistant. Pizza and wing brands, where calls still bring in a large share of orders, are its core market.

Notable customers: Anthony's Coal Fired Pizza & Wings and Wingstop (pilot at select locations).

What's new: A Deliverect partnership arrived in April 2026, so brands already managing orders and menus in Deliverect can add ConverseNow's voice ordering for calls and the lane on top.

Key features:

  • No ringing phone at peak. Calls go to the assistant, which handles the whole order while your cooks and cashiers stay at their stations.
  • Drive-thru coverage. The same voice technology handles orders at the speaker post, built out after the Valyant AI deal.
  • Straight into the POS. Orders land in your point of sale without anyone typing them in again.
  • Steady upselling. Add-on suggestions are offered on each order in the same way, whoever is working.
  • Human backup. Tricky calls can pass to a live agent through the call-center partner OrderSolutions.

What makes them stand out: Conversations run on large language models, so guests can speak the way they normally would, and the Deliverect link lets voice orders sit alongside your delivery channels.

Pricing: Prices aren't published; ConverseNow quotes each brand directly.

It's a great fit for: Multi-store pizza, wing and quick-service brands whose phones and speaker posts get busy enough that answering every order by hand costs real labor hours.
