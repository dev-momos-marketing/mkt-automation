5. Deliverect

Website: Deliverect

Category: Restaurant order management and delivery automation

Deliverect sits between delivery marketplaces, your own online ordering, the POS and the kitchen, so orders from every channel arrive in one place without re-keying. Menus, prices and item availability are managed once and pushed to each platform. In 2026 it added AI agents that work on menus and watch digital orders for mistakes.

Notable customers: Papa Johns, KFC (Iberia), TGI Fridays and Taco Bell.

What's new: In March 2026, Papa Johns chose Deliverect's Smart Dispatch & Delivery Management platform, planning to roll it out across its U.S. restaurants by the end of 2027. In April 2026, Deliverect launched Deliverect AI, starting in the UK, with agents that reorder menus from live order data and flag ordering errors.

Key features:

  • No more tablet farm. Orders from delivery apps and web ordering drop directly into your POS, so nobody retypes tickets at the counter.
  • Change a menu once. Prices, items and sold-out dishes update on every channel from a single menu manager.
  • Your own ordering. Branded online ordering, including ordering from social media, sits alongside the marketplaces.
  • Dispatch and delivery. In-house drivers and third-party couriers are managed from the same platform, with kitchen display and order routing.
  • Sales in one dashboard. Reports compare channels and locations side by side.

What makes them stand out: Deliverect works as the connecting layer across a large number of POS systems and ordering channels, including the Toast partner ecosystem, and its new AI agents automate menu tuning on top of that plumbing.

Pricing: A per-location monthly subscription, billed annually. Deliverect's pricing page estimates your price from monthly order volume and location count, and groups with more than 50 locations get a quote.

It's a great fit for: Multi-location restaurants, franchises and virtual brands handling several delivery and online ordering channels that want everything synced to the POS.
