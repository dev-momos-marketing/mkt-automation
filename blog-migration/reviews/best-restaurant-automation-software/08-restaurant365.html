8. Restaurant365

Website: Restaurant365

Category: Food & labor cost analytics

Restaurant365 takes the repetitive back-office jobs, from posting invoices to building schedules and running payroll, and handles them in one cloud system wired to your P&L. Invoices, POS sales and labor feed the books without manual entry. In 2026 it added R365 AI, which reads across that P&L data to point out cost and labor issues.

Notable customers: Villa Restaurant Group, Wholly Stromboli and Johnson's Landing.

What's new: R365 AI made its first appearance at May 2026's National Restaurant Association Show, bringing automated dashboards, an advisor you can question, new labor tools and an early-access AI scheduler. Kate Hastings took over customer success as Chief Customer Officer in June 2026.

Key features:

  • Automated accounting. Invoices get processed, bank accounts reconciled and separate P&Ls kept for each entity, all set up around restaurant bookkeeping.
  • Theoretical vs. actual. Inventory and recipe costing show where food cost drifts from what your recipes say it should be.
  • Forecast-driven schedules. Labor plans follow projected sales, with payroll and tips handled for hourly teams.
  • Sales posted daily. POS integrations bring each day's sales into the books on their own.
  • Group-level reporting. Head office sees every store's budget and results together, with franchise royalties tracked automatically.

Pricing: Restaurant365 sells its modules as per-store packages, invoiced quarterly or annually. Large groups negotiate a custom quote, and since the company's own pages list differing dollar amounts, rely on its pricing page for today's figures.
