4. Soundhound AI

Website: SoundHound AI

Category: Voice agents for phone, lane and kiosk ordering

Restaurants know the publicly traded SoundHound (Nasdaq: SOUN) for voice agents that pick up phone orders, staff the drive-thru speaker and talk to guests at kiosks. Buying SYNQ3 Restaurant Solutions (2024) and Interactions (2025) widened what it sells. Since 2026 those agents run on OASYS, its system for orchestrating agents across channels.

Notable customers: Five Guys, Chipotle, Red Lobster and Applebee's / IHOP, and Church's Texas Chicken.

What's new: Five Guys renewed and widened its deal in January 2026, after more than one million AI phone ordering interactions and opened the agent to its franchisees. In May 2026, SoundHound launched OASYS, a self-learning agent platform covering phones, drive-thrus, kiosks, web chat and vehicles.

Key features:

  • Phone ordering agent. Beyond taking the order, the agent can tell a caller when you close or whether a dish contains nuts.
  • Drive-thru voice. Guests order at the lane by voice, including at sites using Acrelec drive-thru hardware.
  • Kiosks and cars. Guests can also order by voice at a self-service kiosk or from a connected vehicle's dashboard.
  • POS connection. Each order goes into your point of sale and on to the kitchen, with upsell and promotion prompts along the way.
  • Voice Insights. SoundHound analyzes what happens in lane conversations and reports patterns you can compare store by store.

What makes them stand out: Few vendors stretch across as many ordering points, and with OASYS a chain configures its agent once instead of setting up the phone, lane and kiosk separately.

Pricing: SoundHound doesn't publish prices; deals are quoted per brand.

It's a great fit for: Large quick-service and casual-dining brands planning to take order-taking off staff at scale, with a vendor able to support a rollout across hundreds of stores.
