10. MarketMan

Website: MarketMan

Category: Inventory, purchasing and food cost automation

MarketMan is cloud software for the purchasing side of a restaurant: inventory counts, recipe costing, supplier ordering and invoice processing. Automating those steps means ingredient prices flow into recipe costs as soon as an invoice arrives. Square now sells a version of it to its restaurant sellers.

Notable customers: Westville Restaurant Group, ATFC, Ltd. and Taco del Mar (multi-unit franchisee).

What's new: Since April 2026, Square sellers worldwide can get MarketMan inside Square through Square Restaurant Inventory by MarketMan, which covers recipe costing, supplier orders, invoice scanning and waste logs.

Key features:

  • Live stock levels. Inventory is tracked and counted by location, so you know what's on hand before you order.
  • Recipe costs that move. Menu and recipe costs update as ingredient prices change on incoming invoices.
  • Automatic purchase orders. Orders to suppliers are generated from par levels and sent without phone calls or emails.
  • Invoice scanning. Scanned invoices update prices and trigger alerts when a supplier raises one.
  • Waste and COGS reports. Loss trends and food cost roll up across locations.

What makes them stand out: MarketMan concentrates on purchasing and food cost, and its Square partnership gives Square restaurants a built-in route to the same tools.

Pricing: MarketMan sells per-location plans; ask its team for a current quote.

It's a great fit for: Independent restaurants and multi-unit groups that want tighter control of inventory, purchasing and food cost, including Square sellers.
