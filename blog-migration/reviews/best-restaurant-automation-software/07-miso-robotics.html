7. Miso Robotics

Website: Miso Robotics

Category: AI-powered robotic kitchen automation

Miso Robotics makes Flippy, a robotic arm that works the fry station, and Zippy, an AI operations dashboard it built after buying the software company Zignyl in 2026. Flippy's installed base is still small (Fortune counted 14 units in the field in February 2026), so treat it as an option to pilot. Zippy reaches further, since it came with Zignyl's existing restaurant customers.

Notable customers: White Castle, Jersey Mike's (Zignyl/Zippy customer) and Jamba (Zignyl/Zippy customer).

What's new: In February 2026, Miso acquired Zignyl and folded its operations and staff incentives app into Zippy, which also reports Flippy ROI, live video and maintenance data. In April 2026, Flippy shipped to its first NBA arena, Miso's first stadium deployment.

Key features:

  • Robotic frying. Flippy loads baskets, fries, shakes and dumps them, taking a hot, repetitive station off your crew.
  • Vision-guided cooking. Cameras identify each food and track cook times across fries, onion rings, chicken and other fried items.
  • Fits your current line. The station is designed to slot into existing fry lines with limited construction work.
  • Ask Zippy. Managers type operational questions in plain language and get answers from store data.
  • Staff incentives. Performance tracking and incentive programs from Zignyl sit in the same dashboard.

What makes them stand out: Miso is one of the few vendors here automating physical kitchen work, and it backs Flippy with remote monitoring and maintenance support.

Pricing: No published price. Flippy comes on a robotics-as-a-service subscription, quoted on request.

It's a great fit for: High-volume QSR and venue kitchens, including stadiums, that are open to piloting robotic frying, and operators who want an AI operations dashboard.
