9. Jolt

Website: Jolt

Jolt moves the clipboard work of a shift into an app: opening and closing lists, temperature checks, prep labels, plus schedules and training. In August 2025 Digi International acquired the company for roughly $145.5 million and folded it into SmartSense, Digi's monitoring division. The Jolt name and website remain, and the product is now bundled into SmartSense ONE as well.

Notable customers: A&W Restaurants and White Spot Hospitality.

What's new: At the NRF show in January 2026, SmartSense by Digi unveiled SmartSense ONE, which packages Jolt's task and labeling tools together with SmartSense monitoring as seven modules you can pick from.

Key features:

  • Checklists with proof. You can require a photo plus a manager's sign-off on any task, so a skipped closing step shows up the next morning.
  • Automated temperature logs. Cooler and hot-holding readings are recorded digitally, and SmartSense sensors can take them without anyone walking over with a probe.
  • Prep and date labels. Staff print rotation labels from the app, so use-by dates follow the same rules in every store.
  • Scheduling and time clock. Managers build shifts by drag and drop, and staff clock in through Jolt.
  • Training and messaging. New hires find training content and store information in one hub, and managers reach the team by text.

What makes them stand out: Jolt focuses on shift-level accountability, with reports on task completion across locations, and the Digi ownership pairs it with sensor-based temperature monitoring.

Pricing: Jolt quotes prices individually after you contact sales.

It's a great fit for: Franchise and quick-service operators who want shift tasks and food safety checks automated, particularly groups also looking at wireless temperature monitoring.
