8. Stamp Me

Stamp Me is a digital stamp card app that moves the paper punch card onto the customer's phone. It runs as an independent system with no POS integration, so you can launch a card without adding software at the till.

For a cafe or small restaurant, the appeal is speed: Stamp Me says a simple card takes about 5 minutes to set up, and guests join by scanning a QR code or downloading the app. A Stamp Me blog post from August 2026 explains that members can also save their Member Code to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet.

Key features

  • Digital stamp cards: Customers join by QR code or the Stamp Me app, and each account can publish up to 2 card programs, such as one for coffee and one for sandwiches.
  • Stamping methods: Stamp Codes, StampTags and single-use OneStamps work on every plan, and Pro adds the StampPod tap device at the counter.
  • Stamp Business app: Staff scan a member's code with a phone to add stamps or redeem rewards; the app is in beta.
  • Member messaging: Pro and Elite include unlimited push notifications and SMS, which carries extra fees.
  • Campaigns and games: Birthday Club and Lapsed Customer messages go out automatically, and Scratch & Win games give members another reason to open the app.
  • Zapier integrations: Member data can flow to your email or CRM platform.

Pros

  • Quick to start: The basic card needs no POS integration or new software.
  • Flexible terms: Plans are monthly, with no lock-in contract and no setup costs.
  • Free trial: You get 30 days free, starting when you publish your first card, with no credit card.
  • No member cap: Stamp Me doesn't charge per customer, however many guests join.

Cons

  • No POS link: Stamp Me doesn't integrate with POS systems, so stamps aren't tied to transactions.
  • Lite is basic: Messaging, campaigns and the StampPod start on Pro at $79 per month.
  • One location per plan: Each additional location adds a monthly fee.
  • SMS costs extra: Text messages carry fees on top of the plan price.

Price and plans

Every plan includes a 30-day free trial, with no setup costs or lock-in contract, and covers one location. Prices are in US dollars per month.

  • Lite: $49 per month.
  • Pro: $79 per month.
  • Elite: $199 per month.
