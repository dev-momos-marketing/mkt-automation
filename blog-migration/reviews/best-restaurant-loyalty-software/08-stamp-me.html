8. Stamp Me

Stamp Me is a digital stamp card app that replaces the paper punch card with one on the customer's phone. It runs on its own, so you can launch it without connecting a POS or buying special hardware.

For a cafe or small restaurant, the appeal is how quickly guests can join: they scan a QR code at the counter or download the app, then collect a stamp each visit. Rewards are redeemed from the phone, so your staff never have to hunt for lost cards.

Notable customers: Merriment Cafe, Batch Espresso, Pine Scone Cafe and Le Roi Cafe.

What's new: In August 2026, Stamp Me explained how members can save their Member Code to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet. Staff scan that code with Stamp Business, a new merchant app currently in beta, to add stamps or redeem rewards.

What you can do with Stamp Me:

  • Launch a card fast. Set up your stamp card, reward and join QR code without installing any software at the till.
  • Pick a stamping method. Use the StampPod tap device, QR codes, stamp codes or Stamp Tags, depending on how busy your counter gets.
  • Scan from a phone. With the Stamp Business app, staff use the device camera to read a guest's code and issue stamps.
  • Message your regulars. Send push notifications and SMS campaigns, and set up automatic birthday rewards and Scratch & Win games.
  • Sync member data. Pass loyalty sign-ups to your email or CRM tools through Zapier.

Why Stamp Me stands out: It keeps loyalty as simple as a paper card while giving you a member list you can message. You can test it in one location with a free trial before committing.

It's best for: Independent cafes, coffee shops and takeaways that want a low-effort digital punch card.

Pricing: Three plans (Lite, Pro and Elite), each with a 30-day free trial, no setup fees and no limit on members. Check Stamp Me's site for current prices.
