1. Toast Loyalty

Toast Loyalty is the points program that comes with the Toast restaurant POS. It sits inside Toast's Marketing Essentials package next to gift cards and email and SMS marketing, so loyalty data lives in the same system as your orders and payments.

Because every check already runs through Toast, points land on the guest's account automatically, and a guest who links a card earns without giving a phone number at all. That keeps the program almost invisible for servers during a rush, and redemptions work the same way at the counter, online or in the Toast Local app.

Notable customers: Captain Parker's Pub, Craft Lounge and Kaldi's Coffee.

What's new: In May 2026, Toast relaunched its Toast Local guest app with loyalty built in, so diners can check their points balance, find ways to earn and redeem rewards while they book or order. The same month, Toast introduced Toast IQ Grow, which pairs an AI marketing agent and a dedicated marketing manager with Marketing Essentials.

What you can do with Toast Loyalty:

  • Enroll guests their way. Guests can join with a phone number, an email address or a linked payment card, and linked cards collect points with no extra step at checkout.
  • Pick an earning model. Reward guests per dollar spent or per visit, depending on whether your average check or your visit frequency matters more.
  • Add bonus rewards. Set up welcome offers, birthday treats and double-point promotions from the same dashboard you use for menus and reports.
  • Keep points consistent across channels. Earning and redemption work on the POS, Toast online ordering, kiosks, Toast Go handhelds and the Toast Local app.
  • Reach members directly. Send email and SMS campaigns to loyalty members through Toast Marketing to bring quiet regulars back.

Why Toast Loyalty stands out: Loyalty, payments and guest marketing share one guest record, so you never export lists or reconcile points between two systems. The choice between spend-based and visit-based earning also lets a fine dining room and a quick-service cafe on the same platform run very different programs.

It's best for: Restaurants already on Toast POS that want a low-maintenance loyalty program without adding another vendor.

Pricing: Toast doesn't list a standalone price for Loyalty. It comes in the Marketing Essentials package on top of your Toast POS plan, and Toast sales quotes the cost.
