1. Toast Loyalty

Toast Loyalty is the points program built into the Toast restaurant POS. It comes in Toast's Marketing Essentials package next to gift cards and email and SMS marketing, so loyalty data lives in the same system as your orders and payments.

Because every check already runs through Toast, points post to the guest's account on their own, and a guest who links a payment card earns without giving a phone number. In May 2026, Toast relaunched its Toast Local guest app with loyalty built in and introduced Toast IQ Grow, a premium package that adds an AI Marketing Agent to Marketing Essentials.

Key features

  • Flexible sign-up: Guests join with a phone number, an email address or a linked payment card, at the register, at the table, online or in the app.
  • Two earning models: Choose a visit-based program or an amount-based one that awards points per dollar spent.
  • Rewards and bonuses: Set up to five cash-discount or item rewards, plus welcome offers, birthday rewards and double-point promotions.
  • Earning wherever you sell: Points work on the POS, Toast online ordering, kiosks, Toast Go handhelds and the Toast Local app.
  • Member marketing: Email and SMS campaigns through Toast Marketing reach loyalty members directly.
  • Loyalty analytics: Loyalty and guest reports sit in the same Toast dashboard you use for sales.

Pros

  • One guest record: Loyalty, payments and guest marketing share data, so there are no lists to export or points to reconcile between systems.
  • Hands-free earning: Linked cards collect points with no extra step at checkout, which keeps the program light for servers during a rush.
  • Shared across locations: Restaurants in the same Gift/Rewards Card Group can run one program and one guest account.
  • No points ceiling: There's no cap on points per transaction or on a guest's balance.

Cons

  • Toast POS required: It only works for restaurants that run on Toast.
  • Delivery apps excluded: Orders through DoorDash, Grubhub and Uber Eats don't earn or redeem points.
  • One program at a time: Toast supports a single rewards program, with up to five rewards.
  • No published price: You get the cost from Toast instead of a public price list.

Price and plans

Toast doesn't publish a price for Loyalty. You can buy it in the Marketing Essentials bundle from Toast Shop or ask Toast Care to add the standalone Loyalty subscription, and Toast quotes the cost.
