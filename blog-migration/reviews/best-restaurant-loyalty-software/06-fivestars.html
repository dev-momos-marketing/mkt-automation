6. FiveStars

Fivestars by SumUp is a loyalty and automated texting platform for local businesses in the US. The payments company SumUp bought Fivestars in 2021 and now sells it as SumUp Connect, while members still track their points in the Fivestars by SumUp app.

Guests join by typing a phone number at checkout, so a cafe or counter-service restaurant can offer sign-up on every order without asking anyone to download an app first. Gong cha and Hwy 55 Burgers, Shakes & Fries are among the brands that have run Fivestars programs.

Key features

  • Phone-number check-in: Customers enter their number on the customer-facing device to earn points, then redeem rewards at the register.
  • AutoPilot texts: Welcome, birthday and win-back messages go out on their own.
  • Promotions: Send offers to your list for a slow afternoon or a new menu item; Connect Pro adds unlimited AI-enhanced messaging.
  • Review Boost: On Connect Pro, happy customers are invited to leave a review and get a reward for it.
  • Customer directory: Visits and spending are recorded with every transaction, so you can see who your regulars are.
  • POS choice: Connect runs with SumUp POS and with many third-party POS systems.

Pros

  • Quick sign-up: No card or app is needed to join, which keeps the line moving.
  • Little daily effort: AutoPilot campaigns run without anyone writing each message.
  • Simple terms: SumUp's pricing page promises no complicated contracts.
  • Lower price with SumUp POS: Bundling the POS brings Connect Plus down to $199 a month.

Cons

  • High starting price: The current pricing page starts at $269 a month for Connect Plus on its own.
  • Key tools on Pro: Text-to-join, Review Boost and unlimited AI-enhanced messaging need Connect Pro; Plus includes 2 standard promos a month.
  • No trial listed: The pricing page shows no free plan or trial.
  • Made for local shops: It's built for independent cafes and quick-service restaurants, so a multi-brand group with its own app may need a different platform.

Price and plans

SumUp publishes monthly prices for Connect and says there are no complicated contracts. Buying SumUp POS with Connect lowers the price.

  • Connect Plus: $269 per month.
  • Connect Pro: $349 per month.
  • POS with Connect Plus: $199 per month.
  • POS with Connect Pro: $289 per month.
