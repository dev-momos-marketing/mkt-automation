6. FiveStars

FiveStars by SumUp is a loyalty and automated texting platform for local businesses, owned by the payments company SumUp since 2021. In the US, SumUp now sells it as SumUp Connect, while members still track their points in the Fivestars by SumUp app.

Guests join by typing a phone number on a small screen at the counter, so there's no card to carry and no app to download first. For a cafe or a counter-service spot, that makes sign-up quick enough to offer on every order, and automated texts then do the work of bringing people back.

Notable customers: Gong cha, Hwy 55 Burgers, Shakes & Fries and My Pie in Reno.

What's new: In April 2026, SumUp expanded its US lineup with the SumUp Terminal and new reporting tools in the SumUp app, with loyalty offered alongside SumUp POS. That same month, it launched a card-linked SumUp Loyalty program in the UK and five other European countries.

What you can do with Fivestars:

  • Check guests in at the counter. Customers enter their phone number on the customer-facing device, earn points on each visit and redeem rewards at the register.
  • Automate return visits. AutoPilot sends welcome texts, birthday offers and win-back messages to guests who haven't been in for a while.
  • Send promotions. Push offers to your whole list for a slow afternoon or a new menu item; Connect Plus includes two standard promos a month.
  • Know your regulars. The customer directory keeps each member's visits and spending in one place, so you can see who your best guests are.
  • Keep your current POS. SumUp Connect works with SumUp POS and connects with many third-party POS systems.

Why Fivestars stands out: Phone-number check-in and preset AutoPilot campaigns make it practical for owners who run the floor themselves and have no time to build campaigns. It also gives small shops a polished SMS loyalty program with the backing of a large payments provider.

It's best for: Independent cafes, bubble tea shops and quick-service restaurants that want simple phone-number loyalty and automatic texts.

Pricing: SumUp lists Connect Plus at $269 per month and Connect Pro at $349 per month on its US pricing page.
