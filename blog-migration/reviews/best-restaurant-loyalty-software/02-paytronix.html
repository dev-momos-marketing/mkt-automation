2. Paytronix

Paytronix is a guest engagement suite for restaurants and convenience stores that combines loyalty, online ordering, gift cards, branded apps and messaging. It has been part of The Access Group since an acquisition announced in November 2024 and still trades under its own name.

Paytronix is strongest when your program needs to do more than hand out points. Loyalty, ordering and stored value share one guest profile, so you can reward app orders and in-store visits alike and use that history to target offers. Peet's Coffee and Bruster's Real Ice Cream run their rewards programs on it.

Key features

  • Program types: Points, visits, tiers and subscriptions, with comps and limited-time promotions on top of the core earning rules.
  • Rewards Hub: Released in April 2026, it lets marketers set up rewards, change earning ratios and launch promotions without filing a support ticket.
  • Journey Builder: An AI-powered, no-code tool launched in October 2025 that sends email, SMS and push messages across multi-step guest journeys.
  • Branded app and ordering: Members get their own app for ordering, rewards and account details, as Las Palapas did with its LP Club app.
  • Gift cards with loyalty: Native stored value lets guests pay with a gift card and earn or redeem rewards in the same order.

Pros

  • One guest database: Loyalty, ordering, gift cards and campaigns draw on the same profiles, so you maintain fewer integrations.
  • Marketer control: Rewards Hub hands day-to-day program changes to your own team.
  • Automated journeys: Journey Builder is available to every Paytronix client in Campaign Center.
  • Wide integration list: Paytronix lists more than 450 integrations, including 30+ native POS integrations such as Qu and Revel.

Cons

  • Custom quotes: Prices aren't published and depend on brand size, modules and location count.
  • Enterprise-tier fit: Paytronix's own guide says the smallest single-location operators may find it more extensive than needed.
  • Rewards Hub limits: At launch it supports only the Bankable Points program type, with tiers, clubs and point multipliers due later in 2026.

Price and plans

Paytronix doesn't publish prices. Quotes are based on brand size, the modules you choose and your number of locations.
