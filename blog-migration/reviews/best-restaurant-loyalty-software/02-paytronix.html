2. Paytronix

Paytronix is a guest engagement suite for restaurants and convenience stores that bundles loyalty, online ordering, gift cards, branded apps and messaging. Since late 2024 it has been part of The Access Group, and it still operates under the Paytronix name.

Paytronix is strongest when your program needs to do more than hand out points. Because loyalty, ordering and stored value share one guest profile, you can reward app orders and in-store visits the same way, then use that history to decide who gets which offer and when.

Notable customers: Peet's Coffee, Las Palapas and Bruster's Real Ice Cream.

What's new: In October 2025, Paytronix released Journey Builder, an AI-assisted tool for building multi-step guest campaigns without code. In April 2026, it added Rewards Hub, where your marketing team can change rewards, earning ratios and promotions without filing a support ticket.

What you can do with Paytronix:

  • Design the program you want. Choose points, visits or tiers, and add comps and limited-time promotions on top of the core earning rules.
  • Adjust rewards yourself. Rewards Hub lets you set up a new reward or raise the earn rate for a slow week from your own dashboard.
  • Automate guest journeys. Journey Builder sends email, SMS and push messages that react to what each member orders and how often they come in.
  • Launch a branded app. Give members their own app with ordering, rewards and account details, as Las Palapas did for its LP Club.
  • Tie gift cards to loyalty. Native stored value means guests can use a gift card and earn or redeem rewards in the same online or in-store order.

Why Paytronix stands out: Few loyalty vendors cover ordering, gift cards, apps and campaign automation for restaurants under one roof, which means fewer integrations for you to maintain. The 2026 updates also hand more day-to-day control of the program to your own marketers.

It's best for: Multi-unit restaurant chains, franchise brands and convenience store operators that want one guest database behind loyalty, ordering and marketing.

Pricing: Paytronix doesn't publish prices; you get a custom quote based on your locations, modules and integrations.
