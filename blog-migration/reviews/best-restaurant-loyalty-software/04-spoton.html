4. SpotOn

SpotOn makes restaurant POS software, and SpotOn Loyalty is the rewards program built into it. Because loyalty shares a system with the register, online ordering and the GoTo Place guest app, every order can count toward a reward and points apply on their own at checkout.

Its appeal is how little staff and guests have to do. Setup happens in the POS, and since July 2026 the deals and campaigns can also go out by text to guests who opted in through loyalty, reservations or online ordering. Bascom's Chop House and Big Dave's Bagels & Deli are among the restaurants SpotOn features.

Key features

  • Flexible earning: Set points per visit or per dollar spent, and the POS credits them when the check closes.
  • Sign-up anywhere: Guests join at the POS, through online ordering, on your website, in the GoTo Place app or with a shareable link.
  • Automated deals: Rewards go out for sign-ups, birthdays, first check-ins and lapsed guests without manual list work.
  • Email and SMS campaigns: Every loyalty member becomes a contact for targeted promotions from the dashboard.
  • Loyalty reporting: See spend from loyalty diners, your top customers and which rewards drive results.

Pros

  • Little work at the counter: Guests check in at the POS, a guest-facing display, handhelds or the app, and redemption doesn't slow service.
  • Win-back included: Deals for lapsed guests run automatically once you set them up.
  • Clear price: Loyalty costs a flat $65 a month on top of SpotOn POS.
  • Well rated POS: SpotOn's POS averages 4.4 out of 5 from G2 reviewers.

Cons

  • Tied to SpotOn POS: Loyalty is sold as an add-on to SpotOn POS, so a restaurant on another POS would need to switch systems.
  • No free trial: SpotOn's pricing page offers a demo and no trial.
  • Contract on the All-In plan: The $0 All-In POS plan comes with a 2-year minimum term and processing minimums.

Price and plans

SpotOn Loyalty is an add-on to SpotOn POS. There's no free trial; you start with a demo.

  • SpotOn Loyalty: $65 per month, added to SpotOn POS.
  • All-In POS: $0 per station, per month, hardware included, with a 2-year minimum term and processing minimums.
  • POS Essentials: $55 per station, per month, month-to-month, with hardware bought separately.
  • Build Your Own: Custom pricing for multi-unit and enterprise operations.
