4. SpotOn

SpotOn makes restaurant POS software, and SpotOn Loyalty is the rewards program that lives inside it. Because it shares a system with the register, online ordering and the GoTo Place guest app, every order can count toward a reward without extra hardware or a separate login.

Its main strength for loyalty is how little the guest or the server has to do. Guests join and earn at the POS, online or in the app, points apply on their own at checkout, and birthday deals go out on a schedule you set once.

Notable customers: Smashin' Good Time, Gui Steakhouse and Big Dave's Bagels & Deli.

What's new: In July 2026, SpotOn added text messaging to its marketing tools, so loyalty deals and campaigns can now reach guests by SMS once they opt in through loyalty, reservations or online ordering.

What you can do with SpotOn Loyalty:

  • Choose how guests earn. Set points per visit or per dollar spent, and let the POS credit them automatically when the check closes.
  • Enroll guests anywhere they order. Sign-ups and check-ins work at the counter, through SpotOn Order and in the GoTo Place app.
  • Automate birthday offers. Trigger-based deals send a reward during a guest's birthday month with no manual list work.
  • Send email and text campaigns. Build promotions in the SpotOn Dashboard and deliver them to the guests who opted in.
  • See loyalty next to sales. Guest activity and redemptions sit in the same reports as your POS data.

Why SpotOn stands out: Rewards, ordering, reservations and payments all share one guest record, so your loyalty data stays complete across channels. For an independent restaurant, that means one vendor to call and one dashboard for staff to learn.

It's best for: Independent restaurants and small groups on SpotOn POS that want a rewards program running inside the system they already use.

Pricing: SpotOn lists Loyalty at $65 per month as an add-on to SpotOn POS, with the POS itself priced separately.
