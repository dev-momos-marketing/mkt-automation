5. Thanx

Thanx is a guest engagement platform made only for restaurant brands. It combines loyalty, a guest CRM, automated marketing and first-party web and app ordering, and it adds AI agents that build segments and win back guests after a bad digital order.

Its loyalty approach is designed to protect margin: guests earn through the card they pay with, without scanning anything, and rewards can go well beyond blanket discounts. Velvet Taco, Pokeworks, Sonny's BBQ and Salt & Straw run their programs on Thanx.

Key features

  • Card-linked loyalty: Guests are recognized by their payment card in store and online, so earning doesn't slow the line at the counter.
  • Access Pass rewards: Give your top tiers experiences, secret menu items or early access to new launches.
  • SegmentAI: Describe an audience in plain words, such as lapsed lunch guests at three locations, and it builds the segment for you.
  • Lifecycle automation: Automated campaigns welcome new members and bring back guests who haven't visited lately.
  • RecoveryAI: Follows up with guests after a poor digital ordering experience to bring them back.
  • Owned ordering: A branded app and Thanx Web tie each online order to a guest profile, and integrations with Toast, Deliverect and Kea phone ordering connect other channels.

Pros

  • Restaurant-only focus: Every part of the platform, from tiers to ordering, is built around how restaurant guests visit and order.
  • Margin-friendly rewards: Perks that aren't discounts let you thank regulars without training them to wait for a deal.
  • Self-serve changes: You can adjust rewards, tiers and offers yourself when food costs or results shift.
  • Pay-for-results recovery: RecoveryAI is priced on performance, so you pay when a recovered guest comes back.

Cons

  • No public pricing: Quotes depend on your restaurant count, unit volume and the modules you pick.
  • USA and Canada only: Thanx works only with companies based in those two countries.
  • Loyalty and ordering scope: Reviews and listings aren't part of the platform, so you'd pair it with a reputation tool.
  • Built for multi-unit brands: A single-location restaurant may need far less than the full platform.

Price and plans

Thanx doesn't publish prices; you get a quote after a demo. Pricing depends on your restaurant count, average unit volume and the capabilities you need, such as loyalty, CRM, ordering and marketing automation.
