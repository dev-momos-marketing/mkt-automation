5. Thanx

Thanx is a guest engagement platform built only for restaurant brands. It combines loyalty, a guest CRM, automated marketing and first-party web and app ordering, and it now adds AI agents that handle segmenting and guest recovery for your team.

Thanx is strongest when you want rewards that protect margin. Guests can be recognized by the card they pay with, so they earn without scanning anything, and you can reward your best regulars with experiences, secret menu items or early access in place of blanket discounts.

Notable customers: Velvet Taco, Taco Bueno, Salt & Straw and The Big Greek Cafe.

What's new: In March 2026, Thanx and Deliverect announced a partnership that pairs Thanx loyalty and ordering with Deliverect's order management. In July 2026, Thanx launched RecoveryAI, an agent that reaches out to guests after a poor digital order, and brands pay only when those guests come back.

What you can do with Thanx:

  • Card-linked earning. Guests collect rewards in store and online through the payment card they already use, which keeps the line moving at the counter.
  • Non-discount perks. Access Pass rewards let you offer tastings, hidden menu items or early product drops to top tiers.
  • Plain-language segments. SegmentAI builds an audience when you describe it in words, such as lapsed lunch guests at three locations.
  • Automated winback. Lifecycle campaigns bring back guests who haven't visited in a while, and RecoveryAI follows up after a delayed or wrong digital order.
  • Owned ordering. A branded app and Thanx Web capture guest profiles on online orders, so each order feeds your loyalty program and CRM.
  • Phone order rewards. The Kea integration lets guests who order by phone join the program and collect points.

Why Thanx stands out: You can change rewards, tiers and offers yourself whenever food costs or results shift, without waiting on a vendor ticket. Loyalty, ordering and marketing share one guest record, so each campaign reaches the right regulars.

It's best for: Emerging and mid-sized multi-unit restaurant brands that want a flexible loyalty program tied to their own ordering channels.

Pricing: Custom pricing. Thanx doesn't publish prices, and quotes depend on your number of locations, sales volume and the modules you choose.
