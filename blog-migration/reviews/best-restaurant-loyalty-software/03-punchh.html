3. Punchh

PAR Punchh is an enterprise loyalty, offers and guest engagement platform for restaurant chains, owned by PAR Technology. It's now sold as part of PAR Engagement, which joins Punchh loyalty with marketing, online ordering and guest data in one cloud.

Punchh fits brands whose loyalty program has to work the same way in every store and channel. Krystal, Torchy's Tacos, Church's Texas Chicken and Salsarita's Fresh Mexican Grill run programs on it, and PAR says more than 275 brands use the platform.

Key features

  • Program types: Choose points, visit-based rewards or membership tiers, and set earning rules to fit your menu and margins.
  • Offers engine: Build guest segments from order history and let AI-driven rules trigger an offer when a guest qualifies.
  • Smart Passes: Unveiled in December 2025, they put a loyalty card in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet, with one-tap enrollment and points that update in real time.
  • PAR Games: Gamified loyalty moments run natively on Punchh data.
  • Integrations: Loyalty connects to your POS, web and app ordering and kiosks, so rewards apply wherever the guest orders.

Pros

  • Made for chains: Segments, offers and reporting work across dozens or hundreds of locations from one program.
  • No app required: Wallet passes reach guests who never download your app.
  • One vendor: Through PAR Engagement, loyalty, marketing, ordering and guest data come from the same company.
  • Recent launches: Torchy's Tacos and Krystal both launched loyalty programs on Punchh in 2025.

Cons

  • Quote-only pricing: PAR doesn't publish prices for Punchh, so you'll need a demo and a quote to compare it.
  • Enterprise depth: It's built for multi-unit and enterprise brands, so a single-location restaurant may not need this much.

Price and plans

PAR doesn't publish Punchh prices; you get a custom quote after a conversation with its team.
