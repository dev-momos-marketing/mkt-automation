3. Punchh

PAR Punchh is the loyalty and offers engine inside PAR Technology's guest engagement suite, built for restaurant chains with many locations. It connects points, offers and guest profiles with the POS, online ordering and mobile app a brand already runs.

Punchh is strongest when your loyalty program has to work the same way in every store and every channel. You can segment guests by what they actually order, automate offers around those segments and see the results by location, which matters once you run dozens or hundreds of units.

Notable customers: Krystal, Torchy's Tacos, Church's Texas Chicken and Salsarita's Fresh Mexican Grill.

What's new: In June 2025, PAR folded Punchh into PAR Engagement, a single cloud for loyalty, marketing, ordering and guest data that added membership tiers and AI-driven rule activation. In December 2025, it launched Smart Passes, which put a guest's loyalty card in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet with no app download.

What you can do with Punchh:

  • Design the program you want. Choose points, visit-based rewards or membership tiers, and set the earning rules to match your menu and margins.
  • Target offers by behavior. Build guest segments from order history and let AI-driven rules trigger the right offer when a guest qualifies.
  • Reach guests without an app. Smart Passes let guests enroll with one tap and watch points and perks update in their phone's wallet.
  • Add games to loyalty. PAR Games adds playful, personalized moments tied to everyday guest actions, running natively on Punchh data.
  • Plug into your stack. Connect loyalty to your POS, web and app ordering and kiosks so rewards apply wherever the guest orders.

Why Punchh stands out: It gives large brands one loyalty system that ties guest identity, offers and ordering together under the same vendor. Wallet passes and games give you extra ways to reach guests who never install your app.

It's best for: Regional and national restaurant chains that want an enterprise loyalty program connected to their POS and ordering channels.

Pricing: Custom pricing; PAR doesn't publish rates for Punchh.
