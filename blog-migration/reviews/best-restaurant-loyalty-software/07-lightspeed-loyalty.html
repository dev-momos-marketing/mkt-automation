7. Lightspeed Loyalty

Lightspeed Loyalty is the loyalty and marketing layer of Lightspeed Restaurant, the point-of-sale system used by restaurants, cafes and bars in many countries. Points, rewards and guest profiles live in the same system that takes orders and payments.

The main advantage for restaurant loyalty is that your team doesn't have to think about it at the counter. Members earn on each sale and redeem rewards at checkout, and automated campaigns use that same purchase data to bring guests back.

Notable customers: Lightspeed Restaurant is used by Miriam in New York, DOC Group in Melbourne and Gemelli Group in Queensland.

What's new: In January 2026, Lightspeed launched Lightspeed AI, a conversational assistant inside Lightspeed Restaurant, alongside Tempo for pacing service and Tasks for opening and closing checklists.

What you can do with Lightspeed Loyalty:

  • Reward every purchase. Guests collect points on each order and spend them at the POS or online, so staff never track anything by hand.
  • Celebrate birthdays. Guests add their birthday in the loyalty app or on the web, and a reward goes out automatically each year.
  • Win back quiet regulars. A Time Away campaign offers bonus points to members who haven't ordered in 30 days.
  • Segment your guests. Group members by how much they spend and how often they visit, then target each group with its own offer.
  • Send email and SMS. Build campaigns with a drag-and-drop editor, or set up automated flows for first purchases, birthdays and lapsed guests.

Why Lightspeed Loyalty stands out: Since it shares data with the POS, you deal with one vendor and one support team for both orders and rewards. Setup is quick for existing Lightspeed users, because guest and sales data are already there.

It's best for: Independent restaurants, cafes, bars and small groups already running Lightspeed Restaurant that want a straightforward loyalty program from their POS provider.

Pricing: Lightspeed's US restaurant loyalty pricing page lists the Essential plan at $189/month and Premium at $399/month. Prices differ by country, so check the page for your region.
