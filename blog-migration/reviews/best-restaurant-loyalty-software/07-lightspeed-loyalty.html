7. Lightspeed Loyalty

Lightspeed Loyalty is the loyalty add-on for Lightspeed Restaurant, the point-of-sale system used by restaurants, cafes and bars in many countries. Points, rewards and guest profiles live in the system that takes orders and payments.

There are now two versions. The points-based Lightspeed Loyalty for Lightspeed Restaurant L-Series adds email, SMS and push campaigns, and a card-linked version released in Australia in February 2026 tracks spend from repeat card payments with no app or loyalty card. Lightspeed Restaurant customers include Miriam in New York, DOC Group in Melbourne and Gemelli Group in Queensland.

Key features

  • Points on every order: Guests collect points on each purchase and spend them at the POS or online, so staff never track anything by hand.
  • Birthday and win-back campaigns: A birthday reward goes out each year, and a Time Away campaign offers bonus points to members who haven't ordered in 30 days.
  • Segments and VIP tiers: Group members by spend and visit frequency, and give VIP groups a higher earn rate.
  • Email, SMS and push: Build campaigns with a drag-and-drop editor or set automated flows for first purchases, birthdays and lapsed guests.
  • Card-linked loyalty (Australia): Spend is tallied from a tokenized card ID, staff get POS prompts to enroll guests and apply rewards, and guests can join by QR code or keep a pass in Apple or Google Wallet.

Pros

  • No extra system at the counter: Rewards are earned and redeemed inside the normal sale.
  • One vendor: POS and loyalty share one support team, and the UK page promises free onboarding and 24/7 support.
  • Works across locations: The card-linked version lets regulars earn and redeem at any of a company's sites.

Cons

  • Lightspeed POS required: Loyalty works only with Lightspeed Restaurant, and the UK page lists the points-based version for L-Series only.
  • Newer version limited to Australia: Card-linked loyalty is available only to Australian sites using Lightspeed Payments.
  • Basic by design: The card-linked version handles earning and redeeming without campaigns, segments or marketing tools.
  • No US price: Lightspeed's US loyalty pages redirect to its main POS pages, so ask sales how loyalty is sold in your market.

Price and plans

Lightspeed sells Loyalty as a monthly add-on to a Lightspeed Restaurant subscription, and prices vary by country. The US site doesn't list a loyalty price.

  • Australia: From $39 per month (AUD) for the card-linked Lightspeed Loyalty.
  • United Kingdom: From £49 per month, according to Lightspeed's UK loyalty pricing page.
