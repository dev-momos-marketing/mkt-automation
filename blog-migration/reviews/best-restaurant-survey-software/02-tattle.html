2. Tattle

Tattle is a guest experience management platform made for restaurants, and its survey engine is the center of it: a short questionnaire reaches each guest after an order, sent through the ordering, loyalty or POS system they just used. Answers land under the parts of a visit guests notice most, and Google and Yelp reviews show up in the same place. The company is still independent, and most of its clients are chains with many units in quick service, fast casual or casual dining.

Notable customers: Hooters, Red Robin, Brinker International (Chili's) and Kung Fu Tea.

What's new: In August 2025, Tattle closed a $5 million round to grow its AI tools for restaurant operators. In January 2026, Kung Fu Tea announced it would use Tattle to measure guest sentiment on all of its channels. A manager app for iOS and Android has also arrived, though Tattle hasn't said exactly when it went live.

What you can do with Tattle:

  • Survey after every order. Hooks into Square, Toast, Olo, Thanx, Paytronix and Punchh mean the questionnaire goes out when a ticket closes, with no comment cards for staff to hand out.
  • Read results by service area. You track guest experience across food quality, order accuracy, speed and hospitality, so a low score points at a specific part of the shift.
  • Get one priority per store. AI ranks, for every location, the single improvement most likely to lift its guest scores.
  • Compare surveys with reviews. Yelp and Google reviews sit beside private survey answers, which helps you see whether both tell the same story.
  • Recover and benchmark. Recovery workflows reach unhappy guests, and dashboards trend top box score and recovery rate location by location.

Why Tattle stands out: Because each survey is triggered by the transaction itself, your response volume grows with order count and doesn't rely on staff remembering to ask. Tying every answer to an operational area tells each GM where to start.

Who it's best for: Franchisees and chain operators running many stores, particularly quick service, fast casual and casual dining, who want each store's survey scores connected to how that store actually runs.

Pricing: Tattle doesn't publish prices, so you'll request a quote for your brand.
