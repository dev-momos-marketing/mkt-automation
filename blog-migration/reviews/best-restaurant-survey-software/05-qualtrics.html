5. Qualtrics

Qualtrics calls its category experience management (XM), and its platform spans research on customers, employees and brands. In a restaurant program, that usually means post-visit surveys rolled up into location CX dashboards, plus a module for online reputation and a set of AI Experience Agents. The company has been privately held since Silver Lake bought it in 2023.

Notable customers: KFC and Shake Shack.

What's new: Jason Maynard's first X4 as CEO took place in Seattle in March 2026, and the company reported that its AI Experience Agents had moved into use by paying clients. It also unveiled agents for real-time service recovery and connectors into Genesys, NICE and Salesforce. In May 2026, Qualtrics completed its acquisition of Press Ganey Forsta, priced at $6.75 billion; that deal is mostly about healthcare patient feedback, and it also put the InMoment business under Qualtrics ownership.

What you can do with Qualtrics:

  • Ask guests after they pay. Each transaction can trigger a survey delivered by text or email, through your app or website, in a chat window, or from a QR code.
  • Fold reviews into the data. Google ratings and those from other review sites appear in the same reports as your survey results.
  • Tailor views by job. Location dashboards come in versions for a restaurant manager, a market manager and the executive team.
  • Cluster what people write. AI reads sentiment in survey comments and reviews and groups similar remarks into themes.
  • Hand follow-up to agents. Experience Agents respond to incoming feedback, such as kicking off service recovery the moment a complaint arrives.
  • See what drives scores. Predictive analytics estimates which factors move satisfaction and which guests may stop coming back.

Why Qualtrics stands out: One team can run guest surveys, review monitoring, BrandXM brand research and employee experience programs in the same system, which helps if you want to see how staff sentiment and guest scores move together.

Who it's best for: Big restaurant and hospitality companies with many units and a CX or insights department that has the people to design and run a full survey program.

Pricing: Qualtrics keeps CX suite prices private, so expect a sales conversation and a custom quote.
