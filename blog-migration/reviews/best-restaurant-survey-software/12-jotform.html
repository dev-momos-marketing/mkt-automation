12. Jotform

Category: No-code form builder and survey automation platform

Jotform is a no-code builder for forms and surveys that also takes payments and e-signatures and offers tables and apps, and through 2025 and 2026 it added AI agents and conversational form creation. For a restaurant, one account can cover guest feedback surveys, catering and event inquiries, job applications and opening or closing checklists.

Notable customers: Burger King New Zealand, ATFC, Ltd. (UK hospitality group, 20+ restaurants), CoverManager and Cups and Cakes Bakery.

What's new: Jotform released AI Agents in February 2025, no-code assistants that answer questions and walk people through forms over web chat, phone and messaging apps. In March 2026, Jotform AI arrived to build, style and edit forms from typed or spoken instructions, with a ChatGPT app following a week later. June 2026 saw the announcement of AI App Builder, which generates full apps from a natural-language description.

Key features:

  • Restaurant survey templates. The drag-and-drop editor offers thousands of templates, restaurant feedback surveys among them.
  • QR codes for the table. Print a code on menus, receipts or table tents and guests answer from their own phones.
  • Questions that adapt. Conditional logic reveals or hides fields depending on earlier answers.
  • Reports and tables. Report Builder charts your responses and Jotform Tables lays them out like a spreadsheet.
  • Payments in the form. Collect catering deposits or orders through Square, PayPal, Stripe and other processors.
  • Integrations. Responses can flow to Zapier, Slack, Mailchimp, HubSpot, Salesforce and Google Sheets.

Watch out for: At its core Jotform collects data. It doesn't include NPS benchmarking, side-by-side location comparisons or sentiment analysis, so if you want operational insight from guest feedback, plan on pairing it with a separate analytics tool.

Pricing: The free Starter plan includes 5 forms and 100 submissions a month. With annual billing, Bronze costs $34/month, Silver $39/month and Gold $99/month; monthly billing is $39, $49 and $129. Enterprise is quoted, and nonprofits and schools receive 50% off.
