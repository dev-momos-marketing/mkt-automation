9. InMoment

InMoment sells enterprise CX software where guest surveys, online reviews and social mentions are gathered together, then read by AI that handles both written text and recorded conversations. Press Ganey Forsta (PG Forsta) bought the company in May 2025 for undisclosed terms, and the product is still sold as "InMoment, a PG Forsta company." Restaurant groups use it to see post-visit surveys, reviews and contact-center feedback side by side in reporting broken out by location.

Notable customers: Jack in the Box, The Melting Pot and The Coffee Club.

What's new: AI Auto Responding was the July 2025 addition: which writes a reply to each review based on its sentiment and your brand guidelines, then either posts it or holds it for someone to approve. There hasn't been a dated product announcement from InMoment in 2026.

Key features:

  • Visit-triggered surveys. After a guest interaction, a survey can go out by email, text, web or app.
  • Review handling in one dashboard. You can track and answer Google and other review sites together, letting AI publish replies directly or queue them for sign-off.
  • Analytics across every source. AI looks for themes, sentiment and intent in surveys, reviews and social posts.
  • Contact-center insight. Conversation analytics covers calls and chats from guests who reached out directly.
  • Location rankings. Dashboards line up guest scores by restaurant, region and brand.
  • Drivers for each store. Frontline reports tell every location which positives and negatives weigh most on its scores.

Pricing: InMoment doesn't publish prices; each contract is priced individually.
