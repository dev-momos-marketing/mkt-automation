3. OvationUp

Ovation (the company behind ovationup.com, occasionally referred to as Ovation Up) builds its restaurant feedback product around one idea: a text survey with only two questions. Guests get it right after ordering, a poor answer alerts a manager in real time, and the platform now also covers reviews, listings and AI analysis of guest comments.

Notable customers: Dave's Hot Chicken, Curry Up Now, Bareburger and Taziki's Mediterranean Cafe.

What's new: Ovation's fall release in October 2025 brought in reputation management, gathering Google, Yelp and Facebook reviews plus listings on 50+ directories in one view. Then, in February 2026, Ovation took in $3 million from roughly a dozen of its own restaurant clients, taking total funding to $12 million. The spring release in May 2026 added Brand Voice tone settings and a Menu Item Performance Report that matches guest sentiment with order data.

What you can do with Ovation:

  • Text two questions after each order. POS and ordering integrations send the survey automatically, or guests open it from a QR code on the table.
  • Catch a bad visit early. Negative answers fire a real-time alert so a manager can contact the guest while there's still time to make it right.
  • Answer with AI drafts. The AI proposes responses to both guest comments and online reviews in the tone you set under Brand Voice, and a person can change any draft before it goes out.
  • Sort comments by restaurant topic. AI files written feedback under themes such as cleanliness, speed and food quality.
  • Watch nearby rivals. Competitive Pulse benchmarks each location against up to five competitors.
  • See which dishes draw complaints. Reports link what guests say to individual menu items and order data.

Why Ovation stands out: Two questions by text take a guest only seconds on their phone, which keeps friction far lower than a long email form and tends to bring in more responses. With more answers per shift, you get a truer read on what's happening in each restaurant.

Who it's best for: Restaurant groups with several units or more, above all ones where online orders make up a big share of sales, since small fulfillment slips there can pile up unnoticed. You can also answer public reviews without leaving the dashboard.

Pricing: Ovation prices by quote. Your count of dining rooms plus ghost kitchens sets the figure, with a discount as that count climbs.
