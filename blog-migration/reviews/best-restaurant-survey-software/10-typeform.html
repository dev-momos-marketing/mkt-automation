10. Typeform

Typeform is a general-purpose form and survey builder, recognizable by its one-question-per-screen design that makes a guest survey feel more like a conversation. Restaurants tend to use it for post-visit feedback, catering inquiries and event forms, and it now has AI that drafts forms and summarizes what respondents say.

Notable customers: Barry's (fitness studios), Calm, Designity and Double Denim Marketing.

What's new: Typeform's Winter 2025 release in February brought an AI form builder along with sentiment analysis and insight summaries for responses. At Typeforum in October 2025, it introduced an AI engagement platform: Typeform AI (beta) assembles forms and follow-up workflows from a chat request, and in-form AI can answer a respondent's question or ask them to clarify an answer.

Key features:

  • Single-question screens. Only a single question fills the screen at once, so a guest on a phone never faces a wall of fields.
  • Answer-based branching. Conditional logic picks the next question from what the guest said earlier.
  • Hospitality templates. You can start from restaurant and hotel feedback templates in the library.
  • AI drafting and summaries. Type a prompt to get a draft survey, then let AI summarize open-text answers and their tone.
  • Flexible sharing. Email the survey, share its link, embed it in a web page, or turn it into a QR code for table tents and receipts.
  • Common integrations. Typeform connects to Mailchimp, Google Sheets, Slack, Salesforce, HubSpot and Zapier.

Watch out for: There's no POS integration, closed-loop alerting or location benchmarking, so multi-unit operators who need operational reporting may outgrow it. Typeform's logo stays on forms below the Plus plan, and each tier caps monthly responses, so costs rise as your guest count grows.

Pricing: The free plan allows 10 responses a month. Paid tiers billed yearly run $28/month (Basic, 100 responses), $56/month (Plus, 1,000 responses) and $91/month (Business, 10,000 responses), or $39, $79 and $129 if you pay month to month. Enterprise is quoted.
