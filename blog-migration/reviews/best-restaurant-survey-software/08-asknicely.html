8. AskNicely

Category: NPS-focused customer experience and frontline feedback platform

AskNicely sends a short NPS or CSAT survey after each visit and passes the result to the employee who served that customer, so feedback becomes a coaching tool for the team on the floor. It serves service businesses with many locations across several industries, and its named customers come from home services and fleet software, so restaurants are one use case among many.

Notable customers: Cinch Home Services, Century Fire Protection and IntelliShift.

What's new: AskNicely shipped three updates in a row in 2026. Ask NiceAI arrived in March as a chat interface for quizzing your feedback data, Reputation Manager followed in April to put survey answers, online reviews and listings in one system, and in June two AI agents joined: one that spots trends and mails summaries to Slack or email, and one that writes draft replies to reviews.

Key features:

  • A survey as the visit ends. Email or text surveys with an NPS or CSAT question go out right after the service is delivered.
  • Feedback in each server's pocket. A mobile app shows every staff member the comments and mentions about their own shifts.
  • Coaching built in. Personal scorecards, team leaderboards and recognition tools give managers material for pre-shift huddles.
  • Questions in plain English. Ask NiceAI answers questions about your feedback and pulls out themes and sentiment from comments.
  • Reviews alongside surveys. Reputation Manager sends review requests, monitors review sites and keeps listings current next to your private results.
  • Links to your other tools. Connectors include Zendesk, Slack, Salesforce and HubSpot.

Pricing: Three plans, Learn, Grow and Transform, are billed annually and scale with response volume, and NiceAI costs extra. Ask AskNicely for a quote or check its pricing page for the current starting price.
