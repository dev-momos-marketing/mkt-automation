6. Zonka Feedback

Category: Omnichannel survey and feedback management

Zonka Feedback collects NPS, CSAT and CES answers and runs AI analysis over them, with surveys you can send by email, SMS, WhatsApp or web, or put on a kiosk or QR code. Its appeal for restaurants is breadth of channel: a tablet near the host stand, a code on each table and a text after a delivery order can all report into one account.

Notable customers: Jones the Grocer.

What's new: Zonka introduced AI Feedback Intelligence during 2025, which reads themes and sentiment across surveys, chats, support tickets and reviews. A redesign then split the product into hubs, among them an Intelligence Hub and a Reputation Hub for Google reviews. It hasn't put a date on any 2026 release so far.

Key features:

  • Kiosks that work offline. Tablets in the dining room keep collecting answers without Wi-Fi, next to QR, WhatsApp, text, email, web and in-app surveys.
  • Scores by location. NPS, CSAT and CES dashboards can be cut by restaurant so you see which site is slipping.
  • Theme detection. AI reads open comments and tags each one with a topic and a sentiment.
  • Closing the loop. Instant alerts and case tracking help a manager reach a guest who left a poor rating.
  • Order-linked responses. Pre-filled customer data and survey logic attach answers to a specific order or visit, so guests aren't asked for details you already have.
  • Google reviews and integrations. The Reputation Hub manages Google reviews, and connectors cover Zendesk, HubSpot, Salesforce and Zapier.

Pricing: Zonka lists no prices; according to its blog, plans are annual and quoted.
