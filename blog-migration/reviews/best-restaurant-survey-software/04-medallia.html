4. Medallia

Category: Enterprise guest experience and survey management

Medallia runs feedback programs for very large companies, pulling in surveys, reviews, call-center conversations and digital signals from customers and employees, and it sells a packaged offering for restaurants and food service. Since June 2026 it has belonged to a group of its lenders led by Blackstone, Apollo and FS KKR, which took over from Thoma Bravo in a debt-for-equity recapitalization, and development of the product continues. For a chain with hundreds of units and its own CX staff, it can carry a survey program end to end; a single restaurant will find it far more than it needs.

Notable customers: Noodles & Company and Best Western.

What's new: Medallia used its Experience '26 conference in February 2026 to show AI topic modeling and action planning placed inside front-line workflows. The June 2026 ownership change brought $150 million in fresh capital, and the company says its $500 million AI investment pledge still holds. Its H2 2026 release, out in September, widened multilingual AI analysis, opened up self-service survey distribution and gave an early look at agentic automation.

Key features:

  • A survey on every touchpoint. Guests can respond from a receipt, a QR code, a text, an email, your website or your app, and all of it lands in one program.
  • Unprompted feedback as well. Social posts and online reviews are listened to next to the surveys you send.
  • Urgent comments flagged. Text analytics reads open answers for themes and sentiment and raises anything that needs a same-day response.
  • Calls analyzed too. Phone and chat contacts with your guest services team are transcribed and analyzed with speech and conversation analytics.
  • Views for each level. Dashboards are scoped by role, so a GM sees one store while a regional or brand lead compares many.
  • Follow-up that gets done. Alerts and cases route unhappy guests to the right team, and Frontline-Ready AI writes summaries and next steps for managers.

Pricing: Medallia sets enterprise prices per contract and keeps them off its website.
