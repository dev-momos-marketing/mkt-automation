11. SurveyMonkey

Category: General-purpose survey platform and market research tool

SurveyMonkey is a broad online survey platform with AI survey building and analysis, a template library that includes restaurant feedback surveys, distribution across many channels and a paid respondent panel. After a consortium led by Symphony Technology Group took its parent Momentive private in 2023, the company went back to the SurveyMonkey name. For a restaurant, it's an inexpensive, well-known way to survey guests, test menu ideas or check in with staff, though POS integration and location benchmarking aren't included out of the box.

Notable customers: Hornblower (dining and sightseeing cruises) and Holiday Extras.

What's new: SurveyMonkey opened a free tools hub in February 2026, where anyone can generate an AI-built survey with no account. In August 2026, a SurveyMonkey plugin for ChatGPT followed its earlier Claude connector, so surveys can be created and analyzed from inside those assistants. September 2026 brought a relaunch branded "Today's SurveyMonkey," with a conversational AI builder, automated analysis and HRIS connections to Workday and BambooHR.

Key features:

  • Prompt-based building. Describe the survey you need and SurveyMonkey AI drafts it, then adjust it by chatting; for now that's available to English-language accounts in its US data center.
  • Ready-made restaurant surveys. Templates for restaurant feedback, NPS and CSAT give you a quick starting draft.
  • Logic on paid plans. Skip logic, branching, piping and A/B tests adapt each survey to the person answering.
  • Wide distribution. Share by QR code, text message, email, web link, site embed or social post.
  • Live analysis. Dashboards with filters and cross-tabs update as answers come in, and AI helps summarize open-ended responses.
  • Research panel. The paid SurveyMonkey Audience panel finds targeted respondents for concept or menu testing before a launch.

Pricing: A free Basic plan comes with limited responses, individual paid plans bill monthly or annually, and Team plans bill per user each year with a three-seat minimum; Enterprise is quoted. Prices change by region and billing cycle, so check SurveyMonkey's pricing page.
