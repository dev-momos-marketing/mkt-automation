7. Survey Sparrow

SurveySparrow is a survey and experience management tool whose signature is the chat-style survey, where questions appear one by one like messages on a phone. Around that it has built NPS and CSAT tracking, AI analysis of comments, reputation management and tickets for closing the loop, priced for teams without an enterprise CX budget.

Notable customers: Pechanga Resort Casino, Paragon Casino Resort and OneTable.

What's new: SurveySparrow's most recent dated launch is SparrowDesk, released in October 2025 as a stand-alone AI-first helpdesk that closes routine support tickets on its own and gives human agents an AI copilot. The survey product hasn't had a dated announcement since then.

Key features:

  • Surveys that read like a chat. The conversational layout suits guests filling it in on a phone at the table, and a standard form layout is available.
  • Many ways to send. Share by QR code, text, WhatsApp, email, link, web or in-app widget, or collect at the counter with offline kiosk apps.
  • Scheduled score tracking. NPS, CSAT and CES surveys can repeat automatically on whatever cadence you choose.
  • CogniVue text analysis. The built-in AI groups open answers by theme and by sentiment.
  • Reviews and tickets. Review management sits next to survey results, and tickets assign follow-up on unhappy responses to a staff member.

Pricing: Published trackers list annual plans at Basic $19/month, Starter $39/month, Business $79/month and Professional $249/month, plus a free plan and a 14-day trial; Enterprise and the CX suites are quoted. Some 2026 sources show other tiers, so confirm on SurveySparrow's pricing page before budgeting.
