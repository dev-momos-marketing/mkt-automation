4. Superorder

Superorder, called Forward Kitchens until 2023, sells AI agents to restaurant chains for review management, guest surveys, delivery profitability, scorecards and alerts. Its reputation tools answer reviews on Google, Yelp and the delivery apps with replies built from each guest's order.

Chains such as L&L Hawaiian Barbecue, with 230+ locations, use it to keep up with guest engagement and feedback. If you're weighing whether replies are worth the effort, see why answering reviews can lift restaurant sales.

Key features

  • Order-aware replies: The Review Management Agent reads order details such as items, delivery time and errors, then writes a personalized, on-brand response.
  • Replies by segment: Responses can change with the star rating and with the guest's loyalty, average order value or order frequency.
  • Delivery app coverage: Ratings left on delivery platforms are answered alongside Google and Yelp, which matters for brands with heavy off-premise sales.
  • Private feedback first: The Surveys & Feedback Agent asks guests about their order, giving you a chance to fix a problem before it goes public.
  • Location scorecards: AI scorecards rank stores against each other, and real-time alerts flag guest issues as they happen.

Pros

  • Reputation and margins together: The same vendor handles review replies and third-party delivery profitability, which suits delivery-heavy chains.
  • Recovery built in: A poor rating can trigger a tailored offer that invites the guest back.
  • Room for large teams: The Enterprise plan comes with unlimited users and 1:1 onboarding for your whole team.

Cons

  • No public pricing: Costs are quoted after a demo.
  • No trial period: Superorder offers a 30-day money-back guarantee instead of a free trial.
  • Chain focus: The delivery agents and scorecards are built for multi-unit brands, so a single dine-in location would leave much of the platform idle.

Price and plans

Superorder doesn't publish prices; you get a quote for its Enterprise plan after a demo. Pricing is store-based and billed monthly, and a 30-day money-back guarantee takes the place of a free trial.
