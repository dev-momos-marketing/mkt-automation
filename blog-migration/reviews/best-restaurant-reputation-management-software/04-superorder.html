4. Superorder

Superorder, known as Forward Kitchens until 2023, sells AI agents to restaurant chains. Its agents cover review management, surveys and guest feedback, delivery profitability, location scorecards and alerts, after the company moved away from its earlier delivery and takeout software.

Notable customers: L&L Hawaiian Barbecue, Tasty Restaurant Group, Marco's Pizza and Wow Bao.

What's new: In July 2025, L&L Hawaiian Barbecue chose Superorder to collect more guest feedback and stay in touch with diners at its 230+ restaurants. So far this year, Superorder has published no dated news about new features.

What you can do with Superorder:

  • Gather reviews in one view. The Review Management Agent brings in reviews from Google, Yelp and the delivery platforms so your team works from a single list.
  • Send replies that mention the order. Each AI-written response references the items on the guest's ticket, so it sounds specific to their meal.
  • Catch complaints privately. The Surveys & Feedback Agent asks guests for feedback after they order, giving you a chance to fix things before a public review.
  • Win back unhappy guests. Superorder can send a recovery offer automatically when a guest reports a poor experience.
  • Compare your locations. AI scorecards rank stores against each other, and real-time alerts flag guest issues as they happen.

Why Superorder stands out: It's one of the few review tools that also includes agents for third-party delivery profitability, so delivery-heavy brands can handle ratings and margins with one vendor.

It's best for: Multi-unit restaurant brands with a lot of delivery and takeout orders that want personalized AI replies on Google, Yelp and delivery apps plus feedback flows that recover unhappy guests.

Pricing: Superorder keeps its prices private, so you'll need to ask the company for a quote.
