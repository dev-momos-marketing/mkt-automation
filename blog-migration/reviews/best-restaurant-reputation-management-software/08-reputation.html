8. Reputation

Reputation, the platform long known as Reputation.com, manages reviews, listings, surveys and social media for brands with many locations. Its newer AI tools report how assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity describe your brand and each restaurant.

Restaurant groups use it to run reputation from head office; Mitchells & Butlers and Tortilla are among its customers. A May 2026 release added Reputation IQ, which answers questions about your data in everyday English, and AI Location Presence Insights.

Key features

  • Review management: New Google, Yelp, Facebook and Tripadvisor reviews for all locations appear together, with AI help to respond.
  • Listings and local SEO: Every plan includes business listings management, which keeps hours and details consistent across sites.
  • AI Location Presence Insights: Shows how each location appears in AI-powered search answers, next to the brand-level AI Reputation Manager.
  • Rep Score: One KPI combines every reputation signal, so locations can be compared on a single measure.
  • Social listening and publishing: The Social Suite add-on covers monitoring, posting and replies across social channels.

Pros

  • Wide coverage: Reviews, listings, surveys, social and AI search sit in one enterprise system.
  • AI search tools: AI Reputation Manager and AI Location Presence Insights track what chatbots tell people about your restaurants.
  • Transparent entry price: Rep Core starts at $80 per location per month, with higher tiers listed too.
  • User rating: 4.5 out of 5 on G2, based on 2,470 reviews.

Cons

  • Adds up at scale: Per-location prices of $80 to $150 a month multiply with every restaurant you add.
  • Social and ticketing extra: Social Suite, actions and ticketing and competitive insights are add-ons with prices on request.
  • Demo before access: There's no free trial on the pricing page.

Price and plans

Reputation lists per-location prices for three plans; Enterprise has custom pricing.

  • Rep Core: $80 per location, per month.
  • Rep Core + Pulse: $115 per location, per month.
  • Rep Core + Surveys: $150 per location, per month.
  • Enterprise: Custom pricing for 125+ locations.
