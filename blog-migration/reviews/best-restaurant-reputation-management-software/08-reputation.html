8. Reputation

Reputation.com, now branded simply as Reputation, gives multi-location brands one enterprise system for reviews, listings, surveys, social media and visibility in AI search. The company kept its reputation.com web address and celebrated two decades in business in March 2026.

Notable customers: Mitchells & Butlers and Tortilla.

What's new: Its May 2026 release added Reputation IQ, where you ask questions about your data in everyday English, plus AI Location Presence Insights and a larger AI Reputation Manager for monitoring what ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity say about you. In September 2025 it released a set of AI products for brand intelligence.

What you can do with Reputation:

  • Work through reviews in a shared queue. Your team sees new Google, Yelp, Facebook and Tripadvisor reviews for all locations together and responds without switching sites.
  • Request reviews at the right moment. A dine-in check, delivery order or POS event can trigger an invitation by text, email or QR code.
  • Compare private and public feedback. Survey results appear with star ratings, so you can see whether both point to the same issue.
  • Group feedback by theme. Text and sentiment analytics sort comments into topics like service or cleanliness across all your stores.
  • See what AI assistants say. Two tools focus on AI search, showing how chatbots present your brand and each restaurant to people looking for a place to eat.

Why Reputation stands out: Listings, social listening and publishing, surveys and reviews all live in one enterprise system, and Reputation IQ lets you query that data without building reports.

It's best for: Restaurant and hotel chains (Reputation also serves car dealers and healthcare providers) whose head office wants to oversee reviews, surveys and listings for every site, with reporting built for large organizations.

Pricing: Prices aren't listed; Reputation gives each brand a custom quote.
