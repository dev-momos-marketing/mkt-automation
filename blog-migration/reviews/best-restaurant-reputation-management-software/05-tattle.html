5. Tattle

Tattle was built for restaurants that want to hear from guests after every order. Short surveys go out automatically through your ordering, loyalty and POS systems, tracks public reviews alongside them, and uses AI to tell each location which fix will matter most.

Notable customers: Red Robin, Hooters, Kung Fu Tea and Brinker International (Chili's).

What's new: In August 2025, Tattle raised $5 million to build out its AI tools for restaurants. In January 2026, Kung Fu Tea signed on to follow guest sentiment across channels. A mobile app for GMs and franchise owners is now available too; Tattle hasn't given a launch date.

What you can do with Tattle:

  • Survey guests after every order. Integrations with Toast, Olo, Punchh, Paytronix, Thanx and Square trigger a short survey automatically once a transaction closes.
  • Link feedback to operations. Each answer is tied to an operating area (speed, order accuracy, food or hospitality), so each manager knows which part of service slipped.
  • Know what to fix first. AI recommendations name the change likely to have the biggest effect at each restaurant.
  • Track public reviews too. Google and Yelp reviews appear next to survey results, giving you both the private and public picture.
  • Act from your phone. The mobile app combines surveys and reviews in one inbox, sends push alerts when something serious happens and shows AI Coach tasks.

Why Tattle stands out: Dashboards compare locations and chart measures like top box score and recovery rate over time, and service recovery workflows help you follow up with guests who had a bad visit.

Who it's best for: Quick service, fast casual and casual dining chains, plus their franchisees, who want structured feedback tied to what happens in each store.

Pricing: Tattle doesn't list prices on its site; reach out to the company for a quote.
