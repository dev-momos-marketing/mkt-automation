5. Tattle

Tattle is a customer experience platform for restaurant chains and franchisees that pairs private guest surveys with public review tracking. Surveys go out automatically after each order, and AI turns the results into one clear priority per location.

Its value for reputation is early warning: when a store's survey scores on speed or accuracy drop, you can act before the same complaints turn up in Google or Yelp reviews. Red Robin and Hooters both use it, and Tattle raised $5 million in August 2025 to expand its AI tools.

Key features

  • Post-order surveys: Ordering, loyalty and POS integrations such as Toast, Olo, Square, Revel and Lunchbox send each guest a short survey.
  • Review tracking with replies: The mobile app watches Google, Yelp, Facebook and TripAdvisor and lets managers answer reviewers.
  • Root-cause tags: Feedback is grouped by speed, order accuracy, food and hospitality, down to individual menu items.
  • Location priorities: AI Coach sets each store's top fix and a monthly goal with a projected score gain.
  • Instant alerts: Push notifications flag low-scoring feedback so a GM can reach the guest quickly.

Pros

  • Problems surface sooner: Private survey scores can reveal trouble at a location before it shows in public ratings.
  • Made for restaurants: Categories, integrations and coaching are all designed around how restaurant service runs.
  • Full platform on every plan: Smaller and larger brands get the same features; only the per-location rate changes.
  • Recovery built in: Service recovery workflows help you win back guests who had a bad visit.

Cons

  • Replies at scale cost extra: Reputation Management across review platforms is an add-on, and its price isn't published.
  • Minimum size: The lowest published tier covers brands with 5 to 10 units.
  • Sales-led buying: The pricing page lists no free trial, and every plan starts with a conversation with sales.

Price and plans

Tattle prices per location, per month, with volume tiers; every tier gets the same features.

  • 5 to 10 units: $125 per location, per month.
  • 11 to 20 units: $100 per location, per month.
  • 21 to 50 units: $85 per location, per month.
  • 51 to 200 units: $65 per location, per month.
  • 201+ units: Custom pricing.
