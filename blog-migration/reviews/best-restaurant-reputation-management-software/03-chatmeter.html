3. Chatmeter

Chatmeter manages reviews, listings and local SEO for restaurant chains and other brands with many locations, and uses AI for sentiment analysis across what guests post. Alchemer bought it in September 2025, and the product is now called Alchemer Reputation Management.

Its customers include the restaurant brands A&W and MR MIKES Steakhouse Casual. For chains, the draw is location-level detail: each store's reviews, listings and search visibility can be checked against the others, a habit we describe in restaurant intelligence.

Key features

  • Reviews from 100+ sites: A single dashboard lets you read and answer reviews for every restaurant, wherever guests post them.
  • Rules for automatic replies: You decide which reviews AI Auto-Responder may answer on its own by rating, site and wording, and delicate ones go to your team.
  • Delivery app ratings: Reviews left on Uber Eats, DoorDash and Grubhub show up with the rest, helpful when many orders leave by courier.
  • Competitor view by block: Competitive Intelligence shows how a given location rates against the restaurants around it.
  • Correct store details: Listings management covers Google, Apple Maps and 70+ directories, so hours and addresses stay right on search and maps.

Pros

  • Made for chains: Filters by location, region and review site help a multi-unit operator find the stores pulling the average down.
  • Controlled automation: Auto-replies follow your rules, and anything sensitive waits for a human check.
  • Guest surveys on the same platform: Alchemer's survey tools let you hold private guest comments up against public reviews.
  • G2 score: Alchemer Reputation Management has 4.5 out of 5 on G2.

Cons

  • Quote only: Alchemer doesn't publish reputation management prices, and its online price list is for surveys.
  • Heavy for one location: An independent restaurant may pay for multi-location features it won't use.
  • New owner, new brand: Chatmeter's website now says the product is part of Alchemer and sends visitors there.
