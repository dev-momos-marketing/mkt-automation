3. Chatmeter

Chatmeter handles reviews, listings and local SEO for brands with many locations, with AI that reads reviews for sentiment analysis. Alchemer, a survey and customer feedback company, bought Chatmeter in September 2025, and the product is now sold as Alchemer Reputation Management.

Notable customers: A&W, Driven Brands and MR MIKES Steakhouse Casual, which has a published case study.

What's new: Chatmeter's July 2025 release, Pulse Ai: Competitive Intelligence, summarizes how rivals perform and maps regional results down to the individual store. Then in March 2026 came AI Auto-Responder, which publishes replies in your brand voice under rules you set by star rating, site and wording, while holding back delicate reviews for a human to check.

What you can do with Chatmeter:

  • Manage reviews from 100+ sites. A single dashboard lets you read and answer reviews for every restaurant, wherever guests post them.
  • Automate routine replies. You decide which reviews the AI Auto-Responder may answer on its own, and risky ones are flagged for your team.
  • Include delivery app reviews. Ratings left on Uber Eats, DoorDash and Grubhub show up with the rest, useful when a large part of your orders leave by courier.
  • Query your feedback conversationally. With Pulse Ai: Signals you type a question and get answers drawn from reviews, social posts and surveys, including the photos guests upload.
  • Benchmark against local competitors. Competitive Intelligence shows how a given location rates against the competitors on its block.
  • Maintain correct store details. Each restaurant gets its own listings management and local SEO tracking, which keeps hours and addresses right on search and maps.

Why Chatmeter stands out: Since the Alchemer deal, review and listings data can sit beside survey feedback from the wider Alchemer platform, which gives large chains one place to compare what guests say in public and in private.

Pricing: Alchemer quotes Chatmeter pricing individually; contact its sales team.
