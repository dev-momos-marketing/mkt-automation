7. Marqii

Marqii helps restaurants keep their online presence up to date. From a single dashboard you manage menus, listings, reviews and social posts, with POS menu syncing, AI reply suggestions and tools for gathering more reviews.

Notable customers: California Tortilla, Uccello's Hospitality Group and Romulus Restaurant Group, an IHOP franchisee.

What's new: July 2026 saw a Review Generation launch with Thanx: once a guest pays, an SMS asks them to rate the visit on Google, Facebook or TripAdvisor. Earlier releases were First Party Reviews in November 2025 (your own branded review form, shareable as a link, QR code or embed on your site), and Social Management in May 2025.

What you can do with Marqii:

  • Handle every platform's reviews together. Marqii's inbox covers Google, Yelp, Facebook and OpenTable as well as delivery and catering sites like Uber Eats, Grubhub and ezCater.
  • Draft replies faster. AI suggests a response for each review, and brands with heavy review volume can hand replies to Marqii's managed response service.
  • Push menu changes everywhere. When you update a dish or price in your POS, Marqii carries the change to Google, Yelp and other sites.
  • Fix listing details once. Update opening hours, photos, promotions or a phone number centrally and every location's profiles follow.
  • Plan your posting calendar. Social Management queues up content for Facebook and your Google Business Profile.

Why Marqii stands out: It's one of the few tools in this list with published pricing, billed monthly per location under a yearly agreement. The entry Base plan is $74.99, Pro (which adds reviews) is $119.99 and Kitchen Sync is $149.99, and AI-suggested responses cost $15 more.

It's best for: Restaurant chains and franchise owners who care as much about correct menus and listings as about answering reviews, and prefer a tool designed only for restaurants.
