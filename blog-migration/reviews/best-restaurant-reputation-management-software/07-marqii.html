7. Marqii

Marqii helps restaurants manage how they appear online. From a single dashboard you control menus, listings, reviews, social posts and location pages, with POS menu syncing, AI reply suggestions and texts that invite guests to leave a review.

For restaurant reputation, Marqii's angle is search. Accurate hours and menus, steady review replies and search-optimized location pages all shape what guests find on Google and in AI answers before they decide where to eat.

Key features

  • Menu sync: Update a dish or price in your POS and Marqii carries it to Apple Maps, Google, Yelp, Facebook and other sites.
  • Listings control: Specials, photos, contact details and holiday hours for every location change from one place and reach 70+ publishers.
  • Replies where guests look: You can answer Yelp, Google and Facebook reviews from the dashboard, with AI drafts on Scale or a managed service.
  • Host location pages: Each location gets an SEO and GEO-optimized page with schema markup and embedded menus and hours that update in real time.
  • Social Management: Schedule Google Posts and Apple Showcase content in the same tool you use for listings.

Pros

  • Built for restaurants: Each tool, from menu sync to delivery-app review monitoring, targets a problem restaurant operators deal with daily.
  • Ready for AI search: Structured menus, FAQ widgets and an MCP server connection are designed to help AI tools read your restaurant's details correctly.
  • Published pricing: Marqii lists per-location prices for all plans, which simplifies budgeting across a group.

Cons

  • Delivery reviews are read-only: Marqii monitors DoorDash, Grubhub, Uber Eats and ezCater reviews, and its replies cover Google, Yelp and Facebook.
  • Location pages cost the most: Host comes only with the Marqii Package at $250 per location, per month.
  • Annual contract: Pricing assumes a one-year agreement for each location, with no trial period to test the tools first.

Price and plans

Marqii doesn't offer a free trial or free plan; you can book a free demo. Plans are priced per location, per month, on an annual contract, and managed replies cost extra, from $100 a month for 50 reviews.

  • Base: $90 per location, per month (listings and menus).
  • Growth: $145 per location, per month (adds review monitoring and replies).
  • Scale: $180 per location, per month (adds AI-suggested replies, review generation and social).
  • Marqii Package: $250 per location, per month (adds Host location pages).
