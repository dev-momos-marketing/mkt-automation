6. Qualtrics

Qualtrics sells an enterprise experience management (XM) suite for customer, employee and brand research. For restaurants, it offers location CX dashboards, a module for online reputation management and AI Experience Agents. It has been privately held since Silver Lake took it private in 2023.

Notable customers: Shake Shack and KFC.

What's new: In March 2026, at its X4 event and the first under new CEO Jason Maynard, Qualtrics said paying customers are now using its Experience Agents and introduced real-time service recovery agents and connectors for Genesys, NICE and Salesforce. Qualtrics then closed its Press Ganey Forsta acquisition, worth $6.75 billion, in May 2026; it mainly brings in healthcare patient-experience data and has little effect on the restaurant product.

What you can do with Qualtrics:

  • Collect feedback on every channel. You can send post-visit surveys by email or text, or catch guests on your website, in your app, at the table with a QR code, or in chat.
  • Add public reviews to the mix. The reputation module brings in reviews from sites such as Google and shows them beside what guests told you in surveys.
  • Match dashboards to the org chart. A store GM, an area leader and the C-suite can each get a location view built around the decisions they make.
  • Find themes in comments. AI text and sentiment analysis groups open-ended answers and reviews by topic, so you don't have to read every one.
  • Recover guests in real time. Experience Agents can act on a piece of feedback as it arrives, for example by starting a service recovery step.

Why Qualtrics stands out: Predictive analytics on satisfaction drivers and churn risk, plus brand research and employee programs in the same platform, suit companies that run CX as a research function.

Who it's best for: Big restaurant and hospitality companies whose in-house insights or CX staff run formal listening programs and want reviews, survey answers and store-level feedback managed together.

Pricing: Qualtrics sells its CX suite on custom quotes and publishes no prices; contact sales.
