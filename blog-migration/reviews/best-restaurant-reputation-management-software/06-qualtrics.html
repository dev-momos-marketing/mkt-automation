6. Qualtrics

Qualtrics gives restaurant brands an enterprise experience management suite with an online reputation module, location CX dashboards and AI Experience Agents. Its reputation tools gather what diners write on Google, Yelp, Facebook and TripAdvisor and line it up against what they tell you in private surveys.

KFC and Shake Shack are among its restaurant customers. At X4 in March 2026, the first under CEO Jason Maynard, Qualtrics announced agents for real-time service recovery and connectors for Genesys, NICE and Salesforce.

Key features

  • Multi-site review feed: Diner reviews from the major sites arrive in one place, scored for sentiment, so you see each restaurant's standing at a glance.
  • Location views: GMs get their own restaurant, area leaders their region, and the executive team every brand in the group.
  • On-brand reply drafts: The module proposes responses in your brand's voice that managers can post from their dashboard.
  • Escalation of bad reviews: Negative reviews are routed to the people who can resolve the problem before it spreads.
  • Sentiment trends: Text analytics tracks topics over time, such as wait times or order accuracy, across all locations.

Pros

  • Public and private feedback together: Seeing star ratings next to survey answers helps you tell a one-off complaint from a recurring problem.
  • Fast recovery: Experience Agents can act on a poor review or score while the guest still remembers the visit.
  • Restaurant track record: Large chains such as KFC use it across their locations.
  • Reviewer score: Qualtrics Customer Experience is rated 4.3/5 on G2, based on 751 reviews.

Cons

  • Custom quotes only: The pricing page lists a plan for location-based industries like restaurants, but with "Request Pricing" in place of a price.
  • Built for big programs: The research tools go far beyond what a small independent group usually needs.
  • Reply access rules: Posting replies from dashboards covers Google Places and Facebook, and staff need read and reply rights on the project.

Price and plans

Qualtrics doesn't publish prices. Its CX plans, including one for location-based industries such as restaurants, are priced by interactions, and you get a quote after a demo.
