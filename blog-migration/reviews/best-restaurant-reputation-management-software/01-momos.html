1. Momos

Momos was built for restaurant groups running many stores, and it uses AI agents to look after guests. On the reputation side, it gathers reviews and guest messages from every store into one queue, answers them with an AI agent trained on your voice, and shows which problems keep coming up at which locations.

Notable customers: Caribou Coffee, Condado Tacos, Potbelly and Papa Murphy's are among the restaurant brands that use it.

What's new: In December 2025, Momos summed up its 2025 releases: copilots and ticket summaries powered by AI, brand guidelines the AI follows, mobile-first reports, and connections with Paytronix, Square and Otter. Potbelly signed on in 2025 and now sees reviews, cases and guest messages from all 472 of its stores together.

What you can do with Momos:

  • Stop checking sites one by one. The Unified Inbox holds your Google reviews next to App Store ratings, Instagram and Facebook messages and POS data, while AI triage sends every item to the location that owns it.
  • Answer reviews within minutes. Alfie, the Experience Agent, writes replies in your brand voice and remembers a guest's previous visits and preferences when it responds.
  • Make it right with an offer. When a guest leaves unhappy, Alfie can attach loyalty points, a coupon or a special offer pulled from your loyalty platform, giving them a reason to visit again.
  • Reply in the reviewer's language. Real-time translation lets a location answer a tourist's review or message in the language it was written in.
  • Hand tricky cases to a person. Beyond public reviews, Alfie works in your site's chat, your app's help screen, guest surveys and delivery platforms, and passes the conversation to your team whenever a human should step in.

Momos is one of several AI agents built for restaurant chains, and its replies follow the tone and wording rules you set for your brand.

The analytics view breaks guest satisfaction down by category and flags the biggest incidents at each store, which helps you fix the root cause and improve the guest experience before the same complaint shows up again.

For a sense of scale: across its 1,000+ stores, Papa Murphy's now lets automation handle 97.7% of review responses, and the offers sent to unhappy guests brought back $2M+ in revenue.
