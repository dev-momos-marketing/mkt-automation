1. Momos

Momos was built for restaurant groups with many stores, and its reputation tools sit inside a larger set of AI agents for guests. Reviews from Google, Yelp, OpenTable, TripAdvisor and the delivery apps come into one queue, an agent answers them in your voice, and analytics show which problems keep returning at which locations.

It's one of the AI agents built for restaurant chains, and it plugs into the systems restaurants already run. Condado Tacos, with 30+ locations, has DoorDash, Grubhub, Uber Eats, Yelp, Olo and Punchh connected to Momos, which tags every complaint by type so the same issue gets caught across the chain.

Key features

  • Every review site in one queue: The Unified Inbox holds reviews next to Instagram and Facebook messages and POS data, and AI triage assigns each to its store.
  • Fast, on-brand responses: Alfie, the Experience Agent, replies within minutes and recalls a guest's previous visits and preferences.
  • Win-back offers: For a guest who left unhappy, Alfie can add loyalty points, a coupon or a special offer pulled from your loyalty platform.
  • Root causes by location: Rockie, the Intelligence Agent, surfaces incidents and risks and stack-ranks stores, so one that's slipping stands out.
  • Replies for tourists: Real-time translation lets a store answer a visitor's review in the language it was written in.

Pros

  • Restaurant systems connected: Delivery apps, loyalty programs and POS data feed in directly, so reviews arrive with guest and order context.
  • Rules you control: Replies follow the tone and wording rules you set for the brand.
  • Fixes at the source: Category scores and the biggest incidents per store help you remove a recurring problem and improve the guest experience before the complaint returns.
  • Wide adoption: Momos says more than 600 brands and 20,000 locations use it.

Cons

  • No public pricing: A demo comes before any quote.
  • Built for groups: An independent restaurant with one location may find a lighter review tool enough.
  • Setup with the team: Training Alfie and signing off on its replies takes around a week.

Price and plans

Momos doesn't publish prices; you get a custom quote after a demo, which you can book on the Momos website.
