2. ReviewTrackers

ReviewTrackers gives restaurant chains and franchise systems one account to track, answer and analyze guest reviews. Its case studies include Ted's Montana Grill, and its reports can be split by store, region and owner.

The company sits inside InMoment, its owner since 2022, which Press Ganey Forsta took over in May 2025. ReviewTrackers keeps its own brand, website and plans.

Key features

  • Every location's reviews: Ratings from Google, Facebook, TripAdvisor and other sites flow into one dashboard, and you get alerted as they arrive.
  • Pre-approved wording for GMs: Smart Response templates let a general manager reply fast without drifting from what the brand has signed off.
  • AI-written drafts: AI Auto Responding generates personal replies from prompts your marketing team saves.
  • Review invitations: After a meal, guests can get a text or email asking for a review, so your rating reflects recent service.
  • Nearby competitors: On the Essential plan, competitor insights put your ratings beside restaurants close to each store.

Pros

  • Franchise-friendly access: A franchisee sees only their own restaurants, while a regional director sees the whole market.
  • Every manager can log in: The Starter plan includes unlimited users.
  • Broad review coverage: Its data comes from 100+ sources, which helps catch reviews outside Google.
  • Rated 4.6 on G2: The score comes from 161 user reviews.

Cons

  • Add-ons for local search: Listings management and local search rank cost extra.
  • Competitor data on the higher tier: Starter leaves out competitor insights and experience analysis.
  • Prices by quote: There's no price list, so you'll need a call with sales.

Price and plans

ReviewTrackers doesn't publish prices. Plans are priced per location, per month and paid annually; you book a call with its team to get a quote.
