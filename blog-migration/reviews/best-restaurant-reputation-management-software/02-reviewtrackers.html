2. ReviewTrackers

ReviewTrackers gives chains and franchise systems one place to track, answer and analyze their online reviews. InMoment bought it in 2022, and InMoment in turn became a Press Ganey Forsta company in May 2025. You can still buy it under its own brand, on its own site.

Notable customers: Ted's Montana Grill, PACK & SEND and Chem-Dry.

What's new: July 2025 brought AI Auto Responding to InMoment's Reputation Management product; the feature generates personalized review replies from brand prompts you save. The announcement lists the Locations Starter and Locations Essential plans, which share ReviewTrackers' tier names, though it doesn't name ReviewTrackers itself.

What you can do with ReviewTrackers:

  • Watch every location's reviews. Google, Facebook, TripAdvisor and other sites feed one dashboard, and alerts tell you when a new review comes in.
  • Hand GMs pre-approved wording. With Smart Response templates, a general manager can answer a review in a few clicks and stay within wording your brand has signed off on.
  • Ask happy guests for reviews. After a visit, ReviewTrackers can text or email guests a review invitation, so your rating reflects recent service.
  • Compare yourself with nearby rivals. Competitor tracking puts your ratings and review data next to restaurants close to each of your stores.
  • Spot recurring complaints. Topic and sentiment reports sort what guests write into themes, so a slow-service pattern at one location stands out quickly.

Why ReviewTrackers stands out: You can set up reports and user permissions to mirror how your franchise is organized, so a franchisee sees their own restaurants while a regional director sees the whole market.

It's best for: Franchise and multi-location restaurant groups that want centralized review monitoring and brand-approved responses, particularly if you already use InMoment or Press Ganey Forsta for customer experience.

Pricing: ReviewTrackers charges per location and doesn't publish prices; you get a quote after a call with its sales team.
