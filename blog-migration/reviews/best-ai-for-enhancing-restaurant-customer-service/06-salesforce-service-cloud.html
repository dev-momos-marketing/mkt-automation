6. Salesforce Service Cloud

Salesforce Service Cloud, now marketed as Agentforce Service, manages cases across channels on top of Salesforce CRM data and adds AI agents. Salesforce says the rebrand leaves its products and functionality unchanged. OpenTable runs its customer service on it, a sign of how it fits hospitality at scale.

Notable customers: OpenTable, NightOwl Entertainment, Finnair and Pandora.

What's new: Agentforce Contact Center arrived in March 2026 as a built-in add-on joining phone calls, digital messages, customer records and AI agents, with general availability in the US and Canada. The Winter '26 release in October 2025 added more Agentforce features for service teams.

What you can do with Salesforce Service Cloud:

  • Manage cases in one console. Calls, emails, chats, messages and social posts become cases with the guest's CRM history alongside.
  • Put AI agents on the front line. Agentforce agents answer guests at any hour and hand over to staff when a person is needed.
  • Speed up your agents. AI drafts replies, summarizes cases and classifies them.
  • Run voice in the same place. The Agentforce Contact Center add-on brings phone support into the platform.
  • Control service levels. Entitlements, SLAs, routing and omni-channel queues help large teams hit response targets.
  • Report on performance. Dashboards track service metrics across teams and regions.

Why Salesforce Service Cloud stands out: Its configurability and CRM connection suit enterprises with complex processes, such as a group that runs restaurants, catering and franchising side by side. Plan on an admin or implementation partner to set it up well.

It's best for: Large and enterprise brands that already run Salesforce and want a configurable service desk with AI agents.

Pricing: Starter Suite begins at $25 per user per month billed annually. Higher editions cost more, and the Agentforce for Service add-on is $125 per user per month billed annually, or per-conversation pricing from $2.
