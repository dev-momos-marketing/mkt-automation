12. Sprinklr Service

Sprinklr Service is an AI-native contact center platform that puts voice, social, messaging and other digital channels, more than 30 by Sprinklr's count, into one agent console. It's designed for very large brands, so it fits global restaurant and hospitality companies with big contact centers better than a regional chain.

Notable customers: Deutsche Telekom, 3M, Telefonica and Gulf Bank.

What's new: Sprinklr's April 2026 update (Spring '26) brought Autonomous Evaluation to AI+ Studio, running AI agents through simulated cases ahead of release and grading them continuously afterward, while Agent Copilot began offering proactive nudges. In October 2026, Sprinklr announced Voice AI Agents for spoken conversations.

What you can do with Sprinklr Service:

  • Cover the channels guests use. WhatsApp, LINE, WeChat, social, email, chat, SMS, voice and review sites feed one console.
  • Route by intent and sentiment. Routing weighs skills, capacity, intent and mood, so a furious guest can reach a senior agent first.
  • Deploy AI agents and voice bots. Guest-facing automation answers calls and messages.
  • Test before going live. AI+ Studio is where you build, test and evaluate AI agents.
  • Hear the voice of the customer. Sentiment analysis surfaces recurring themes across every channel.
  • Staff and score the team. Workforce and quality management sit alongside contact center analytics.

Why Sprinklr Service stands out: Few platforms cover this many social and messaging apps in one place, which helps brands in markets where WhatsApp, LINE or WeChat dominate. The trade-off is enterprise-level complexity and a custom contract.

It's best for: Large, multi-country brands running big contact centers across voice, social and messaging.

Pricing: Custom enterprise pricing, quoted on request.
