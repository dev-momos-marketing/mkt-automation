5. HubSpot Service Hub

Service Hub is HubSpot's support software, running on its CRM and bundling ticketing, help articles, a portal for customers, satisfaction surveys and an AI helper called Breeze Customer Agent. It makes most sense for restaurant groups that already use HubSpot for marketing or catering sales, because each guest conversation is saved on the same contact record.

Notable customers: Twelve (an ordering and payments app), Sesame HR and Unific.

What's new: In June 2026, the help desk gained routing through assignment workflows, for example by VIP status or language, plus conditional Customer Agent handoffs and a Coaching Opportunities page. A year before, in June 2025, Breeze Customer Agent opened to all Pro and Enterprise customers through HubSpot Credits.

What you can do with HubSpot Service Hub:

  • Answer from one help desk. Email, chat, WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger and calls come into a shared workspace, with calling on Professional or Enterprise.
  • Let Breeze take routine questions. Customer Agent replies using your knowledge base and passes the guest to a person when needed.
  • Publish answers guests can find. A knowledge base and customer portal cover gift card, catering and loyalty questions.
  • Route and track tickets. Workflows, SLAs and automation keep each request moving toward the right owner.
  • Survey guests after contact. NPS, CSAT and CES surveys show how each interaction landed.
  • See the full history. Every conversation sits on the shared CRM record next to marketing emails and sales activity.

Why HubSpot Service Hub stands out: Support, marketing and sales share one database, so a catering manager can see a client's open complaint before a renewal call. That pays off only if the rest of your team also works in HubSpot.

It's best for: Restaurant brands already on HubSpot that want guest support tied to their marketing and sales records.

Pricing: Free tools are available. HubSpot's pricing page lists Professional at $90 per seat per month and Enterprise at $150; the Starter price shifts with promotions, so check the page. Customer Agent usage draws on HubSpot Credits.
