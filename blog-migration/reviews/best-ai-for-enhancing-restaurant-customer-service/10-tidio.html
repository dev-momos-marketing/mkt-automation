10. Tidio

Tidio gives smaller companies website chat, an AI agent named Lyro, a no-code automation builder called Flows and a basic ticket desk in one subscription. A restaurant can add the chat widget to its website without coding and let Lyro handle questions about hours, menus or reservations.

Notable customers: Suitor, Gecko Hospitality and Pastreez.

What's new: In October 2026, Tidio announced that Lyro led G2's AI CX Agent Evaluations as shown by default, with the best policy-compliance score of the agents G2 compared.

What you can do with Tidio:

  • Put chat on your site. A website widget connects to mobile and desktop apps, so staff can reply from anywhere in the restaurant.
  • Let Lyro answer FAQs. The AI agent replies using your knowledge base and website content.
  • Pass tricky chats to staff. Lyro hands a conversation to a person when a guest needs more help.
  • Automate greetings and leads. Flows, a visual builder, sets up welcome messages, FAQ replies and lead capture for inquiries like private dining.
  • Handle email too. Help desk ticketing covers email, and Lyro can answer those tickets as well.
  • Track what the AI resolves. Analytics report on conversations and AI resolutions.

Why Tidio stands out: You can get it running in an afternoon, and a no-cost tier means you can try it on real guests first. The G2 ranking comes from Tidio's own announcement, so weigh it alongside your own trial.

It's best for: Independent and small multi-unit restaurants looking for website chat with AI answers and no developer work.

Pricing: There's a free plan. Starter is $24.17 per month billed annually ($29 monthly), Growth is from $49.17 monthly on an annual plan, and Plus is priced individually. Lyro is an add-on priced by AI conversations.
