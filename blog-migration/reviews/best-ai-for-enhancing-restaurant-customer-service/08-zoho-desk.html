8. Zoho Desk

Zoho Desk is a multichannel help desk in the Zoho suite with ticketing, self-service portals, automation and the Zia AI assistant. It's a cost-conscious choice for restaurant groups that want structured guest support, especially if they already use Zoho CRM or Zoho Analytics.

Notable customers: Summerfield Resources, RJG Inc. and Mercedes-Benz India.

What's new: Since July 7, 2026, Zia features such as sentiment analysis, auto tags and notifications are included in every paid Zoho Desk edition from Standard up. A year earlier, in July 2025, Zoho released a homegrown language model, Zia LLM, for its whole suite, plus Zia Agents and a no-code Agent Studio.

What you can do with Zoho Desk:

  • Collect tickets from everywhere. Messages from your inbox, calls, website chat, social accounts and contact forms show up on one screen.
  • Read the mood of each guest. Zia detects sentiment, adds tags, suggests replies and raises an alert when activity looks unusual.
  • Answer common questions automatically. Answer Bot and a self-service help center cover hours, policies and FAQs.
  • Build escalation paths. Assignment rules, SLAs, escalations and Blueprint workflows define who handles what, and by when.
  • Track satisfaction. CSAT surveys and agent reports show how your team is doing.
  • Reply from the floor. Mobile apps let managers answer tickets away from a desk.

Why Zoho Desk stands out: Low entry prices and a free tier make it easy to start, and the link to other Zoho apps puts support data next to CRM and analytics. Prices vary by region, so check the rate shown for your country.

It's best for: Budget-minded restaurant teams, particularly those already working in Zoho.

Pricing: Free for up to 3 agents. Express starts at $7 per agent per month for up to 5 agents, and Standard is $14 per agent per month, both billed annually.
