14. Podium

Podium helps local businesses talk to customers by text, gather reviews and take payments, and its AI Employee replies to new leads and schedules bookings. Today Podium mostly packages this as vertical AI systems for car dealers and repair shops, home services and medspas; restaurants sit outside those priority markets. It can still help a restaurant manage texts and review requests, though most new features target other trades.

Notable customers: Mattress Firm, Jackson Hewitt, Ashley Furniture HomeStore and Mountaineer Heating & Cooling.

What's new: In October 2026, Podium said its AI agent business had passed $150 million in annual recurring revenue (a company-reported figure). Its January 2026 AI operating system for aesthetic practices and the December 2025 release of Jerry 2.0, an AI employee for automotive, HVAC and medspa businesses, show where its focus lies.

What you can do with Podium:

  • Answer leads by text and phone. The AI Employee replies over text, phone, email and social and can book appointments after hours.
  • Pick up calls with AI. A voice agent answers the phone when staff can't.
  • Ask for reviews by text. A text after the meal links guests straight to your Google page or another review platform.
  • Share one inbox. Texts, webchat, Google and social messages land together.
  • Take payment in a text. Text-to-pay links let a guest settle a deposit or bill inside the conversation.
  • Send text campaigns. Bulk texts promote offers or bring lapsed guests back.

Why Podium stands out: Texting, reviews and payments in one tool cover much of what a local business needs to stay in touch with customers. For restaurants, though, its newest AI work is aimed at appointment-based trades.

It's best for: Appointment-driven local trades that win business over the phone and SMS; restaurants may find closer fits elsewhere on this list.

Pricing: Podium doesn't publish prices. Core, Pro and Signature are all priced through a sales call, and the AI Employee costs extra on top of whichever plan you choose.
