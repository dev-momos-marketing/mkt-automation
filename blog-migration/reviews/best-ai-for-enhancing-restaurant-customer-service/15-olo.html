15. Olo

Olo runs online ordering for restaurant chains and connects it with delivery partners, payments, guest profiles and, from late 2025, a loyalty program. The private equity firm Thoma Bravo bought Olo and delisted it in September 2025; the business keeps trading as Olo. Its value for customer service comes from ordering data: when you know what a guest ordered and through which channel, problems are easier to fix and follow-up offers are easier to target.

Notable customers: Ben & Jerry's, Gong Cha, Pilot Travel Centers and Jason's Deli.

What's new: In March 2026, Olo unveiled the Olo App at its Beyond4 conference, an app where diners can order from any restaurant on Olo, with brands paying a fixed fee per order in place of commissions. Industry outlets covered its release that October. In December 2025, it closed its purchase of Spendgo and introduced Olo Loyalty.

What you can do with Olo:

  • Take orders on your own channels. Your branded website and app can handle pickup, curbside, delivery and catering orders.
  • Pull marketplace orders into the POS. Rails sends DoorDash, Uber Eats and Grubhub orders straight to your point of sale, which cuts re-keying mistakes.
  • Deliver from your own site. Dispatch finds a courier from outside delivery networks when a guest orders directly from you.
  • Know your guests. The Guest Data Platform and marketing tools draw on real ordering history.
  • Reward repeat visits. Olo Loyalty, from the Spendgo deal, lets guests enroll with a phone number in store.
  • Simplify sign-in. With Olo Accounts, a guest signs in once, without a password, at every restaurant using Olo.

Why Olo stands out: It's built for restaurants, so menus, POS connections and multi-location setups are handled natively. Its strength for guest service is the order and loyalty data it holds, which gives your support team context when something goes wrong.

It's best for: Chains and franchise groups that want direct ordering under their own brand, with guest profiles and rewards built from those orders.

Pricing: Custom; contact Olo for a quote.
