7. Gorgias

Gorgias is an ecommerce helpdesk with an AI Agent, built around Shopify order data and covering email, chat, social, SMS and voice. It fits restaurant brands that also sell products online, such as sauces, coffee or merch, better than it fits dine-in or delivery support.

Notable customers: BrüMate, Tommy John and Try The World.

What's new: In April 2025, Gorgias introduced Shopping Assistant, a beta AI feature that recommends products, suggests upsells and can offer discounts in chat. The company's roadmap frames 2026 around a more mature AI Agent: better control over what it knows and faster answers on rebuilt infrastructure.

What you can do with Gorgias:

  • Handle orders inside the ticket. Agents can edit, refund or cancel a Shopify order without leaving the conversation.
  • Let AI Agent answer "where's my order?" It resolves common questions about order status and returns.
  • Keep channels in one inbox. Email, chat, social, SMS and voice share the same queue.
  • Suggest products while people browse. Shopping Assistant offers picks and add-ons to visitors on your online shop.
  • Automate repeat replies. Macros and rules answer and route the questions you get every day.
  • Measure support's sales impact. Revenue statistics link conversations to orders.

Why Gorgias stands out: Order actions sit right inside the support view, which saves time when a customer wants a refund on a damaged shipment. That Shopify link is the main reason to choose it, so it adds little if you don't sell online.

It's best for: Food and beverage brands with a Shopify store that field shipping and product questions.

Pricing: Starter runs $10 a month and includes 50 billable tickets. AI Agent is charged per resolved conversation: $0.90 on most annual plans and $1.00 on monthly billing.
