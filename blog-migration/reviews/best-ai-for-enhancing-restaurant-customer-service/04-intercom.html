4. Intercom

Intercom combines Fin, its AI agent, with a human helpdesk covering inbox, ticketing, help articles and calls. The firm rebranded as Fin in May 2026 and was then acquired by Salesforce, a deal that closed in September 2026; the helpdesk product is still called Intercom. For restaurants, the draw is an AI agent that can field most routine guest questions on chat, email, phone and messaging apps.

Notable customers: Anthropic, Lightspeed and Synthesia.

What's new: In September 2026, Salesforce completed its purchase of Fin, the former Intercom, which now runs within Salesforce AI Labs. The rename to Fin had been announced on May 12, 2026.

What you can do with Intercom:

  • Put Fin on the first reply. Drawing on your published articles, Fin handles hours, allergen and refund questions over chat, email, WhatsApp, SMS and social.
  • Cover the phone line. Fin Voice takes incoming calls, which helps on a Friday night when nobody at the store can reach the phone.
  • Finish the rest with people. A shared inbox and ticketing give your team a place to close out anything Fin can't resolve.
  • Draft replies faster. Copilot writes suggested answers for your human agents.
  • Reach guests proactively. Messages triggered by behavior can point app users to a new feature or a store update.
  • Compare AI and team results. Reports show how Fin and your staff perform side by side.

Why Intercom stands out: The product is organized around Fin resolving as many questions as possible, and the AI is priced per outcome. Because Fin answers only from your content, well-kept FAQs and policies shape the quality of its replies.

It's best for: Brands that want an AI agent to take most routine guest questions across channels while a lean team handles the rest.

Pricing: Paid annually, Essential costs $29 for each seat monthly, Advanced $85 and Expert $132. Fin is charged separately at $0.99 per resolved conversation.
