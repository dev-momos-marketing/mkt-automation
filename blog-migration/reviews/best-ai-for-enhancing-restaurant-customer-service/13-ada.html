13. Ada

Ada builds AI agents that take over customer conversations on chat, messaging apps, email and the phone. Your team configures it in a no-code builder, and helpdesk integrations pass a guest to a person when the AI can't finish the job. Restaurant brands with heavy support volume can use it to take routine questions off their staff.

Notable customers: Betsson, Cebu Pacific, monday.com and Malaysia Airlines.

What's new: A July 2026 builder update let Ada generate answers natively in 60 languages and send pictures inside automated chats, and it added audit logs for enterprises plus extension APIs. Earlier, in February 2026, it introduced the Unified Reasoning Engine behind its agents on every channel and brought Playbooks to voice.

What you can do with Ada:

  • Run one agent everywhere. The Unified Reasoning Engine powers the same AI-powered chatbot on chat, messaging, email and voice.
  • Script multi-step requests. Playbooks give the agent a structured workflow for tasks such as a refund or an order check.
  • Reply in the guest's language. AI generation works natively in 60 languages.
  • Add visuals to answers. The bot can send a picture mid-conversation, for instance a screenshot showing guests where their receipt lists the order ID.
  • Plug into your stack. Through its APIs, the agent can reach your online ordering, rewards program and other back-office software.
  • Escalate to staff. When a person has to step in, connectors to helpdesks like Dixa move the chat across.

Why Ada stands out: Ada focuses entirely on automation, so it fits teams that already have a helpdesk and want a stronger AI layer on top. Wide language coverage helps brands serving guests in several countries.

It's best for: High-volume brands that want an AI agent to resolve routine questions before staff step in.

Pricing: There's no public price list; Ada quotes each deal, largely according to how many conversations you expect.
