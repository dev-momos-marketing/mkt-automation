2. Zendesk

Zendesk is a customer service suite, branded the Resolution Platform, that houses your ticket queue, chat and phone channels, FAQ pages for guests and automated AI responders under one login. For a restaurant group it works as the back office for guest contact, turning refund requests, missing-item complaints and catering questions into trackable conversations. Food companies such as Grubhub and Grill'd run their customer care on it.

Notable customers: Grubhub, Grill'd and Impossible Foods.

What's new: In March 2026, Zendesk closed its deal for Forethought, whose chat, email and voice agents now sit inside the platform and improve their own procedures over time.

What you can do with Zendesk:

  • Keep every guest channel together. Email, chat, social messages and calls arrive in one workspace, so a complaint that starts on social media and moves to email stays in one thread.
  • Automate the repeat questions. AI agents reply to frequent requests over chat, email and voice and escalate anything unusual to your staff.
  • Help people write faster. Copilot, sold as an add-on, proposes replies and next steps while one of your team works the case.
  • Send cases to the right desk. Routing and triggers can separate catering leads, delivery complaints and gift card issues without anyone sorting them by hand.
  • Offer self-service answers. A help center lets guests look up allergen, refund or reservation policies before they reach out.
  • Measure response quality. Dashboards show contact volume and response times broken down by channel.

Why Zendesk stands out: Few support platforms match its mix of channels, automation and marketplace apps, which matters once you run dozens of stores or several brands. Since the Forethought purchase, the AI agents and the human queues also come from one vendor.

It's best for: Restaurant groups and food brands with a dedicated guest care team handling a steady stream of contacts across several channels.

Pricing: Support Team costs $19 per agent per month on annual billing ($25 month to month). Suite Team adds messaging, phone and AI agents for $55 per agent monthly on an annual contract, and Copilot costs extra.
