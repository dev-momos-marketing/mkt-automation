16. Medallia

Medallia is an enterprise experience management platform that gathers feedback from surveys, reviews, contact-center conversations and digital channels, then uses AI to find themes and route action to front-line teams. Its creditors, headed by Blackstone, Apollo and FS KKR, took control from Thoma Bravo in June 2026, and Medallia is still selling and building the platform. It suits large restaurant and hospitality brands with dedicated CX teams, while independents will find it heavy.

Notable customers: Noodles & Company and Best Western.

What's new: In September 2026, the H2 2026 release expanded multilingual AI analysis, added self-service survey distribution and previewed agentic automation. That came after the June 2026 recapitalization, in which the new owners put in $150 million of fresh capital and Medallia said it would keep its $500 million AI investment commitment.

What you can do with Medallia:

  • Collect feedback everywhere. Web, email, SMS, QR code, in-app and receipt surveys capture what guests think after a visit.
  • Add reviews and social posts. Listening tools sit next to the feedback you ask for.
  • Find themes automatically. AI text analytics spot topics, sentiment and urgent issues.
  • Analyze calls. Speech and conversation analytics cover your contact center.
  • Compare locations. Role-based dashboards show results by store, region and brand.
  • Close the loop. Alerts and case management prompt managers to follow up with unhappy guests.

Why Medallia stands out: It handles feedback volume and location comparisons at a scale most survey tools can't, which helps a national chain tell whether a problem is local or systemic. Expect an enterprise rollout and a CX team to run it.

It's best for: Big restaurant and hotel groups with a CX team that wants detailed feedback analysis across many sites.

Pricing: Medallia doesn't publish prices; it quotes custom enterprise pricing.
