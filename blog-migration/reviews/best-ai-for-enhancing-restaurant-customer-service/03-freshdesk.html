3. Freshdesk

Freshdesk is the helpdesk from Freshworks, handling tickets from email, chat, phone and social channels with Freddy AI built in, and Freshdesk Omni is its edition for omnichannel teams. It suits restaurant brands that want an organized support queue at a moderate price, with AI they can switch on as volume grows.

Notable customers: Decathlon, Bridgestone, Body & Fit and Dineout.

What's new: In November 2025, Freshworks introduced the Freshdesk Command Center, one screen for multichannel conversations with Freddy AI Copilot inside, alongside industry-specific AI agents and Freddy AI Insights. It followed the June 2025 release of Freddy AI Agent Studio for building agents without code.

What you can do with Freshdesk:

  • Work all channels from one queue. Email, chat, phone and social tickets land together, so a shift lead can see what's waiting at a glance.
  • Build an AI agent without code. Freddy AI Agent, set up in Agent Studio, answers routine chat and email questions such as opening hours or a late order.
  • Get drafting help. Freddy AI Copilot summarizes threads and suggests replies and next steps, as a paid add-on on Pro and Enterprise.
  • Spot spikes early. Freddy AI Insights flags trends and anomalies, like a sudden jump in complaints about one menu item.
  • Automate the routine handling. Rules assign tickets, watch SLAs and send follow-ups so nothing sits unanswered.
  • Look up online orders. Integrations with Shopify, Stripe and FedEx help agents check orders for brands that also sell packaged goods online.

Why Freshdesk stands out: Its cost and setup effort sit well below enterprise suites, yet you can add AI agents, a copilot and insights later. Private notes and parent-child tickets help when one complaint needs input from both operations and finance.

It's best for: Small and mid-size restaurant brands that want a structured, affordable helpdesk with space to add AI later.

Pricing: On annual billing, an agent seat costs $19 on Growth, $55 on Pro and $89 on Enterprise each month. Freddy AI Copilot is extra, and AI Agent sessions are bought in packs.
