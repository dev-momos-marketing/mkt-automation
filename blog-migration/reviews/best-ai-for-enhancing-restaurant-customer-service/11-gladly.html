11. Gladly

Gladly is a people-centered customer service platform that keeps one lifelong conversation for each customer across channels, and it now positions itself as an agentic commerce platform with Gladly AI. Its site and blog also run on gladly.ai. For hospitality brands, the appeal is that agents see a guest's whole history instead of starting a fresh ticket for each contact.

Notable customers: JOANN, Rothy's and Ulta Beauty.

What's new: In May 2026, at Gladly Connect Live, the company announced agentic commerce features: one AI system that handles sales questions and service requests from discovery before a purchase to retention after it. A June 2026 blog post described the next version of the platform, with AI handling conversations, assisting agents and resolving requests across channels.

What you can do with Gladly:

  • Keep one thread per guest. Voice, email, chat, SMS and social contacts all join a single, lifelong conversation.
  • Show agents the whole picture. The profile displays past interactions and order data.
  • Resolve requests with AI. Gladly AI answers and closes out requests on every channel.
  • Support staff mid-conversation. AI assistance helps agents while they talk with a guest.
  • Feed the AI your help content. Self-service articles double as the knowledge Gladly AI draws on.
  • Route by person. People-based routing and queues come with reporting on team performance and satisfaction.

Why Gladly stands out: Guests never need to repeat their story, because every past contact is in one view. That matters for brands where regulars expect to be recognized, such as upscale dining groups.

It's best for: Mid-market and enterprise hospitality and consumer brands that run high-touch service across phone and digital channels.

Pricing: Prices aren't listed publicly, so you'll need a quote from Gladly.
