9. LiveAgent

LiveAgent, made by Quality Unit, is an all-in-one help desk that combines live chat, ticketing, a built-in call center, social channels and an AI chatbot. For restaurants that still take a lot of phone calls, having phone and chat in one affordable tool is the main appeal.

Notable customers: Huawei, Slido and FrëschKëscht.

What's new: In the first quarter of 2025, LiveAgent rolled out an AI Chatbot powered by FlowHunt that runs from the existing chat button, and its August 2025 update brought an AI Answer Generator.

What you can do with LiveAgent:

  • Use one inbox. Email, live chat, phone and social messages share a universal inbox.
  • Run a call center in the same tool. IVR, call routing and recording come built in.
  • Answer with AI in the guest's language. The AI Chatbot replies from your content and detects which language the guest is writing in.
  • Polish agent replies. AI Answer Improver helps staff tidy up their wording before sending.
  • Offer self-service. A knowledge base and customer portal take care of frequent questions.
  • Watch performance live. Real-time reports track agent activity, while automation rules distribute tickets and enforce SLAs.

Why LiveAgent stands out: You get chat, phone and AI in one product at a low per-agent price, which saves stitching several tools together. That suits a small group whose guests still prefer to ring the restaurant.

It's best for: Small and mid-size restaurant teams that need phone, chat and social support in one help desk.

Pricing: Starter is $15 per agent per month billed annually, promoted at $11 until November 22, 2026. Team is $29 and Business $49 per agent per month, and Enterprise is a flat $3,199 per month for 80 agents. A free trial is available.
