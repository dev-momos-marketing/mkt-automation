2. Restaurant365

Restaurant365 is a cloud back office that franchisees with several units often shortlist as an all-in-one restaurant management platform. Because the ledger, stock counts, rotas and payroll all draw from the same data, a franchise owner can read each store's P&L without moving spreadsheets between systems. Its ledger follows the way restaurants close their books, which sets it apart from generic restaurant management tools.

Notable customers: Villa Restaurant Group, Wholly Stromboli and Johnson's Landing.

What's new: In May 2026, Restaurant365 showed R365 AI at the National Restaurant Association Show, adding AI dashboards, an AI advisor and a labor suite, with AI scheduling in early access. A month later it named Kate Hastings chief customer officer.

Key features:

  • Accounting shaped for restaurants. Your bookkeeper stops keying in supplier invoices by hand, reconciliation happens against the bank inside R365, and every LLC in a multi-store franchise portfolio closes its own P&L.
  • Food cost you can trace. Recipe costing and inventory counts feed a report that compares theoretical with actual food cost for each unit.
  • Schedules from forecasts. Labor planning draws on projected sales, which helps managers staff each shift for the volume they expect.
  • Payroll for hourly crews. Payroll and HR handle tips and restaurant pay patterns, so you can drop a separate payroll provider.
  • Royalties and budgets. Multi-location reports and budgeting include franchise royalty tracking, so you can see what each store owes the brand.
  • Sales posted from the POS. Integrations bring every day's sales into the books with no manual entry.

What makes them stand out: Store operations and the general ledger live in one place, so a franchisee can compare P&Ls across units, set fiscal periods that match the brand calendar and budget store by store.

Pricing: You pay for a bundle per store, invoiced every quarter or once a year, and bigger franchise groups negotiate a custom quote. The dollar amounts differ from page to page on the vendor's site, so ask for a current figure before you plan the spend.

It's a great fit for: Franchisees and multi-unit groups that want accounting, inventory and labor in one system and less time spent on back-office admin.
