3. FranConnect

FranConnect is franchise management software built for the franchisor side of the relationship, from recruiting candidates and opening units to audits, royalties and franchisee support. By its own count, close to 1,500 brands with many locations run on it. Starting in 2025, a family of generative AI helpers called Frannie AI has spread across its recruiting, ops, finance and learning modules.

Notable customers: Tropical Smoothie Cafe, Capriotti's and Papa Johns.

What's new: In July 2026, FranConnect gave development teams Candidate Coach, an AI helper for working prospects, standard project tracking for operations and World Manager training inside its app. Next on the October 2026 roadmap is a Franchisee Support Agent whose replies point back to sources in the franchisor's vetted Hub library; a phone version of that agent went live in May 2026.

What you can do with FranConnect:

  • Run a development pipeline. A CRM follows candidate leads through to signed deals, and an AI coach helps your development team work each prospect.
  • Track new openings. Onboarding projects show which tasks still stand between a signed franchisee and opening day.
  • Train in the same app. World Manager learning content sits inside FranConnect, so franchisees and their crews avoid another login.
  • Audit stores and follow up. Field inspections create corrective tasks tied to the exact question a location failed.
  • Watch royalties and collections. Royalty reporting comes with AR aging and collections dashboards that show which units are behind.
  • Answer franchisee questions. A communication hub and an AI support agent reply to franchisees with citations from content the brand has approved.

Why it stands out: The whole franchise lifecycle lives in one system, and integrations with HubSpot, Salesforce, QuickBooks and POS systems connect it to tools many brands already run.

Best for: Restaurant franchisors who need one file per owner that follows the relationship from first inquiry through opening, audits and royalty payments. It suits franchisors more than single-brand operators.

Pricing: Custom. FranConnect doesn't publish prices, so you'll get a quote from its sales team.
