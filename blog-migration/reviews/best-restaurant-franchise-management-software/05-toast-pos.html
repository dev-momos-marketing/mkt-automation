5. Toast POS

Toast POS is a restaurant point of sale and management platform, listed on the NYSE as TOST, that handles ordering, kitchen display, payroll, marketing and multi-location controls from a single vendor. By the end of June 2026 its customer base had grown to roughly 180,000 live locations, a mix that runs from one-off cafes to large franchise groups.

Notable customers: Captain Parker's Pub, Kaldi's Coffee and Craft Lounge.

What's new: In May 2026, Toast's spring release brought out Toast IQ Grow, a paid upgrade where software (the AI Marketing Agent) and a named person (a marketing success manager) both help run campaigns, and gave its Toast Local guest app a fresh start with rewards baked in. August's second-quarter report added that the company signed up about 9,500 more locations in three months.

Key features:

  • Hardware for every station. Orders come in on counter terminals, self-service kiosks and Toast Go handhelds carried to the table.
  • One menu across stores. Franchisors manage menus and prices centrally and send changes to every location.
  • Kitchen display. Cooks read orders on screens at each station, so paper tickets stop getting lost.
  • Direct ordering. Your own web ordering, links to delivery marketplaces and the Toast Local app give guests ways to buy without a third party in between.
  • Payroll and scheduling. Crew pay runs, staff records and shift planning through Sling by Toast all connect to the registers.
  • Guest marketing. Points programs, gift card sales and text or email blasts are handled in Toast, with an AI marketing agent in Toast IQ Grow.

What makes them stand out: For franchise groups, central menu management means a price change or new item reaches every store from one screen, which keeps locations in step with the brand.

Pricing: Plans are quoted after a call with Toast's reps. A no-cost Starter Kit and several paid tiers have appeared on toasttab.com at times, so confirm what's on offer today before you set a budget.
