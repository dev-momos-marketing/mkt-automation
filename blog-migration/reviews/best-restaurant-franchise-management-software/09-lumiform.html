9. Lumiform

Lumiform is a mobile inspection and checklist app that multi-location restaurant and food businesses use for brand audits, HACCP checks and corrective actions. It now builds forms with AI, translates them and checks uploaded photos. It's a natural match for operators in Europe, where the German chain Sausalitos has used it since 2019.

Notable customers: Sausalitos, EDEKA Nord and AmRest.

What's new: Lumiform hasn't put out dated product announcements recently. Its AI form builder, photo checks and Learning Paths appear on its site and in app release notes without launch dates.

Key features:

  • Forms with logic. Inspection, audit and checklist forms change their questions based on earlier answers.
  • AI form builder. Describe a check in a prompt or upload an existing file, and Lumiform turns it into a template.
  • Forms in 60+ languages. Automatic translation helps when crews across a franchise speak different languages.
  • Photo validation. AI reviews uploaded photos to confirm they show what the task asked for.
  • Corrective actions with proof. Findings become assigned actions, completed with a photo or document as evidence.
  • Offline audits. The mobile app works without signal and captures photos and signatures, feeding real-time multi-site reports and alerts.

What makes them stand out: The inspection workflow is the core of the product: when an auditor flags an issue, a corrective action is assigned and tracked until someone closes it with proof.

Pricing: A free Starter plan is available. Professional is licensed per user (Lumiform's pricing FAQ uses EUR 20 per user per month as an example) and comes with a 14-day free trial, while Enterprise is quoted.

It's a great fit for: Franchise groups that need structured brand audits, health and safety inspections and compliance records across many locations.
