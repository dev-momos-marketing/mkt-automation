12. ZenPut

Zenput is now called Crunchtime Ops Execution: Crunchtime, which bought Zenput in 2022, renamed it in February 2026 to bring it fully into its suite. The software lets multi-unit restaurant brands push checklists, audits, food safety checks and new initiatives to every store and confirm each one is done.

Notable customers: Chipotle, Domino's and Freddy's Frozen Custard & Steakburgers.

What's new: In February 2026, Zenput took the Crunchtime Ops Execution name. In July 2026, Crunchtime rolled out new AI capabilities spanning inventory, operations execution and analytics.

Key features:

  • Tasks for every store. Checklists and to-dos go out to all locations from one place.
  • Brand standards audits. Area managers run inspections and field visits against the same standards everywhere.
  • Automated temperature logs. Food safety records draw on automatic temperature monitoring.
  • Follow-up on failures. Corrective action workflows chase each failed item until it's fixed.
  • Rollout tracking. When the brand launches a new item or procedure, you can see which stores have completed it.
  • Connected to Crunchtime. It links to Crunchtime's inventory, labor and learning products, with dashboards for multi-location reporting.

What makes them stand out: The product centers on one question franchisors care about: did every store do the task the same way on every shift? Real-time dashboards and a mobile app for store teams and area managers answer it.

Pricing: Custom. Crunchtime doesn't publish prices for Ops Execution.

It's a great fit for: Franchise brands of any size that need consistent execution and up-to-date visibility into whether standards hold on the floor each day.
