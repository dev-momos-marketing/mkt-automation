8. Square for Franchises

Square for Franchises is a franchisor layer on Square's POS that adds centralized reporting, brand-wide menu management and royalty and fee calculation. Each franchisee keeps its own Square account and payouts, while the brand sees results across corporate and franchise stores. A 2026 Square press release calls the product Square Franchise Suite, which may be a newer name.

Notable customers: Whit's Frozen Custard, Steak Escape, Cascadia Pizza Co. and Groucho's Deli.

What's new: In March 2026, Steak Escape moved its 23 locations to Square, and in September 2026 Whit's Frozen Custard put roughly 100 locations in 11 states on Square Register, Square KDS and Square for Franchises. Cascadia Pizza Co. came back to Square in August 2026.

Key features:

  • Sales across every store. Reports roll up corporate and franchise locations so the brand can compare units side by side.
  • Menus set by the brand. A menu change made once reaches all locations.
  • Royalty rules you choose. Fees can be a percentage of gross or net sales or a fixed amount.
  • Royalty reports and invoices. Reports by location, location group and date feed Square Invoices for collection.
  • Separate franchisee accounts. Franchisees keep their own Square accounts and receive their own payouts.
  • A full restaurant stack. It runs on Square for Restaurants POS, KDS, kiosk and online ordering, with loyalty, marketing and integrations such as Restaurant365.

What makes them stand out: POS, payments, royalty calculation and franchisor reporting come from one vendor, which keeps the setup manageable for brands without a dedicated IT team.

Pricing: Square for Franchises is priced through Square's sales team. Each location also pays for a Square for Restaurants plan (Free, Plus or Premium, listed per location per month on Square's pricing page) plus card processing fees.

It's a great fit for: Emerging and growing QSR, cafe, fast casual and dessert franchise brands that want POS and franchisor controls together without an enterprise rollout.
