4. Operandio

Operandio is an Australian-built operations app that gives franchise and multi-unit restaurant groups digital SOPs, checklists, food-safety logs, audits and training in one place. It runs on phones and in-store kiosks, which suits crews who never sit at a desk. In February 2026 it bought FranchiseLab, a franchisee recruitment tool that keeps its own brand.

Notable customers: Flagship Restaurant Group, Cold Rock Ice Creamery, Riverland Group and Abacus Dining Group.

What's new: In February 2026, Operandio acquired FranchiseLab, co-founded by Greg Nathan, so franchisors can recruit new owners inside the platform they already use to run stores. It hasn't announced other dated releases since.

Key features:

  • SOPs staff can search. Procedures sit in a knowledge base with AI search, so a new hire can type a question and find the right step.
  • Shift checklists. Opening, closing and food-safety lists go out as tasks, and managers see what each location has finished.
  • Audits that raise actions. When an inspection finds a problem, Operandio creates the corrective action on its own.
  • Courses from your documents. Upload an SOP and the AI course builder drafts lessons plus quiz questions from it.
  • Labels and sensors. Staff print food prep labels, and sensors record fridge and freezer temperatures without manual checks.
  • Playbooks for new sites. Location LaunchPad gives each new franchise a pre-opening checklist, and translation covers multilingual teams.

What makes them stand out: The app is designed for frontline restaurant work, with kiosk and mobile modes that floor staff can use mid-shift and announcements that track who has read them, so compliance rests on the system instead of one manager's memory.

Pricing: Custom, based on the number of locations and modules you need. Operandio doesn't publish prices.

It's a great fit for: Restaurant franchises that need HACCP records, food-safety logs and identical execution in every location, plus franchisors who want recruitment in the same platform.
