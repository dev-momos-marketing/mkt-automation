10. BrandWide

BrandWide is franchise management software from Soffront Corporation, a CRM vendor, covering franchise development, onboarding, training, audits, royalties and franchisee marketing. It's general-purpose software that serves franchisors in many industries, restaurants among them. The International Franchise Association lists it as a supplier.

Notable customers: BrandWide doesn't name its customers publicly.

What's new: The vendor hasn't announced any dated releases or features lately.

Key features:

  • Lead pipeline. A development CRM tracks prospective franchisees and nurtures them toward signing.
  • Onboarding paperwork. Document handling and e-signatures, through a DocuSign integration, move new owners from signing toward opening.
  • Training records. Learning management covers courses for franchisees and their staff.
  • Field visits. Audit and compliance checklists guide consultants through each location visit.
  • Royalty billing. Royalties are calculated, invoiced and collected, with QuickBooks Online and Stripe integrations.
  • Support and local marketing. A helpdesk and intranet handle franchisee requests, and local marketing tools sit beside system-wide dashboards.

What makes them stand out: BrandWide puts the full franchise lifecycle in one platform and aims it at mid-sized brands that have no need for enterprise-level complexity.

Pricing: Custom. BrandWide doesn't publish prices, so you'll need to contact the vendor for a quote.

It's a great fit for: Emerging and mid-sized restaurant franchisors that want one platform for the franchisor-franchisee relationship without an enterprise-scale project.
