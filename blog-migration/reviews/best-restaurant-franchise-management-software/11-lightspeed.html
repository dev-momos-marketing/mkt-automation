11. Lightspeed

Lightspeed Restaurant is a cloud POS from Lightspeed Commerce (NYSE/TSX: LSPD) aimed at sit-down restaurants, bars and expanding groups. It bundles table management, integrated payments, inventory and roll-up reports for every location. In Europe the same platform runs as K-Series. Since late 2025 it has added service-pacing insights and AI assistants.

Notable customers: Five Guys (Koeppel Companies franchise), Dean & David and Cafe Lola.

What's new: Lightspeed AI arrived in January 2026, putting chat-style assistants inside Lightspeed Restaurant that answer questions about sales and operations. In March 2026 it added Lightspeed Tasks checklists and updates to table pacing and reservations, building on Tempo, which it unveiled in November 2025.

Key features:

  • Floor and table control. The iPad POS handles floor plans, tables and coursing for sit-down dining rooms.
  • Checkout without the walk. Guests settle the bill where they sit, or servers take a card tap on a phone, through Lightspeed Payments.
  • Kitchen screens. Orders appear on displays by station, so the grill and the salad cook only see their own tickets.
  • Inventory and recipes. Recipe tracking and menu performance reports show which dishes earn their place.
  • Central menus. Multi-location menu management and consolidated reports keep franchise units consistent.
  • Pacing and AI. Tempo shows table pacing, order flow and occupancy, and the AI assistants answer questions about the numbers.

What makes them stand out: Lightspeed pairs dining-room tools such as Tempo and coursing with recipe-level inventory and consolidated reports, a combination that suits franchisees running sit-down concepts.

Pricing: US list prices begin at $189/month on Essential and $399/month on Premium. Card fees, kitchen screens, reservations and other extras are billed on top, big groups negotiate their own rate, and figures vary outside the US.

It's a great fit for: Sit-down franchise brands and multi-unit owners looking for a cloud POS with detailed reporting and pacing tools for the dining room.
