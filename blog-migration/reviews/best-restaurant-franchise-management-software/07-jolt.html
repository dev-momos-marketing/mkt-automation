7. Jolt

Jolt gives restaurant and c-store crews one app for digital checklists, food safety logs, labeling, scheduling and training. In August 2025 it became part of Digi International, which runs it inside the SmartSense unit. Jolt still runs under its own brand and site, and this year Digi also bundles it into SmartSense ONE.

Notable customers: A&W Restaurants and White Spot Hospitality.

What's new: At the NRF show in January 2026, Digi's SmartSense team unveiled SmartSense ONE, which packages sensor monitoring and Jolt's task tools into seven core modules you can mix. The launch followed the roughly $145.5 million deal that brought Jolt under Digi the previous August.

Key features:

  • Checklists with proof. Closing duties can demand a snapshot plus a supervisor's approval, leaving evidence that the step got done.
  • Temperature records. Food safety logs pair with SmartSense sensors that watch coolers automatically.
  • Label printing. Crews print date code and prep labels straight from the app.
  • Scheduling and time clock. Managers build rotas by dragging shifts around, and staff punch in from the same app that holds their lists.
  • Training and info hub. A library holds training material and reference documents staff can open during a shift.
  • Reports across stores. Owners compare completion rates store by store, and a PAR Brink link is among the available POS connections.

What makes them stand out: Jolt is organized around the individual shift, and its photo proof and sign-offs give franchise owners a record of who did what in each store. Groups shopping for temperature sensors can get both from one vendor through SmartSense.

Pricing: Custom. Jolt prices each deal after a conversation with its reps.

It's a great fit for: QSR franchisees who want every shift to leave a trail of completed tasks and food safety logs, and who may add wireless temperature sensors later.
