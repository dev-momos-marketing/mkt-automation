13. MarginEdge

Website: MarginEdge

Category: Invoice processing & daily food cost (Inventory)

MarginEdge combines your invoices with POS sales to give you food cost, inventory and a P&L every day, so you aren't waiting for month-end to learn a store went over. You pay one fee and get the invoice handling plus paying vendors, placing orders and costing recipes.

Notable customers: Skopos Hospitality Group, Crooked Hammock Brewery, South Block and The Tavern at Ivy City Smokehouse.

What's new: MarginEdge celebrated its tenth anniversary and 10,000 customers in April 2025. A combined equity and debt package worth $80 million followed in August 2026 (Schooner Capital and Ten Coves Capital led it), with the money going toward AI features that draw on restaurant purchasing data.

Key features:

  • Invoices in any form. Photos, emails and EDI feeds are all captured and processed automatically.
  • Food cost every morning. Daily food and beverage cost combines yesterday's sales with invoice data.
  • Bill pay in the price. Paying suppliers happens inside MarginEdge without a separate fee.
  • Ordering and order guides. Managers place supplier orders from guides set up for each store.
  • Counts, recipes and price alerts. Inventory counts, menu-item costing and warnings on price changes live together.
  • P&L and accounting sync. Budgets and P&L views sync to tools like QuickBooks, with integrations for dozens of POS systems.

What makes them stand out: Seeing food cost the next morning lets an operator fix an over-portioning or price problem within the same week.

Pricing: MarginEdge charges a flat monthly fee per location that includes unlimited invoices and bill pay. Its own pricing page has listed $330 per location per month. Outside trackers in 2026 say that rose to $350, and that the Freepour add-on for bar inventory costs $150 more. Confirm the number with MarginEdge before you sign.

It's a great fit for: Independent operators and small-to-mid restaurant groups, including franchisees, who want food cost and accounting connected.
