10. Kitro

Website: KITRO

Category: Automated food waste measurement (Kitchen & BOH)

Made in Switzerland, KITRO's unit mounts above the trash bin, photographs and weighs each disposal, and classifies it with AI. Chefs then see in a dashboard which foods are being lost, in kilos and in money, and adjust ordering and batch sizes. Like Winnow, it serves hotels and buffets far more than drive-thru restaurants, so fast food operators should weigh it mainly for central kitchens.

Notable customers: Marriott International, Hyatt, Pestana (Algarve hotel) and Hyatt Inclusive Collection.

What's new: KITRO became a Marriott International preferred partner in 2025, backing Marriott's aim to cut its food waste in half. A February 2026 Hyatt EAME case study reported more than EUR 1.7M saved and 31% less waste (figures from the vendor). In May 2026 it opened in Portugal with a Pestana hotel in the Algarve.

Key features:

  • Hands-off capture. A camera and scale record every item dropped in the bin, so nobody fills in a waste sheet.
  • AI sorting. The software labels each discard by food category and flags whether it could have been eaten.
  • Waste priced by category. Cost values per food group translate weight into the money you're losing.
  • Dashboards for chefs. Reports point to where purchasing, production or buffet sizes should change.
  • ESG reports. Sustainability reporting supports compliance with EU food waste rules.
  • Rented hardware. The device comes on a monthly subscription, and KITRO offers a free assessment first.

What makes them stand out: Waste is logged automatically and shown in currency, which gets a finance team's attention faster than a weight chart does.

Pricing: The Business plan starts at CHF 349 a month for one kitchen; Enterprise is custom-priced, and longer contracts, bigger properties or more units bring the price down.

It's a great fit for: Hotels, resorts and high-volume catering kitchens. For a fast food brand, it suits a commissary better than individual stores.
