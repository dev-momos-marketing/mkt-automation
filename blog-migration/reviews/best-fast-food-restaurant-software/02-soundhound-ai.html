2. Soundhound AI

Website: SoundHound AI

Category: Drive-thru, phone and kiosk voice ordering

Think of SoundHound as the extra order taker a burger joint never has to schedule: its AI answers the store phone, talks to cars at the speaker and runs voice kiosks. The company trades on Nasdaq and has grown by buying Amelia, SYNQ3 Restaurant Solutions and Interactions. Its restaurant products have been rebuilt on OASYS, an in-house agent platform it introduced in May 2026.

Notable customers: Five Guys, Chipotle, Red Lobster, Applebee's / IHOP and Church's Texas Chicken.

What's new: SoundHound's August 2025 earnings release listed fresh restaurant wins at Red Lobster, Applebee's / IHOP and Peter Piper Pizza, and more Chipotle stores. Five Guys renewed in January 2026 once its phone agent had handled over one million calls, opening it to franchisees too. OASYS followed in May 2026.

Key features:

  • Calls handled during the rush. When nobody can step off the fryer, the agent takes the phone order and answers allergen, menu and store-hours questions.
  • A voice at the speaker post. Drive-thru guests order by talking to the AI, including on lanes fitted with Acrelec equipment.
  • Kiosks and cars, too. Self-order kiosks and connected vehicles can use the same voice engine for ordering.
  • No re-keying. Because it connects to the POS, each order prints in the kitchen exactly as a cashier-entered ticket would.
  • Add-on prompts. Combos, upsizes and limited-time promos get offered the same way on every order.
  • Insight from lane audio. Voice Insights converts drive-thru conversations into data operators can study for patterns.

What makes them stand out: Few vendors cover phone, lane and kiosk ordering under one contract, which keeps a multi-unit fast food brand on a single voice system.

Pricing: SoundHound keeps its rates private; expect a custom quote after a sales conversation.

It's a great fit for: Large burger, chicken and casual-dining brands looking to hand calls and lane orders to AI at dozens of sites, with the vendor helping on rollout.
