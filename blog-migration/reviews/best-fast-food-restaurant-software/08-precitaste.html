8. PreciTaste

Website: PreciTaste

Category: Kitchen forecasting & prep guidance (BOH)

PreciTaste acts as a forecasting brain for the back of house: it reads POS history, weather and local events, then works out how much your crew should prep, how much to cook and the right moment to drop the next batch. Cameras watch pans and display cases so the guidance updates as the shift unfolds. Fast food and bakery-case operators use it to keep the line full at noon without binning trays at close.

Notable customers: Bluemont Group (Dunkin' franchisee, 90+ locations) and Mac Haik Restaurant Group (First Watch franchisee).

What's new: PreciTaste and PAR Technology partnered in December 2025 so past sales flow in from PAR's POS and new stores onboard faster. Bluemont Group switched on Do'Cast AI donut and Munchkin ordering at its 90+ Dunkin' shops in February 2026. In July 2026 Round2 Capital provided seven-figure revenue-based financing for European expansion.

Key features:

  • Prep lists built from forecasts. Prep Assistant produces each morning's list from demand predicted for that store.
  • A plan for the whole day. Planner Assistant spreads production across dayparts so rushes don't catch the kitchen short.
  • Live cues on the line. Station Assistant tells cooks when to start the next batch while service is under way.
  • Cameras on pans and cases. Computer vision tracks how much of each ingredient or baked item is left.
  • Automatic bakery orders. Do'Cast combines forecasts with shelf images to set donut orders.
  • Works with your POS. Integrations include PAR and Toast.

What makes them stand out: Guidance comes at the station level during service, so a newer crew member can cook to demand the way an experienced one would.

Pricing: PreciTaste sets prices per deal and doesn't publish them.

It's a great fit for: Burger, chicken, donut and bakery operators, from franchisees to whole chains, that lose money to overproduction and running out on the line.
