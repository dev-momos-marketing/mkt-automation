15. 7Shifts

Website: 7Shifts

Category: Labor & Workforce Performance Analytics

7shifts builds workforce software only for restaurants: scheduling, time clocks, tip management, payroll and, since 2026, staff training. It works as a labor-focused restaurant analytics platform too, putting labor cost next to POS sales. Fast food operators with high crew turnover use it to fill shifts from a phone app and keep labor percentage in check.

Notable customers: HCI Hospitality (Freddy's Frozen Custard & Steakburgers franchisee), Boston Pizza (Regina), Conshy Girls Restaurant Group and Pitango Gelato.

What's new: Plans were reorganized on July 2, 2025 into four tiers, with Comp staying free. Premium subscribers got Employee Training in September 2026: managers write lessons and quizzes, send them to particular roles, departments or stores, and see who has finished.

Key features:

  • Forecast-aware scheduling. You drag shifts onto a grid while forecasted sales and labor show how many people each daypart calls for.
  • A crew app for swaps. Staff trade shifts, update availability and message the team from their phones.
  • Time clock that enforces the schedule. Early clock-ins can be blocked, with punches synced through POS and payroll integrations.
  • Labor budgets. Reports compare labor spend with sales, so you see when a shift ran heavy.
  • Tip-outs and payroll. The system splits pooled tips and handles payouts, and a restaurant payroll module is sold on top.
  • Onboarding, training and checklists. New-hire packets, in-app courses, tasks and shift checklists help managers get crew up to speed.

Pricing: Top tier Premium runs $134.99 per location monthly on an annual contract ($149.99 month to month). Pro sits at $79.99 ($89.99), and Essentials, which allows up to 30 employees, at $39.99 ($44.99). Comp is free for one store with no more than 15 people. Enterprise is priced by quote, payroll is an extra charge, and Pro comes with a two-week free trial.
