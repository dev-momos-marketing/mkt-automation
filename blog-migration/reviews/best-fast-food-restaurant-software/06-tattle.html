6. Tattle

Website: Tattle

Category: Guest feedback & operations insight

Tattle is an independent CX platform for restaurant chains whose quick post-purchase questionnaires reach guests via the chain's app, loyalty program or register. It sorts answers into areas a fast food manager can act on, such as speed, order accuracy and food quality, and adds online reviews to the picture. AI then picks out the one fix likely to move each store's scores the most.

Notable customers: Hooters, Red Robin, Brinker International (Chili's) and Kung Fu Tea.

What's new: Fresh funding of $5 million arrived in August 2025, earmarked for the AI side of the product. The bubble tea chain Kung Fu Tea became a client in January 2026, using Tattle to watch how guests feel across every channel. More recently, GMs and franchisees got an iOS and Android app with a combined survey and review inbox, alerts for serious incidents and AI Coach tasks.

Key features:

  • Surveys triggered by the order. Guests get a request after buying through connected ordering, loyalty or POS systems (Toast, Olo, Square, Punchh, Thanx and Paytronix among them).
  • Answers mapped to operations. Comments land under speed, accuracy, food quality or hospitality, with sentiment analysis on the written part.
  • A ranked to-do per store. AI points each location at the change with the biggest expected payoff.
  • Reviews beside surveys. Google and Yelp ratings sit in the same view as solicited feedback.
  • Store-by-store comparisons. Dashboards track trends, top box score and recovery rate across the estate.
  • Recovery and mobile alerts. Unhappy guests can be followed up, and managers get push notifications on their phone.

What makes them stand out: Feedback arrives already sorted by the parts of the operation it concerns, which tells a district manager where each store is slipping without a long read.

Pricing: Tattle quotes each brand individually and doesn't post prices online.

It's a great fit for: Multi-unit QSR and fast casual brands, and their franchisees, who want survey results turned into a short list of fixes for every location.
