12. xtraCHEF

Website: xtraCHEF

Category: Invoice automation & food cost for Toast (Inventory)

Toast bought xtraCHEF in 2021, and the product keeps its name today as xtraCHEF by Toast. The software reads supplier invoices line by line, then matches those costs against what your Toast POS says you sold. For a counter-service store already on Toast, that turns invoice photos into food cost and recipe margins without manual entry.

Notable customers: Toast doesn't publish a list of xtraCHEF customers.

What's new: Toast hasn't announced any dated xtraCHEF releases in the past couple of years.

Key features:

  • Invoices read for you. Snap or upload supplier invoices, and line-item costs are pulled out automatically.
  • Recipe costing in Pro. Sub-recipes, yields and unit conversions let you cost a burger down to the sauce.
  • Inventory counts in Pro. Stock counts feed the cost picture alongside invoices.
  • Actual vs. theoretical. Variance reports show the gap between what food should have cost and what it did.
  • Vendors and ordering. Supplier management, product catalogs and ordering sit in one place.
  • Sales-linked costs. Toast POS data ties each cost to what actually sold, and accounting integrations take the numbers onward.

What makes them stand out: Because it lives inside Toast, cost data and sales data come from the same system with no extra integration to maintain.

Pricing: Toast doesn't publish xtraCHEF prices. It comes in Essentials and Pro tiers through Toast sales; invoice automation is in both, while recipe costing and inventory are Pro only.

It's a great fit for: Quick-service stores running Toast at the counter whose managers spend evenings keying invoices and want food cost visible weekly.
