7. Ovation

Website: Ovation

Category: Text-message feedback & reviews

Ovation texts guests two quick questions after they order, then flags bad experiences to the store fast enough to make things right the same day. Since its fall 2025 release it also handles public reviews and business listings, so one dashboard covers private feedback and what people post online. It's built for multi-unit brands, and plenty of its customers are counter-service chicken, burger and fast casual concepts.

Notable customers: Dave's Hot Chicken, Curry Up Now, Bareburger and Taziki's Mediterranean Cafe.

What's new: October 2025 added review and listings management with AI reply suggestions. Back in February 2026, a $3 million raise was sourced almost entirely from operators on the platform, close to a dozen of them, and took the funding tally to $12 million. May 2026 brought Brand Voice for tone control and a Menu Item Performance Report tying sentiment to what guests ordered.

Key features:

  • A two-question text. Once the POS or ordering system logs a purchase, the guest gets a text automatically; dine-in stores can use table QR codes instead.
  • Instant heads-up on complaints. Managers hear about a bad visit in real time and can contact the guest before they write a public review.
  • Replies drafted for you. AI suggests answers to feedback and reviews in your chosen tone, and staff edit before sending.
  • One review inbox. Google, Yelp, Facebook and other sites can be filtered by platform, star rating or store.
  • Listings and themes. It updates profiles on 50+ directories and tags comments under topics like speed, cleanliness and food quality.
  • Menu and competitor reports. Sentiment links to menu items, and Competitive Pulse benchmarks each store against up to five rivals.

What makes them stand out: A two-question text takes seconds to answer, which suits fast food guests who won't sit through a long survey.

Pricing: No public price list. Your quote reflects your count of stores plus any ghost kitchens, with a lower rate per unit as the footprint grows.

It's a great fit for: Chicken, burger and other fast food groups with several stores that need to win back upset guests the same day and grow their count of 5-star reviews.
