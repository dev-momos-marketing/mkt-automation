9. Winnow

Website: Winnow

Category: AI food waste tracking for commercial kitchens

Winnow puts a camera and scale over the kitchen bin: Winnow Vision recognizes and weighs whatever gets scraped in, then prices it, so a chef sees waste in dollars by item and shift. A newer app, Winnow Foresight, forecasts demand per item to stop overproduction before it happens. Its client list is mostly hotels and contract caterers, so for a fast food unit with little prep waste it's a stretch; it makes more sense for groups with commissaries or large production kitchens.

Notable customers: Hilton, IHG Hotels & Resorts, Accor, Marriott and Mandarin Oriental.

What's new: Winnow bought Lumitics, a Singapore food waste startup, in July 2026 as a base for expansion across the Middle East and Asia-Pacific. Two months later, in September 2026, it released Foresight, built alongside Hilton chefs, which predicts dish-level demand from occupancy and service history and needs no hardware.

Key features:

  • Bin that sees and weighs. Winnow Vision's camera and scale log each item thrown away without anyone typing it in.
  • Throw and go. Staff scrape the plate and walk off, so tracking doesn't slow a busy shift.
  • Waste broken down with costs. Data splits by food item, shift and stage, whether prep trim, spoilage or plate waste.
  • Screens for the crew. Kitchen displays show what's being binned and what it's worth, right where it happens.
  • Demand forecasts. Foresight predicts what each service will need, item by item, to guide production.
  • A cheaper manual tier. For kitchens happy to tag items themselves, a manual version costs less than the camera setup.

What makes them stand out: Showing waste and its cost on a screen next to the bin changes kitchen habits without a manager standing over the team.

Pricing: Winnow prices each project privately and lists no rates.

It's a great fit for: Hotel kitchens, buffets, caterers and large restaurant groups with real production waste; most single fast food stores won't generate enough to justify it.
