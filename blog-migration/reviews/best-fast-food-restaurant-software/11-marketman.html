11. MarketMan

Website: MarketMan

Category: Inventory, purchasing & food cost (Inventory)

MarketMan is cloud back-office software for counting stock, costing recipes, ordering from suppliers and processing invoices. For a fast food operator running on thin food-cost margins, its value is catching a supplier price jump on beef or oil before it eats a month of profit. Square sellers can also get it built into their POS.

Notable customers: Westville Restaurant Group, ATFC, Ltd. and Taco del Mar (multi-unit franchisee).

What's new: A global launch in April 2026 made MarketMan the engine behind Square Restaurant Inventory, putting ingredient and recipe management, purchase orders, vendor lists, invoice scanning and waste tracking inside Square.

Key features:

  • Counts by store. Inventory updates in real time for each location, so a manager knows what's on the shelf before the truck arrives.
  • Recipe costs that move with prices. Menu items are costed from what you currently pay for each ingredient.
  • Automatic purchase orders. Supplier orders can be generated and sent from the platform.
  • Invoice scanning and price alerts. Scanned invoices update item prices and warn you when a supplier raises one.
  • Waste and loss tracking. Logged waste shows trends you can act on at each store.
  • Multi-unit COGS reports. Food cost compares across locations, with POS and accounting integrations, an API add-on and Square support.

What makes them stand out: Inventory, ordering and invoice data share one system, so a price change shows up in your recipe costs without anyone retyping it.

Pricing: Current plan rates couldn't be verified from MarketMan directly, and outside price trackers say they shifted in 2026. Get a quote from the vendor before you budget.

It's a great fit for: Fast food franchisees and multi-unit groups that want tight control over purchasing and food cost, including restaurants on Square.
