4. ConverseNow

Website: ConverseNow

Category: AI order taking for restaurant phones and lanes

ConverseNow gives fast food stores a virtual employee for the phone: an LLM-driven assistant that chats with callers, builds the order and sends it to the register. Its 2024 purchase of Valyant AI extended that to drive-thru speakers. Pizza and wing shops, where call volume spikes at dinner and on game nights, are its home turf.

Notable customers: Anthony's Coal Fired Pizza & Wings and Wingstop (pilot at select locations).

What's new: ConverseNow and Deliverect teamed up in April 2026. Brands that already run menus and incoming orders through Deliverect can now switch on ConverseNow for calls and drive-thru lanes from there.

Key features:

  • No missed calls at peak. The assistant answers whenever the phone rings and builds a complete order, even with every staffer on the line.
  • Lane ordering. Drive-thru voice capability came with Valyant AI in 2024.
  • Direct POS entry. Orders flow into the register on their own, removing a common source of keying mistakes.
  • Steady upselling. Extra wings, dips or a bigger pizza get suggested on each call in the same way.
  • Conversation that flexes. Large language models handle callers who switch items or ask something halfway through.
  • Escalation to people. OrderSolutions, a call-center partner, can take over calls the AI shouldn't handle alone.

What makes them stand out: Deep roots in phone-heavy pizza and wing concepts, and an easy route in for Deliverect users.

Pricing: ConverseNow quotes each brand privately and publishes no price list.

It's a great fit for: Pizza, wing and similar fast food chains where a large chunk of revenue still arrives by phone.
