14. TimeForge

Website: TimeForge

Category: Labor scheduling, timekeeping & forecasting (Labor & Scheduling)

Around since 1998 and headquartered in Lubbock, Texas, TimeForge sells labor software to restaurants and retailers: it builds the rota, records who clocked in and keeps you on the right side of labor rules. In August 2024 it added Lineup.ai's AI sales and labor forecasting to its platform, so that forecasting now runs inside TimeForge. For a fast food manager, the result is a schedule built from predicted sales in 15-minute blocks, with an alert when labor creeps past target mid-shift.

Notable customers: Hard Eight BBQ and Great Harvest Bread Co. (Great Harvest Franchising, preferred vendor).

What's new: The most recent dated news is from July 2025: a Square integration listed in Square's ecosystem, which pipes sales from Square registers into TimeForge so its forecasts can set crew levels.

Key features:

  • Forecasts in quarter-hours. Lineup.ai's models read your register history and estimate sales, and the staff needed, for every 15-minute slot.
  • Schedules that follow demand. The software drafts a rota around predicted volume, putting extra hands on the lunch peak and trimming the 3 p.m. lull.
  • Clock-ins with overrun alerts. Punches are logged as crew arrive, and the shift lead gets pinged the moment hours exceed the labor target.
  • Compliance guardrails. The scheduler checks drafts against overtime limits and fair workweek (predictive scheduling) laws.
  • Self-service for crew. Crew members handle their own availability, time-off requests and shift trades from the app.
  • Labor vs. sales across units. Multi-store reports compare labor cost to sales, with POS links including Toast and Square.

What makes them stand out: Forecasting, scheduling, timekeeping and compliance sit in one product, which saves a franchise group from stitching a forecasting tool onto a separate scheduler.

Pricing: TimeForge quotes on request; the figures in older blog posts and directories conflict, so ask the company directly.

It's a great fit for: Burger, sandwich and casual-dining franchisees who want the register to shape the schedule and one tool to handle punches and compliance.
