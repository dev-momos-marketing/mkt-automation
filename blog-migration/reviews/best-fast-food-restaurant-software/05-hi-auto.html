5. Hi Auto

Website: Hi Auto

Category: Speaker-post order automation

Hi Auto, founded in Israel, builds a single product: a drive-thru assistant trained to hear guests over idling engines, wind and tinny speakers. It passes each order to the POS and kitchen screen and suggests add-ons along the way. Fried chicken and burger brands put it at the speaker so the crew can concentrate on cooking and getting bags out the window.

Notable customers: Bojangles, Checkers & Rally's and Lee's Famous Recipe Chicken.

What's new: During 2025 the company said it was processing more than 100 million lane orders annually across its client base. In 2026 Bojangles extended for several years and set a goal of adding the assistant at 100 additional stores by mid-2027, beyond the roughly 500 running it today.

Key features:

  • Hands-free speaker post. Each car is greeted and its order captured by the AI, so no one needs to wear a headset.
  • Trained on lane conditions. Noise handling targets traffic, bad weather and worn speaker hardware.
  • Live POS and KDS sync. Tickets show up on the register and kitchen display the moment ordering wraps up.
  • Queue-aware upselling. Offers can scale back when cars stack up, which protects speed of service.
  • Orders in Spanish. Checkers & Rally's switched on Spanish-language ordering at its drive-thrus.
  • Your own AI persona. Brands can name and voice their assistant; Bojangles calls its version "Bo-Linda".

What makes them stand out: A pure drive-thru specialist that is also piloting Window Intelligence, a tool that watches the pickup window, with Lee's among the test brands.

Pricing: Hi Auto sells on custom terms and keeps prices off its site.

It's a great fit for: Fast food chains with packed drive-thru lanes that want a dependable, branded AI voice in each store.
