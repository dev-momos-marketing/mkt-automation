4. Restaurant365

Category: End-to-end restaurant management software

Built as a cloud back office, Restaurant365 connects restaurant accounting with inventory, food costing, scheduling and payroll. All of it lands on one P&L, so your controller and your general managers argue over the same figures.

The R365 AI layer reads that P&L and nudges managers toward the labor and cost problems most worth fixing.

Notable customers: Villa Restaurant Group, Wholly Stromboli and Johnson's Landing.

What's new: R365 AI made its first appearance in May 2026 at the restaurant industry's NRA Show, bundling AI dashboards and an AI advisor with new labor management tools and AI scheduling, the last still in early access. June 2026 brought a new Chief Customer Officer, Kate Hastings.

Key features:

  • Restaurant accounting. Invoice processing runs automatically, bank reconciliation is built in, and every legal entity carries its own P&L.
  • Theoretical versus actual. Counts and recipe costs combine to show where food cost drifts from what it should be.
  • Scheduling to forecast. Shifts are planned around projected sales, with payroll and HR designed for hourly workers who earn tips.
  • Automatic sales posting. Daily POS totals flow into the general ledger with nobody keying them in.
  • Budgets for every unit. Reporting spans the whole portfolio, with royalty tracking available for franchisors.
  • An AI advisor. R365 AI dashboards call out shifts in P&L, labor and cost lines so a manager knows which number to dig into.

What makes them stand out: Because the ledger and store operations share a database, nobody has to reconcile a separate accounting package against inventory or scheduling tools at month-end.

Pricing: Restaurant365 sells bundles priced per location, paid every quarter or every year, and builds custom quotes for bigger groups. The dollar amounts on its own pages don't agree, so confirm current rates with sales.

It's a great fit for: Franchisees and multi-unit groups who want the books, the stockroom and the schedule all tied to one P&L.
