10. MarginEdge

Category: Real-Time Food Cost & Back-Office Automation

Feed MarginEdge your invoices and POS data, and it returns food cost, inventory and P&L numbers every day, so a jump in beef prices shows up while you can still adjust the specials. The one subscription bundles invoice handling, vendor payments, ordering and recipe costing.

Notable customers: Skopos Hospitality Group, Crooked Hammock Brewery, South Block and The Tavern at Ivy City Smokehouse.

What's new: An $80 million round in August 2026, mixing Series D equity with debt, is earmarked for new AI products built on the restaurant purchasing data flowing through MarginEdge. Back in April 2025, MarginEdge celebrated its tenth birthday alongside a customer count of 10,000 restaurants.

Key features:

  • Invoices without typing. Photos, emailed PDFs or EDI feeds are all processed automatically.
  • Daily food cost. Food and beverage cost refreshes every day by matching POS sales against invoices.
  • Vendor payments built in. Bill pay comes with every subscription, alongside order guides for placing orders.
  • Counts linked to recipes. Inventory counts drive the cost of each recipe and menu item.
  • Price watch. MarginEdge alerts you whenever something you regularly buy changes price.
  • Budgets and the books. Budget and P&L views sync with accounting tools like QuickBooks, and dozens of POS systems plug in.

Pricing: One flat fee per location each month covers unlimited invoices and bill pay. The pricing page has displayed $330; 2026 trackers report $350 instead, and Freepour, the add-on for bar inventory, adds $150, so double-check before signing.
