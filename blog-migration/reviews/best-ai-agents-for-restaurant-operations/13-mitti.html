13. Mitti

Known until August 2026 as SafetyCulture, Mitti is a mobile platform for frontline teams that covers inspections, checklists, follow-up actions, training, sensors and equipment records across multiple sites. Restaurant groups lean on it for food safety, cleanliness and brand-standard audits done on a phone, with any failed item turned into a task for someone to fix.

Notable customers: Snooze, an A.M. Eatery, Fishbowl, Trippas White Group and Sodexo.

What's new: The August 2026 rebrand to Mitti came with new AI tools, including an AI Assistant, issues the AI raises on its own and agents that monitor routine checks. Multi-site operators also got 5 Star Benchmarking, a single rating per restaurant. Earlier, in July 2026, a pricing tracker noted that every plan had started including monthly AI credits.

Key features:

  • Audits from a phone. Each inspection records photos, comments and a score, and you can begin from one of many public templates for food safety.
  • Assigned fixes. Anything that fails becomes an action with an owner and a deadline.
  • Comparing restaurants. Analytics across sites, including the 5 Star Benchmarking score, reveal which locations are sliding.
  • Bite-size training. Lessons and courses are delivered to staff on their mobile devices.
  • Sensors and assets. Temperature and environment sensors trigger alerts, and equipment maintenance history stays on file.
  • AI agents and restocking. Monitoring agents and the AI assistant take on repetitive checks, while Mitti Supply can reorder items an inspection flags.

Pricing: Teams of up to 10 can use Mitti free. Third-party listings put Premium near $24 per seat monthly on an annual contract or $29 paid month to month, and cheaper Lite seats suit crew who only fill in inspections or take training. Enterprise pricing is quoted, and it's worth confirming every figure with Mitti since the rebrand.
