7. HotSchedules

Category: Enterprise Labor Forecasting & Workforce Analytics

Many restaurant managers still call it HotSchedules, but since a 2019 merger the scheduling product sits inside Fourth's platform and is sold under the Fourth name. AI predicts how busy each shift will be, schedules get built from that prediction, and the Fourth iQ app pushes recommendations to managers across labor, HR and inventory.

Notable customers: Noodles & Company, Texas Corral, FreeRange Concepts and Snooze, an A.M. Eatery.

What's new: Starting July 2025, forecasts automatically take nearby ticketed events, like a concert or a play down the street, into account. Fourth shipped Fourth iQ 2.0 with its iQ App that September, and the 2.2 update in November 2025 brought warnings that help a manager avoid running out of stock.

Key features:

  • AI-driven demand forecasts. Past sales, the weather and local happenings shape the expected traffic for every shift.
  • A score for each schedule. Schedules are drafted from demand and graded on how well they balance coverage against cost.
  • Shift marketplace. Employees trade shifts, post availability and send messages on their phones.
  • Labor law controls. Time and attendance tools help keep punches and breaks within the rules.
  • Planned against worked. Labor budget tracking shows the gap between scheduled hours and hours actually clocked.
  • Alerts for managers. The Fourth iQ App raises scheduling, HR and inventory issues, and links to Fourth payroll, inventory and your POS.

Pricing: Not published; Fourth's sales team prepares a custom quote.
