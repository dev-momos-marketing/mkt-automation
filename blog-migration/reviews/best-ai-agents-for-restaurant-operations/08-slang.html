8. Slang

Category: Voice AI Phone Answering & Guest Communication

Slang AI is a voice agent that picks up a restaurant's phone, answers routine questions and books or changes reservations, so hosts aren't pulled away from guests at the door. It connects with reservation and event platforms, and its Superhost product adds memory of returning callers for full-service dining rooms.

Notable customers: Gusto54 Restaurant Group.

What's new: February 2026 brought a $36M Series B for Slang, with US Venture Partners leading and total funding reaching $68M; at that point the company reported serving 2,000+ restaurant locations. October 2026 saw the arrival of Superhost, which recalls earlier conversations, preferences and visit history, follows policies the restaurant sets and speaks nearly 100 languages.

Key features:

  • Round-the-clock answering. Calls are picked up 24/7 in a natural-sounding voice, including during the dinner rush.
  • Routine questions handled. Callers get answers about hours, location, menu and policies without staff involvement.
  • Reservations through your platform. Bookings are made and changed through OpenTable, SevenRooms and Yelp integrations.
  • Events and handoffs. Private-event inquiries route to Tripleseat, and the call passes to a person when it needs one.
  • Links by text. Callers who want takeout receive an online ordering link by text, since Slang doesn't take orders by voice.
  • Guest memory and call insights. Superhost recognizes regulars, and call summaries and analytics show what people ask about most.

Pricing: Custom; Slang AI doesn't publish its prices, so you'll need a quote.
