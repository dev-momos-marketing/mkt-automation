16. Tenzo

Category: POS sales, labor, inventory, reservation analytics

Tenzo, headquartered in London, makes reporting and forecasting software that pulls POS, labor, inventory, reservation and review data onto a single dashboard. It layers over the tools your restaurants use today and can pass its sales forecasts to your scheduling software. Tenzo sticks to analytics and forecasting, so you'd still need another system for stock counts and recipe costing.

Notable customers: Lettuce Entertain You, PrimoHoagies, Parker Restaurant Group and Pizza Pilgrims.

What's new: Tenzo announced a $5M first close on its Series A extension in April 2026, with Edge VC at the head of the round. Over the next 18 months it plans to have twice as many integrations and a bigger US presence, and Jane O'Riordan became board chair.

Key features:

  • One view of the business. Revenue, staffing, inventory, reservations and guest reviews appear on one screen.
  • Calendar-aware forecasts. A machine learning model factors in weather, public holidays and events nearby.
  • Forecasts that reach the rota. Tenzo hands its predictions to schedulers like Deputy and Planday.
  • Benchmarks between sites. Comparisons show your strongest restaurants and the ones that need a visit.
  • Reports that arrive on their own. Performance summaries land in your inbox each day and each week.
  • AI assistant access. Through an MCP connector, your team can ask questions of Tenzo data from AI tools, backed by a wide set of integrations.

Pricing: Custom; Tenzo doesn't publish prices.
