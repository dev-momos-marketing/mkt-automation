5. MarketMan

Category: Restaurant inventory & food cost management software

On the purchasing side of operations, MarketMan is a cloud tool where you count stock, cost recipes, send supplier orders and process invoices. Each new invoice updates what your recipes cost, so the food cost figure tracks what you really paid for chicken and produce that week.

Square also uses MarketMan to power its own restaurant inventory product.

Notable customers: Westville Restaurant Group, ATFC, Ltd. and Taco del Mar (multi-unit franchisee).

What's new: Square made Square Restaurant Inventory by MarketMan available globally in April 2026. Square sellers now get recipe and ingredient tools, supplier records, purchase orders, waste logs and invoice scanning without leaving their Square account.

Key features:

  • Counts by location. Real-time stock levels for each store let you check the walk-in before you order.
  • Costing that keeps up. Recipe and menu costs shift as soon as invoice prices change.
  • Ordering from the app. MarketMan generates purchase orders and sends them to your vendors based on usage and pars.
  • Price-change warnings. Scanned invoices refresh item prices and flag any supplier increase.
  • Waste you can see. Logged waste reveals patterns, such as a slow dish that the line keeps over-prepping.
  • Portfolio food cost. COGS reports cover all locations, with POS and accounting connections plus API access for bigger groups.

What makes them stand out: MarketMan sticks to food cost, so invoices, purchasing and recipes, the three things that move your margin, are managed side by side.

Pricing: Current prices aren't listed on MarketMan's website. Plans are per location, so request a quote.

It's a great fit for: Independents, multi-unit groups and Square sellers who want a firmer grip on stock, ordering and food cost.
