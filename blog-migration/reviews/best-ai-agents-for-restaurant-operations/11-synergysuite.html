11. SynergySuite

Category: Enterprise Back-of-House Management Suite

Restaurant chains pick SynergySuite modules to run the back of house in the cloud: stock, purchasing, recipe costs, labor, food safety and reporting. AI forecasts recommend each store's order, which keeps food and labor cost from drifting apart between locations of the same brand.

Notable customers: Playa Bowls, Church's Chicken, Friendly's Restaurants and Shipley Do-Nuts.

What's new: A $12 million raise in August 2025 is funding further work on its AI tools for chains and franchise systems. A month later, Playa Bowls, with 200+ locations, chose SynergySuite to oversee food and labor and to automate its inventory and purchasing.

Key features:

  • Chain-wide stock view. Headquarters can see live counts for every store.
  • Recommended orders. AI demand forecasts power dynamic ordering, giving managers a suggested quantity to approve.
  • Purchasing to plate cost. Supplier records, invoices and recipe costs roll up into a theoretical-against-actual food cost view.
  • Labor planning. Scheduling gets AI assistance and connects to time and attendance.
  • Digital food safety. Compliance checklists are logged and tracked for each site.
  • Cash and reporting. Controls on voids and refunds sit next to multi-location reports, and POS links include Toast and PAR Brink.
