15. Lightspeed

Category: Restaurant cloud POS & multi-location management software

Lightspeed Commerce, which trades on the NYSE and TSX, sells Lightspeed Restaurant as its cloud POS for dining rooms, bars and groups, covering tables, orders, built-in payments, stock and reporting across locations. In 2026 it added Lightspeed AI, conversational assistants that answer an operator's questions about sales and operations.

Here's a look at the features Lightspeed offers restaurants:

Notable customers: Five Guys (Koeppel Companies franchise), Dean & David and Cafe Lola (Johnson City, Tenn.).

What's new: In January 2026, Lightspeed launched Lightspeed AI with assistants inside Lightspeed Restaurant. In March 2026, it added digital checklists through Lightspeed Tasks, along with table pacing and reservation improvements. That builds on Tempo, unveiled in November 2025, which uses live dining-room data to show table pacing, order flow and occupancy.

Key features:

  • iPad POS for the floor. Floor plans, table management and coursing help servers pace each table.
  • Payments at the table. Guests can settle up at the table, and staff can accept tap payments on a phone, through Lightspeed Payments.
  • Kitchen routing. A kitchen display system sends each order to the right station.
  • Inventory and menu reports. Recipe and stock tracking connect to reports on menu performance.
  • Central control for groups. Menus are managed across locations, with consolidated reporting for the whole group.
  • Pacing and AI answers. Tempo shows service flow in full-service rooms, and Lightspeed AI answers questions about your numbers.

What makes them stand out: Lightspeed combines a POS for full-service dining with service-pacing tools and AI assistants, which suits restaurants where table turns and coursing drive revenue.

Pricing: US plans start at $189 per month for Essential and $399 per month for Premium. Card processing is charged on top, and so are KDS hardware and booking features; bigger groups receive a custom quote, and rates vary outside the US.

It's a great fit for: Bars, sit-down restaurants and expanding groups looking for a cloud POS that bundles payments, in-depth reports and help pacing the dining room.
