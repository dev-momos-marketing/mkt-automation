12. TimeForge

Category: AI Labor Forecasting & Scheduling

Restaurants and retailers use TimeForge to plan labor: schedules, time and attendance, and staying on the right side of labor law. The Texas company dates back to 1998, and in 2024 it brought Lineup.ai onto its platform, so Lineup.ai's AI forecasts of sales and labor now feed every schedule TimeForge builds.

Notable customers: Hard Eight BBQ and Great Harvest Bread Co. (Great Harvest Franchising, preferred vendor).

What's new: TimeForge's most recent dated move came in July 2025: a listing in the Square app marketplace, so restaurants on Square POS can send their sales over and let those numbers decide projected labor hours and headcount per shift.

Key features:

  • Forecasts every quarter hour. Lineup.ai technology reads your POS history and projects sales and labor needs in 15-minute slices.
  • Auto-drafted schedules. Shifts are generated to match expected volume, so a manager edits a draft instead of building a week from a blank grid.
  • Over-target warnings. Punches are tracked live, and managers get an alert the moment labor exceeds its goal.
  • Compliance checks. Overtime limits and fair workweek, or predictive scheduling, rules are checked as schedules are made.
  • Self-service for staff. Employees handle their own availability, trades and time-off requests.
  • Labor as a share of sales. Reports across units weigh labor spend against revenue, drawing on Toast, Square and other POS connections.

Pricing: Custom; TimeForge quotes pricing on request.
