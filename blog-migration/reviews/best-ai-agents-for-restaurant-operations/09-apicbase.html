9. ApicBase

Category: Inventory and Food Cost analytics

Multi-site foodservice groups use Apicbase to keep recipes, menu costing, stock, purchasing and production planning in one back-of-house system, which the company now markets as AI-powered. It earns its place when you run a central kitchen or many outlets, because a single recipe tweak has to reach every site at once.

Notable customers: Poule & Poulette, Pentahotels and The Avocado Show.

What's new: There's no dated product launch from Apicbase to report lately. What it did do, in November 2025, was host the first APX·25, an executive event built around its partner network in hospitality, foodservice and technology.

Key features:

  • One recipe book. Ingredients and recipes are stored centrally, and costs update on their own when inputs change.
  • Allergens and menu analysis. Menu engineering and allergen sheets pull directly from recipe data.
  • Multi-site stock. Counts and on-hand quantities can be checked for each location.
  • Purchasing in context. Suppliers and purchase orders are handled next to the recipes that use them.
  • Commissary planning. Production plans tell a central kitchen how much to make for every outlet.
  • Forecasts and integrations. Sales-based forecasts and cross-site food cost reports connect with tools such as Square and Deliverect.

Pricing: Apicbase quotes each deal individually, based on your location count and the modules you pick.
