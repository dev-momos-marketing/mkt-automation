3. 7Shifts

Category: Restaurant workforce & employee management software

For the labor half of daily operations, 7shifts brings schedules, the time clock, tip splits and payroll into one restaurant-only app. Details like tip pooling and POS-enforced schedules show who it was designed for.

A training module arrived in 2026, so new hires can check next week's shifts and work through onboarding lessons without switching apps.

Notable customers: HCI Hospitality (Freddy's Frozen Custard & Steakburgers franchisee), Boston Pizza (Regina), Conshy Girls Restaurant Group and Pitango Gelato.

What's new: Employee Training went live in September 2026 on the Premium plan. Managers write lessons and quizzes, hand them out to particular roles, departments or stores, then watch completion rates. Back in July 2025, the plan lineup changed to Comp, Essentials, Pro and Premium.

Key features:

  • Schedules built on forecasts. The drag-and-drop editor leans on projected sales and labor, which helps you staff Friday for the covers you expect.
  • Phone app for crew. Team members pick up open shifts, change availability and chat with each other on mobile.
  • Clock-in rules. Hooks into your POS and payroll make staff follow the posted schedule, cutting down on early punches and unplanned overtime.
  • Live labor percentage. Budgets and reporting compare what you're spending on labor with what the restaurant is selling as the week unfolds.
  • Tips, then payroll. Pooling and tip payouts come standard, while restaurant payroll is sold as an extra.
  • Checklists and courses. Shift tasks, onboarding packets and lessons inside the app make each opening and closing run the same way.

What makes them stand out: Everything centers on hourly crews that turn over often, so a single employee profile connects their schedule, tips and training.

Pricing: Comp is free for a single location with no more than 15 employees. Billed yearly, each location pays per month: Essentials runs $39.99 for teams of 30 or fewer, Pro $79.99, Premium $134.99. Skip the annual commitment and those three become $44.99, $89.99 and $149.99. Payroll is extra, Enterprise gets a custom quote, and you can trial Pro free for 14 days.

It's a great fit for: Single stores through franchise groups that need tighter labor cost and want both kitchen and floor staff working from the same schedule, tip and pay records.
