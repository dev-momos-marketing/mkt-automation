10. Freshchat

Freshchat is Freshworks' messaging product: a shared inbox for web chat, in-app, WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook Messenger, SMS and email, with Freddy AI Agent replying to questions automatically. It's also sold inside the Freshdesk Omni suite, which makes it a natural add for groups already on other Freshworks tools.

Notable customers: Orderin, Fantastic Services and FinAccel.

What's new: Freshworks' November 2025 launch gave Freshdesk Omni, where Freshchat lives, a new Command Center workspace, shipped together with the agentic version of Freddy AI.

What you can do with Freshchat:

  • Train Freddy on your FAQs. Point Freddy AI Agent at your help articles and it replies to guests on each channel you connect.
  • Build agents without code. In the Agent Studio, you can set up workflows such as collecting details for a group booking request.
  • Route chats automatically. Conversations are assigned to the right person, such as catering questions to your events lead.
  • Reach past guests first. Proactive campaigns over WhatsApp and web chat carry promotions and updates.
  • Support the people answering. Staff answering chats can get AI help from Freddy AI Copilot, sold separately.
  • Track bot and team results. Analytics cover both the AI agent and your human team.

Why Freshchat stands out: Every channel lands in a single inbox, and as part of Freshworks it extends Freshdesk or Freshsales without bringing in another vendor.

It's best for: Multi-site groups that want every guest message, on every channel, answered from one support desk with an AI front line.

Pricing: Free for up to 10 agents. Third parties report Growth at $19, Pro at $49 and Enterprise at $79 for each agent monthly on annual billing. The AI agent is charged by session (reportedly $49 for each 100), Copilot is extra, and it's worth confirming all of this with Freshworks.
