5. Slang AI

Slang AI is a voice AI phone host made for restaurants that answers calls, handles questions and reservations, and links to reservation and event platforms. Think of it as the phone-line counterpart to a website chatbot: it doesn't take orders by voice, and it texts callers a link to order online instead.

Notable customers: Gusto54 Restaurant Group.

What's new: In October 2026, Slang launched Superhost for full-service restaurants, a version that recalls past calls, guest preferences and visit history, stays within rules you configure and can talk in nearly 100 languages. Earlier, in February 2026, a $36M Series B brought its funding to $68M; at that point Slang put its footprint at more than 2,000 restaurant locations.

What you can do with Slang AI:

  • Pick up every call. The AI answers day and night in a natural voice and covers hours, location, menu and policies.
  • Book and move tables by phone. Integrations with OpenTable, SevenRooms and Yelp let callers make or change a reservation during the call.
  • Send event calls to the right place. Private-event inquiries are routed through Tripleseat, and staff can take over a call when needed.
  • Text takeout callers a link. Guests who want to order are sent your online ordering link by text.
  • Greet regulars by name. Superhost recognizes returning guests and their preferences, within the policy controls and greetings you define.
  • Read why people call. Call analytics and summaries show which questions come up most.

Why Slang AI stands out: It concentrates on the reservation line at full-service restaurants, and guest memory lets it treat a regular differently from someone calling for the first time.

It's best for: Full-service and reservation-led restaurants that miss calls during service and want an AI host to take bookings and answer questions.

Pricing: Slang AI doesn't publish prices; each restaurant gets a custom quote.
