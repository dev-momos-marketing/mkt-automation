11. Intercom

Intercom sells support software built around an AI agent called Fin, backed by a helpdesk where your team handles tickets, calls and help articles. In May 2026 the business took Fin as its company name while the helpdesk stayed Intercom, and since September 2026 it has belonged to Salesforce.

Notable customers: Anthropic, Lightspeed and Synthesia.

What's new: On September 10, 2026, Salesforce closed its purchase of Fin, formerly Intercom, which now serves customers as part of Salesforce AI Labs. The deal had been signed in June 2026, a month after the company adopted the Fin name.

What you can do with Intercom:

  • Answer guests with Fin. Fin reads your help articles and uses them to reply over web chat, email, WhatsApp, text, social apps and phone.
  • Cover the phone with Fin Voice. Calls about hours, gift cards or bookings can be answered by the same AI.
  • Work the rest in a shared inbox. Human agents handle tickets, and Copilot drafts suggested answers for them.
  • Publish a searchable help center. Guests can look up allergen notes, parking or group policies, and Fin uses the same articles.
  • Message guests proactively. Outbound messages can trigger from guest behavior, such as a visit to your catering page.
  • Measure AI and team performance. Reports show what Fin resolved and how your team handled the rest.

Why Intercom stands out: Fin is billed per resolved conversation, so the cost follows what the AI actually settles, and a single agent covers chat, email, phone and messaging apps.

It's best for: Restaurant groups and hospitality brands with a central guest-services team that want an AI agent for routine questions on every channel, backed by a full helpdesk.

Pricing: Helpdesk seats cost $29 (Essential), $85 (Advanced) or $132 (Expert) each month on annual billing. Fin comes on top at $0.99 for every resolved conversation.
