9. Hyperleap AI

With Hyperleap AI, a single bot trained once on your restaurant's information replies to guests in four places: your site's chat box, WhatsApp, Instagram DMs and Messenger. Its restaurant bundle, sold as an "AI front desk", deals with menu and opening-hours questions, dietary queries and booking links, a good match for cities where diners text on WhatsApp before they'd ever phone.

Notable customers: Jungle Lodges & Resorts, a state tourism resort group in Karnataka, India.

What's new: Hyperleap hasn't put out dated product news recently.

What you can do with Hyperleap AI:

  • Reply from your own menus. Upload menus, FAQ sheets and other files, and the bot bases what it says on them.
  • Share a booking link in chat. When a guest asks for a table, the front-desk flow sends your reservation link inside the conversation.
  • Pass allergy questions to staff. Questions about allergens go to a person, so the bot never guesses about ingredients.
  • Collect event and large-party requests. Details are captured as leads your team can follow up on.
  • Serve several locations in several languages. One knowledge base can cover multiple sites, and replies come in the guest's language.

Why Hyperleap stands out: It runs on the WhatsApp Business API next to Instagram and Messenger, so one inexpensive bot covers the apps guests use most in WhatsApp-first markets, with a Studio and API add-on for custom builds.

It's best for: Owners of one to a few venues, watching costs, whose diners usually make contact through WhatsApp, Instagram or the website chat box.

Pricing: Three monthly tiers: $40 (Plus), $100 (Pro) and $200 (Max). You can trial any of them for 7 days after entering card details. Hyperleap's own pages quote different AI reply limits per plan, so confirm the allowance before you choose.
