4. Popmenu

Popmenu is a marketing platform made only for restaurants, bundling your website, interactive menus, online ordering, email and SMS marketing, reservations, reputation tools and AI phone answering. Among chatbots, it's the option for operators whose guests still call more than they type, since its AI picks up those calls and can text an ordering link back.

Notable customers: La Parrilla, Steam Boys, The Hampton Social and B&B Butchers & Restaurant.

What's new: Spotlight Menu, which Popmenu describes as new menu technology, was announced in October 2026. In April 2026, a partnership with Yext let restaurants manage listings and reputation across more than 70 platforms from Popmenu, and since April 2025 Oracle Simphony users can buy it on Oracle Cloud Marketplace.

What you can do with Popmenu:

  • Let AI take the phone. AI answering handles common questions, orders and reservations, so the host stand isn't stuck on calls during the rush.
  • Text callers an ordering link. When someone rings to order takeout, the AI texts them the link to your ordering page.
  • Send campaigns to guest segments. Automated and AI-drafted email, SMS and social posts go to audiences built from guest behavior.
  • Take orders without commission. Online ordering carries a flat per-order fee, and there's a branded app guests can order from.
  • Answer reviews sooner. AI drafts replies to reviews alongside the feedback and reputation tools.

Why Popmenu stands out: Because it's built only for restaurants, the phone AI, website, menus and marketing share one set of menu and guest data, and it connects to POS systems including Square, Oracle and CAKE.

It's best for: Independent restaurants and small to mid-size groups that would rather get ordering, guest marketing and phone answering from a single restaurant vendor.

Pricing: Monthly plans run $179 for Starter, $299 for Essentials and $499 for Premier, or $159, $269 and $449 when prepaid for a year; Enterprise is quoted. Online orders cost a flat $1.00 each, with no percentage of sales. Ask which plan includes AI phone answering before you sign.
