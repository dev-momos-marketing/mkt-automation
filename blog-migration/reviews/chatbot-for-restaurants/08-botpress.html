8. Botpress

Botpress lets you assemble a chatbot of your own design and deploy it to web chat, messaging apps and voice, mixing a drag-and-drop builder with code where you need it. It's general-purpose and has no restaurant case studies, so a restaurant chatbot built on it is a project for developers or an agency.

Notable customers: Windstream, Able and Ruby Labs.

What's new: From May 14, 2026, new Botpress workspaces moved to a different pricing structure, while existing ones stay on the plan-plus-AI-Spend model. The company's last funding was a $25M Series B in June 2025; Framework Venture Partners led it, joined by the venture arms of Deloitte and HubSpot.

What you can do with Botpress:

  • Design the conversation yourself. A visual studio maps each flow, and code hooks add custom rules, such as a deposit policy for large parties.
  • Let the agent take action. An AI agent can use outside tools through APIs, for example looking up open tables before suggesting a time.
  • Add staff-facing bots. Restaurant chatbot roundups such as Hostie point to internal uses too, like answering employees' training and policy questions.
  • Answer from your own documents. Knowledge bases built from menus, allergen sheets and web pages keep replies tied to your information.
  • Publish one bot everywhere. The same build runs on web chat, WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram, SMS and other channels.
  • Hand off inside a helpdesk. Botpress Desk passes chats to humans, or you can connect Zendesk, Intercom or Freshdesk.

Why Botpress stands out: You own the logic and integrations, so the bot can reach your POS, CRM or reservation tool through APIs instead of waiting on a vendor's integration list.

It's best for: Chains and groups with a developer on staff, or budget for an agency, who want a guest chatbot built around their exact tech stack.

Pricing: You can build on a free tier. Outside pricing guides quote roughly $89 monthly for Plus and $495 for Team, with AI usage billed on top, and Enterprise is custom. Botpress reworked pricing for workspaces opened after mid-May 2026, so check its site before budgeting.
