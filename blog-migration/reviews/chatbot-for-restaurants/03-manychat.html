3. ManyChat

Manychat automates marketing conversations across Instagram DMs, Messenger, WhatsApp, TikTok and text messages. For a restaurant, it acts as a chatbot inside the apps where guests already message you, answering comments, story replies and DMs with menus, offers and sign-up links.

Manychat's blog includes restaurant stories such as La Catrina and a chain served by Astral Restaurant Systems, whose campaign was credited with 7,000 visits and $52,000 of added revenue. Manychat published both stories itself, so read the numbers as its own claims.

Notable customers: Nike, The New York Times and a restaurant chain served by Astral Restaurant Systems.

What's new: Since March 2026, what you pay depends on how many active contacts you message each month, and the free tier stops at 25. Follow to DM arrived in October 2025, sending an automatic hello to every new Instagram follower. The company's $140M Series B, from April 2025, is funding AI agents for DMs and chat apps.

What you can do with ManyChat:

  • Turn comments into conversations. If a follower types "menu" under a post or answers a story, Manychat can send the menu link or tonight's specials straight to their inbox.
  • Handle questions typed freely. AI steps make sense of questions in the guest's own words and can collect an email address along the way.
  • Message opted-in followers. Broadcasts and sequences deliver discount codes, new dishes and event news to people who asked to hear from you.
  • Build a contact list from posts and ads. Keywords and ads that open a DM turn a popular post or paid campaign into tagged contacts you can reach again.
  • Let staff take over. A live chat inbox lets someone on your team step into any conversation the bot can't finish.

Why ManyChat stands out: It puts the chatbot in Instagram and Messenger, where many diners already send questions, so nobody has to be pushed to your website first. Drag-and-drop flows and templates mean a manager can launch an automation without a developer.

It's best for: Restaurants whose guests find them on Instagram or Facebook and that want comment replies, DMs and promotions running on their own. It handles social messaging only, so reservations and operations need other tools.

Pricing: Plans are priced by active contacts each month, and the free plan covers 25. Pro covers 2,500 contacts for $39 monthly ($29 on annual billing), and Business covers 7,500 for $99 monthly ($69 on annual billing). Essential and Advanced tiers are also listed, extra contacts are charged, Meta bills WhatsApp fees separately, and prices vary by region.
