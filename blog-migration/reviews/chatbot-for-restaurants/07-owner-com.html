7. Owner.com

Owner.com gives independent restaurants one system for their website (built by AI), direct ordering without commissions, their own app, loyalty and marketing that runs on its own. It now also offers a POS and AI phone ordering that places real orders, so its guest-facing automation reaches the phone as well as text and email.

Notable customers: Saffron Indian Restaurant, Goi Cuon, Doo-Dah Diner and Township Line Pizza.

What's new: Owner.com's $240M Series D closed in August 2026 with Goldman Sachs Alternatives in the lead and a $2.3B price tag on the business. The plan for that cash is becoming an AI operating system for all kinds of local businesses. Reporting on the round said its products now include a restaurant POS and AI phone ordering.

What you can do with Owner.com:

  • Take phone orders with AI. An AI answers calls and enters orders directly, so a ringing phone doesn't pull staff off the line.
  • Message past guests on autopilot. Texts and emails are timed and targeted using each guest's order history.
  • Win back delivery-app customers. Targeted outreach nudges people who found you on third-party apps to reorder directly.
  • Launch a search-friendly site. AI builds the website and ordering flow, catering orders included.
  • Run loyalty in your own app. A rewards program and branded mobile app keep regulars ordering from you.

Why Owner.com stands out: It gives independents the kind of digital ordering and marketing stack big chains build in-house, at published flat prices, and Owner handles customer support for your guests.

It's best for: Independents and small groups paying heavy delivery-app commissions who want guests ordering from them directly.

Pricing: Features are the same on both options, and you can leave without a long commitment. Flex charges $249 monthly and takes 5% of each order from you; Flat Rate charges $499 monthly and takes nothing per order. Diners also pay a 5% support fee when they check out, while card fees run 2.9% + $0.30 a transaction per Owner's blog.
